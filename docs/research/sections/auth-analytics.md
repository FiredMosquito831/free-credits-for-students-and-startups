### Auth0 Free Plan

- **Auth0 Free Plan (always-free tier)** (high) — $0/month, permanent. Up to 25,000 MAU; 1 tenant; 1 custom domain; 1 Enterprise Connection (added Feb 2026); Self-Service SSO and Inbound SCIM (added Feb 2026); unlimited Social Connections; unlimited Okta Connections (subject to system limitations); 5 Organizations on B2C, Unlimited* Organizations on B2B; 3 Admin/Contributor seats; 5 Actions + Forms; 1 Event Stream; 1,000 M2M tokens; 1-day log retention; 2 Token Vault slots; passwordless auth, passkeys, brand customization, Basic Attack Protection (brute force + suspicious IP throttling), community support, Inbound SCIM, Okta Universal Logout integration. No credit card needed to sign up. Sign up at https://auth0.com/signup (or dev.auth0.com/signup); the Free plan is the default subscription. Email Workflow is NOT available on Free. Exceeding 25,000 MAU triggers a quota warning; you cannot self-upgrade past the free cap without contacting sales.
  - *Eligibility:* Anyone globally — no .edu email, no startup proof, no funding or age cap. No credit card required for the free plan itself. Free tenants historically available in 5 regions (US, UK, EU, JP, AUS).
  - *How to claim:* Sign up free at auth0.com/signup; the Free plan is automatic — no application, no card. Custom domain: add it in dashboard (a card is collected purely for verification and, per Auth0's FAQ, you are never charged).
  - *Expiry:* No expiry — free forever, as of 2026-10-22. Existing free tenants had entitlements auto-applied when the plan was upgraded (Sep 2024).
  - *Gotchas:* 1 tenant max on Free (one 'team' per team-owner email; a second team needs a different email). Credit-card verification required to add the 1 custom domain (verification only, never billed, per official FAQ). 1-day log retention, no email workflows, no log streaming, no Pro MFA, no Role Management/Account Linking, no CIBA — many 'everything in Free, plus' claims in the marketing copy don't change these exclusions. 25,000 is an MAU cap (users who authenticated in a calendar month), not a total-user cap. Unlimited* is 'subject to system limitations'.
  - *Source:* https://auth0.com/pricing

The pricing page itself does not advertise the 22-day trial, so treat exact day count as community-confirmed (21–22 days reported in different threads). No CC required for the trial itself (CC only if you later add a custom domain or subscribe).
