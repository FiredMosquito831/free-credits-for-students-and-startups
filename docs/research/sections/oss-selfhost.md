### AppFlowy

- **Personal plan: free cloud tier at $0/month** (high) — Pricing page lists Personal at '$0/ month' with Billing 'None (free)'. Pro is '$12/ member / month' billed annually ($144 per member per year), or $16 per member per month billed monthly. Pro removes the storage cap, sets revision history to 90 days, and makes forms and charts unlimited.

  - *How to claim:* Open https://appflowy.com/pricing/ and click 'Get started' on the Personal card, or 'Start for free' in the header. Both link to https://appflowy.com/app. The pricing page describes no payment step for Personal.
  - *Expiry:* No end date stated for Personal. Per the pricing FAQ, cancelling Pro moves the workspace 'immediately' to Personal, and 'any usage above the free limits may be restricted'.
  - *Gotchas:* The 100 MB storage cap and the 1-form and 1-chart caps are reached quickly by a small team. The changelog (v0.14.5, 2026-09-22) mentions file-size and workspace-storage limit messages, which suggests the limits are enforced. The brief's 'unlimited guest editors' is NOT confirmed for Personal: the pricing table shows Personal guest access as 'Not shown', and the FAQ's free guest-editor wording is attached to Pro. No credit card requirement is mentioned for Personal; this was not tested at sign-up.
