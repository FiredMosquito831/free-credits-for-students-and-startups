### Airtable

- **Airtable Free plan (permanent, no card)** (high) — Free forever: 1,000 records per base (cumulative across all tables), 1,000 API calls per workspace/month (5 req/sec rate limit), 1 GB attachment storage per base, 2 weeks revision/snapshot history, 75 unique automations per base, 100 automation runs per workspace/month, 500 AI credits per editor+/month. Max 5 Editor/Creator collaborators + 50 Commenters + unlimited read-only. No extensions, no sync integrations, no 'Run a script' automation action, 'Send email' action limited to base collaborators, no record coloring, Airtable branding on forms cannot be removed. Data is never deleted when you exceed limits; new records/attachments are blocked until upgrade.
  - *Eligibility:* Anyone; no credit card, no trial. Free plan workspaces can coexist with paid workspaces on the same account.
  - *How to claim:* Sign up at airtable.com (any email) → first workspace auto-starts a 14-day Team trial → simply do NOT add a payment method → workspace stays Free. No application needed.
  - *Expiry:* No expiry; plan persists indefinitely. API limits reset first day of each calendar month.
  - *Gotchas:* 1,000 API calls/month is a hard cap enforced since Jan 2025 (was previously effectively unlimited) — Zapier/Make/custom integrations can break silently. Record limit is per base, not per table: two tables of 500 records each already hit the cap. Automations cap was only raised from 50→75 in Sep 2026. The 14-day Team trial is one-time per account, so it can mask the real Free limits when evaluating headroom.
  - *Source:* https://support.airtable.com/articles/2277136852-airtable-plans-overview
- **14-day Team trial (automatic, first workspace only)** (high) — First workspace of a new account is auto-upgraded to a 14-day Team trial. Trial caps: 50,000 records/base, 10 GB attachments, 365-day revision history, 25,000 automation runs/month, 10 synced tables, 7,500 AI credits/editor. Excludes 'Run a script' automation and premium form features (custom headers, branding removal, conditional groups).
  - *Eligibility:* Any new Airtable account; no credit card required to start. Future Free workspaces under the same account do NOT get another trial.
  - *How to claim:* Automatic on account creation. To keep it free: don't add a payment method — after 14 days the workspace reverts to the Free plan. Adding a payment method during the trial auto-upgrades to paid Team and bills accordingly.
  - *Expiry:* 14 days, once per account.
  - *Gotchas:* Trial revision history is only 2 weeks, not Team's 1 year. Easy to accidentally convert to a paid subscription just by saving a card. Paid Team is $24/user/month monthly (billed annually $20/user/month).
  - *Source:* https://support.airtable.com/articles/2277136852-airtable-plans-overview
- **Airtable Student plan — free Team for 6-24 months** (high) — Qualifying college/university students: 120-day Team trial granted immediately when the account/workspace uses a .edu address, then application for a FREE Student plan = Team-level features for a single workspace for 6-24 months (length based on expected graduation date). Student plan caps: 50,000 records/base, 20 GB attachments/base, no 'Run a script' automation action, some form/view customization omitted.
  - *Eligibility:* Requires .edu email (international .edu / accredited-institution emails also accepted; non-.edu applicants can apply but may need extra documentation). Must have an existing Airtable account tied to the applied email or Airtable cannot review the application. Reviews typically ~2 business days.
  - *How to claim:* 1) Create an Airtable account with your .edu email → 120-day Team trial starts on first workspace. 2) Apply via 'Apply for a free student plan' (support.airtable.com/articles/4920135568, linked from airtable.com/pricing's 'Special plans for Nonprofits or Education'). 3) On approval, account's plan type is switched by Airtable support; use the .edu email to log in.
  - *Expiry:* 6-24 months from approval, tied to expected graduation date. No auto-renewal; after the window you revert to Free unless you (re-)apply while still a student or pay for Team.
  - *Gotchas:* This supersedes the older 'Airtable Classrooms' branding — no standalone Classrooms product is listed on current official pages. Student plan covers ONE workspace only. The 120-day trial clock runs BEFORE any approval, so apply early in the trial. .edu required in practice; no regional lock (international students apply via the same form, per 2020 newsroom + current FAQ).
  - *Source:* https://support.airtable.com/articles/4920135568-applying-for-non-profit-education-and-student-airtable-plans
- **Education plan for staff/educators — 50% off Team** (high) — Employees of accredited educational institutions: 50% off the monthly Team rate → $12/user/month (based on $24/user/month monthly Team; annual-plan discount does NOT apply to discounted plans, so effectively $144/user/yr). Includes 1-year revision/snapshot retention. Same 120-day Team trial via .edu email before application.
  - *Eligibility:* Requires .edu email + certification that the institution is accredited and not-for-profit (usually provided by the institution's IT administrator). Only accredited educational organizations qualify; government, religious, and political organizations excluded. Application must match an existing Airtable account. Discounted plans require credit-card payment; no one-time payments.
  - *How to claim:* 1) Account with .edu email (120-day Team trial auto-starts). 2) Apply via 'Apply for a discounted education plan' link on support.airtable.com/articles/4920135568. 3) Institute admin supplies accreditation proof; Airtable responds within ~2 business days. 4) Higher tiers (Business/Enterprise Scale edu discounts) are sales-only via Airtable Sales.
  - *Expiry:* Runs while the person is employed at the institution; re-verification at renewal; no public auto-renewal term stated.
  - *Gotchas:* Not free — 50% off, card required. Unaccredited/unregistered institutions cannot apply. AI features not covered by the discount per third-party summary (airtable.com/solutions/education says edu discounts on Business/Enterprise need a sales conversation).
  - *Source:* https://support.airtable.com/articles/4920135568-applying-for-non-profit-education-and-student-airtable-plans
- **Airtable for Startups — up to $500 credit (direct application)** (medium) — One-time $500 in Airtable credit applied to a named workspace, plus access to startup content/office hours. Credit does not require equity. Applicants must create an Airtable account and submit the application form with personal/company info and the workspace ID where credits are applied. Offer details (yayimages listing, re-published Airtable offer text): limited to companies that have raised up to Series B; not available to companies already on an invoiced Airtable plan; one redemption per company; existing Airtable customers are also eligible.

  - *How to claim:* Create an Airtable account with your company email → fill the Airtable for Startups application form (current form: airtable.com/appGGpHNlmMUkfLol/shrghfGlQLt411Tyr; landing page airtable.com/startups) → wait 3-10 business days → credit appears in workspace settings → use credit toward Team/Business plans.
  - *Expiry:* One-time $500; partner-distributed credits (see next finding) explicitly state they do not expire. No stated expiration on the direct $500 credit — confirm in the approval email.
  - *Gotchas:* The official airtable.com/startups page could not be fully rendered for automated fetch (browser-gated), so exact current terms come from third-party listings that republish the offer text — verify amounts in the official form before applying. Credit applies to a single workspace; don't create new workspaces after applying. One startup listing (TechScoop) claims '1 year free Enterprise' for this program — this contradicts Airtable's sales-led Enterprise model and is almost certainly wrong; do not rely on it.
  - *Source:* https://www.airtable.com/startups

Companies within the partner's stage caps (HubSpot for Startups: seed to Series B-scale, under $10M raised as a program rule).   - *How to claim:* Redeem through the partner's marketplace/dashboard: sign up for free Airtable using your company email → submit the partner's form with the same email → credit posts to workspace settings after approval.
  - *Expiry:* Credits state explicitly 'do not expire' (partner terms, as of 2025-2026 listings). One redemption per company per partner.
  - *Gotchas:* Amount varies by partner ($500 direct vs $2,000 partner). Credits are product credit, not cash. (2) Q3 2025 — ProductCentral add-on discontinued; Enterprise Scale Portals pricing moved to 'contact sales'. (3) Sep 2026 — a new 'automations per base' cap of 50 was imposed on ALL plans, then reversed two days later into tiered caps: 75 (Free) / 100 (Team) / 150 (Business) / 200 (Enterprise Scale) — automation RUN limits unchanged. ### Algolia Free Plan

- **Algolia Free Plan (free tier)** (high) — Standing no-cost tier: 10,000 search requests/month, 50,000 records, 5,000 recommendation requests/month, 5,000 resources crawled (crawls)/month. Includes core Search, Recommend, SDKs and UI components; explicitly usable in production. Hard caps: 10 KB/record, 1 GB/application data, 1 GB/index, 1 application.
  - *Eligibility:* Open to anyone (developers, startups, teams). No .edu email, no company-age or funding cap, no region limit. No credit card required. Self-serve.
  - *How to claim:* Self-serve sign up at https://dashboard.algolia.com/users/sign_up (no application, approval, or code). Then open https://dashboard.algolia.com. FAQ links a demo request at https://www.algolia.com/demorequest.
  - *Expiry:* No expiry stated (ongoing free tier). If account is inactive >30 days, Algolia may remove Subscriber Data. Reaching the usage limit: service may be suspended or an Algolia attribution may be added to search results.
  - *Gotchas:* Not unlimited: 50K-record and 10K-request caps simply stop (no billing on the free tier, unlike Grow). 50K records vs 100K on paid plans. No hosting-location choice on Free (US/UK/EU only appear under Grow). At usage limit results may carry Algolia attribution; idle accounts can lose data.
  - *Source:* https://www.algolia.com/pricing/free-plan
- **Algolia Startup Program (trial credits)** (high) — $10,000 in Algolia credits, valid for 12 months, plus world-class support. Bonus perks: credits for other SaaS products (DataDog, Notion and more) and Algolia Academy education resources. After credits are used, the account is automatically moved to Pay-as-you-go, or committed plans with ~20% discounts.
  - *Eligibility:* Startups only, and new Algolia customers only. Main-page criteria: company less than 7 years old AND less than $15M total funding raised. The page's FAQ contradicts this: less than 3 years old AND less than $5M raised. No founder-count requirement; no .edu requirement; no region limit stated.
  - *How to claim:* Apply via the form linked from the page (a support-ticket form): https://support.algolia.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=5005635939217 . Unresolved questions: startups@algolia.com.
  - *Expiry:* Credits lapse after 12 months; unused credits expire. No renewal mechanism described (appears to be a one-time grant).
  - *Gotchas:* Credits are non-transferable. Not eligible if you have used any other coupon code in the past. Eligibility numbers conflict on-page (7yr/US$15M on the main block vs 3yr/US$5M in the FAQ) - confirm current thresholds before applying. Post-credit auto-moves to pay-as-you-go.
  - *Source:* https://www.algolia.com/industries/startups
- **Algolia for Open Source (AOS) free offer** (high) — A Standard PAYG plan with 200 units free, which translates to 200,000 records + 200,000 search requests + up to 2,000,000 indexing operations per month - a stated ~$180/month value.
  - *Eligibility:* Three buckets: (1) licensed open-source software under a valid OSS license; (2) developer-community projects that make content discoverable/searchable and useful; (3) legally-recognized non-profit or non-governmental organizations. All must be non-commercial, publicly available, and exceed the free tier's limits. Commercially-backed non-commercial projects are reviewed case-by-case. No .edu requirement; no startup age/funding cap.
  - *How to claim:* Submit your project through the application/submission form on the page (or use the 'Start Free' sign-up link). Questions: community@algolia.com.
  - *Expiry:* Ongoing for qualifying projects; no stated expiry. If a project is terminated, acquired, or shifts to a commercial model, the account is downgraded 'in a timely manner.'
  - *Gotchas:* Requires a 'Search by Algolia' logo attribution in your search results/site, linking to algolia.com (alternatives can be discussed). Must remain non-commercial and publicly available. Credit-card requirement not stated on the page. 200 units (~$180/month value) is the cap.
  - *Source:* https://www.algolia.com/for-open-source

  - *Eligibility:* Legally-recognized non-profit / non-governmental organizations; must be non-commercial and publicly available. No .edu requirement; no startup age/funding cap.
  - *How to claim:* Submit through the application form on the page; questions to community@algolia.com.
  - *Expiry:* Ongoing; account downgraded on status change, termination, or acquisition.
  - *Gotchas:* Logo attribution required. The non-profit category overlaps with the open-source program. Credit-card requirement not stated. Status must remain non-profit/non-commercial or the account is downgraded.
  - *Source:* https://www.algolia.com/for-non-profit
