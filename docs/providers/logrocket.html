LogRocket: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: 14-day free trial of all LogRocket features, no credit card. 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

14-day free trial of all LogRocket features, no credit card high confidence

Official pricing page states: "Start free trial — no credit card required" and "Access all of LogRocket's features free for 14 days. Cancel any time." The trial covers all features across Core/Pro plans: session replay, product analytics, error monitoring, logs (analytics/error/log events are unlimited on every plan); price after trial starts at $176/mo (annual-basis, at the 25K sessions/mo default slider) or ~$860/mo with Conditional Recording. Final price also depends on seats, retention, and add-ons. Verified directly at logrocket.com/pricing on 2026-10-22.

Eligibility
Anyone; no student/startup status, .edu email, funding cap, or region limit published. No credit card required to start.
How to claim
Go to logrocket.com/pricing -> click "Start free trial" -> create account (no credit card) -> add LogRocket SDK to your app; trial of paid plans begins. Sales line (855) 564-7625 for questions.
Expiry
14 days from signup; "Cancel any time"; no published renewal — re-signup after trial converts to a paid Core/Pro/Enterprise plan (contact-sales billing).
Gotchas
Trial is time-boxed, not a free tier: the perpetual Free plan (1,000 sessions/mo, 1-month retention, 3 seats) was killed in mid-2026, so nothing is free forever. No CC on file means no auto-convert, but continued use past day 14 requires a sales-negotiated paid contract. Pro plan bundles 500 free API/MCP credits/mo and Enterprise 2,000/mo — but only on paid plans.
Source
logrocket.com

Startup discount for companies under $50M funding (sales-negotiated, not free) medium confidence

The official pricing page's "Want a discount?" section lists "Startups under $50M funding" as a discount-eligible category (alongside bulk volume, non-profits, open-source projects, multi-year contracts, case-study partners, and students). No fixed discount percentage or credit amount is published — LogRocketCreds/StartupCreds confirm there is no dedicated apply URL: logrocket.com/startups returns HTTP 404 (verified today). Third-party trackers (StartupPerks, innovarastudios) describe it as: any startup (seed/Series A/B+ or bootstrapped) with <$50M total raised, global eligibility, no VC/accelerator backing required; benefit is a custom discount that persists "while eligible".

Eligibility
Startups with less than $50M total funding raised (per official page). Bootstrapped founders reportedly eligible. No accelerator/VC requirement. Company email on own domain (per StartupPerks).
How to claim
No self-serve application (logrocket.com/startups is 404). Contact sales via live chat on logrocket.com/pricing or phone (855) 564-7625, and verify funding stage; third-party trackers say approval is sales-mediated.
Expiry
No published end date; discount persists while the company stays under the $50M funding threshold.
Gotchas
This is a discount, NOT a free offer — nothing is free. Discount size is unpublished and negotiated by deal size ("The bigger the deal, the more there is to talk about"). A separate channel deal (Ramp partner program) reportedly offers 20% off the first year via application. Eligibility is claimed on the official page but mechanics are third-party-sourced (medium confidence).
Source
logrocket.com

Student discount via sales (no .edu education license exists) medium confidence

"Students" is listed on the official pricing page among discount-eligible categories, same as startups/non-profits/OSS. There is no student-free license or education program: logrocket.com/education returns HTTP 404 (verified today), logrocket.com/free-accounts also 404, and no .edu-verified free tier exists or was offered. LogRocket does not appear in student-perk directories (StudentPerks, GitHub Student Pack, etc.) as of 2025-2026.

Eligibility
Students per official category list; no published .edu email requirement or self-serve verification — discount is negotiated through sales.
How to claim
Contact LogRocket sales (live chat on logrocket.com/pricing or (855) 564-7625) and request the student discount; there is no dedicated application page.
Expiry
No published end date; presumably valid while student status holds (unverified).
Gotchas
Discount, not free. No amount published. Students get no standalone free account — the only time-limited free access is the 14-day trial. Third-party "free for students" lists do not include LogRocket.
Source
logrocket.com

Third-party $500 LogRocket credits for startups via SaaSOffers (not a LogRocket program) low confidence

SaaSOffers lists a "$500 in credits" deal for LogRocket session replay + error tracking, labeled "Verified October 2026" and targeting startups debugging user experience. The deal is brokered by SaaSOffers, not run by LogRocket: claiming requires a SaaSOffers account with their premium tier ($79/yr, per the deal page), and the page publishes no funding cap, credit expiry, or redemption mechanics. Treated as a trial-credit adjacent channel only.

Eligibility
Per SaaSOffers listing: startups (early-stage, growing SaaS teams, solo founders); sole stated requirement is "startup debugging user experience." No funding cap published.
How to claim
Create SaaSOffers account -> upgrade to SaaSOffers premium ($79/yr) -> click Claim Deal on the LogRocket page -> follow activation steps.
Expiry
No expiry published; deal re-verified Oct 2026; access gated behind SaaSOffers premium subscription.
Gotchas
Effectively not free: the $79/yr premium gate must be paid to access the $500 credits. It is third-party-sourced; LogRocket's own site publishes no such credit offer. Also, SaaSOffers' sibling page still quotes an old plan structure (Free 1K sessions, Team $99, Professional $295) that no longer matches LogRocket's current page — verify before relying on it.

2025–26 changes

  • LogRocket's one perpetual free offer is GONE. The Free plan — \"$0/mo free forever, 1,000 sessions/month, 1-month data retention, 3 seats, unlimited analytics/error/log events\" (plan history: Free/Team/Professional/Enterprise with Free + Team $69 + Professional $295, per trackers' captures through May 2026) — was removed when LogRocket repriced to session-based Core/Pro/Enterprise pricing. Removal window per independent trackers: PricingSaaS records the Free tier killed between its 2026-06-29 and 2026-07-06 captures (\"Logrocket kills free plan, quadruples Enterprise API credits to 2,000/mo\"); AgentDeals discovered it between 2026-06-08 and 2026-08-19 captures; stackwrite's 2026-10-03 re-check confirms \"LogRocket's free plan, which no longer exists at all.\" As of 2026-10-22 the official pricing page lists no free tier at all — only the 14-day trial. Trial packaging also churned in 2025-2026: Q1 2025 \"Free PoC\" became fixed \"21-Day Trial/Pilot\" for Professional/Enterprise; by the 2025-07-06 capture it was reworded to duration-less \"Free Trial/Free Pilot\"; the current page (verified today) says 14 days, no credit card required. Dedicated program URLs are dead: logrocket.com/startups = 404, logrocket.com/education = 404, logrocket.com/free-accounts = 404 (all checked 2026-10-22). Caveat: stale third-party listings still advertise the dead free tier — GitHub Marketplace shows \"Free 1,000 sessions/month\", G2 lists entry price \"Free, 1000 Sessions/Month\", and DriftingDesk lists a \"1,000 Free Sessions\" program \"Verified Active 2026\"; all contradict the official page and should be treated as outdated. Paid-plan perks that changed alongside: Pro API/MCP credits 200->500/mo and Enterprise 200->2,000/mo (July 2026).
