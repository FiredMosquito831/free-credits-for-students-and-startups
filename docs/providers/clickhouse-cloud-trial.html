ClickHouse Cloud free trial: $300 credits over 30 days (no credit card) high confidence

Official pricing page (verified 2026-10-22): "$300 in credits over 30 days", 1 ClickHouse Credit (CHC) = $1 USD of usage. FAQ: "Is there a free tier? ClickHouse Cloud starts with $300 in credits over 30 days, no credit card required." Trial ends after 30 days or once credits are consumed, whichever comes first; account then converts to pay-as-you-go with no monthly minimum (invoiced in $0.01 increments). No permanent free tier: entry paid tier is Basic (up to 1 TB storage, 8-12 GiB total memory, 24h backups retained 1 day, 1 availability zone). Compute scales to zero when idle ($0 for idle compute time; storage still bills continuously).

Eligibility
Anyone. Sign up at https://console.clickhouse.cloud/signUp via email, Google SSO, or Microsoft SSO. No .edu email, no startup status, no company size limits stated. Available on AWS, GCP, and Azure. Credit card explicitly NOT required.
How to claim
Single step: sign up at console.clickhouse.cloud/signUp; the $300 trial credit is granted to new organizations automatically (campaign page: "Get started for free... Create a free account"). No application or form.
Expiry
30 days or credit exhaustion, whichever comes first; no renewal or re-application. After the trial the account continues on pay-as-you-go usage-based billing.
Gotchas
Trial is not a free tier: once the 30 days/credits end, usage billing begins (no card on file doesn't mean no bill - you can accrue charges after trial, so monitor console). Egress/data transfer, backups, and ClickPipes ingestion are billed separately; public internet egress is from ~$0.1152/GB since the Jan 2025 price change (egress used to be free - now a migration 'divorce fee'). Storage bills even when compute is scaled to zero. Basic plan caps (1 TB, 8-12 GiB, single AZ) apply to the cheapest tier.
Source
clickhouse.com

Open-source ClickHouse - free forever (self-hosted) high confidence

"Open source (free forever)" per the official pricing page; Apache 2.0 core, no license cost, no per-unit billing, no duration limit. Official one-liner install: `curl https://clickhouse.com/ | sh`. This is the genuine free option for students on any budget: run it on a laptop, student cluster, or own cloud VM. Certain advanced/enterprise features are reserved for the proprietary/Enterprise edition or Cloud.

Eligibility
Anyone (students, academics, startups, enterprises); no .edu or application required.
How to claim
Install locally or on self-managed infrastructure via the curl one-liner or package manager; no signup.
Expiry
No expiry; license is free indefinitely.
Gotchas
Self-hosted means you own ops (upgrades, replication, hardware, cloud instance costs). Open-core model: enterprise-locked features unavailable in the OSS build; OSS version lags behind Cloud features.
Source
clickhouse.com

ClickHouse Startup Program: $5,000 credits for 12 months (partner-gated) high confidence

Official startup-offer page (verified 2026-10-22): "Founders can apply for $5000 in ClickHouse Cloud credits, expert training, and promotion opportunities". Credits valid for 12 months. Package also includes ClickHouse Basic Support, expert training, a joint blog-post feature/promotion on ClickHouse channels, and speaking slots at ClickHouse community events.

Eligibility
All of: company less than 3 years old; pre-Series D ("haven't raised a Series D or later funding round"); has NEVER been a paid ClickHouse Cloud customer; belongs to a program run by one of the listed partners (Antler, Bessemer, Blackbird, Google For Startups, Lightspeed, Merantix, Station F, TechLeap, TechStars - selected via dropdown in the form); holds a valid verification code obtained from the accelerator/incubator/VC. No .edu/region limits stated on the page. Headcount limits not stated.
How to claim
Three steps on the page: (1) sign up for a free ClickHouse Cloud trial (console signup); (2) complete the application form with your ClickHouse Organization ID, company details, and the partner-program dropdown; (3) wait for approval - the team reviews and confirms the credits.
Expiry
Credits valid for 12 months from approval; no renewal or re-application window described on the page. One-time, new-customer-only offer.
Gotchas
Partner-gated: you cannot apply directly - must be inside a listed partner's program and have its verification code, or the application can't be validated. New-to-ClickHouse requirement excludes any prior paid usage. Hitting Series D before applying disqualifies. Support included is 'Basic' tier only, not enterprise support. No stated extension of the 12-month window.
