Supabase Free Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Supabase Free Plan (free tier) — the only direct, self-serve free offer. 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Supabase Free Plan (free tier) — the only direct, self-serve free offer high confidence

$0/month, verified on the official pricing page today: 500 MB Postgres per active project (Shared CPU, 500 MB RAM), 1 GB file storage (50 MB max upload), 5 GB egress + 5 GB cached egress, 50,000 MAU included (50K third-party-auth MAU too, raised from 50 in March 2025), 500,000 Edge Function invocations/month, 25 Edge Functions allowed, 2M realtime messages/month, 200 realtime peak connections, 1 GB log ingestion + 100 GB log query, unlimited API requests, 2 active projects per user (limit applies across ALL orgs where the user is Owner/Admin), community support only. Quotas are organization-level. 500 MB database limit is per ACTIVE project (paused/deleted projects don't count). No trial needed: this is the permanent free tier.

Eligibility
Everyone — no .edu email, no startup requirements, no funding cap, no region limit, credit card NOT required. 2 active projects cap per user, counted across every Free-plan org they own/admin.
How to claim
Sign up at supabase.com/dashboard -> create an organization (plan: Free) -> create up to 2 projects. No application, no verification. 'Start for Free' on the pricing page.
Expiry
Indefinite / free forever. Projects auto-pause after 1 week of inactivity (data kept; paused projects don't count toward the 2-active-project limit and can be resumed). Paid-plan behavior (no pausing) is not available on Free.
Gotchas
Free projects sleep after 7 idle days — bites students between deadlines; data persists while paused but the DB is down until resumed. Hitting quota on a Free org doesn't auto-upgrade: overages simply aren't billed on Free; you must upgrade to Pro ($25/mo + $10/mo compute credits) to get more. SSO MAU and Storage Images Transformed are unavailable on Free. Only 1-day log retention. To mix plans you must create separate orgs. No backups on Free. Third-party sites' 'up to $10K' / '$2,500' / '6 months Team plan free' Supabase startup-credit claims are unverified against any Supabase page and contradict each other.
Source
supabase.com

Student/education offers: NONE (no education license, no .edu discount, not in GitHub Student Developer Pack) high confidence

Supabase has no student program, no education pricing, and no application path. supabase.com/education returns 404; the pricing page contains no student/academic wording; Supabase does not appear in the GitHub Student Developer Pack offer list (checked today); a 2024 Supabase-maintained GitHub discussion confirms the stance is 'use the free tier.' A co-founder once said YC Summer Fellows students (a YC run, not Supabase-run, grants program) received $10K in Supabase credits (2025 cohort only). Students effectively get exactly the general Free plan.

Eligibility
N/A — students get no special tier beyond the general Free plan (no .edu verification, no academic license).
How to claim
Nothing to claim; sign up for the Free plan like anyone else. If a class project needs more, email support@supabase.io (no program exists to apply to).
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
Pricing-page footer copy 'free projects for students and hobbyists' (2020 founding post) is aspirational marketing, not a program. Do not rely on third-party 'Supabase for students' deal pages — they all just restate the free tier plus paid upsells.
Source
supabase.com

Startup credits via partner channels: $300 over 12 months (AWS Activate) and $300 over 1 year (Mercury Perks), plus unpublished YC/accelerator/investor partner tiers medium confidence

No self-serve application on supabase.com. Credits are distributed through partner deal books: (1) AWS Startups/Activate marketplace offer: '$300 Supabase credits for free over 12 months' (AWS-official listing; page now sits behind login at startups.aws.com). (2) Mercury Perks (mercury.com/perks/supabase, live-verified by a third party 2026-10-20): $300 in Supabase credits over 1 year, new Supabase customers only, for Mercury business-account holders (Mercury requires US-formed company). (3) YC and 80+ accelerator/VC partner tier: amounts unpublished, delivered through the partner's own portal (Supabase blog, Oct 2025: 1,000+ YC companies on Supabase; 'Supabase YC Program' gives YC companies credits). Non-dilutive, no equity.

Eligibility
AWS route: AWS Activate membership (per third-party detail, institutional-VC-backed, pre-seed to Series A, < $5M raised — verify in the AWS portal since the offer page is login-gated). Mercury route: Mercury business customer + NEW Supabase customer. YC/accelerator route: being in that partner's portfolio. No funding cap/age cap published for the $300 tiers themselves.
How to claim
AWS: redeem the Supabase offer inside the AWS Activate/Startups portal and attach to a Supabase org. Mercury: redeem from the Mercury Perks portal. YC/investor: claim through your accelerator or VC's startup-perks portal. Credits attach to one Supabase organization (moves only via support ticket).
Expiry
Credits delivered over 12 months and reduce only FUTURE invoices (not already-issued ones); no published review SLA; partner terms can change.
Gotchas
Page-gating: AWS offer page redirects to login, so current terms must be confirmed inside the AWS portal. The '$2,500', '$3,000 over 12 months', '6 months Team plan free' figures circulating on deal aggregators conflict and are NOT published on any Supabase page — treat as unverified. No public application means bootstrapped startups with no partner affiliation get only the Free plan.
Source
mercury.com

Trial credits: NONE — no free Pro/Team trial or new-signup credit high confidence

Supabase has no trial-credit program for Pro/Team. Paid plans are billed monthly up front (Pro $25/mo + $10/mo compute credits covering one Micro instance; Team from $599/mo). The only 'credits' a new user gets are partner-distributed (AWS/Mercury/YC, above). The 2026-09 OrioleDB public-beta and AI/model-gateway features carry standard Postgres pricing, no student/startup-specific credit.

Eligibility
N/A
How to claim
Nothing to claim.
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
'Pro plan trial' language on third-party perk sites (e.g. 'Pro plan free 12 months, $300 value') describes partner credit redemptions, not a genuine self-serve trial; don't expect one on signup.

Core Free-tier numbers (500 MB DB, 1 GB storage, 5 GB egress, 50K MAU, 500K invocations, 2 projects) have been unchanged since the 2024 org-based-billing migration, which added +1 GB egress. 4) NO TRIAL CREDITS ever existed for paid plans; verify current partner terms inside the (login-gated) AWS Startups portal since the AWS offer page redirected to auth as of today.
