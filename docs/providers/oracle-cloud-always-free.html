Oracle Cloud Always Free: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: OCI Always Free tier (VPS: 2 AMD micro VMs + 2 OCPU/12 GB ARM Ampere A1 pool + storage/DB/networking). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

OCI Always Free tier (VPS: 2 AMD micro VMs + 2 OCPU/12 GB ARM Ampere A1 pool + storage/DB/networking) high confidence

Perpetual ('life of the account') Always Free resources, home region only: (1) up to 2 VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro AMD instances, each 1/8 OCPU (~200 MHz, burstable) + 1 GB RAM, 1 VNIC, 1 public IPv4, up to 50 Mbps internet / 480 Mbps intra-region; (2) Ampere A1 ARM (VM.Standard.A1.Flex): a monthly pool of 1,500 OCPU-hours + 9,000 GB-hours = equivalent to 2 OCPUs and 12 GB total, usable as one instance or up to two 1-OCPU instances (min boot volume 47 GB/instance); (3) 200 GB block volume total (boot + data, home region) with 5 volume backups; (4) object storage: 20 GB combined Standard+Infrequent Access+Archive for free-only tenancies (10 GB per tier while trial/PAYG is active); (5) 10 TB/month outbound egress; (6) 2x Autonomous AI Database (20 GB storage each, 1 Oracle CPU, serverless Exadata, 20 max concurrent sessions); (7) MySQL HeatWave standalone single-node cluster, 50 GB data + 50 GB backup storage; (8) NoSQL: 133M reads + 133M writes/mo, 3 tables x 25 GB (Phoenix region only); (9) 2 VCNs, 1 Flexible Load Balancer + 1 Network Load Balancer, 50 IPsec site-to-site VPN connections, 5 CAs + 150 certificates, 3,000 emails/mo Email Delivery, plus free APEX, Resource Manager/Terraform, Monitoring/Logging, Bastion, Vault (software keys unlimited; 20 HSM key versions), Security Advisor, Content Management Starter. NO free bare-metal or dedicated-server shapes exist — OCI bare metal (BM.Dense/BM.Standard, incl. 80-core Ampere bare metal) is paid-only.

Eligibility
Open to anyone who signs up (one free trial/Always Free account per person; one cloud account per email). Credit or debit card REQUIRED at signup: major-network cards (Visa/Mastercard/Discover/Amex) that do not require a PIN; virtual, prepaid, single-use and PIN-debit cards are rejected. A mobile phone number is needed for most users. Available in all regions where commercial OCI is offered (Free/Always Free NOT available in US Government Cloud regions); '$300 credit' part is 'available in select countries'. .edu email NOT required for the free tier. Startup age/funding caps: none for the free tier.
How to claim
Sign up at https://signup.oraclecloud.com (linked from https://www.oracle.com/cloud/free/): select country, enter name + valid email, verify email (link valid 30 min), set password (8-40 chars, complexity rules), optionally enter company name, create a cloud account name, pick Home Region (irrevocable; Always Free compute + ADB can only be created in the home region), enter address + phone, add credit/debit card as payment-verification method (you may see a small temporary ~$1 verification hold that auto-reverses), accept ToU, 'Start my free trial'. Always Free resources are usable immediately with no upgrade; $0 charged unless you upgrade to PAYG. No separate claim form for the Always Free tier itself.
Expiry
Perpetual — 'available for an unlimited period of time, for the life of the account.' Trial portion: $300 credits expire 30 days after signup. No renewal needed.
Gotchas
HOME-REGION LOCK: Always Free compute and ADB provision only in home region; use in other regions bills at PAYG rates. IDLE RECLAMATION: Always Free VMs (and bare metal) can be reclaimed when over any 7-day window CPU p95 < 20% AND network < 20% (AND memory < 20% for A1 shapes). Accounts idle 30+ days may be deemed abandoned and suspended/terminated. 2026 ARM CUT-ENFORCEMENT: since 2026-08-18, A1 instances exceeding 2 OCPU/12 GB are auto-terminated; once an existing oversized instance is terminated it 'may not be possible' to recreate it above the new limit. Capacity: free-tier accounts frequently hit 'out of host capacity' on A1/micro shapes in US regions; PAYG upgrade generally provisions more reliably. Eligible OS images limited (Oracle Linux/Ubuntu/CentOS for micros; Oracle Linux/Ubuntu + Oracle Linux Cloud Developer for A1, latter needs >=8 GB). No SLA and no Oracle Support unless a paid account. If trial ends with more A1 OCPUs/memory than 2/12, excess instances are disabled then deleted after 30 days unless you upgrade. Paid resources made with trial credits are deleted (unrecoverable) if you don't upgrade.

OCI Free Trial: US$300 cloud credit for 30 days (all eligible OCI services, on top of Always Free) high confidence

One-time US$300 in cloud credits valid up to 30 days from account creation, spendable on all eligible OCI services (compute beyond free limits, GPU, K8s, analytics, up to ~8 instances / 5 TB storage while credits last, per the trial marketing). Credits are consumed at discounted trial rates. Unused credits expire at 30 days. After trial: 30-day grace to upgrade; paid resources are otherwise reclaimed/deleted; Always Free resources persist. Upgrading carries a ~$100 card authorization. Extensions/additional credits possible only via an Oracle sales representative (console Upgrade page).

Eligibility
Same as free tier signup: one per person; credit/debit card (major network, no PIN required) + phone number for identity verification; US$300 credit 'available in select countries' per the FAQ asterisk. No .edu, no startup-age requirement — this is a general trial, not a special program.
How to claim
Automatically granted at https://signup.oraclecloud.com — no separate claim. Steps: country -> name -> email (verify within 30 min) -> password -> optional company -> cloud account name -> Home Region -> address/phone -> add card verification (small temporary ~$1 hold) -> ToU -> 'Start my free trial'. Upgrade to PAYG at any time via Console > Billing & Cost Management > Upgrade and Manage Payment.
Expiry
Credits: 30 days from signup or $300 exhaustion, whichever first. Trial resources: 30-day grace after expiry to upgrade before deletion. Always Free portion: no expiry.
Gotchas
Card IS required (contrary to 'no credit card' marketing assumptions): FAQ says credit cards and PIN-less debit cards only; virtual/prepaid/single-use cards rejected; periodic re-validation holds possible. 'Some special Free Trial offers' (e.g., email-invite promotions): after expiry the account is suspended for 30 days then permanently deleted — they do NOT convert to an Always Free account. No SLA/support once credits gone. Billing begins after trial if you upgraded (credit balance preserved until trial period ends). No downgrade from paid back to free. Data in trial-created resources is unrecoverable after reclamation.
Source
oracle.com

No .edu-gated OCI VPS license found on official pages as of 2026-10-22. The free tier FAQ explicitly targets 'a student wanting to learn, or an academic developing curriculum in the cloud' — students simply use the standard Always Free + $300/30-day trial (no .edu email required; the signup email is only used to check 'if you are eligible for any special offers', so education special offers can appear at signup if targeted). Free education/training: (a) Oracle Learning Explorer — free entry-level training courses and accreditation across Oracle's product portfolio; (b) Oracle University 'Free Foundations training' — free training and certifications for OCI, Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications and AI (oracle.com/education/training, 'New free Foundations training... free training and certifications'); (c) a promo on that page: 'Free certification on OCI, AI, multicloud, and data services — Now through October 31, join the Race to Certification 2025' (note: page footer is © 2026, the 'Race to Certification 2025' banner looks like stale promotional copy — the offer may have already expired on 2025-10-31; do not count on it without re-checking).

Eligibility
Training: open to anyone, no .edu email, no card. Cloud: standard free-tier eligibility (one account/person, card + phone). .edu email NOT required anywhere.
How to claim
Cloud: standard signup at signup.oraclecloud.com (any special education offer is surfaced there via the email eligibility check). Training: enroll at mylearn.oracle.com / oracle.com/education/training ('Explore free training and certifications'); Learning Explorer via oracle.com/education ('Start learning for free').
Expiry
Free Foundations training: ongoing/promotional ('New free Foundations training' as of the page's © 2026). Race to Certification: 'through October 31' (year unclear — likely already ended 2025-10-31; stale banner).
Gotchas
Oracle University free tier is training-only, no infrastructure credits; the 'free' training does not include the full exam voucher unless the specific Foundations course lists it; verify 'Race to Certification' status in the console/marketing site before assuming it's live; no .edu-only benefits exist in OCI as of this check.

2025–26 changes

  • Material 2025-2026 changes: (1) ALWAYS FREE AMPERE A1 HALVED, SILENTLY — Oracle's docs changed without a public announcement so that the A1 pool went from 3,000 OCPU-hours + 18,000 GB-hours/month (equivalent to 4 OCPU / 24 GB, i.e., 'up to four Ampere instances' as the older MySQL-free page still claims) to 1,500 OCPU-hours + 9,000 GB-hours/month (= 2 OCPU / 12 GB total, 'one or two instances, 2 OCPUs total'). Per InfoQ (2026-07-03) the docs change took effect 2026-06-15 with no blog post or customer notice; hard enforcement (auto-termination of over-limit instances) began 2026-08-18 per a customer email quoted by CNELECAR. Ambiguity remains: Oracle support reportedly told PAYG tenancies the old 4/24 limits still apply free, while the docs say 'All tenancies get the first 1,500 OCPU hours...' — unconfirmed either way. Once an oversized A1 instance is terminated it likely cannot be recreated above the new limit. AMD micros, 200 GB block, object storage, 10 TB egress, and 2x20 GB ADB limits are unchanged. (2) $300/30-DAY TRIAL — unchanged through 2025-2026; card still required at signup. (3) STARTUP PROGRAM — not verifiable this pass (bot-blocked global page); no evidence it was sunset, but no official docs corroborate it either. (4) BARE-METAL / DEDICATED-SERVER FRONT — Oracle has no free bare-metal or dedicated-server offer at any tier; bare-metal/dedicated is paid-only (PAYG or Always-Free-exempt). (5) EDUCATION — no .edu-gated program currently advertised; free Foundations training/certifications remain available per the ©2026 Oracle University pages.
