Contentful Free Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Contentful Free plan ("Start for free" / Starter Space). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Contentful Free plan ("Start for free" / Starter Space) medium confidence

Permanently free platform plan, no credit card. Current limits (official pricing page, Nov 2025 snapshot via search index, corroborated by two 2026 trackers): 10 users, 2 roles (Admin, Editor), 2 locales, 25 content types, 10,000 records total (entries + assets combined), 2 environments, 100K API calls/month, 50 GB CDN asset bandwidth/month, 50 MB asset upload. 'No overages' on the free plan (usage at the cap is blocked/throttled, not billed). One Starter Space included with every plan; cannot be purchased separately; no paid add-ons for extra API calls or bandwidth on free. Community support only. Historically aimed at personal projects, hack weeks, local non-profits; the 2020 FAQ says 'not intended for commercial purposes'. Aims at 'innovation and education' per the FAQ.

Eligibility
Anyone: sign up with any email, no credit card required, no .edu required, no region restrictions documented, no application/approval. Students, individual developers and non-profits are the stated audience; commercial use is discouraged (per FAQ).
How to claim
Step 1: create a free account at contentful.com (email only, 'no credit card required' per get-started page). Step 2: the free Starter Space and free Platform tier are provisioned automatically. No forms, no sales call.
Expiry
Indefinite; no expiry or renewal. Monthly API-call/bandwidth quotas reset each month; no overage billing on the free plan.
Gotchas
Free tier was CUT sharply in the 2025 platform restructure: API calls 1M→100K/month, CDN 0.1TB (plus 0.75TB Intro Space) → 50GB/month, users 5→10 but roles 4→2. The 'known so far' baseline (20M content API requests/month, 10K assets, 3 authors, 15-day paid trials) matches no documented 2024–2026 official configuration (last documented free-tier API cap was 1M/month). 10,000 records counts entries AND assets together, so the 10K-asset figure is wrong. 2-locale wall: a third language forces the ~$300/mo Lite plan. No workflows on free. Official page could not be read live on 2026-10-22 (site behind Vercel bot-checkpoint, HTTP 429 to automated fetches), so figures rest on the Nov 2025 official-page snapshot plus dev.to (Oct 2026) and Spyingbee (Jun–Aug 2026) corroboration.
Source
contentful.com

Time-limited trial of paid plans (reported 14–15 days, no credit card) low confidence

Contentful has historically offered a limited trial of its paid plans without a credit card (third-party guide, Nov 2024: 'Contentful also offers a free trial for its basic plan'; the 2020 free-plan FAQ contrasts 'limited, 14-day trials' against the free plan). The 'known so far' note cites 15-day trials of paid plans. The Nov 2025 official pricing-page snapshot shows no trial duration at all — the permanent 'Start for free' tier is now the entry point — so whether a paid-plan trial still exists as of 2026-10-22 is unconfirmed.

Eligibility
New customers exploring paid tiers (Lite ~$300/mo, Premium/Enterprise custom); no .edu or region requirement; historically no credit card for the trial itself (a payment method becomes required to stay on a paid plan).
How to claim
If the trial persists: sign up free, then start a Lite trial from account billing settings or via 'contact sales'. Unverifiable from the public pricing page as of 2026-10-22 (no trial text in the page snapshot; live page was bot-blocked).
Expiry
Reported 15 days (older materials say 14 days); then convert to a paid plan or drop back to the free plan.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free gotcha: the free plan is permanent and needs no trial, so the trial only matters for evaluating Lite/Premium. Exact duration (15 vs 14 days) unverified. After trial, Lite is ~$300/mo — a $0→$300 cliff with no mid tier. Adding a card is required to keep a paid trial running past its window.

Contentful for Startups (reported ~$1,000 in credits) low confidence

Third-party deal aggregators list a 'Contentful Startup Credits: $1,000 in credits' offer (SaaSOffers, labeled 'Verified October 2026', details behind their paywall; StartupFlowAI directory entry: pre-seed/seed stage, global, no stated funding limit, no referral needed). No official program or application page exists on contentful.com, and StartupFlowAI explicitly states 'we have not confirmed a direct application page for this programme yet, so this opens the provider's main site'. SaaSOffers' own FAQ on the same page is internally inconsistent (it claims a 'Community plan' with 25K API calls/month — a figure matching no official source; the free plan is 100K/month), so treat the $1,000 claim as unverified.

Eligibility
As reported by aggregators: early-stage startups (pre-seed/seed); SaaSOffers FAQ claims bootstrapped startups without VC funding qualify; no published company-age or funding cap; no .edu requirement. Eligibility caps are unconfirmed by any official source.
How to claim
Per the aggregators: follow their link to Contentful and create an account; 'apply the credits' via the account. In practice this is likely routed through Contentful sales/partnerships ('check for account credit... negotiate a custom deal', per procurement guides). No public application form found as of 2026-10-22 — verify by contacting Contentful sales before relying on it.
Expiry
Unknown. If credits exist, deals of this kind are typically valid ~12 months and unused credits lapse; no official terms are published.
Gotchas
Likely a sales-led credit/discount rather than a self-serve public program. Aggregator-sourced amounts and FAQ details are unreliable (contradictory 'Community plan' numbers on the same page). Confirm directly with Contentful sales; do not build a plan around the $1,000 figure.

Education / student license: none found — students use the standard free plan medium confidence

No .edu-eligible license, student discount, or university program was found on contentful.com or in any 2025–2026 report. CostBench (pricing verified Aug 2026) says of Contentful: 'No published discount programs' — its nonprofit/education/startup sections are generic boilerplate with unverified terms. The official free-plan FAQ states the free plan is offered because Contentful believes in 'supporting innovation and education', which makes the standard no-credit-card free plan the de facto student offer.

Eligibility
Open to everyone including students and educators; no .edu email required; no enrollment proof; no region limits documented.
How to claim
No separate claim process: create a free Contentful account with any email (no credit card) and use the free Starter Space. For institutional/enterprise education deployments, contact sales.
Expiry
Indefinite, same as the free plan; nothing to renew.
Gotchas
Absence of a published program does not prove none exists privately — institutions should still ask sales. The free-plan FAQ wording ('not intended for commercial purposes', 2020) suggests monetized student startups outgrow it quickly. Any institutional license would likely be sales-negotiated, not public.

contentful.com

2025–26 changes

  • 2025–2026 changes: (1) The free plan was materially CUT in the 2025 "Platform" pricing restructure documented on the official page from Nov 2025: API calls 1M→100K/month, CDN 0.1TB platform + 0.75TB Intro Space→50GB/month, users 5→10, roles 4→2, content types 25 and 10K records retained in the included Starter Space. (2) "Basic" was renamed "Lite" (~$300/mo: 20 users, 3 roles, 3 locales, 1M API calls, 100GB CDN) and an explicit "Enterprise" custom tier plus "Personalization" tiers appeared in 2026 (Spyingbee, Jun–Aug 2026; dev.to, Jul/Oct 2026). (3) The "15-day trials of paid plans" from the baseline note are no longer advertised on the current pricing page (no trial text in the Nov 2025 snapshot; the permanent free plan is the entry point) — trial existence unconfirmed. (4) The baseline "20M content API requests/month, 10K assets, 3 authors" matches no documented 2024–2026 official configuration; the last documented free-plan API cap was 1M/month (2024–25 guides), and the current official cap is 100K/month. (5) No education license or public startup program was found; the only startup-credit lead (~$1,000) exists solely in third-party deal aggregators with no confirmed official application page. Caveat: contentful.com pages were not readable live on 2026-10-22 (Vercel security checkpoint returned HTTP 429 to all automated fetches; WebSearch budget exhausted), so confidence levels reflect search-indexed official-page snapshots plus independent 2026 trackers rather than a same-day read of the official pages.
