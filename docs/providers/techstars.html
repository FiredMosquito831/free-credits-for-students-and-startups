Techstars: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Techstars accelerator investment: $220K day-one (APAC: $120K). 7 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Techstars accelerator investment: $220K day-one (APAC: $120K) high confidence

$220,000 invested at acceptance in most programs worldwide: $200,000 via uncapped MFN SAFE (no valuation cap, no discount; auto-adopts the lowest-cap or most-favorable terms of any SAFE issued between MFN start and the priced round) + $20,000 via post-money Convertible Equity Agreement (CEA) for 5% common stock (post-money, incl. existing option pool). Wired within 3 weeks of acceptance; offer issued 1-2 weeks after acceptance with standardized docs. Both instruments convert when the company closes a priced round of at least $1M. Asia-Pacific programs: $100K MFN SAFE + $20K CEA = $120K total. 'No fee (i.e., monetary cost) to join a Techstars accelerator program' — official. Total package advertised as $1.5M+ including credits. 3-month mentorship program; network access 'for the life of the company'.

Eligibility
Startups only (no .edu email or student requirement); idea-stage OK ('we've accepted companies with no more than an idea, and a dream') up to companies already holding $1M+ funding. Founded anywhere; must be legally present in program country during the 3-month program. Must be a US C-corp or foreign equivalent; companies incorporated in non-approved jurisdictions (e.g., India) need a 'flip' reorg first. Pre-Series A seed stage in practice. Applications via apply.techstars.com for a specific program; acceptance is competitive.
How to claim
1) Apply to a specific Techstars program at apply.techstars.com (application takes ~30 min). 2) On acceptance: upload pitch deck, answer questions; accelerator offer + MFN SAFE term sheet issued within 1-2 weeks. 3) Complete Techstars' checks (background, incorporation, ownership, IP, company bank account) — $20K wired as soon as checks pass; legal close of standardized MFN SAFE docs in <=3 weeks. 4) $220K wired to company account; partner cloud credits activated across AWS/DigitalOcean/Google platforms at the same step.
Expiry
SAFE + CEA have no expiry; they convert on the first priced round of $1M+. Ongoing side-letter shareholder relationship (pro-rata, digital-assets, drag-along rights; company must report key operating metrics and burn). Cloud/AI credits: 24 months per Techstars' capital page (see separate findings).
Gotchas
Not a grant: $20K is equity, $200K is a convertible instrument — Techstars' future ownership = 5% + SAFE conversion value (e.g., $200K converts to 1% extra on a $20M pre-money round per official example). Uncapped MFN means if you later issue a better-term SAFE (lower cap or 20% discount), Techstars automatically inherits it. Side letter imposes reporting and exit obligations. APAC deals are only $120K. $20K/5% is structured as a CEA (converts at the priced round), though the marketing /capital page describes it as '5% common stock for $20,000'.
Source
techstars.com

AWS Activate — Techstars portfolio tier: $100,000 AWS credits medium confidence

$100,000 in AWS promotional credits over 24 months, 'PORTFOLIO' tier, per Techstars' official capital page (lists H100, SageMaker, EC2; infrastructure access). Zero equity taken by AWS. Techstars has been a long-standing AWS Activate Provider (a ~10-year partnership per Amazon). 2026 notes from AWS: Founders tier starts at $1,000; Portfolio tier can be up to $200,000 depending on provider; credits now redeemable on third-party models in Amazon Bedrock; new AWS accounts start on a Free plan that is NOT eligible for Activate credits (must be on Paid plan); eligibility = pre-Series B, founded within last 10 years, functioning website, business email on company domain, most recent funding round within 12 months.

Eligibility
Requires Techstars acceptance (or an AWS Activate Provider Org ID — each Org ID usable once, and AWS does the final approval above the $5K tier). No .edu email requirement. Global. Startup-stage constraint: pre-Series B per AWS rules.
How to claim
For Techstars companies: nothing to do separately — credits are 'activated across all partner platforms' at the capital-wire step after acceptance (Step 3 of Techstars' capital deployment). Standalone: apply at the AWS Activate portal (aws.amazon.com/activate) with your provider's Organization ID; decision in 5-10 business days.
Expiry
24 months from activation per Techstars' capital page (AWS generally states 1-2 year validity and now enforces expiry strictly with no extensions).
Gotchas
Credits are promotional dollars on a paid-plan AWS account — overage beyond the credits is billed; AWS Marketplace third-party software, Route 53 domain registrations, and some support tiers are excluded. Free-plan AWS accounts cannot receive Activate credits. The AWS Techstars partnership page (aws.amazon.com/startups/partnerships/techstars → startups.aws.com) returned 404 today, so the $100K/24-month figure rests on Techstars' own official page; AWS may grant less than $100K at its discretion (the partner 'recommends' a tier, AWS approves).

techstars.com

DigitalOcean Startups — HATCH tier: $100,000 credits (2026 premier partner) medium confidence

$100,000 in DigitalOcean infrastructure credits over 24 months, 'HATCH' tier, per Techstars' official capital page (lists H100 GPU access). DigitalOcean is a former Techstars portfolio company and was welcomed in March 2026 as a 2026 'Premier Partner': Techstars founders gain access to DO's Gradient AI Platform and the DO startup community. DO's own program is tiered (historically HATCH ~$100K / SPARK ~$25K, ~12 months, 1-2% equity on HATCH, 0% on SPARK); the Techstars deal line states 24 months.

Eligibility
Requires Techstars acceptance (or a qualifying accelerator/VC partner path). No .edu email requirement. Global. Early-stage startup.
How to claim
For Techstars companies: no separate application — HATCH credits are activated at the same capital-deployment step as the AWS/Google credits after acceptance. Standalone: apply through the DigitalOcean Startups program with a partner reference.
Expiry
24 months per Techstars' capital page (DO's standard tiers historically ~12 months — confirm current tier at claim time).
Gotchas
Credit-card-free access to DO itself: DO Startups tiers do not take meaningful equity on HATCH beyond a small % and SPARK is equity-free; DO pages are currently unreachable via the standard partner URLs (digitalocean.com/partners/startups 404 today), so the 24-month/HATCH details rest on Techstars' official page plus the March 2026 premier-partner announcement. Unused credit expires; overage beyond credits is billed on the DO account.
Source
techstars.com

Google Cloud for Startups — via Techstars: $100,000 GCP credits medium confidence

$100,000 in Google Cloud credits over 24 months, 'FOUNDERS_PROGRAM' tier, per Techstars' official capital page (lists A100, GKE, Vertex AI, Cloud GPUs). Google co-hosts Techstars investor events (e.g., Fall 2025 NYC Investor Day hosted by Google for Startups). Google's own startup portal has been reorganized (cloud.google.com/launch now 404s; program now under the 'Google Cloud for Startups' umbrella) — Google's publicly cited figures are up to $200K credits generally, with partner-accelerator cohorts getting the top package; Techstars' deal line is $100K/24 months.

Eligibility
Requires Techstars acceptance (or Google's accelerator-partner path). No .edu email requirement. Typical GCP startup program rules: early-stage (<5 years, funding/revenue caps per Google's current terms — not re-verifiable today as the program pages 404).
How to claim
For Techstars companies: no separate application — GCP credits activated at the capital-wire step after acceptance, alongside AWS/DigitalOcean. Standalone: apply via the Google Cloud for Startups portal or through a partner (Techstars, YC, 500 Global, Antler).
Expiry
24 months per Techstars' capital page (GCP credits historically 12 months at claim; confirm at activation).
Gotchas
Google's own program pages currently 404/moved, so the $100K/24mo details rest on Techstars' official page (high) rather than Google's current terms (could not be read today). Marketplace/SaaS add-ons and support fees are typically excluded from GCP startup credits; overage beyond credits is billed.

AWS + Techstars Impact Bootcamps (free for underrepresented founders, incl. students/early founders) medium confidence

Free 2-day bootcamps (launched 2025, 6 cities: Atlanta, New York, Mexico City, London in 2025; Tokyo, Berlin in early 2026) managed locally by Techstars: hands-on AWS technical training, business coaching, mentors/investors. Each participating founder gets $5,000 in AWS promotional credits. Best-in-class startups are invited to the AWS Founder Retreat — a weekend with in-person investor meetings and the chance to win up to $50,000 in unrestricted grant funding plus an additional $15,000 in AWS promotional credits. Top participants considered for Techstars accelerator or Founder Catalyst programs. Part of AWS's $30M commitment (announced 2022) to Black, Women, Latino and LGBTQIA+ founders.

Eligibility
Founding teams must identify as a member of an underrepresented community (Black, Women, Latino, Indigenous, LGBTQIA+, founders with disabilities) or demonstrate work/engagement supporting such communities; self-funded, angel-backed, or raised up to Series A; clear need for cloud technology; interest in scaling/fundraising. No .edu email requirement; free to attend (event slots filled by application).
How to claim
Apply via the AWS Impact Bootcamps page (https://aws.amazon.com/startups/lp/aws-impact-bootcamps) for one of the six city cohorts; attend the 2-day workshop; receive $5,000 AWS credits as a participant; winners advance to the AWS Founder Retreat for the up-to-$50K grant + $15K credits and Techstars pipeline.
Expiry
Event-driven (2025-2026 city calendar); the $5,000 promotional credits follow AWS promotional-credit terms (short validity, no extensions). No standing free tier — only while a cohort is running.
Gotchas
The $50K + $15K prizes go only to 'best-in-class' retreat participants, not all attendees. Bootcamp pages may be city-specific and time-boxed. 'Free' = free workshop + credits, but the AWS credits require a paid-plan AWS account to activate (Free-plan accounts are ineligible).
Source
press.aboutamazon.com

Techstars Founder Catalyst (free pre-accelerator, no equity) high confidence

10-week part-time, primarily virtual pre-accelerator for early-stage/idea-stage founders: training, tools, mentorship, cohort community, and network access. 'There is no equity exchange in Founder Catalyst programs.' No tuition listed. Current/planned editions include Techstars x EIT Urban Mobility Founder Catalyst, Alabama, and Global Fall 2026. Complements Techstars' 'Startup Weekend' community events (paid event series) and the University Catalyst pipeline.

Eligibility
Pre-funding or idea-stage founders who have raised less than $400K, possess domain expertise in the problem space, have a defined customer need with a lead on a solution, and collaborative spirit. No student/.edu requirement — open to any early founder. Free to join; no equity.
How to claim
Sign up via the Airtable 'Hear About Future Programs' form on the Techstars Founder Catalyst page or through apply.techstars.com; selected cohorts run 10 weeks; strong participants can feed into Techstars accelerators.
Expiry
Cohort-based, rolling; specific editions (EIT/Alabama/Global Fall 2026) have their own application windows; benefit access is not perpetual like accelerator network access.
Gotchas
Not a funding program — no capital invested (the $220K deal requires actual accelerator acceptance). Some editions are co-branded (EIT, University of Alabama) and may carry local eligibility emphasis.
Source
techstars.com

Techstars University Catalyst / university-partnered accelerators (student & alumni pipeline) medium confidence

Techstars University Catalyst (with USC) targets the student/alumni pipeline, referenced on Techstars' own pages with a 2026 class in progress; 'USC and Techstars Accelerator' is an official accelerator in the Spring 2026 cycle (alongside Johns Hopkins/CareFirst AI Health Baltimore, Northwestern Medicine, Columbus/Ohio State, etc.). Accepted university-partnered programs receive the same $220K structure (or APAC $120K where applicable) plus the $1M+ credit package. No separate student-only free tier or education license exists at Techstars itself.

Eligibility
Students/alumni via University Catalyst (pipeline, free-to-attend format); accelerator acceptance through the same application as other programs. No .edu email requirement for the investment itself.
How to claim
Attend the University Catalyst cohort offered at your university (USC partnership), or apply to a university-branded accelerator (e.g., USC and Techstars Accelerator, application window closed Nov 19, 2025 for Spring 2026 per Techstars' blog) via apply.techstars.com.
Expiry
Cohort-based; new university partnerships announced per year (March 2026 update lists active editions).
Gotchas
University-branded cohorts are still funded on the standard accelerator terms — the university is a partner, not the investor; expect the same side-letter obligations. Student-facing 'free' elements are the pipeline events, not the $220K deal.
Source
techstars.com

2025–26 changes

  • Changes/sunsets in 2025-2026: (1) April 17, 2025 — Techstars replaced its $120K deal ($100K MFN SAFE + $20K/5%) with the $220K deal ($200K uncapped MFN SAFE + $20K post-money CEA/5%) for Fall 2025 cohorts onward, explicitly mirroring YC; the $120K structure now applies only to Asia-Pacific programs. (2) The ARM Labs Lagos Techstars accelerator (Techstars' Africa-specific program, launched Dec 2022, 2 cohorts) closed in late 2024 as part of a global restructuring; African founders can still apply to global programs. (3) No Techstars program was sunset in 2025-2026 that would reduce the credit stack — DigitalOcean Startups was upgraded to 2026 'Premier Partner' (announced March 2026), expanding rather than reducing access. (4) Verification gaps today (2026-10-22): the AWS Techstars partnership page (aws.amazon.com/startups/partnerships/techstars → startups.aws.com/...), DigitalOcean's partner/startup pages (digitalocean.com/partners/startups, /startup-cloud), and Google's startup program pages (cloud.google.com/launch, /application/startup-faqs) all return 404, so AWS/DO/GCP per-credit-line details above rely on Techstars' official /capital page (read today) plus partner-program public docs; treat exact $100K/24-month figures as Techstars-stated until re-confirmed against the partners' current portals. (5) There is no Techstars student free tier, trial-credit self-serve offer, or education license (no .edu requirement anywhere in the funnel); student access is via University Catalyst and the free AWS Impact Bootcamps.
