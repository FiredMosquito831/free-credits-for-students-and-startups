Paystack: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free to integrate — no setup, monthly, or minimum fees (pay-per-transaction only). 3 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free to integrate — no setup, monthly, or minimum fees (pay-per-transaction only) medium confidence

Paystack has no integration fee, no maintenance fee, and no monthly or minimum fees; you pay only a per-transaction fee when a real payment is made. Applies equally to startups and students. Exact per-transaction rates (e.g., % + fixed fee in NGN for Nigeria; % + fixed in USD for the US) could NOT be verified live today (official page 403'd) and should be reconfirmed on paystack.com/pricing.

Eligibility
Any developer or business worldwide, including students and startups. No .edu email required, no funding/age cap, no minimum revenue. Standard KYC/business details are required to enable live processing, but not to sign up or to test.
How to claim
1) Create a free account at paystack.com/dashboard. 2) Complete business verification (KYC) to enable live payments. 3) Generate your secret/API key. 4) Integrate via REST API / SDKs. No credit card is required to sign up or to use test mode.
Expiry
Ongoing; no expiry or renewal. Free-to-integrate has been Paystack's standard model since launch (2015).
Gotchas
Not free to actually move money — a per-transaction fee applies to every live payment, and fees differ by country/region/currency. Payouts have settlement times. Fee figures were not verified live (403 today); confirm current rates before relying on them.
Source
paystack.com

Free test-mode / sandbox account with test keys (credit card NOT required) medium confidence

Every Paystack account includes a free test mode: test public key (pk_test...) and test secret key (sk_test...), plus documented test card numbers. Test transactions use simulated funds and never move real money. No credit card or bank details required to create an account or to use test mode — a real, free, no-strings sandbox for students building projects.

Eligibility
Anyone who can create a Paystack account (global, including students and startups). No .edu required, no funding cap, no credit card required.
How to claim
1) Create an account at paystack.com/dashboard. 2) Copy your test API keys (pk_test/sk_test) from the dashboard. 3) Use Paystack's published test card numbers to simulate successful/failed payments. 4) Go live by switching to the corresponding pk_live/sk_live keys, which require completed KYC.
Expiry
No expiry; test mode is available indefinitely on any account.
Gotchas
Test mode is NOT a free production tier — it cannot collect real money. Going live needs a separate live key and completed business verification (KYC). developer.paystack.com failed DNS today and paystack.com pages 403'd, so confirm the current test card numbers and doc URLs before relying on them.
Source
paystack.com

No dedicated student program, startup program, trial credits, or education license low confidence

As of 2026-10-22, Paystack offers no student-specific program, no startup program with discounted fees/equity, no trial credits/free spend balance, and no education license. The only free value is free-to-integrate (pay-per-transaction) plus the free test-mode sandbox above. Any fee accommodation for a startup would be a commercial agreement, not a published offer.
