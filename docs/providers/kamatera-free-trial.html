Kamatera Free Trial: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: 30-day $100 Free Trial (VPS). 3 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

30-day $100 Free Trial (VPS) high confidence

Free 30-day trial with cloud services worth up to $100: 1 cloud server (custom CPU/RAM/storage config) valued up to $100/month, plus 1 TB cloud block storage, 1 TB traffic (inbound+outbound), full platform access, scaling within the $100 config, technical consultation and 24/7 support. Trial clock does NOT start at signup - it starts when the first server is created; account stays on hold until then. Trial ends after 30 days, then standard billing begins; account may be placed in 'deposit mode' requiring a minimum deposit to stay active. Overages during the trial (beyond 1 server/$100 config or 1 TB traffic) trigger an email alert; unadjusted overage is charged pro-rata to the card and credited against the first monthly invoice. A small validation charge (~$1-2) may be taken on card; a fully-refundable deposit (e.g., $10) can be requested when unusual billing activity is detected.

Eligibility
Open to anyone - no .edu email, region limit, or user-type restriction stated. Credit card NOT optional: a valid credit/debit card is required to activate the trial; if the card is not accepted, a pre-paid refundable deposit via PayPal is allowed instead. One account and one 30-day free trial per customer - multiple trials via multiple accounts is explicitly prohibited. Password must be 8+ chars with lowercase, uppercase and number.
How to claim
1) Sign up at kamatera.com/free-trial with email + compliant password; 2) verify email; 3) complete Billing profile in console (personal info, phone verification, card entry); 4) card validation charge may occur; 5) create first server - trial starts; 6) within limits no charge; to exit, terminate servers - no charge.
Expiry
30 days from creation of first server; no renewals - after 30 days it converts to a paid account (deposit mode applies if no valid payment). No sunset date published; page carries (c) 2026.
Gotchas
Trial vs free: this is a limited trial, not a perpetual free tier. CC (or PayPal deposit) is required even though nothing is charged within limits. 1 TB traffic includes BOTH directions. Server with 'powered off' state still incurs small hourly storage/IP charges. First invoice after trial generated on 1st of the month cycle. 1-server cap.

RiseUP Cloud Start-Up Program - up to 24 months of infrastructure credit high confidence

Startup program at startup.kamatera.com: 'Twenty-four months of runway, on us.' Up to 24 months of infrastructure services credit (dollar cap per month NOT disclosed on the page) plus, all 24 months: a personal account manager, a dedicated cloud consultant, a business consultant on request (Tech/Sales/Marketing/Finance/HR), and marketing & PR access through Kamatera's partner network. Available across Kamatera's 25 datacentres / 21 locations / 4 continents. Applying is free and non-binding; the team reviews and contacts applicants to 'discuss your options.' Page is live with (c) 2026.

Eligibility
All five required: (1) within the first 5 years of the company's funding, (2) a company website - live or in development, (3) an active LinkedIn page - live or in development, (4) 'the intention to become a unicorn', (5) a signed Start-Up Program agreement. If close on one criterion, the page invites applying anyway and explaining in the message. No .edu/student path; no funding cap amount stated beyond the 5-year-from-funding window.
How to claim
Fill the application form at startup.kamatera.com (name, email, phone with 8+ digits, company name, company website, optional free-text), submit; Kamatera's team reviews and follows up. Corporate office phone listed: 074-70-50-340.
Expiry
Credit runs from month 0 to month 24 ('first 24 months of credit'). No renewal/extension terms disclosed on the page.
Gotchas
No dollar figure or per-month cap published - 'credit' is opaque; program terms are set via a signed agreement and individual review, so actual allocation is discretionary. No CC/billing mechanics disclosed on the page. Program is targeted at startups (companies), not individual students/hobbyists. Program appears newly launched in the 2025-2026 period (could not independently date the launch - see sunset_notes).

No dedicated student/education program, no perpetual free tier (students use the generic trial) medium confidence

Kamatera publishes no .edu-verified student program, no education license, and no perpetual 'free tier' for VPS. Students are served by the same $100/30-day trial, which third-party student-hosting roundups (ProBlogGuru, HostAdvice, LinuxBuz 2026 lists) recommend to students precisely because no student ID is required. Kamatera is a VPS/IaaS-only provider - it has no bare-metal/dedicated-physical-server line, so its offers cover virtual servers only (dedicated vCPU configs available inside the trial).

Eligibility
Students: same as generic trial (anyone with a credit card). No .edu email, no student verification, no regional restriction stated.
How to claim
Same claim path as the $100 trial; no separate student application exists.
Expiry
n/a - absence of an offer; generic trial remains live as of 2026-10 (page (c) 2026).
Gotchas
Third-party 'Kamatera student' content is repackaged trial marketing, not a distinct product. Do not treat any '1MONTH300'-style promo codes quoted in blogs as current without verifying in the console.
