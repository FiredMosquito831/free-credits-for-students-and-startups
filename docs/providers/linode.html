Akamai Linode: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: US$100 promotional credit trial (the successor to the "Linode $100 free trial"). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

US$100 promotional credit trial (the successor to the "Linode $100 free trial") high confidence

New-customer promotional credit of US$100 applied automatically at signup; the current Akamai Cloud pricing/FAQ gives the window as 60 days ("if you received a $100 credit for 60 days..."). Credit covers Compute, Object Storage, Block Storage, and NodeBalancers; GPU plans are excluded. After the credit is exhausted or the window expires, the card on file is charged for remaining balance at standard rates; unused credits expire automatically. One promo code/redemption per business; transferred codes cause account termination.

Eligibility
New customers only ("not yet signed up for a free trial"). A valid payment method is required — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, or PayPal (Google Pay with associated card); a card/PayPal backup is mandatory up front and a pre-authorization may be sent, but you are not charged during the trial. No .edu requirement. No region limit stated on the page (offer localized into 9 languages; Linode data centers still available). No free-CC path: credit card or PayPal IS required.
How to claim
1) Go to login.linode.com/signup (via the 'Try Akamai Cloud with US$100 in credits*' banner on akamai.com/cloud/pricing, or directly to https://www.akamai.com/lp/free-credit-100). 2) Sign up with Google SSO, GitHub SSO, or email. 3) Add a valid payment method (not charged during trial). 4) Agree to ToS; the US$100 credit is applied automatically to the new account. Fine print lives in the 'Promotion Redemption Rules & Conditions' linked from the asterisk.
Expiry
60-day credit window per current FAQ example (standard promos historically 30–60 days; always check the exact period shown in your signup/confirmation). Promotional codes themselves expire after one year per the legal rules page; promo is new-customers-only with no published end date.
Gotchas
It is a trial/credit, not a perpetual free tier — card gets charged for any active service after the credit window; no published extension/renewal. The 60-day figure comes from the pricing-page FAQ example; the LP page itself does not state a fixed day count (the legal rules page is a generic promo-code document: 'Credit amounts are nonstandard and granted at the sole discretion of the Akamai sales team', 'Promo codes expire after one year'). Older Linode links (linode.com/trial) now 404 — the offer only runs through the Akamai Cloud pages/signup. Promo codes don't apply to Marketplace/third-party services.

akamai.com

Rise start-up program (Akamai Cloud start-ups) high confidence

3-year start-up program. Year 1: 'between $500 and $120,000 in cloud credits' (tiered, decided case-by-case; no published formula). Years 2–3: continued discounted cloud services (no published percentage). Includes architecture/migration consulting (valued ~$250/hour), prioritized 24/7 support, dedicated account management. First-year credits do not roll over month-to-month or to subsequent years; overage in a month is billed that month. Contact: startups@akamai.com.

Eligibility
Early-stage, for-profit company fewer than 7 years old; at least one of: paid staff beyond founders, active non-affiliated users, initial revenue, or VC backing; must commit to a concrete infrastructure scaling plan and use Akamai Cloud as a primary provider. Must register a commercial account with a corporate email on a matching domain plus a valid credit card. Existing Akamai customers cannot join; managed hosting firms, MSPs, system integrators, and resellers excluded; SaaS, media, and gaming prioritized.
How to claim
1) Complete the intake form on https://www.akamai.com/cloud/start-ups/rise (or email startups@akamai.com). 2) Internal review with status update within ~2 weeks. 3) Brief discovery call for qualified applicants. 4) Onboarding — credits loaded onto your account and next steps set with a program coordinator.
Expiry
Program runs 3 years; year-1 credits expire monthly (no rollover). No published application deadline — rolling intake.
Gotchas
Credit amount is highly variable ($500–$120,000) and sales-discretionary — most applicants will get far less than the max; monthly credit allotments burn down per month with no rollover. Not a free tier: you're expected to pay for overage. 'Discounted' years 2–3 have no published discount level. Corporate email + card required; individual/education applicants don't qualify.
Source
akamai.com

Partner start-up program (VCs / accelerators / investors) high confidence

Enrollment for VCs, accelerators, and investors, on behalf of portfolio companies. Credits 'up to $3,000/month for a year' for qualified new Akamai Cloud customers (i.e., up to ~$36,000 total per portfolio company). Also technical enablement workshops and a path for strong portfolio companies into Akamai's Tech Partner Program. Developers in a partner's portfolio can start using credits immediately.

Eligibility
The partner (fund/accelerator/investor organization) must apply; credits are restricted to 'qualified new customers' of Akamai Cloud (existing Akamai customers excluded). No fund-size or stage thresholds published.
How to claim
Fill out the 'Become a partner' form on https://www.akamai.com/cloud/start-ups/partner, or email startups@akamai.com — Akamai contacts you to discuss the partnership; portfolio companies then get credits loaded.
Expiry
Credits: up to 12 months at $3,000/month. No published deadline — rolling partner enrollment.
Gotchas
This is an investor-channel program, not something an individual startup can apply to directly — a startup without VC/accelerator sponsorship should use the Rise program instead. Monthly cap ($3,000) means large month-1 spikes bill to the company; 'qualified' is not defined on the page.
Source
akamai.com

Enterprise cloud credits (up to US$5,000) — context only, not a student/startup offer medium confidence

Akamai Cloud's main cloud page promotes 'up to US$5,000 in cloud credits' for new AND existing enterprise customers, requested via a contact/apply form (in-page '#request-credits'). Per the promo rules, credit amounts are nonstandard and granted at Akamai sales team discretion; one redemption per business; codes expire after one year. Included here for completeness because it's the only other published dollar-amount credit; it targets enterprise sales motion, not students or early-stage startups.

Eligibility
Enterprise customers (new and existing), sales-driven; no .edu or startup-age criteria; a valid payment method on account is a standing requirement of the Master Services Agreement.
How to claim
Use the 'request credits' apply link on https://www.akamai.com/cloud (or Akamai sales); credits are provisioned by the sales team, not self-service.
Expiry
Promo codes expire after one year; enterprise grants have no published duration beyond code expiry.
Gotchas
Amount is discretionary (up to $5,000, not guaranteed); no self-service claim; don't confuse with the self-serve $100 new-customer promo.
Source
akamai.com

Education/Academy: no free student hosting tier exists; free learning content moved to Akamai Academy low confidence

No perpetual free-tier VPS plan, and no free student/educator hosting license was found on any current page. linode.com/education, linode.com/linode-education and the old Linode trial/education URLs all 404 today. The free learning asset that remains is the (former) Linode Academy, now Akamai Academy at training.akamai.com/learn (Cloud Computing Foundations and other free courses; certification is separate). Academy = free courses, NOT free hosting. I verified no education program is linked from the Linode homepage or the Akamai Cloud homepage (only 'Cloud Computing Foundations Certification' under training).

Eligibility
Anyone can take Academy courses for free; no .edu email requirement found. No hosting entitlement attaches to it.
How to claim
Visit https://training.akamai.com/learn for free courses. (Note: this site is a JS app; I could not extract course/certification pricing today, so treat certification cost as unverified.)
Expiry
n/a — no such free hosting offer is currently published; verify only via the official pages above.
Gotchas
Old Linode student-facing pages are gone post-rebrand; do not rely on pre-2025 Linode 'education' or 'student trial' claims found in third-party blog posts. If a source cites a Linode student free tier, it is outdated.

The old standalone 'Linode $100/30-day trial' link (linode.com/trial) now 404s — the trial is now the 'US$100 in credits' promo on Akamai Cloud pages, with the FAQ example stating a 60-day window (older promos were 30 days; ~30-60 days is the right mental model, but the exact window is per your signup/confirmation). (2) No perpetual free VPS tier has ever existed at Linode — the only recurring 'free' element on the pricing page is Object Storage's monthly free outbound egress allowance before overage (quantity not quantified on the page).
