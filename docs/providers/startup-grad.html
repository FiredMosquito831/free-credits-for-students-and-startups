Startup Grad: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Microsoft for Startups — self-serve offer (no investor code). 8 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Microsoft for Startups — self-serve offer (no investor code) high confidence

Milestone-based Azure Startup credits, current structure per official docs dated May–Oct 2026: $200 starter credit immediately (LinkedIn identity check); after approval (~3 business days) $1,000 in credits valid 90 days; +$4,000 valid 180 days after business verification = $5,000 total at Milestone 2. Unlocks without separate applications: $25,000 (use 5+ Azure workloads, ~60 days sustained), $50,000 (7+ workloads, ~60 days), $150,000 (10+ workloads AND ~$3,000/month usage over ~60 days; each workload must stay >$1 spend for the full 60-day window; non-sequential skips allowed). One-time 2-year extension to use credits at the $25K milestone. Credits apply to eligible Azure services including Azure AI / Microsoft Foundry models.

Eligibility
Startup (or founder 'with an idea'): privately held for-profit, software-based product/service owned by the company, pre-seed through Series B / not Series C-or-later funding, HQ in a country where Microsoft for Startups operates, lifetime free Azure credits received <$350,000, not an educational institution/government/consultancy/agency, no cryptocurrency mining. Sign-in must use a personal consumer Microsoft account (work/school/company-domain emails rejected); phone number required. No .edu requirement.
How to claim
startups.microsoft.com (or microsoft.com/en/startups 'Get started now') → sign in with personal MS account → 'Continue without a code' → Verify with LinkedIn (personal, not business) → create Azure account (profile, billing address, payment card) → $200 unlocked → decision email in ~3 business days → $1,000 → complete business verification → $5,000. Credits viewable in Azure Portal > Cost Management + Billing > Payment methods. If declined: reapply after 14 days.
Expiry
Credits valid up to 2 years once activated (extensions not available except the one-time 2-year extension at the $25K milestone). Credits must be activated within 90 days of accepting the program agreement or the offer lapses and you must reapply. Subscription auto-converts to pay-as-you-go when credits are exhausted or expired. Cannot transfer credits between Microsoft accounts/subscriptions.
Gotchas
Credit card IS required (identity verification, uncovered services, PAYG transition). Services not covered by credits, egress, or usage on a different billing profile still bill while credits remain. 'Already redeemed' error if your MSA previously redeemed another Microsoft program's credits. Country/region chosen at account creation cannot be changed later. The headline 'up to $150,000' is end-state: an unfunded solo startup realistically lands at $5,000 after verification. Trial-vs-free: this is a program, not a trial, but the first $200/$1,000 are time-boxed trial-style credits.
Source
learn.microsoft.com

Microsoft for Startups — Investor Network offer (referral code; YC/VC/accelerator front) high confidence

With a 10-character referral code from a Microsoft for Startups Investor Network partner (VC, accelerator, incubator, angel network, university), most startups begin with $100,000 in Azure credits 'with the opportunity to earn more', up to $150,000. Includes Azure Standard Support, technical expert access, go-to-market support, and free/discounted tools: GitHub Enterprise, LinkedIn Premium Business, Microsoft 365 Business Premium, Visual Studio Enterprise, Dynamics 365, Power Platform; higher levels add unlimited Azure Pairing consultations and Expert Network access plus third-party benefits.

Eligibility
Same company criteria as self-serve (privately held, for-profit, software-based, not Series C or later, <$350K lifetime free Azure credits, HQ in an MfS-supported region) PLUS a valid referral code; YC is an explicit partner — YC and M12 were first to access Microsoft's free GPU cluster preview (Nov 2023), and YC is among organizations that can supply codes. Microsoft does not publish the list of participating investors.
How to claim
Ask your VC/accelerator (e.g., YC) for the 10-character code → startups.microsoft.com → enter code → verify contact email + phone → business info must exactly match legal documents (mismatches fail verification) → application review ~3 business days → $1,000 starter credits immediately → approval unlocks expanded credits/benefits. Alternatively 'Sign up here' via the investor-referral link on microsoft.com/en/startups.
Expiry
Same mechanics as self-serve: credits valid up to 2 years once activated; activation due within 90 days of acceptance; PAYG after exhaustion.
Gotchas
Benefits often cannot be redeemed on an existing enterprise account or on an account that previously redeemed the same benefit. YC-affiliated founders without YC's code only get the self-serve track. Business verification failure stalls the high-value tier. Credits locked to one MSA.
Source
learn.microsoft.com

YC / M12 free Azure GPU clusters (Founders Hub GPU program) medium confidence

Free Azure AI infrastructure: high-end GPU virtual machine clusters for training/running LLMs and deep learning, delivered as a private preview. Announced 8 Nov 2023 with Y Combinator's cohort first (M12 portfolio next); 11 Jan 2024 Microsoft extended it to AI Grant Fund, Alchemist Accelerator, Conviction, The House Fund, OpenAI Startup Fund, Neo, and Elad Gil. This is the 'GPUs' element of the 2025–2026 successor framing to Startup Grad.

Eligibility
YC/M12 startups: limited preview cohort via the accelerator relationship — not a general self-serve application. Extension partners: founders 'affiliated' with the listed organizations.
How to claim
No public self-serve claim: access flows through the YC/investor relationship and Founders Hub application (referral-code path above).
Expiry
Described as private preview; no public sunset date published. Availability may have changed since the 2024 announcements (unverified in 2026).
Gotchas
Cohort-based and capacity-limited; GPU access is separate from Azure credit tiers; 'free' applies to the preview infrastructure, not necessarily to all surrounding egress/storage. Do not assume a YC alum can self-claim in 2026 — confirm with YC/Microsoft.
Source
microsoft.com

Azure free account — $200 trial credit + 12-month free services high confidence

$200 credit usable on Azure services within 30 days; 12 months of free monthly amounts for 20+ popular services (750 hrs each of B1s/B2pts v2/B2ats v2 VMs; 750 hrs PostgreSQL/MySQL Flexible Server B1MS with 32 GB storage+backup; 720 vCore-hrs SQL Managed Instance; 1 ACR Standard registry with 100 GB; 1M Function requests/mo; etc.); plus 65+ always-free services with free monthly amounts (Cosmos DB 1,000 RU/s + 25 GB, App Service 10 web apps, Container Apps 180k vCPU-seconds, etc.), which continue after 12 months at their monthly limits.

Eligibility
New Azure customers; one free-account offer per person; phone number and a credit card or non-prepaid debit card required (credit card only in HK/Brazil); Microsoft or GitHub account.
How to claim
azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free → 'Try Azure for free' → 3-step account creation (profile/country, billing address, payment card) → $200 usable for 30 days.
Expiry
$200 credit expires at 30 days (unused credit persists only within the 30-day window); 12-month free service amounts end at 12 months → pay-as-you-go for overages; always-free service amounts continue indefinitely.
Gotchas
Trial vs free: the 'free forever' services keep their monthly caps after the trial; 'spending protection' means the card is not charged while the offer is active (a temporary $1 authorization may post and reverse), not that no card is needed. After expiry the sponsorship subscription auto-converts to PAYG with no commitment. Egress/data beyond free amounts is billed. A .edu email does NOT upgrade this offer (student Azure access comes via the GitHub Pack instead).
Source
azure.microsoft.com

GitHub Student Developer Pack (incl. Azure for students, no credit card) high confidence

Free GitHub Pro while a student; GitHub Copilot for verified students (unlimited completions + AI-credit allowance, limited chat/agent); free Pro-level GitHub Codespaces; GitHub Pages (one user site); free domain offers (Namecheap .me + SSL 1 yr, Name.com free domain, .TECH 1 yr). Microsoft Azure track for 18+: 25+ Azure services free plus $100 Azure credit, explicitly 'no credit card required'. Track for ages 13–17: limited services (App Services, Functions, Notification Hubs, MySQL, Application Insights, Azure DevOps). Other partner benefits: Heroku (24 months), Datadog Pro (2 years), JetBrains, DataCamp, Notion, MongoDB, 1Password, ~50 more.

Eligibility
Current enrolled students (verified via .edu/school email or manual GitHub enrollment verification); separate age bands 18+ vs 13–17 for the Azure offers; the Azure portion is 'no credit card required', unlike the general free account.
How to claim
Sign up at education.github.com/pack on a GitHub account, verify student status, then redeem each partner offer (Azure credit via the Azure redemption inside the pack).
Expiry
Benefits last while student status is verified; individual partner offers carry their own time boxes (e.g., domains 1 yr, Heroku 24 mo, Datadog 2 yr).
Gotchas
Redeeming the pack's Azure offer can make an MSA 'already redeemed' for other Microsoft program credits (see MFS FAQ). $100 is small — pair with MFS self-serve for more. Free Pro/Copilot stop when you're no longer a student. No explicit 2-year duration stated for the core pack itself.
Source
education.github.com

Microsoft 365 A1 for Education + limited-time college Premium offer high confidence

Free 'A1' plan (donated by Microsoft): web Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote plus PC-only Publisher and Access for educators/students of qualified K-12 and higher-ed institutions; no OneDrive quota or desktop apps stated on the page. Limited-time college offer: 12 months of Microsoft 365 Premium free using a college email. Paid institutional tiers for contrast: A3 ($3.25 faculty/$2.50 student per user/mo), A5 ($8/$6).

Eligibility
.edu/institutional email required — 'working at, or enrolled in, a qualified educational organization' (K-12 and higher ed); college Premium offer requires a college email. No credit card.
How to claim
microsoft.com/en-us/education/products/office → 'Get started for students' (or 'for institutions'); automated email verification is usually instant, manual institution checks can take up to a month. College Premium: sign up with college email.
Expiry
A1 lasts while enrolled/employed; Premium college offer lasts 12 months, after which apps drop to reduced-functionality (view-only) mode.
Gotchas
A1 is web-centric: no full desktop suite and no stated 1 TB OneDrive. If the institution's plan lapses, existing files become view-only. 'Premium on us' is limited-time and regional — confirm availability.
Source
microsoft.com

Visual Studio free tiers (Community, VS Code, Dev Essentials) high confidence

Visual Studio Community edition (current VS18-generation IDE) free for individual developers on Windows; VS Code free on Windows/macOS/Linux; 'Visual Studio Dev Essentials' bundle offering free tools, Pluralsight training, and Azure credit for free. This is the surviving student IDE path: the dedicated 'Visual Studio for Students' download no longer exists (visualstudio.microsoft.com/visual-studio-for-students/ now returns 404 and the free-developer-offers page lists no student-specific program), consistent with Microsoft's 2025 shift to free IDE licensing for individuals.

Eligibility
Any individual developer, students included; no credit card; Dev Essentials is free via devessentials.microsoft.com. Community edition may not be used as an enterprise tool in large organizations (standard MS license terms).
How to claim
visualstudio.microsoft.com/free-developer-offers/ → download Community or VS Code; Dev Essentials registration for the bundle + Azure credit.
Expiry
Licensing is ongoing while free; no time-boxed expiry for Community/VS Code. Dev Essentials Azure credit has its own (small, unspecified) duration.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: 'Visual Studio 2022 for individual developers' is now a permanent free edition, not a trial; VS Enterprise/for Teams are paid. If you specifically need the 'Visual Studio for Students' SKU, it is gone — students should use Community or VS Code.
Source
visualstudio.microsoft.com

Azure OpenAI / Microsoft Foundry models — free rate-limited quota tier medium confidence

Microsoft Foundry (Azure OpenAI) model quotas now run on seven tiers: Free Tier plus Tiers 1–6 with automatic upgrades based on usage/payment history. Free quota tables published per model (e.g., current docs: gpt-5 GlobalStandard ~1,000 RPM / 250,000 TPM; gpt-4o-mini GlobalStandard ~20,000 RPM / 2,000,000 TPM in the lowest tier; several models show 'N/A' for the Azure-for-Students/free-trials column, meaning no special free quota). MFS participants get model access 'through Microsoft Foundry' inside their startup credits.

Eligibility
Any Azure subscription; free tier available to new/free-trial accounts; no .edu requirement and no credit-card requirement beyond the underlying Azure account (which does need a card for the standard free account — the card-free student path is the GitHub Pack offer).
How to claim
Create an Azure resource for Foundry/OpenAI and select the free deployment tier; MFS startups simply use their startup credits on the same Foundry endpoints.
Expiry
Rate limits, not a dollar allowance; the page documents no hard monthly token cap or end date for the free tier today (older 90-day time limit unverified on the current page); auto tier-upgrades can be opted out of (NoAutoUpgrade flag).
Gotchas
Rate limits can throttle heavy dev use; model availability is region-dependent; free-tier endpoints may exclude data retention guarantees paid tiers get; 'free tier' ≠ the $200 trial credit — it coexists with pay-as-you-go billing once limits are hit.
Source
learn.microsoft.com

2025–26 changes

  • 1) Startup Grad (Microsoft x YC, historical ~$50,000 Azure credits per YC company) is no longer offered: superseded by Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub (up to $150K via milestones/Investor Network, verified on official docs dated May–Oct 2026) plus the 8 Nov 2023 YC/M12 free GPU-cluster preview (extended 11 Jan 2024 to other incubators). The ~$50K figure could not be re-verified on any official Microsoft page today (secondary sources only), so the historical detail is low-confidence. 2) Major 2025 change (reported 2 Feb 2026 by theainewsdigest; corroborated by the current official docs' milestone structure): on 2 Jul 2025 Microsoft restructured Microsoft for Startups — the old four-level grid ($1K/$5K/$25K/$100K-up-to-$150K, where Level 1 gave every approved startup $1,000) was replaced by the milestone ladder (self-serve $200 → $5K → $25K → $50K → $150K) plus an Investor Network referral-code track (most investor-backed startups start at $100K). Unfunded startups effectively dropped from up to $150K to $5K total. Eligibility also shifted: lifetime-free-credit cap raised from $10K to $350K, and the funding ceiling moved from pre-Series D to pre-Series C. 3) 'Visual Studio for Students' appears sunset: its URL now 404s and the free-developer-offers page (fetched 2026-10-22) lists no student program; VS Community/VS Code are the free path (consistent with MS's 2025 move to free individual IDE licensing). 4) YC's 'successor' claim in the brief — 'up to $150K + AI Foundry + GPUs' — verified: up to $150K milestones on MFS docs, Microsoft Foundry model access inside MFS benefits, and the YC-first GPU cluster program (status in 2026 unconfirmed). Web search budget was exhausted mid-task; all 2026 items above were read directly from official pages today, except the 2 Jul 2025 restructuring (secondary source, corroborated by current docs).
