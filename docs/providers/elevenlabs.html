ElevenLabs: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: ElevenLabs Startup Grants Program: 12 months free, 33M characters. 2 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

ElevenLabs Startup Grants Program: 12 months free, 33M characters high confidence

Official pricing page lists the Startup Grants Program with '12 Months free', '33M Characters', 'Valid for 12 months' and 'To build, launch & test'. Official blog (Aug 12, 2025) says the term was extended from 3 to 12 months and every recipient gets 33 million credits. Official grants page says '33,000,000 characters', 'over 680 hours of Agents usage', 'More than $5,500+ in value', plus founder community events and startup support. The grant covers the full platform (Text to Speech, Speech to Text, voice cloning and design, sound effects, music, Conversational AI agents, API), not only agents, so the index description 'for startups building conversational agents' is too narrow. A May 13, 2026 LinkedIn post describes the same 33M credits across ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents and ElevenAPI. Credit costs in the shared pool (pricing FAQ): Text to Speech 1 credit per character (V2 Flash/Turbo API models 0.5-1), Speech to Text about 330 credits per minute, Eleven Music about 900 per minute, Sound Effects 200 per generation, Voice Changer and Isolator about 1,000 per minute, Dubbing 2,000 to 10,000 per minute. A third-party summary says the 680-hour figure is an estimate that depends on features used.

Eligibility
Startups and small companies with fewer than 25 employees at the time of application, anywhere in the world. Must have a business or monetization strategy and a valid business email, and be building a product for the long term. Not eligible: one-off projects or campaigns, agencies, consulting firms, SMBs, existing enterprise customers, and projects for children aged 18 or under. Paying self-serve customers are eligible. One application per company; a founder with several companies can apply once for each.
How to claim
Click 'Apply' on https://elevenlabs.io/startup-grants, which leads to https://elevenlabs.io/grants-application. Describe what you are building, your team and expected growth, using a business email. Applications are rolling with no deadline; ElevenLabs says it will reply within one week. If accepted, the grant plan and credits are applied to the account you applied with (the blog says the account is upgraded immediately), so apply from the account you will build with. The grants-application page's captured text shows only a 'Sign up' link and no form fields, so the full application flow was not confirmed.
Expiry
12 months from award ('over the next 12 months' per the blog). The grant also ends early once all 33M characters are used. The account converts automatically to the Free plan when the grant period ends or once fewer than 10,000 credits remain. ElevenLabs emails you when you near the cap.
Gotchas
(1) Auto-conversion: at term end, or when fewer than 10,000 credits remain, the account drops to the Free plan unless you choose otherwise. The official page does not say whether a card is charged on conversion; a third-party summary says no charge. (2) Non-transferable: 'You cannot exchange or resell your text characters'; reselling terminates the grant immediately. (3) Monetization required: hobby, student, coursework or one-off projects are out of scope. (4) After the grant you can move to a paid plan (Starter $6, Creator $22, Pro $99, Scale $299 per month on the pricing page), contact sales@elevenlabs.io for a long-term plan, or pause, per the blog. (5) Third-party sites (e8.team) say an ElevenLabs badge or link must be displayed, previous recipients are excluded, and access is Scale-tier. None of these appear on the official pages checked, so treat them as unverified. The official heading reads 'Scale for 12 months' without defining the features. (6) One anecdotal Reddit report (cited by e8.team) describes weeks 'in queue' with no follow-up, against the official one-week promise. (7) Commercial use during the grant is implied by the monetization requirement but was not explicitly confirmed on the pages checked.

ElevenLabs Free plan: 10,000 credits per month at $0 high confidence

Official pricing page lists Free at $0 per month with 10k credits per month; the comparison table lists about 10 minutes of Text to Speech. Free includes Text to Speech, Speech to Text, Sound Effects, Voice Design, Music, Productions, Image, and 3 Projects in Studio. Not included on Free: Commercial License, Instant Voice Cloning and Dubbing Studio (first listed on Starter), and Professional Voice Cloning (first listed on Creator). No end date is stated. This is a standing tier open to anyone, not a student or startup offer; Startup Grant recipients drop to it at the end of the grant.

Eligibility
Anyone who signs up. Nothing on the pricing or terms pages limits it to students or startups. Terms of Use section 1(c) 'Use Restrictions': users of the Services free of charge may only use them for non-commercial purposes.
How to claim
Sign up on elevenlabs.io and use the monthly allowance. The official quickstart describes creating an account and an API key in the dashboard, with no application or claim step.
Expiry
None stated. Free is listed as a $0 plan with no end date.
Gotchas
(1) Non-commercial use only per Terms of Use section 1(c). The WebFetch summary of that section adds that non-commercial use requires a personal e-mail address. (2) No voice cloning on Free. (3) No rollover: unused credits roll over only with an active paid subscription; otherwise they expire at cycle end. (4) Shared pool: the 10k credits cover about 10 minutes of Text to Speech per the table, but Speech to Text costs about 330 credits per minute and Dubbing 2,000 or more per minute, so the pool stretches much less for those. (5) Unverified: 'no card for the API key'. The quickstart docs are silent on cost and cards, and the pricing page does not mention a card.

2025–26 changes

  • Verdict: a genuine free offer exists for startups (Startup Grants Program, still listed on the official pricing page as of 2026-10-22; a May 2026 post also describes it as live). No student offer found: the pricing page compare table says 'Student program: Not mentioned', and https://elevenlabs.io/education has no discount or credits (education appears only in its mission statement). The Free plan is a real standing tier open to everyone, not a student or startup offer. Changes: the Startup Grant term was extended from 3 to 12 months on Aug 12, 2025 (official blog); older community posts describe earlier structures (a 3-month term, per-month minute allowances) that are not the current terms. No discontinued offer was found. Near-miss promos, not free and not targeted at students or startups: Starter first month shown at $1 ('83% off', 'Until Nov 1' per the pricing page, year not stated), Creator first month 50% off, and annual billing described as 'two months free' (annual price is ten times monthly). Limits of this check: the WebSearch budget ran out after one successful search, so a dedicated student-discount search and an independent free-tier attribution check could not run; those points rest on official pages only (pricing, terms, startup-grants, grants-application, education, quickstart). Unverified: card requirement for the Free API key; whether grant-to-Free conversion charges a card (a third-party site says no); whether badge or link and previous-recipient rules apply (third-party claims absent from the official pages checked).
