Flutterwave: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free developer account + free sandbox/test environment. 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free developer account + free sandbox/test environment high confidence

Signup at app.flutterwave.com/register grants a 'free test environment where you can simulate your preferred transactions' with mock data: 8 success + 7 failure test cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve, Afrigo) with test OTP/PIN, Access Bank test accounts 0690000031-0690000041 (+blacklisted 0690000036), any mobile-money number with OTP 123456, Capitec test numbers, BVN test values, mock wallets and virtual cards. Transfers default to PENDING and update after 10 minutes (overridable per-transaction with _PMCK / _PMCK_ST_F / DU_{minutes} suffixes). Webhooks: 60s timeout, 3 retries at 30-minute intervals; testing is done by pointing your endpoint at webhook.site (no native in-platform webhook simulator). No fees, volume caps, or paid tiers for the test environment are stated anywhere in the docs.

Eligibility
Open signup - no .edu email, no credit card, no startup age/funding cap, and no stated region limit for test mode. KYC verification is only required to enable LIVE transactions; test mode works with unverified accounts. Any OTP in test transactions passes validation (5548/6648 simulate specific failures).
How to claim
Register at app.flutterwave.com/register, receive test API credentials from the dashboard, and build against the test endpoints using the published mock cards/accounts. Contact support for mock data tied to a specific flow. Go-live requires completing KYC, after which settings can be transferred from the test environment to the live account.
Expiry
No expiry, renewal, or sunset date stated for the test environment; it is a standing free capability of every account.
Gotchas
The previously reported '30-day test-data retention' could NOT be confirmed in the current docs (testing.md, quickstart.md, api-basics.md) - treat as unverified/possibly outdated. Webhook testing relies on external webhook.site, not a built-in simulator. Checkout test URLs must use localhost:port (127.0.0.1 errors). Transfer integration testing requires IP whitelisting of your calling servers. Test receipts are emailed to your business email, not to customers. Live transactions cost per-transaction fees (e.g. NGN: 2% local = 1.4% + 0.6% platform, 4.8% international; transfers NGN 10/25/50; fees subject to 7.5% VAT), so the free part ends at going live.
Source
developer.flutterwave.com

Free Store / free e-commerce website (Flutterwave Store, SME) high confidence

Official Store page FAQ: 'It's totally free to setup a store.' Marketing copy: 'Create a free ecommerce website and start selling worldwide with just a few clicks' and 'Create a free online store that helps you find customers, and accept payments globally.' Included: multiple products & variants, mobile store management, low-stock notifications, delivery-fee charging, customer discounts, multiple payment options, and listing on Flutterwave Market ('Africa's fastest growing marketplace'). No setup charge stated; transaction fees apply per the country pricing schedule.

Eligibility
General merchant/SME - no student, .edu, or startup-specific restrictions stated. Region: site localized (e.g. /us/store); no funding/age caps published.
How to claim
Primary CTA on the page is 'Contact sales' (/us/contact-sales); the standard flow per the page's own steps is: create account, set up store with product photos/specs, share the store link and start selling. Footer also points 'Create a free account' at the contact-sales flow.
Expiry
No expiry or limits stated on the free store itself; fee schedule has no published effective date.
Gotchas
No product caps, tier limits, or fee schedule disclosed on the store page - live charges follow the per-country pricing (e.g. Ghana: 2.6% local cards, 4.8% international cards; NGN 2%/4.8%). Setup is free but setup may be routed through sales rather than self-serve.
Source
flutterwave.com

No recurring subscription - live fees are purely per-transaction (verified Nigeria & Ghana schedules) high confidence

Server-rendered country pricing pages confirm there is no subscription, setup, or seat fee: fees are per-transaction. Nigeria: local collections 2% per transaction (1.4% transaction + 0.6% platform), international 4.8%; transfers to bank/Mobile Money NGN 10 (<=5k), 25 (5,001-50k), 50 (>50k); all transaction fees subject to 7.5% VAT. Ghana: local cards 2.6%, local bank transfer & wallets/mobile money 2%, international cards 4.8% (settled in local currency by default); transfers GHS 10 (bank) or 1.5% (mobile money); pricing excludes VAT; next-day settlement for local payments. No free-transaction allowance (e.g. 'first N free') appears on these pages.

Eligibility
Applies to all live accounts; no student/startup discount tiers found on the official pricing pages.
How to claim
No separate claim - this is the fee baseline: after the free test environment, costs accrue only per live transaction; there is no paid plan to subscribe to.
Expiry
No effective dates or sunset notices shown on the pricing pages; figures verified today (2026-10-22).
Gotchas
The US pricing page (/us/pricing) is JS-rendered and its fee table could not be extracted via fetch - US rates unverified in this pass. VAT/tax excluded from displayed rates. 'Why you got charged' link now redirects to a separate dispute subdomain (dispute.flutterwave.com).
Source
flutterwave.com

Flutterwave MFB (Meridian Bank) business account - waitlist, unquantified low confidence

Flutterwave holds an MFB license (FinCEN-reg Flutterwave Payments LLC, NMLS ID 2391594). The /us/bank page is a waitlist/announcement page: consumer banking inside SendApp ('get your own account number', wallets, Tap-to-Pay coming soon) and business banking ('open a business account', multi-currency, payroll/mass payouts, working-capital loans 'powered by real transaction data'). Compliance: PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 1 & SOC 2.

Eligibility
Not student/startup-specific; aimed at consumers and businesses. No amounts, minimum balances, eligibility thresholds, or free-account terms published yet - the page contains zero exact monetary figures.
How to claim
Join the waitlist at flutterwave.com/us/bank; features not all live yet (Tap-to-Pay 'coming soon', 'coming soon' items present).
Expiry
Roadmap/waitlist stage; no published dates or limits.
Gotchas
Do not count on a 'free bank account' offer - no fee schedule or free-tier terms are stated; lending/treasury features are described without caps or APRs.
