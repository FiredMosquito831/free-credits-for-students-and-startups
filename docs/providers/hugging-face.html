Hugging Face: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Inference Providers free account: NO monthly credits (as of Oct 2026). 15 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Inference Providers free account: NO monthly credits (as of Oct 2026) high confidence

Official pricing docs (read 2026-10-22): Free Users row in the included-credits table is 'None' — free accounts get no monthly compute credits. Free users can still access Inference Providers (18 providers, 200+ models: Baseten, Cerebras, Cohere, DeepInfra, Fal, Featherless, Fireworks, Groq, HF Inference, Novita, Nscale, OVHcloud, Public AI, Replicate, Scaleway, Together, WaveSpeed, Z.ai) via pay-as-you-go 'credits purchase required', or via a custom provider key. The PRO marketing page claims free-tier users get a 100k token/month inference usage cap vs 2M for PRO, but the pricing docs define no free credit and I could not find a published limits table — treat the 100k figure as marketing, not docs.

Eligibility
Any Hugging Face account (free, global, no .edu, no credit card to create the account or to browse; a credit card IS required before any pay-as-you-go usage, since extra usage requires purchasing credits via Stripe). No student or startup special tier.
How to claim
Sign up at huggingface.co, generate a fine-grained token with 'Make calls to Inference Providers' permission; add a credit card in Billing settings and purchase credits to exceed the free usage cap.
Expiry
Ongoing; no stated expiry
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: there is no trial-credit offer on Inference Providers — the only ever-free allowance was $0.10/month for free accounts, discontinued 2026-10-07 (docs update). No credit card stated as required at sign-up, but one is required to buy pay-as-you-go credits. Monthly tokens cap (100k, unverified in docs) is a usage limit, not a free grant.

Custom Provider Key: $0 on Hugging Face (bring-your-own provider key) high confidence

Documented in the official pricing page (read 2026-10-22): you can set a custom provider key in HF settings (or pass it to the InferenceClient / OpenAI-compatible client) and HF routes requests for free — 'Hugging Face will not charge you for the call'; billing goes directly to the provider you already pay. This is the only genuinely card-free-on-HF way to use Inference Providers.

Eligibility
All account types incl. free. You need your own account/key with the provider; no region limit stated; no credit card needed on HF.
How to claim
Settings → Inference Providers → add custom provider key, or pass the key to InferenceClient; code and OpenAI-compatible SDK usage are unchanged.
Expiry
Ongoing
Gotchas
Included monthly credits do NOT apply to custom-provider-key usage. The provider's own free tier/trial terms now govern cost — this is a 'no markup' pass-through, not a free allowance. If the provider later requires its own payment method, the 'free' path ends there.
Source
huggingface.co

PRO plan: $9/mo subscription including $2.00/mo compute credits (paid, not free) high confidence

PRO ($9/month, credit card only, Stripe, no stated trial) includes $2.00/month of general-purpose compute credits usable on Inference Providers, Inference Endpoints, upgraded CPU/GPU Spaces hardware incl. ZeroGPU usage beyond the 5-min free daily quota, and Jobs. PRO also raises the inference usage cap to 2M tokens/mo (vs 100k free claim), 1 TB private storage (base price $12-18/TB/mo above), 10 ZeroGPU Spaces, 8× daily ZeroGPU quota (40 min RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell), dev-mode Spaces. No student discount, no free PRO tier, no student-specific enrollment found on huggingface.co/pro or /pricing today.

Eligibility
Any user globally; credit card required for the subscription. No .edu requirement, no regional restriction stated.
How to claim
Subscribe at https://huggingface.co/subscribe/pro; credits are credited monthly and applied automatically before pay-as-you-go billing.
Expiry
Renews monthly; cancel anytime in billing settings. Docs say credits are 'credited every month' but do not promise rollover of unused credits.
Gotchas
The $2 credit does not cover the $9 subscription. 'Pay Pro with credits' was reportedly possible in the past and now appears removed (HF forums, Feb 2026). Not a student/startup offer.
Source
huggingface.co

Team plan: $20/user/mo incl. $2/seat/mo shared credits (paid, not free) high confidence

Team orgs ($20/user/mo; self-serve, credit card or AWS/Google Cloud Marketplace billing) get $2.00/month compute credits per seat, shared across members, spendable on Inference Providers, Inference Endpoints, Spaces upgrades and Jobs. Org admins can set spending limits and disable providers; usage billable centrally via X-HF-Bill-To header or bill_to client parameter.

Eligibility
Orgs globally; card or cloud-marketplace billing. No startup/education discount published.
How to claim
Subscribe Team in org settings; members specify bill_to org (or X-HF-Bill-To header) to draw on the shared credit pool.
Gotchas
Credits only apply to HF-routed usage, not custom provider keys. Not free; included only for completeness of the 'credits' picture.

Free static Spaces hosting (unlimited) high confidence

Static Spaces (plain HTML, or built React/Svelte/Vue) are $0, free for everyone, unlimited in number, with no hardware charge and no card required. Default per-Space limits: 2 vCPU / 16 GB RAM / 50 GB non-persistent disk; CPU Basic (2 vCPU / 16 GB) has 'no hourly cost' but as of mid-2026 creating a compute Space (Gradio or Docker, incl. CPU Basic) requires a paid plan (PRO $9/mo personal; Team $20/Enterprise $50 per user for orgs). Static SDK Spaces never require a paid plan. All free for anonymous visitors too; no egress fee noted for Spaces.

Eligibility
Any account, free; no credit card, no age/region limit stated for static Spaces. No .edu requirement.
How to claim
Create a Space with the Static SDK at huggingface.co/spaces -> 'Create new Space', push your site; it deploys free forever. Zero setup cost, card only needed if you later add paid hardware.
Expiry
Indefinite while account exists; auto-rebuild on each push. Space goes to sleep when idle.
Gotchas
As of ~July 2026 free accounts can no longer create Gradio/Docker compute Spaces (incl. free CPU Basic) — static only; CPU Basic downgrade from ZeroGPU also requires PRO. Non-persistent 50GB disk, 16GB RAM ceiling. PRO is $9/mo card-only; free tier has no hourly charge but compute creation is gated.
Source
huggingface.co

ZeroGPU free GPU demos (host up to 2 Spaces + free usage quota) high confidence

ZeroGPU dynamically allocates NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell (half = 48GB VRAM 'large', full = 96GB 'xlarge', xlarge costs 2x quota). Free personal accounts 'in good standing' (verified email, account older than 30 days) can HOST up to 2 ZeroGPU Spaces at $0. Visitors get daily GPU quotas: unauthenticated 2 min, free account 5 min, PRO 40 min (8x), Team 40 min, Enterprise 60 min; quota resets exactly 24h after first usage. Default per-call runtime 60s; over-quota usage $1 per 10 min, but only PRO/Team/Enterprise can extend (pre-paid credits). No card required for the free tier.

Eligibility
Free personal accounts: email verified + account >30 days old; Gradio SDK only; PyTorch-first (transformers/diffusers best). No .edu or startup requirement; no card required.
How to claim
Create a Gradio Space with ZeroGPU hardware selected, decorate GPU code with @spaces.GPU, push; hosting is free for up to 2 such Spaces. Usage by any visitor is free within their daily 5-min (free account) quota.
Expiry
Indefinite; daily quota resets every 24h. PRO is $9/mo (card required) for 10 Spaces, 40 min quota and overage billing.
Gotchas
Free accounts cannot buy over-quota credits (paid tiers only). xlarge (96GB) burns 2x quota (45s task = 90s quota). After 2026 hardware migration some workloads auto-fell back to xlarge, doubling effective quota consumption (forum reports May 2026). Queue priority rises as your remaining daily quota falls. Gradio-only; Docker Spaces can't use ZeroGPU. Historically an undocumented run-count limit (~3 runs/day, staff-confirmed April 2026) may still apply on top of the 5-min time quota. A forum answer (July 2026) suggests new free accounts see ZeroGPU as the only creatable runtime — CPU Basic now PRO-gated.
Source
huggingface.co

Community GPU grants (in-kind, rolling) medium confidence

HF offers 'community GPU grants' for side projects: in-kind dedicated GPU hardware that ZeroGPU can't cover (non-PyTorch heavy init, models >96GB VRAM, always-on serving) or an extra Gradio Space beyond the free 2-Space cap without needing PRO. No dollar amount, duration or pool size published; decisions can take days; approved hardware attaches automatically (Static Spaces just flip SDK to gradio).

Eligibility
Public, open use cases — 'open research demo, hobbyist project, educational tool, institutional showcase' — that 'can't pay for the hardware'. No funding-cap or age limit; free account works; card not needed for the grant itself.
How to claim
1) Build your Space (ZeroGPU or Static). 2) Post a Community Tab discussion titled 'Apply for a GPU community grant: <Personal|Company|Academic> project' with a one-paragraph description + justification (default: 'Public open-source demo, can't cover the hardware cost'). 3) Wait for approval; hardware attaches automatically.
Expiry
Rolling, no published deadline; no published grant duration.
Gotchas
In-kind only, no cash; approval is discretionary and slow (days+); not a formal startup program; don't rely on a guaranteed quota.
Source
huggingface.co

Gotchas
Compute (GPU) is not part of the free classroom bundle — it's storage/hub hosting of repos, datasets, models and static demos; hardware upgrades are separate paid services. Limits not published, so heavy use could hit general free-tier storage limits.

Academia Hub (education license — PAID, free only at partner universities) high confidence

Institution-level education plan: from $10/seat/month with volume discounts; minimum 250 annual seats; includes 1TB private + 1TB public storage per seat, $2/month/seat of compute credits (Inference Providers, Inference Endpoints, upgraded Spaces hardware, Jobs), higher daily ZeroGPU priority, SSO/SCIM/SOC 2. Students get nothing for free as individuals — HF staff confirmed there is no student Pro plan (Oct 2025 forum). Partner universities (e.g., ESCP, 12,000+ students) negotiate free access for their community.

Eligibility
University IT/procurement must initiate from a university email; individual .edu email is NOT enough. Free only at partner institutions that have negotiated a deal.
How to claim
Contact HF from your university's IT/procurement team to negotiate; or at a partner university, your admin provisions your seat — then you get PRO-equivalent features + $2/mo compute credits free of personal cost.
Expiry
Subscription term, renews automatically; per-institution negotiation.
Gotchas
Not free for most students; the $2/mo compute credits are for paid seats the institution funds; minimum 250 seats excludes most small departments (Team plan suggested instead).

ZeroGPU free GPU inference (Spaces) high confidence

Shared NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs allocated on demand to Gradio Spaces. 'large' = half card, 48GB VRAM (1x quota); 'xlarge' = full card, 96GB VRAM (2x quota). Daily GPU quotas: unauthenticated 2 min; free account 5 min; PRO/Team 40 min; Enterprise 60 min; paid plans can extend via pre-paid credits at $1/10 min. Quota resets exactly 24h after first daily GPU use. No credit card required for the free tier; no stated regional limits. Free accounts host max 2 ZeroGPU Spaces (PRO: 10; orgs: 50). Gradio SDK only; @spaces.GPU decorator, default 60s function runtime (extendable via duration=...); no torch.compile (use PyTorch AOT, torch 2.8+).

Eligibility
Any free HF account in good standing: verified email address and account older than 30 days. No .edu required, no startup status, no credit card, no stated region limits. Using (visiting) existing ZeroGPU Spaces is free for everyone, even unauthenticated (2 min/day).
How to claim
Sign up at huggingface.co (free), verify email; create a Gradio Space and select ZeroGPU hardware in Space Settings (free accounts: up to 2 such Spaces, allowed only after account is 30+ days old); import spaces and decorate GPU functions with @spaces.GPU (default size large; size='xlarge' for 96GB at 2x quota cost); deploy and use within the 5 min/day free quota.
Expiry
Quota renews daily (resets 24h after first use). Hosting cap (2 ZeroGPU Spaces) persists while account stays in good standing; no sunset date published for the program.
Gotchas
Gradio-only (Docker/static cannot use ZeroGPU); xlarge doubles quota consumption and queue wait; free tier cannot buy quota extension ($1/10-min credits only for PRO/Team/Enterprise); model must be loaded to 'cuda' at module level (CUDA is emulated outside @spaces.GPU functions); remaining quota determines queue priority, so idle free users queue behind paid ones; as of 2025-2026 creating new CPU Gradio/Docker Spaces now requires a paid plan - the 2-Space ZeroGPU exception is the only free Gradio path.
Source
huggingface.co

Static Spaces + free Hub tier (free hosting, no GPU) high confidence

Static HTML Spaces are free for everyone on any plan. CPU Basic hardware (2 vCPU/16GB, no accelerator) is $0/hour. Free personal accounts get 100GB private storage (public storage 'best-effort' free), unlimited public models/datasets repos, and free CPU Spaces. No credit card needed. Inference Endpoints/Jobs (paid GPU) are NOT free - there is no free notebook/training offering.

Eligibility
Any free HF account, worldwide, no verification beyond the account itself; static Spaces need no email-verified/30-day status the way ZeroGPU hosting does.
How to claim
Create a free account, create a Space with SDK 'static HTML' (or CPU Basic Gradio/Docker if already on a paid plan); no payment setup required.
Expiry
Ongoing free tier; no expiry stated. Note the 2025-2026 change: new Gradio/Docker Spaces (even on free CPU Basic) now require PRO (personal) or Team/Enterprise (orgs) to create or duplicate - only Static Spaces remain free for free accounts.
Gotchas
The marketing page /classrooms still says demos are 'hosted for free', but the Spaces docs explicitly require a paid plan for new Gradio/Docker Spaces; duplicating a Gradio/Docker Space follows the same paid rule (ZeroGPU exception only). Free spaces sleep when idle; protected/private visibility requires PRO/Team/Enterprise. Free private storage capped at 100GB, overflow is billed.
Source

Community GPU grants (free dedicated GPU hardware for Spaces) medium confidence

Case-by-case grants that cover Space hardware upgrade costs (dedicated GPU, or ZeroGPU slots beyond the free 2-Space cap) for public-facing community use cases: open research demos, hobbyist projects, educational tools, institutional showcases; company projects may apply ('Company project' option). No fixed dollar amount - the grant attaches the needed hardware to the Space. Official docs link to applying 'using the link in the Settings tab of your Space'. No credit card; no stated startup age/funding caps.

Eligibility
Qualifying public, non-commercial-leaning Space use cases where the user 'can't pay for the hardware' (open source, research, educational, institutional). Private/commercial/closed-source projects are steered to PRO instead. No .edu required for company grants.
How to claim
Build the Space (as ZeroGPU if you still have a free slot, else a free Static Space with ZeroGPU-ready code), then open a Community Tab discussion on the Space titled 'Apply for a GPU community grant: <Personal|Company|Academic> project' with one paragraph on what the app does and one on why it should run on free GPU; wait days for review; approved grants attach hardware automatically (Static holding Spaces must flip sdk to gradio). The entry point is also the 'apply for a community GPU grant' link in the Space Settings tab.
Expiry
Grant covers the attached hardware while the Space/project qualifies; no published renewal term. The old public application page (huggingface.co/community-grants) is reported dead as of 2026-Q2; the current channel is the per-Space Settings tab link / Community discussion.
Gotchas
Approval takes days and is discretionary ('see if yours makes the cut'); open public demos typically approved, commercial ones not; grant docs come mainly from the official huggingface/skills repo rather than a dedicated marketing page, so policy can shift without announcement; no guarantees on hardware size.
