Zoho CRM Free Edition: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Zoho CRM Free Edition (free forever, 3 users). 12 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Zoho CRM Free Edition (free forever, 3 users) high confidence

Forever free Zoho CRM. Limits: up to 3 users; 5,000 API calls/day; up to 10 custom email templates; free data export up to 10 times; spreadsheet import free. Includes contact management, follow-up reminders, workflow automation, tasks/meetings/call tracking, standard reports, mobile app. Excluded: mass email, forecasting, CPQ, custom portals, reporting hierarchy, sandbox, field-level encryption, AI agents (paid tiers only). No billing/credit-card for the free plan. No region limit stated.

Eligibility
Anyone; up to 3 users; no .edu, no startup cap, no credit card; appears available in all Zoho-supported regions.
How to claim
Go to zoho.com/crm/pricing.html, click 'Start Free' / create account on the Free edition; no payment method entered.
Expiry
Free forever; no renewal. Limits (3 users, 5,000 API calls/day, 10 email templates, 10 exports) are the permanent caps.
Gotchas
No storage cap or automation-count cap is published; data export capped at 10 times total; API quota 5,000 calls/day is lower than Standard paid (15,000/day) — API workloads will hit the ceiling; paid-tier features are hard-gated; local taxes added to any later paid upgrade.

Zoho for Startups — Zoho Wallet Credits program (startup program) high confidence

Early-stage startups get Zoho Wallet Credits to trial Zoho's 55+ app SaaS suite (any Zoho app/suite incl. Zoho One, Workplace Stage 1, People Plus; also upgrades, add-ons, monthly renewals; Workplace/Mail excluded from Stage-2 credits). Two stages: base credit after validation + extra credit if the startup hosts its domain with Zoho within 15 days. Credits valid 360 days, then balance resets to 0, no extensions; one-time benefit; not transferable, not refundable. Amounts in $ are not published on the page (left blank as fetched today).

Eligibility
Must fit Startup India definition; registered with a collaborating ecosystem enabler (incubator/accelerator/coworking space) OR DPIIT/Startup India recognition; must be new/free Zoho user with no paid-subscription history; MSMEs excluded; one claim only. Limited to startups in ~24 countries (India, UK, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, UAE, Brazil, Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana-listed neighbors, etc.).
How to claim
1) Create Zoho account with the org's primary admin email. 2) Register at the Zoho founders-registration page, selecting your associated startup hub. 3) Validation takes 5-7 business days. 4) Credits land in Zoho Wallet; follow the confirmation email to enable domain hosting for Stage-2 credits within 15 days.
Expiry
Credits expire 360 days from deposit, no extension; program is one-time per startup.
Gotchas
Credit values are undisclosed on the public page (verify at registration); strictly gated on enabler/DPIIT association — no open application; deleting the account zeroes the balance; weekdays-only classic email support and free English consultation only; training via paid Zoho Partners; no US in the country list as fetched today (confirm current list on the page).
Source
zoho.com

Zoho CRM paid-edition free trial (Standard/Professional/Enterprise/Ultimate, Bigin, CRM Plus) medium confidence

Free trial of all paid editions via 'START FREE TRIAL' CTAs on the pricing page; 'flexible free trial' signup; no trial duration in days and no credit-card statement appears anywhere on zoho.com/crm/pricing.html as fetched today. Trial signups for free editions elsewhere in Zoho use email only (no billing).

Eligibility
Anyone signing up; new accounts; no .edu requirement; credit card not required for trial sign-up on Zoho product pages (email/password only).
How to claim
On zoho.com/crm/pricing.html click 'Start free trial' for the chosen paid edition and complete email sign-up; downgrade/choice of plan available during signup.
Expiry
Trial window not stated on the CRM page (sibling Zoho products state 14-day Books/Cliq trials and 15-day Desk/Creator trials); at trial end you are charged only if you convert to paid.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: after the trial you must actively subscribe (paid) — unlike Desk/Creator, Zoho CRM has no public auto-downgrade-to-Free path stated on the pricing page; free edition remains available separately; duration unverified on official page today (medium confidence).
