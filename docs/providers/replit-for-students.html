Replit for Students: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free Starter tier (no subscription, no claim needed). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free Starter tier (no subscription, no claim needed) high confidence

Replit's free plan is called the Starter tier. Per official docs: 1 free published app (Lite build or Design Canvas) whose link automatically goes down after 30 days; daily Agent/AI chat credits 'up to a monthly cap' (exact numbers not published); monthly cloud credits covering databases, object storage, and publishing; 0 active background tasks; 0 collaboration seats; Lite build only (full builds require Core); 'Made with Replit' badge cannot be removed; third-party connectors, AI integrations, Plan Mode and non-web/mobile artifact types require Core/Pro. The /edu/students marketing page lists the free tier as 'Unlimited public apps, Replit AI basic, community support, build on any device' — note this conflicts with the docs' 1-published-app limit. Current paid reference points (replit.com/pricing, read today): Core $20/mo ($18/mo billed annually), Pro $100/mo ($90/mo billed annually).

Eligibility
Anyone with a free Replit account; no student/startup status required; no credit card requirement is stated on the plan docs or pricing page.
How to claim
Sign up free at replit.com; the Starter tier is the default. No verification or application needed.
Expiry
Permanent free tier; published free app links auto-take-down after 30 days; AI agent chat is on a daily cap with a monthly cap; cloud credits reset monthly.
Gotchas
The single free published app expires after 30 days and requires Core ($20/mo) to publish additional/longer-lived apps. Once included Agent credits are exhausted, usage-based (effort-based) pricing and credit packs apply — pay-as-you-go overages, not a hard stop. Data transfer, databases and compute are billed usage-based on top of monthly cloud credits (docs: Publishing and Database Billing). Marketing 'unlimited public apps' vs docs '1 published app (30-day)' is a real discrepancy to verify in-account.
Source
docs.replit.com

Student discount: 50% off Replit Core (~$10/mo vs $20/mo) with verified .edu email medium confidence

Official /edu/students page (text via search index; page is JS-rendered): 'Free to start. 50% off Core with your .edu email.' Core is $20/mo, so students pay ~$10/mo, auto-applied at checkout when the account email is recognized as academic — no coupon, no GitHub Student Pack, no document upload. Core unlocks 10x more AI compute, faster builds, more CPU/RAM, private projects, and priority support on top of Starter. Launched Sept 19, 2025; early reports described it as '$10/mo for the first six months', but the current official page states no time cap or renewal requirement.

Eligibility
Currently enrolled student with a valid academic email (.edu or equivalent institutional domain); .edu convention is US-centric — non-US students may need to confirm with Replit support. Discount is tied to the account email, so existing accounts must add/switch to the student email. No funding/age restrictions (it's a student, not startup, program). No credit card requirement stated for verification; a payment method is of course needed to pay for Core.
How to claim
1) Visit replit.com/edu/students (or sign up at replit.com/signup using your .edu email). 2) Create/sign in to a Replit account with the academic email. 3) Verify the email via the confirmation link. 4) Go to replit.com/pricing, choose Core — the 50% student price (~$10/mo) is auto-applied at checkout.
Expiry
No end date or duration stated on the official page as of late 2026; discount reportedly was 6 months at launch (Sept 2025) and is now advertised without a time cap. Re-verify eligibility at renewal; the school must keep issuing the academic email.
Gotchas
Discount applies to Core only, not to extra credit packs or Pro. September 2026 community reports (replit.discourse.group) of .edu-verified accounts NOT getting the discount at checkout, and a support form that requires an active paid subscription — a closed loop. Not combinable in writing with yearly billing (the pricing page does not say yearly + student stack). Region: .edu verification is US-centric; non-US academic domains may fail auto-detection.
Source
replit.com

Replit Startup Program: up to $25,000 in Replit credits; free year of Replit Pro for other venture-backed founders high confidence

Official replit.com/startups page (FAQ JSON-LD read today): 'Startups backed by a Select Partner (e.g. a16z, Khosla, SVB) qualify for up to $25,000 in Replit credits. If your fund isn't listed yet, you can still apply — we review every application.' Page meta: 'Startups backed by a Select Partner qualify for up to $25,000 in Replit credits; other venture-backed founders get a free year of Replit Pro. Apply in minutes.' Credits cover compute, deployments, Replit Agent (AI-assisted development) and all Pro features. A free year of Pro (currently $100/mo) is worth ~$1,200. Confirmed in the FAQ: credits are valid 12 months from approval; Pro subscription remains active as long as the partnership is in effect. For VC/accelerator funds: partnering with Replit is free; a partner application form exists on the page.

Eligibility
Venture-backed startups (no stated age, funding-cap or headcount limit). 'Select Partner' status (fund listed in the page's logo grid, e.g. a16z, Khosla, SVB) => up to $25K credits; other venture-backed founders => free year of Replit Pro. Unlisted funds can still apply and are reviewed case by case. No credit-card requirement stated (page says apply in minutes). Region limits: not stated.
How to claim
1) Go to replit.com/startups. 2) Check the Select Partner logo grid for your investor. 3) Submit the application form ('Apply to the Replit Startup Program', targets replit.com signup) — takes minutes. 4) Replit confirms eligibility at application approval; credits land on the account. Funds can apply via the VC partner form instead.
Expiry
Credits valid 12 months from date of approval. Pro subscription (for the free-year tier) stays active as long as the startup–partner fund relationship is in effect. No published program end date.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free trap: if you already have an existing Core or Pro subscription, you receive the equivalent in credits INSTEAD of the free trial (FAQ). 'Up to $25K' — actual amount per approval, not guaranteed to every Select-Partner startup. Credits expire 12 months after approval. The Select Partner list is on the JS-rendered page and can change; verify your fund is actually listed before assuming $25K.
Source
replit.com

Replit for Education: free Replit for verified educators + credits for students (teacher/education license) medium confidence

Official replit.com/edu page: 'Teach the AI generation. Free Replit for verified educators. Credits for your students. Curriculum, templates, and 1:1 onboarding.' Also: 'Run Replit on your campus' — host events, earn commissions on signups, direct access to the Replit team. Page meta (read today): 'Replit equips students, educators, and campus leaders to build real apps with AI — no setup, no busywork. Free to start, trusted by top universities.' The exact plan tier given to verified educators (presumed free Core or Pro) and the amount of student credits are not published on the page (JS-rendered; body not extractable), so specifics must be confirmed via the education application flow.

Eligibility
Educators (teacher verification implied); students of those educators receive program credits. No .edu requirement stated for educators on the page; 'verified' process handles it. No credit card required for the free educator tier.
How to claim
1) Visit replit.com/edu. 2) Apply for educator access (verified-educator flow, plus curriculum/templates/1:1 onboarding offered). 3) Students in the program get credits; campus programs earn commissions on signups.
Expiry
No expiry or renewal terms published on the page.
Gotchas
Amounts are not publicly quantified — do not assume $120/yr or a specific plan tier; confirm at application. This is a separate offer from the .edu 50% student discount (a student with a .edu email gets the 50% Core discount regardless; an educator's classroom 'credits for your students' is a distinct programmatic offer).

One-time free Agent checkpoint (trial credit for new accounts) high confidence

Per official AI billing docs: 'All subscribers receive one-time free Agent checkpoints.' This is Replit's only documented trial credit: new users get their first Agent work checkpoint free, after which effort-based (usage-based) pricing applies — simple tasks cost less than complex builds, one checkpoint per request, and third-party model calls (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) are billed at provider public API rates, deducted from Replit credits.

Eligibility
Any new Replit account/user (no student or startup status needed). No credit card requirement stated in the docs.
How to claim
Just start Replit Agent on a new account — the first checkpoint is free automatically; nothing to apply for.
Expiry
One-time per account, not renewable.
Gotchas
It is a trial, not a recurring free tier: after it, all Agent work consumes Starter's daily/monthly free caps, then pay-as-you-go credit packs. Plan Mode can trigger billable reasoning even when no code changes are made. Spend controls: usage alerts, hard budget caps, and a billing dashboard (data may lag ~30 min).
Source
docs.replit.com
