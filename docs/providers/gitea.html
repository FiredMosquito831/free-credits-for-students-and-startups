Gitea: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Self-hosted open-source Gitea (free tier). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Self-hosted open-source Gitea (free tier) high confidence

The core Gitea release is open source under the MIT license and is free to run: 'Full control, unlimited users and repositories.' Bundles code hosting, issue tracking, pull requests, project management, Gitea Actions (GitHub-Actions-compatible CI/CD), and a package registry. Free features listed on the pricing page: code hosting, issue tracking, PRs, project management, Gitea Actions, packages, rapid updates, active contributions, community support. No repo/user/storage cap is published — you are limited only by your own infrastructure. This is the primary free path for students and startups: replace paid GitLab/GitHub tiers at $0 license cost.

Eligibility
Open to anyone: individuals, students, startups, and companies. No .edu email, startup-age or funding cap, and no credit card required. You must supply your own host (VPS/bare metal/K8s) and bear your own compute/data/egress costs.
How to claim
No application needed. Download the free binary or Docker image (gitea.com products page / github.com/gitea) and install on your own Linux/Windows server or VPS; configure a DB (SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL). Unlimited users/repos forever. Enterprise extras (SAML SSO, audit logs, K8s autoscaling runners, SLA support) are NOT in the OSS build.
Expiry
None — perpetual MIT license, no sunset date.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: this is a genuinely free, permanent license (not a trial). Self-hosted = you pay your cloud provider for CPU/egress; Gitea's license is free but the box is not. No managed CI runners are provided — OSS Gitea Actions requires you to run your own runners. Enterprise-only features absent; support is community-only.
Source
about.gitea.com

gitea.com — free first-party hosted Gitea instance medium confidence

Gitea operates gitea.com, a free public first-party Gitea instance: anyone can register a free account and host repositories on Gitea's own infrastructure at no charge. The sign-up form (gitea.com/user/sign_up) has only username/email/password plus OAuth (GitHub/GitLab/Google/OpenID) — no credit card or payment field. It is described as a 'free public first-party instance' hosting public repos (59 public repos on the Gitea org). No repo/storage cap is published on the page.

Eligibility
Open to everyone (individuals, students, startups). No .edu requirement, no startup/funding cap, no credit card required. Free to register.
How to claim
Register at gitea.com/user/sign_up, then create repos/projects. Hosts Gitea.org's own public open-source code (gitea/gitea, runner, tea CLI, etc.).
Expiry
Ongoing free service, subject to Gitea's terms; no published sunset date.
Gotchas
Free CI runners are NOT available on gitea.com (as of Nov 2025, maintainers confirmed there are no free managed runners; Gitea Actions needs a self-hosted runner or paid Gitea Cloud managed runners). The gitea.com root now 303-redirects to about.gitea.com (marketing); use gitea.com/<org>/<repo> for actual hosting. Exact repo/storage caps and private-repo policy are not published — treat as a public/mirror-friendly instance, not a guaranteed production private-code host. Source on no-free-runners: https://forum.gitea.com/t/how-to-make-gitea-actions-work-on-gitea-com/11793
Source
gitea.com

Gitea Cloud — 30-day free trial (managed Git hosting) high confidence

Gitea Cloud is a fully managed, dedicated, single-tenant Gitea instance where you choose provider and region (incl. Frankfurt EU for data residency). A 30-day free trial is offered ('Start your 30-day free trial'); after it ends, it becomes pay-as-you-go (no permanent free hosted tier). Managed Gitea Actions CI/CD runners are included automatically. Unlimited network traffic: incoming traffic is free & unlimited; once the high-speed egress quota is hit, a speed cap applies only to outgoing traffic. A recurring 50% discount is available on most plans (Extra/Max/Ultra unlimited-user tiers excluded). Instances provision in ~2 minutes; public repos can allow anonymous access; 2FA supported.

Eligibility
Open to individual developers, startups, and enterprises — no .edu requirement and no dedicated startup program. No published funding/age cap. Payment is pay-as-you-go after the trial (credit card likely required at checkout; the 30-day trial itself is advertised without a stated CC requirement — not confirmed on the pages read).
How to claim
Go to cloud.gitea.com/signup → 'Start your 30-day free trial' → choose provider/region and a plan (S/M/L or Extra/Max/Ultra) → provision → use fully managed Gitea for 30 days → pay-as-you-go thereafter (apply the 50% discount where offered).
Expiry
Trial lasts 30 days, then pay-as-you-go. No published free-tier renewal; the 30-day trial is one-time.
Gotchas
This is a trial, not a permanent free tier — costs begin after 30 days (pay-as-you-go; add-on object storage is extra). Egress: high-speed quota then speed-capped outgoing (incoming free). 50% discount does not cover Extra/Max/Ultra tiers. Exact $/month per plan tier and the CC-required policy for the trial are not published in the pages read (Cloud site is an SPA). No free managed CI runners outside the trial.
Source
cloud.gitea.com

Gitea Enterprise — 30-day free trial (self-hosted enhanced edition) high confidence

Gitea Enterprise is the enhanced, closed-source edition (SAML SSO, audit logs, Kubernetes AutoScaling Runners, mandatory 2FA, IP allowlist, enterprise themes, priority SLA support, installation/upgrade assistant, emergency remote support). A 30-day free trial is offered via the customers portal with full Enterprise features. List price is $19/user/month, with a recurring 50% discount shown as $9.50/user/month; a 1-year commitment is required with online payment. Licenses are user-count based and can be resized at any time; after a trial you can keep paying or revert to open-source Gitea (fully data/config compatible).

Eligibility
For companies/enterprises (and 'small companies' — discounts available for non-profits and small companies; FAQ notes discounted or even free quotes for education/non-profit on request). Not a dedicated free startup program. No .edu requirement for the trial itself; paid tier needs online payment (credit card) with a 1-year commitment.
How to claim
Go to customers.gitea.com → sign in with an organization account → 'Request Trial' → specify desired user count → a 30-day trial license is auto-issued → download license.key → in Gitea Enterprise admin: Site Administration → Enterprise License → Upload License. During the trial all Enterprise functions are free.
Expiry
Trial = 30 days. Paid licenses run for the committed term (typically 1 year, resizable), renewable; if unpaid after trial the instance falls back to open-source Gitea behavior (no data loss).
Gotchas
Trial-vs-paid: free only for 30 days, then $19/user/month list ($9.50 with the 50% recurring discount) on a 1-year commitment. Exceeding the user count just blocks new sign-ups (resize for free). Resale of Gitea Enterprise is prohibited. Non-profit/small-company discounts and even free quotes require contacting Gitea — they are not self-serve.
Source
docs.gitea.com

Gitea Education Program — free 12-month Gitea Enterprise license (institutions) high confidence

Eligible non-profit K-12 and higher-education institutions receive a no-cost Gitea Enterprise license for 12 months, self-hosted, with community technical support only (no commercial support / no SLA). Renewable annually after an eligibility review. Covers coursework, labs, capstones, student group projects, non-commercial academic research, and internal school collaboration tied to the academic mission.

Eligibility
Institution-level, NOT individual students. Eligible: non-profit K-12 schools, colleges, and universities officially recognized/authorized/accredited/licensed or otherwise approved by the relevant education authority. Applications must come from full-time faculty/staff using an institutional email. Excluded: for-profit training firms, commercial bootcamps, private coaching, unrecognized/unaccredited organizations, and requests from students, alumni, or external consultants. Use is limited to academic/non-commercial — no commercial software delivery, paid hosting, or revenue-generating services. A .edu address is not strictly required, but an institutional email from a recognized non-profit institution is; proof of recognition/accreditation is required.
How to claim
Apply via the contact form at about.gitea.com/contact/form/?program=education. Provide: institution name/website/country, a link or document showing education-authority recognition/accreditation, non-profit confirmation, applicant name/role/institutional email, expected user count + short usage summary, and acknowledgement that only community support is included. Every request is manually reviewed; on approval you get a tailored 12-month Enterprise license.
Expiry
12 months, renewable annually after re-confirming eligibility and usage scope. Notify Gitea if eligibility or usage changes.
Gotchas
Students cannot apply directly — they benefit only via their school's license. Community support only (no commercial/SLA support). Non-commercial academic use only; commercial/resale/revenue use is prohibited and could void the license. No fixed seat count — scoped to the institution's expected users.
Source
about.gitea.com

2025–26 changes

  • try.gitea.io — the free hosted "try" instance listed in the known-so-far notes — no longer resolves (DNS getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND on two attempts as of 2026-10-22), indicating it has been retired or repurposed; no current official page or terms could be found. Treat it as defunct. gitea.com's root domain now 303-redirects to about.gitea.com (marketing site); the free first-party public instance still serves orgs/repos under gitea.com/<org>/<repo>, but gitea.com no longer offers free managed CI runners (confirmed by a Gitea maintainer on the forum, Nov 2025 — Gitea Actions on gitea.com needs a self-hosted runner or paid Gitea Cloud managed runners). No dedicated Gitea startup program with free credits exists as of 2026-10-22 (startups' free path = self-hosted OSS Gitea + the Gitea Cloud 30-day trial). Recurring 2024→2026 changes still in effect: Gitea Cloud added a Frankfurt (EU) region, add-on object storage, unlimited network traffic (incoming free, outgoing speed-capped past a high-speed quota), and a 50% recurring discount on most plans; Enterprise lists at $19/user/month with a 50% discount to $9.50/user/month on a 1-year commitment. The Education Program (free 12-month Enterprise license for non-profit institutions) is current and live on about.gitea.com/education.
