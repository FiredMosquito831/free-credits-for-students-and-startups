LaunchDarkly Developer Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Developer plan (free tier, renamed from 'Personal Use'). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Developer plan (free tier, renamed from 'Personal Use') high confidence

LaunchDarkly Developer plan: $0/mo, 'forever'. Limits read today from the official pricing page: unlimited feature flags; unlimited seats (no per-seat pricing); 1 project and 3 environments; 5 service connections; 1K client-side MAU/mo; 10M logs + 10M traces/mo; 5K session replays and 5K errors/mo (ingestion stops at 5K, no overage billing on this tier); 100K experimentation MAU/mo at no charge; 5,000 AgentControl AI runs/mo included with 14-day data retention. Docs add: free up to 1,000 users/contexts/mo and 10,000 experiment keys. All plans include SSO/SAML; Developer includes 2FA. No region limits stated. The plan was renamed from 'Personal Use' to 'Developer'.

Eligibility
Open to anyone (individuals, students, startups, teams). No .edu email, no funding/age cap, no credit card required ('No credit card required' shown on the Developer plan). No region limits stated on the page.
How to claim
Go to launchdarkly.com/pricing, click 'Get started free' under the Developer plan, create an account (email/password or SSO); no credit card asked; SDKs immediately usable in one project.
Expiry
No expiry or renewal; the Developer tier is free indefinitely. 2025-09-08 terms update does not change the free tier.
Gotchas
It's a free tier, not a trial: free forever, but strict caps. Usage beyond caps = ingestion stops (errors/replays), not billed overage. 14-day AgentControl data retention vs 30/100+ on paid tiers. 1 project/3 environments only. In-progress guarded rollouts from a trial complete, but no new guarded rollouts without Enterprise+Guardian. 'No per-seat' means whole team can use it, but the usage caps are account-wide, so a busy team burns 1K MAU / 5 service connections fast. Foundation pay-as-you-go (first 5 service connections included, then $10/SC/mo; $8.33 per 1K client-side MAU/mo; $5 per additional 1K AI runs; billed yearly) is the upgrade path, so plan egress/usage before upgrading.
Source
launchdarkly.com

14-day free trial (full Enterprise + Guardian features, auto-converts to Developer) high confidence

14-day free trial covering 'all of the LaunchDarkly Enterprise and Guardian features'. Official getting-started docs: after 14 days the trial 'automatically converts to a developer tier plan' free up to 1,000 users/contexts/mo and 10,000 experiment keys. Plans docs: no credit card is required for the free Developer tier; the trial itself has no stated card requirement. Enterprise also offers a 'Get Enterprise trial' path on the pricing page.

Eligibility
Anyone who can create an account. No .edu, no funding cap, no stated region limits. Trial does not advertise a credit-card requirement.
How to claim
Sign up at launchdarkly.com ('Sign up for a free trial'); 14-day trial starts immediately; nothing to do afterward — it auto-converts to the free Developer plan. Optionally upgrade to Foundation/Enterprise during the trial.
Expiry
14 days per trial. At expiry the account auto-converts to the free Developer plan (or you upgrade to paid Foundation/Enterprise). Not renewable as a free offer.
Gotchas
Trial vs free tier: trial includes ALL Enterprise + Guardian features; after conversion those features become read-only — LaunchDarkly retains the data but you cannot interact with it (Guardian guarded rollouts, custom roles revert to base roles, offline Relay Proxy still works but cannot download new flag rules; in-progress guarded rollouts complete, new ones blocked). The free tier's hard caps apply immediately post-trial. You cannot stay on 'Enterprise features for free' — the trial is a 14-day window, not a renewable offer.
Source
launchdarkly.com

LaunchDarkly Academy (free training/certification, no payment info) medium confidence

LaunchDarkly Academy (academy.launchdarkly.com): free self-guided courses and public virtual classes, free LaunchDarkly certification, no payment information required at signup, guest accounts available. It is a free education/training resource (not a classroom license for product use): FAQ confirms courses free, no card required. Confidence medium because the official support FAQ page blocked direct fetch (HTTP 403 to bots); content comes from the official support site's FAQ as surfaced today.

Eligibility
Open to all (guests, students, educators, partners). No .edu required; no fee. Not an institutional product license: it covers training content, not free classroom SaaS accounts.
How to claim
Visit academy.launchdarkly.com, select 'Sign In' → 'Sign Up' (guest account); no payment info is requested. Existing LaunchDarkly customers can use their login instead.
Expiry
No expiry; account-based, no renewal. Courses reviewed/refreshed quarterly.
Gotchas
Free tier covers self-guided content and public virtual classes only; 'private paid-engagements' cost extra. Academy access is separate from the product free tier — you still need the Developer plan for actual flags. Do not share one Academy account among students per FAQ guidance. Third-party 'education discount' entries for LaunchDarkly are unverified templates, not real offers.
Source
support.launchdarkly.com

(2) No active public education license or startup credits program: launchdarkly.com/education and /startup 404, and the official plans/pricing/blog pages reference no such program — the historical Startup Program (if it existed) is not publicly available now; treat any such offer as sunset/unverifiable. DBA LaunchDarkly, usage-based pricing with 'service connection' as billing unit, no per-seat fees on paid tiers); Foundation is $10/SC/mo + $8.33/1K MAU with first 5 SCs included.
