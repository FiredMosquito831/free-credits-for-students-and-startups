Scaleway Startup: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: New-account free trial: €100 credit (Business/Professional account). 7 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

New-account free trial: €100 credit (Business/Professional account) high confidence

Create a Business account (older pages say Professional) and get €100 free credit across cloud services. The trial does not auto-apply: if eligible, a voucher code is emailed within 24 hours and the trial starts when you redeem it. Credit expiry is not officially stated. Payment method is required: ordering resources needs a valid credit card (€1 authorization is placed and auto-reimbursed within 2 days); virtual prepaid cards are not accepted; KYC identity verification is part of the account process.

Eligibility
New Business/Professional customers with a valid payment method who have NEVER had an invoice issued by Scaleway (a €0 invoice counts as an invoice and disqualifies you). Not advertised for Personal accounts. No .edu or startup-status requirement; effectively open to any new account.
How to claim
1) Create an account at console.scaleway.com, choosing a Business (formerly 'Professional') organization type. 2) Add a valid credit card (Visa/MasterCard/CB/UnionPay/Amex; SEPA with a physical card on file). 3) Wait for the voucher-code email (within 24h of qualifying) and redeem it in the console to activate the €100 trial.
Expiry
Official expiry not stated; voucher must be redeemed (emailed within 24h). Credit not cash-outable.
Gotchas
Any prior Scaleway invoice (even €0) disqualifies — a forgotten test account silently blocks the offer. Virtual/prepaid cards rejected. GPU is not excluded from the credit, but new-account quotas may limit larger GPU types. Dedicated-server and some products bill separately; egress/data transfer rules vary per product.

scaleway.com

Startup Program — Founders tier: €1,000 credit, 12 months high confidence

Entry tier of Scaleway's Startup Program for startups migrating to Scaleway or doing an initial build. €1,000 in cloud credits valid 12 months, not renewable; support is self-serve (no dedicated CSM); benefits include collective onboarding, private Slack with peers/alumni, product betas, masterclasses, co-marketing, and VC-network access. Application review takes under 21 business days; admission is rolling.

Eligibility
Entity under 5 years old; fewer than 50 employees; must NOT already be a Scaleway customer. No funding-stage or location cap stated. Open regardless of vertical.
How to claim
Apply via the Startup Program application (scaleway.com/en/startup-program/ or email startup-program@scaleway.com). Committee reviews applications; program dates are set per cohort (6 or 12 months after credits activation).
Expiry
Credits valid 12 months; non-renewable. Overage beyond the voucher is billed.
Gotchas
Being an existing customer (even a trial account) disqualifies you — coordinate with the €100 trial so an invoice isn't issued first. No funding cap published, but committee discretion applies; 'up to €1,000' vs '€1,000' phrasing differs between the nav and the benefits block.
Source
scaleway.com

Startup Program — Early Stage tier: €1,500/month x 6 = up to €9,000 high confidence

For startups going to market at pre-seed to seed stage. €1,500 per month of cloud credits over 6 months (total €9,000), with community support, co-marketing, and technical guidance from a CSM on request. Implied consumption floor of €500/month (the FAQ states this tier starts at '500€/month consumption').

Eligibility
Less than 5 years old, fewer than 50 employees, not yet a Scaleway client; pre-seed to seed stage. Cannot move from Early Stage directly into Growth Stage.
How to claim
Same rolling application as the program (scaleway.com/en/startup-program/, or startup-program@scaleway.com); selected startups join a cohort running 6 months from credit activation.
Expiry
6-month credit window; overage beyond the voucher billed.
Gotchas
Consumption floor (~€500/month) means this is a ramp-up grant, not a small grant; usage below the floor is not expected. Page says 'up to €9,000' and '1 500€ per month for 6 month'.
Source
scaleway.com

Startup Program — Growth Stage tier: €3,000/month x 12 = up to €36,000 high confidence

For seed-to-Series-A startups scaling up. €3,000 per month of cloud credits over 12 months (total €36,000), with dedicated CSM, 24/7 technical support (page typo '27/7'), co-marketing, events, and VC-network access. Implied consumption floor of €1,500/month.

Eligibility
Less than 5 years old, fewer than 50 employees, not yet a Scaleway client; seed to Series A stage. An Nvidia-dedicated variant has conditions similar to Growth.
How to claim
Apply via scaleway.com/en/startup-program/ (rolling admission; a page FAQ says the committee reviews weekly/monthly — see gotchas). 12-month cohort from credit activation.
Expiry
12-month credit window; overage billed.
Gotchas
Highest exposure — floor of ~€1,500/month; the €36,000 only materializes if consumption matches the monthly tranche. Program FAQ wording conflicts internally on review cadence (weekly on the main page, monthly on the Founders page FAQ, 'under 21 business days' on Founders).
Source
scaleway.com

Object Storage trial: 750 GB free for 90 days (new users) high confidence

New users get 750 GB of free storage total, splittable between Standard Multi-AZ and Standard One Zone classes, across all regions, for 90 days. If you exceed 750 GB during the trial, only the excess is billed. Trial is activated from the Object Storage section of the console and cannot be paused/restarted once started; after 90 days regular pricing applies.

Eligibility
New users who had never used the Standard Multi-AZ class before 30 Nov 2023 23:59:59 (stated in the official FAQ). No card needed specifically for the trial itself, though ordering resources generally requires a valid payment method.
How to claim
Activate in the Scaleway console under Object Storage. Free quota applies to buckets in your Organization across regions.
Expiry
90 days from activation; one-shot (cannot deactivate and resume).
Gotchas
The 2023 cut-off eligibility date is a hidden disqualifier for older accounts. Marketing pages say '750 GB/h free for 90 days' (GB per hour of trial, not 750GB/hour sustained) — it is 750 GB total. Data egress after trial follows regular pricing.
Source
scaleway.com

Ongoing free tiers: serverless monthly quotas, free Kapsule control plane, 1M free Generative API tokens high confidence

Recurring per-account free quotas on the official Serverless pricing page: Functions — 1,000,000 requests/month and 400,000 GB-s/month free; Containers — 400,000 GB-s/month and 200,000 vCPU-s/month free; Jobs — 400,000 GB-s/month and 200,000 vCPU-s/month free. Kapsule (managed Kubernetes) control plane is free (dedicated control-plane option is paid). Generative APIs: every new customer gets 1,000,000 free tokens (pay from the 1,000,001st). Docs billing concepts confirm free tiers on select products have no expiration date but may change.

Eligibility
Account-level: applies per account, resets monthly for the serverless quotas (Provisioned/warm resources have NO free tier and bill from the first second). No .edu requirement; Generative API token grant targets new customers.
How to claim
No application — quotas apply automatically to each account; Kapsule control plane is simply free when you create a cluster (nodes are billed from ~€0.027/h).
Expiry
Ongoing monthly resets (serverless), token grant is a one-time 1M-token welcome allowance; 'no expiration date but subject to change' per docs.
Gotchas
Free quotas cover compute/consumption only — data transfer (egress), provisioned instances, and storage attached to serverless workloads are billed. Prices are ex-VAT. These are not free VPS: the smallest Learning ('Stardust') instance is a paid ~€4.47/month box.
Source
scaleway.com

Education / students: no real free credit program found medium confidence

No .edu-based free credit or education license program exists on Scaleway's official site as of today. The closest offerings are: (1) 'Learning Instances' (Stardust class: 1 shared vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 10 GB storage, 100 Mbps; available only in FR-PAR-1 and NL-AMS-1, max 1 per AZ, limited monthly supply) — cheap, not free; (2) Scaleway Learning — paid certification (€300 ex-VAT exam, badges valid 2 years). No university agreement or student voucher surfaced on official pages.

Eligibility
None education-specific. A student with a normal account gets the same offers as everyone (€100 trial requires Business type, 750 GB/90-day storage trial, monthly serverless free tiers).
How to claim
No student-only claim path exists; students use the standard new-account offers above.
Gotchas
Third-party 'student' listings (e.g., StudentPerks) only point back to the general startup program, not a student tier. Don't cite .edu eligibility that isn't documented.
Source
scaleway.com
