Replit: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Starter free plan (current as of 2026-10-22). 10 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Starter free plan (current as of 2026-10-22) high confidence

$0/mo, no dollar figure listed ('available at no cost'). Includes: (1) daily-reset Agent credits with a monthly cap (no public numbers — check Settings → Usage); (2) monthly credits for production databases, object storage, and publishing (autoscale/static) that reset each month and do NOT roll over; (3) exactly 1 free published app that auto-expires 30 days after publishing and carries a 'Made with Replit' referral badge (badge removal requires Core); app can be re-published; (4) 'Lite build' only — Full build (autonomous agent), design-to-artifact backend conversion, third-party connectors (Google, Stripe, Replit AI Integrations), Plan Mode, and non-web/mobile artifact types all require Core/Pro. Resource facts: development databases are free on every app with 20 GiB free dev storage (50 GiB on Pro/Enterprise); production PostgreSQL storage capped at 1 GiB on Starter; static hosting is free but outbound transfer bills at $0.05/GB; autoscale is $2/mo base + $0.60/million compute units + $0.40/million requests once credits run out; object storage $0.015/GiB/mo + $0.05/GiB transfer + $0.0004–0.005/thousand ops with a 7-day minimum billing floor; 'Made with Replit' referrals earn credits only after upgrading to a paid plan.

Eligibility
Any Replit account (email or Google/GitHub OAuth signup); no .edu email, no student status, no region limit stated in docs. Credit card: NOT stated on any current official page — free-plan signup has historically required no card; a card/payment method is only involved when you buy credit packs or subscribe to Core/Pro. Treat 'no card required' as true-but-unconfirmed-in-current-docs.
How to claim
Create a free account at replit.com — you are on Starter automatically, no card step. Publish up to 1 app; view live credit caps in Settings → Usage; upgrade via replit.com/settings/billing when you hit limits.
Expiry
Agent credits reset daily; monthly cloud credits reset each month (no rollover); the free published app goes down 30 days after publishing (re-publish to extend; each republish resets the window).
Gotchas
Legacy numbers circulating for Starter (1,200 agent/dev minutes per month ≈ 40/day, 100 AI completions, 0.5 vCPU / 1 GB RAM / 2 GB disk) no longer appear on any official page I read today — the platform moved to a credits + Free Mode system in 2025–2026, so those figures are likely stale. Egress/autoscale/object-storage can produce real charges if your free app gets traffic (outbound data $0.05/GB has no stated free allowance). Routines and heavy agent work need paid credits. 'No card' is not explicitly documented today.
Source
docs.replit.com

Free Mode on the free Starter plan (launched 2026-08-18, OpenAI partnership) high confidence

Free Mode is a no-cost Agent mode using 'intelligent model routing' (model picked by Replit; per the announcement, powered by OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna). It is available on Starter, Core, and Pro. Core users get 'up to 30 hours per month of chat' in Free Mode and up to 60 projects in Free Mode; Pro gets even larger allowances; the allowance on Core/Pro 'refreshes every five hours'. On the free Starter plan Free Mode also applies but Replit publishes no number — the docs say only 'Your Free Mode limits depend on your plan' and point to Settings → Usage. Routines cannot run in Free Mode (they need a per-run paid budget). Free Mode replaces everyday work that previously consumed credits: 'everyday tasks will no longer use credits'; the older Economy Mode was renamed Power Mode at the same cost.

Eligibility
All accounts including the free Starter plan; no card needed for Free Mode itself; no regional or education gating stated.
How to claim
It is the default Agent experience — just chat/build; when the 5-hour or daily allowance is exhausted Replit warns you before a request shifts to a paid mode (Power/Max). Monitor the counter in Settings → Usage.
Expiry
Allowance refreshes every 5 hours (Core/Pro) and daily (Starter); Free Mode shipped Aug 18–19, 2026 and is ongoing.
Gotchas
No model picker in Free Mode (auto routing only); Starter's exact allowance is not public; Free Mode excludes Routines, Plan Mode (Core-only), and third-party AI integrations (paid, billed at provider API rates from your credits).
Source
docs.replit.com

Starter plan (free tier) high confidence

$0, no time limit. Daily Agent credits that reset daily, capped by a monthly cap (exact credit count is NOT published by Replit; Settings > Usage is the source of truth). Monthly cloud credits for production databases, object storage, and publishing (autoscale/static) that reset monthly (no dollar figure published). Lite build only (Full build requires Core). Exactly 1 free published app: the link auto-goes offline 30 days after publish (you can re-publish), and it carries a mandatory 'Made with Replit' badge with your referral link. Includes Design Canvas + Visual Editor and web & mobile app projects.

Eligibility
Open to anyone; no credit card required; no .edu required; no region limit stated; no funding cap. Not gated on startup backing.
How to claim
Sign up free at replit.com; Agent usage and remaining allowance show in Settings > Usage; publish one app via Lite build or Design Canvas.
Expiry
No time limit; daily Agent and monthly cloud allowances reset daily/monthly; the single published app goes offline after 30 days.
Gotchas
Exact credit amounts are not published (check Settings > Usage). 'Free Mode' is NOT a standalone free plan (see below). As of 2026 the public pricing page (replit.com/pricing) no longer lists a free Starter card - it now opens on Core ($18 billed annually / $20 monthly); a third-party pricing tracker reports the free Starter plan was dropped from the pricing page between Aug 28 and Sept 11, 2026. Docs still list Starter as an active free tier, so it remains available but is no longer the advertised self-serve entry point (a new signup now lands on paid Core). Referral credits from your badge only vest after you upgrade to a paid plan. Connectors (Google/Stripe), Replit AI Integrations, Plan Mode, artifact types beyond web/mobile, badge removal, and 2nd+ published apps all require Core.
Source
docs.replit.com

Free Mode (no-cost Agent allowance inside plans) high confidence

Launched Aug 18, 2026. No-cost Agent usage within each plan's allowance. Core: up to 30 hours/month of chat on Free Mode and up to 60 projects on Free Mode; marketing claims up to 30x more creation vs before. On Core/Pro the Free Mode allowance resets every 5 hours and has a weekly limit; Starter keeps its own daily limits. Paid modes: Power (mid-tier, formerly 'Economy') and Max (top-tier, formerly 'Power').

Eligibility
Subscribers to Starter, Core, or Pro. No extra eligibility; Replit picks the model in Free Mode (no manual model choice). Not available on Enterprise.
How to claim
Open a new Agent chat - it starts in Free Mode by default; a paid mode only runs after your explicit one-time confirmation per request.
Expiry
Core/Pro Free Mode allowance resets every 5 hours with a weekly cap; Starter uses daily limits.
Gotchas
Replit's help page states explicitly that Free Mode 'is not a free plan on itself' - it is an allowance bundled into paid Starter/Core/Pro. A paid escalation (Power/Max) applies to that one request only, then returns to Free Mode. After the Aug 2026 restructure the genuinely free entry point is effectively the Starter plan, and Free Mode is framed around the $20 Core subscription.
Source
replit.com

Replit for Startups (up to $25K credits / free Replit Pro) medium confidence

Launched live April 23, 2026. Two tiers: (1) Up to $25,000 in Replit credits for startups backed by a 'Select Partner' fund or accelerator - a partner offer (SVB) lists $25K credits with 6-month validity plus a dedicated onboarding call, priority support community, and co-marketing. (2) Free Replit Pro for startups backed by any other recognized fund/accelerator in the broader 'Partner Network' - a partner offer (JPMorgan) lists 1 year of Pro (free for the duration of the partnership): $100/month credits, access to the most powerful models, private deployments, up to 15 collaborators and 50 viewers, 28-day database restore, premium support. No fixed company-age or funding cap is stated publicly; partner pages say 'eligible at any funding stage' - the gate is being backed by a partner fund/accelerator, not a headcount/dollar threshold.

Eligibility
VC / fund / accelerator-backed startups (not .edu-based). Backed by a Select Partner fund -> up to $25K credits; backed by any other Partner Network fund/accelerator -> free Pro tier. Requirements: apply with a professional (work) email + company domain; one application per company. Unbacked founders may still apply and are reviewed; Replit may add your fund to the Partner Network.
How to claim
Apply at replit.com/startups with a professional email + domain, complete the application, and redeem via the partner link. If your fund is not listed, use replit.com/startups/fund-not-listed to request it be added, then apply.
Expiry
Credits: ~6-month validity on the $25K tier (per partner offer). Free Pro: 'for the duration of the partnership' (1 year on partner offer).
Gotchas
CC not required to receive the perk, but a Replit account is needed to redeem. The $25K tier is reserved for 'Select Partners' only - most funds land in the free-Pro tier. Exact specs differ by fund partner (SVB vs JPMorgan pages disagree on term length). Program existence + cap + apply-anyway path are confirmed high-confidence on Replit's own pages; the tier breakdown / validity / Pro specs came from third-party partner offer pages, so treat those specifics as medium. Unbacked/solo founders are NOT the target (use Race to Revenue instead).
Source
replit.com

Race to Revenue (builder program: $10K credits + $25K cash prize) medium confidence

Pilot launched Dec 9, 2025. 8-week structured program; 20 builders per cohort (pilot cohort ~21 founders across 4 continents). Each selected builder receives $10,000 in Replit Agent + Cloud credits. The winner gets a $25,000 CASH prize at the 2-day Final Showcase capstone in San Francisco. Zero equity. Partner perks: Ramp (corporate cards/expense tools), Stripe Atlas (free incorporation ~$500 value + $2,500 Stripe credits + up to $50K partner perks), Linear 6 months free, Rippling 6 months free, ElevenLabs 12 months free (600+ hrs), HubSpot 90% off, Make Industry toolkit, Gunderson Dettmer legal.

Eligibility
Solo builders and early-stage/bootstrapped founders who are building on or deeply integrating with Replit. No prior revenue required to apply; no funding/backing requirement. Must commit to full 8-week participation (miss >1 week = dropped) and travel to SF for the showcase; international applicants allowed (travel logistics on them).
How to claim
Apply at replit.com/racetorevenue: provide name/email/location/team size, what you're building + ICP, a traction signal, whether you build on Replit, 6-week availability + SF travel, and a 2-minute video explaining the project and why you're the one to build it.
Expiry
Cohort-based, 8 weeks; explicitly a pilot/iterating program. Credits and prize allocated per cohort.
Gotchas
You are FILMED (it is a documentary series) and you pitch live on camera in SF. The $10K is Replit-platform credits, not cash; only the winner receives the $25K cash prize. Partner perks are limited/subject to partner terms. It is a competitive selection, not an open grant.
Source
replit.com

No time-limited free trial for Core/Pro (clarification) high confidence

Replit's pricing docs state explicitly that there is no time-limited free trial for Core or Pro; the free Starter plan is the 'no-cost, no time limit' on-ramp. Third-party partner channels do run limited-time Core promos (e.g., a 'Startup Grind x Replit Core' page advertising 3 months of Core free for new accounts only), which are separate, time-boxed, new-account-only partner offers - not a standing free tier.

Eligibility
Clarification, not a grant. Partner Core promos require a new Replit account; professional (non-personal) email required on some partner forms.
How to claim
For a no-cost start, use the free Starter plan. Partner Core promos are claimed only via the specific partner link while the promo is live.
Expiry
N/A for the docs statement. Partner Core promos are time-boxed (e.g., 3 months) and new-account-only; confirm current availability.
Gotchas
Don't conflate Free Mode with a free plan (it is a paid-plan allowance). Startup Grind-style '3 months Core free' offers are limited partner promos, not a recurring program - verify they are still live before relying on them.

2025–26 changes

  • Material 2025-2026 changes: (1) The free Starter plan was removed from the public replit.com/pricing page between ~Aug 28 and Sept 11, 2026; the page now opens on Core ($18 billed annually / $20 monthly), so new self-serve entry is paid. The free Starter tier still exists in Replit's docs (docs.replit.com/billing/plans/starter-plan) and remains $0/no-time-limit, but it is no longer the advertised entry point. (2) Agent modes were restructured on Aug 18, 2026 with the launch of Free Mode: paid modes renamed 'Economy'->'Power' and 'Power'->'Max'; free Agent work is now framed as a paid-plan allowance (Core: up to 30 hrs/month chat, 60 projects; resets every 5 hrs + weekly limit). (3) 'Replit for Startups' launched April 23, 2026 (up to $25K credits for Select-Partner-backed startups, free Pro for the broader Partner Network), superseding earlier ad-hoc startup perks. (4) 'Race to Revenue' pilot launched Dec 9, 2025 ($10K Replit credits per builder + $25K cash prize). (5) Plan-structure change per a third-party pricing tracker: Feb 2026 the Pro plan launched, Core dropped to $20, and the old 'Teams' plan was sunset; Aug 2026 Core's credit wallet was trimmed to $20 and Pro annual set to $90 (medium confidence - tracker, not Replit). Net: no generic free trial exists today; the genuinely free options are the Starter plan (unadvertised on the pricing page since late 2026) plus the grants in Replit for Startups and Race to Revenue. Data caveat: this run hit the session web-search budget, so education-page specifics and JS-rendered program bodies could not be fully read; confidence ratings reflect that.
