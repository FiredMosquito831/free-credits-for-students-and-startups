DigitalOcean: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: DigitalOcean Startups program (formerly Hatch) — up to $100,000 in compute credits over 12 months + 15 months free Standard-tier support. 18 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

DigitalOcean Startups program (formerly Hatch) — up to $100,000 in compute credits over 12 months + 15 months free Standard-tier support high confidence

DigitalOcean Startups (rebranded from 'Hatch', program running since 2016). Official program page (read 2026-10-22): '12 months to scale' — credits for 12 months, amount varies by partner and is stated in the onboarding email, with a $10,000 monthly limit and charges for any monthly use above it. January 22, 2025 press release (investors.digitalocean.com, official) states 'up to $100,000 in Compute Credits' plus 'Complimentary Premium Support' and marketplace access to 600,000+ customers; BusinessWire's release also stated 'up to 3 Months of Free GPU Usage' for qualified startups. Current page lists 'Free access to Standard-tier paid support for 15 months.' Official ToS (last updated May 11, 2026) confirms a 12-month program term and 1-year credit window. Enrollment itself is free ('no fee to participate'); non-transferable credits.

Eligibility
Must have raised $10M or less total funding. Requires a company website with a matching company-domain email (no Gmail etc.) and a new DigitalOcean team account on that email. A valid credit card IS required on the account (for billing/overages). Startups that have previously used DigitalOcean credits are excluded. Service-based businesses (consulting, web/app dev shops, marketing agencies) are ineligible. AI-native startups are prioritized but not required. No .edu requirement. No published country restriction; apply through a partner (VC/accelerator/incubator, e.g. Techstars) or directly via the application form ('Other').
How to claim
1) Create a NEW DigitalOcean team account with a company-domain email (free-mail not accepted) and a valid credit card; 2) Apply via digitalocean.com/startups / do.co/AIStartups — through an approved partner (accelerator/VC such as Techstars) or directly by selecting 'Other'; 3) On acceptance the credit amount is set per partner and stated in the onboarding email; 4) Spend within the 12-month window, max $10,000 usable per month. 15 months of Standard-tier support and the Welcome Kit partner perks (Stripe Atlas 20% off, QuickNode up to $5k, Airtable $2,000, 100ms $2,500, Miro $1,000, Notion $1,000, Cloud66 $500, Bolster $1,000, Mercury $500 cash on $50k deposit, HubSpot up to 90% off year one, ChargeBee free until $1M revenue, Shortcut 2 months free, Ovvy 90 days free) are included on acceptance.
Expiry
Credits expire 12 months after DigitalOcean accepts you into the program (one year from acceptance per ToS, §1.2); unused credits are forfeited with no extension or rollover. Free Standard-tier support lasts 15 months. ToS auto-terminates the arrangement on acquisition, IPO, insolvency, or ceasing normal operations; liability capped at $100; no resell/sublicense; crypto mining prohibited.
Gotchas
Credit card required at signup — not 'free' in the no-CC sense. Core credits as of May 13, 2026 explicitly EXCLUDE: GPU Droplets, NVIDIA H100 GPU Kubernetes, Bare Metal GPUs, Dedicated Inference, Serverless Inference/third-party AI models, Paperspace Machines, Cloudways, and third-party pass-through charges — GPU access is a separate invite-only benefit, and the January 2025 'up to 3 months free GPU' is no longer published. Amount is unpublished and partner-set; the $100,000 ceiling comes only from the Jan 2025 press release. Usage above $10,000 in a month is billed to your card. Powered-off Droplets still bill. Egress beyond bundled allowances is charged separately.

GPU credit packages ('Early-Stage Boost') — separate, invite-only GPU credits for accepted startups high confidence

Official page states 'Selected startups will be invited to apply for GPU credit packages.' under 'Early-Stage Boost'; GPU credits are 'a separate benefit' from the core credits, and core credits do not cover GPU Droplet usage — your payment method is charged for it. The H100 pool / GPU benefits were a headline of the Jan 22, 2025 announcement ('generous credits for our GPU-powered Droplets and other AI/ML resources', 'priority access to GPU power').

Eligibility
Accepted DigitalOcean Startups participants (i.e., same eligibility as the core program: <= $10M raised, new DO customer, company-domain email, credit card on account, product not service business), then invitation from the DigitalOcean team. No .edu requirement.
How to claim
Invitation-only: acceptance into the program does not guarantee GPU credits. The DO team reviews application and infrastructure needs, invites selected startups to submit a separate GPU credit application. In 2025, per the press-release package, qualified startups got 'up to 3 Months of Free GPU Usage' (terms apply), with H100 GPUs available for AI/ML workloads; no amount/duration is currently published on the official page. To run H100s without GPU credits you pay list price (~$1.90/GPU-hour, third-party figure — unverified on official pages today).
Expiry
GPU credit package terms (amount/duration) not published; determined per invitation. Core program credit window is 12 months from acceptance.
Gotchas
Since the May 13, 2026 exclusion update, GPU Droplets, H100 GPU Kubernetes and Bare Metal GPUs are off the core credit table — teams relying on GPUs must get the separate GPU package or pay list. Acceptance into the main program does NOT guarantee the GPU package. Exclusions also cover third-party model hosting on inference products.
Source

New-account trial credit: $5 for 90 days (current trial; replaced the older $200/60-day offer) high confidence

Official pricing page FAQ (read 2026-10-22): 'if you received a $5 credit for 90 days, then that $5 credit is automatically applied to your account.' Credit card required at signup (a ~$1 temporary pre-authorization is taken and reversed). No perpetual free compute tier exists: Droplets start at $4/month, managed databases from $15/month, Spaces from $5/month. Billing is per-second with a 60-second/$0.01 minimum.

Eligibility
Any new DigitalOcean account (all regions where DigitalOcean is available). A payment method (credit card) IS required before creating resources — no CC-free path. No .edu, no age/funding limits. Applies once, to the first team.
How to claim
None — the credit is applied automatically to the first team on a new account; just sign up at digitalocean.com with a payment method. The current offer is $5/90 days; the previously advertised $200/60-day trial no longer appears anywhere on the official pricing page or in docs.
Expiry
$5 credit expires 90 days after signup; then normal pay-as-you-go billing begins for anything provisioned.
Gotchas
Do not expect the old $200/60-day trial — the official pricing/docs pages list only the $5/90-day grant as of today. Payment method required up front, so 'free' is really a trial with a card on file; overage is billed at list price. Stopped Droplets still bill (resources stay reserved) — only destroying them stops charges.

Always-free platform inclusions (de-facto free tier: static sites, Functions, registry, DNS, firewall, VPC, DOKS control plane) high confidence

Official pricing page 'Platform benefits' (read 2026-10-22): free to build/deploy 3 static sites on App Platform; 90,000 GiB-seconds/month of Functions compute free; free Container Registry with 1 repository and 500 MiB storage; free DNS management; free cloud firewalls; VPC free (ingress free; inter-datacenter VPC peering $0.01/GiB); Droplets include free outbound transfer starting at 500 GiB/month per Droplet; DOKS starts at $12/month with a free control plane. Spaces object storage is NOT free (from $5/month).

Eligibility
All DigitalOcean accounts; no eligibility filters, no .edu, no card-for-free (a card is required for the account, but the listed free inclusions do not require spending). No published regional restriction.
How to claim
No claiming needed — all existing/new accounts get these inclusions automatically; provision the resources. The 'starting at $0/month' products (App Platform, Functions, Container Registry, VPC) have a free base allowance but paid tiers for usage beyond it.
Expiry
No expiry — ongoing inclusions as long as the account is active. There is no sunset date published for these allowances; treat them as permanent but subject to change without notice.
Gotchas
This is not a free-tier substitute for compute: there is no perpetual free Droplet. Free allowances are per-resource (e.g., 500 GiB outbound is per Droplet plan; extra egress beyond bundled allowances is billed — list rate is ~$0.01/GiB per third-party pricing trackers, unverified on the official page today). App Platform's free static sites cap at 3 sites with 1 GiB outbound each.

DigitalOcean Startups program (formerly Hatch) — startup credits high confidence

12 months of DigitalOcean credits; amount 'varies' per startup/partner and is stated only in the onboarding email, with a general $10,000/month consumption cap (usage beyond the cap in a month is charged at published prices). Credits cover nearly all DO services (Droplets/VPS, App Platform, Kubernetes, Spaces, managed databases, CPU bare metal) but as of 2026-05-13 EXCLUDE: GPU Droplets, NVIDIA H100 GPU Kubernetes, Bare Metal GPUs, Dedicated Inference, Serverless Inference, third-party AI models outside DO infrastructure (incl. Anthropic/OpenAI), Paperspace Machines, Cloudways, and pass-through third-party charges. GPU credits are a separate, by-invitation benefit ('Early-Stage Boost'). Also includes 15 months of free Standard-tier paid support, expert 1:1s with DO PMs/engineers, Marketplace access, and a Welcome Kit partner perks: Stripe Atlas 20% off, Shortcut 2 months free, Cloud66 $500, Ovvy 90 days free, QuickNode up to $5,000, Bolster $1,000, Mercury $500 cash on $50k deposit, Airtable $2,000, Notion $1,000, HubSpot up to 90% off year one, Miro $1,000, Chargebee free until $1M revenue, 100ms $2,500. Migration assistance/credits for moving customers from other clouds (amounts unpublished).

Eligibility
Must have raised $10M or less total funding; product/digital startups only (service-based businesses: agencies, consulting, web/app dev firms are not eligible); AI-native startups are prioritized but AI is not required; corporate website with a matching company-domain email required (personal email fails); a registered DigitalOcean team account (company email, not personal) required; applicants must be new to DO credits (no prior DO credit use); a valid credit card must be on file; no geographic restrictions published (global). No .edu or enrollment requirement.
How to claim
1) Create a DO team account with a company email and valid credit card. 2) Apply at digitalocean.com/startups (about 10-minute application; if unaffiliated with a partner organization, select 'Other'), or via an approved partner (VCs, accelerators, incubators — Techstars is a 2026 premier partner). 3) Acceptance at DO's sole discretion; your credit amount arrives in the onboarding email; benefits are distributed through approved partners.
Expiry
Credits end exactly 12 months from acceptance; unused credits are forfeited and are not transferable; no extensions, no renewal mechanism (program term fixed at 12 months per ToS, last updated 2026-05-11). DO may change or discontinue the program at any time. Termination events (crypto mining, IPO, insolvency, prohibited change of control) end all benefits immediately and any fees owed become due.
Gotchas
Headline credit amount is not published ('varies') — the Jan 2025 Hatch press release claimed up to $100,000 in compute credits, but the current page no longer advertises any total; budget against the $10,000/month cap, not a headline number. GPU/inference/third-party AI models are excluded as of 2026-05-13 — a major gotcha for AI workloads. Overage beyond the monthly cap and beyond Droplet bandwidth allowances is billed. Powered-off Droplets still bill. Egress beyond the included bandwidth allowance costs extra. No free trial exists for bare-metal or dedicated servers; they are covered only if your credits apply and usage stays under the monthly cap (bare-metal CPUs eligible; bare-metal GPUs excluded).

Automatic $5 / 90-day signup credit (new accounts) high confidence

Per official docs: 'When you create your first team, DigitalOcean automatically applies a $5 signup credit.' No promo code required. The credit applies to all products except SaaS Add-Ons — so it covers Droplets (VPS), bare metal and dedicated servers, App Platform, etc. A valid payment method must be on file before you can create resources; your card is charged only after the credit is exhausted or the 90-day window expires ('if you received a $5 credit for 90 days, then that $5 credit is automatically applied to your account' — pricing FAQ). Credit expires 90 days after signup; it cannot be transferred to other teams. Governing 'Promotional Credit & Discount Terms' (last updated Jun 7 2024): no cash value, non-transferable, no stacking with other promotions, exclusions for Marketplace/Support/crypto mining, voiding for accounts created solely to obtain credits. Docs page last verified 15 Jul 2026.

Eligibility
All new accounts — any person/company's first DigitalOcean team. No student or startup status needed, no .edu email. A credit card or other valid payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Google/Apple Pay) is required on file, so it is NOT a no-credit-card offer; billing only happens after credit exhaustion or the 90-day expiry. No region limits stated on the page.
How to claim
Automatic: create your first team; the $5 credit is applied with no code and no application. Add a payment method first. Claim: free on everything non-SaaS Add-On up to $5.
Expiry
90 days from account signup; no renewal; one-time only (first team only, non-transferable to later teams).
Gotchas
Payment method required (trial-style, not card-free). Amount is small ($5) vs the wound-down former $200/60-day trial. 'All products except SaaS Add-Ons' still covers bare metal and dedicated, but DO Marketplace products and DO Support are excluded from promotional credits per the legal terms. Overage beyond $5 (or any spend after day 90) bills normally; free outbound bandwidth allowance (e.g., 500 GiB/mo on small Droplets) and free reserved IP/DNS are separate product inclusions, not credit. Fraud/accounts created solely for credits are voided.

Always-free services (free tier): static sites on App Platform + included add-ons high confidence

Up to 3 static sites can be built and deployed on App Platform for free with no expiration (~1 GiB outbound transfer per app; additional static apps cost $3/month). App Platform entry is listed as '$0/month'. Also always-free for all accounts: DNS management, Cloud Firewalls, monitoring & alerting, one free Container Registry repository (500 MiB), and free round-the-clock technical support.

Eligibility
All DigitalOcean accounts - not student/startup-specific. No .edu or funding requirements.
How to claim
Sign up (with the $200 trial or a standard account); deploy static sites for free; the free add-ons are included automatically with a paid account.
Expiry
No expiration on the free static-site allowance; free add-ons are ongoing with a paid account.
Gotchas
Only STATIC apps are free - dynamic App Platform apps bill by resource units. Free static outbound transfer is capped (~1 GiB/app/month); egress beyond included allowances and third-party/marketplace services are billed. This free tier is separate from, and smaller than, the startup credits.
