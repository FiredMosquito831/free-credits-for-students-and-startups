Honeycomb Free Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Honeycomb Free plan (free forever). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Honeycomb Free plan (free forever) high confidence

Free plan verified on today's pricing page: up to 20M events/month AND up to 100M metrics (time-series) data points/month, 2 triggers, 0 SLOs, no Service Map. Included features: Distributed Tracing, BubbleUp, OpenTelemetry support, Team Query History, query result permalinks, Honeycomb Metrics, Canvas AI Copilot, Hosted MCP, Agent Timeline, Honeycomb Intelligence. Marketing states 'Unlimited seats' and 'Unlimited querying'. No trace-count cap, dashboard cap, or retention window is printed on the pricing page today; the 60-day high-resolution retention figure is the historical figure from the plan's docs (not re-verified today, docs were restructured). Optional add-on Telemetry Pipeline (extra ingest, OTel processing) starts at $0.10/GB; Pro starts at $150/month for 50M events (up to 750M).

Eligibility
Anyone (no .edu, no startup criteria, no funding cap). No region limits stated. No credit-card requirement is stated anywhere on the pricing page (unconfirmed; page is silent).
How to claim
Sign up at https://ui.honeycomb.io/signup ('Start for free') — free plan is the default plan; no sales gate on this tier. Then send events via OTel SDKs or API key.
Expiry
No expiry — explicitly 'free forever'. Upgrade trigger: exceeding 20M events/month (or 100M data points) requires Pro, starting at $150/month.
Gotchas
Genuinely free tier, not a time-boxed trial. Only 2 triggers free (no SLOs, no Service Map) — alerting beyond 2 triggers is paid. Retention not printed on today's pricing page (60-day figure carried over from historical plan docs, unconfirmed). Optional Telemetry Pipeline add-on is metered at $0.10/GB and adds cost on top of free ingest. CC requirement unverified without running the signup flow.

Honeycomb for Builders — Pro Bronze (academic / nonprofit / open-source) high confidence

Official builders program (verified today on honeycomb.io/honeycomb-for-builders): 'Pro500 for qualifying open source, non-profit, and academic projects in perpetuity' — complimentary Pro plan at the Pro500 volume tier (≈ up to 500M events/month; Pro's max tier per pricing page is 750M, so Pro500/Pro750 denote event-volume tiers, not seats). Includes all Pro features, community onboarding sessions, office hours, and Honeycomb swag. Free in perpetuity.

Eligibility
Academic projects/organizations, non-profits, and open-source projects. No .edu-email requirement published; eligibility is vendor-determined via application. Individual students are covered only through their academic organization or OSS project — there is no student-specific tier.
How to claim
Apply via 'Talk to us' on https://www.honeycomb.io/honeycomb-for-builders (routes to /get-a-demo; third-party listings say the application form is embedded on that page). No published review timeline; approval at vendor discretion.
Expiry
In perpetuity for qualifying projects — no renewal trigger or end date published; implicitly revocable if the project stops qualifying.
Gotchas
The tier is for projects/organizations, not named individuals — a student must apply on behalf of their academic org or OSS project. Third-party directories note award timing/duration are not published. Pro500's ~500M events/month allowance dwarfs the free tier — most valuable academic offer.

honeycomb.io

Honeycomb for Builders — Pro Silver (early-stage startup discount) high confidence

Official builders program (verified today): 'Pro750 at 50% off for 1 year, available to early stage startups (Pre-seed, Seed round, Early series A)'. Pro750 = Pro plan at the 750M events/month volume tier (Pro's top tier; base Pro starts at $150/month for 50M events, so Pro750 is a high-volume custom-priced configuration — 50% discount applies to that configuration, absolute $ not public). Includes all Pro features, onboarding sessions, office hours, swag.

Eligibility
Startups at Pre-seed, Seed, or Early Series A stage; no age cap published beyond funding stage. No accelerator requirement at this tier. A third-party directory (StartupCreds) notes bootstrapped founders as eligible — not stated on Honeycomb's own page.
How to claim
Same application path: 'Talk to us' form at https://www.honeycomb.io/honeycomb-for-builders. Vendor-side approval; not self-serve.
Expiry
1 year at 50% off; no renewal/extension terms published — after 12 months presumably full (custom) Pro pricing unless re-approved.
Gotchas
Discount, not free — do not confuse with Pro Gold. Pro750 list price is not public, so '50% off' cannot be converted to dollars from public pages. No numeric funding cap (e.g., <$X raised) stated beyond the 'Early series A' ceiling.

honeycomb.io

Honeycomb for Builders — Pro Gold (accelerator startup free year) high confidence

Official builders program (verified today): 'Pro750 free for 1 year for qualifying startups', two required conditions: (1) 'Active in a recognized startup accelerator (including Y Combinator and accelerators supporting underrepresented founders)' and (2) 'Actively using or building with OpenTelemetry'. = full Pro plan at the 750M events/month volume tier at $0 for 12 months, plus all Pro features, community onboarding sessions, office hours, swag. No purchase or commitment required.

Eligibility
Startup must (a) be active in a recognized accelerator — Y Combinator explicitly named, plus accelerators supporting underrepresented founders — and (b) actively use or build with OpenTelemetry. No .edu requirement, no separate funding cap published. YC's own 'YC AI Stack' free-credits pack (Fall 2025, $25k+ in credits) does NOT include Honeycomb — YC teams still apply via the builders form; nothing auto-redeems through deals.ycombinator.com.
How to claim
Apply via the 'Talk to us' form at https://www.honeycomb.io/honeycomb-for-builders; approval vendor-side. Third-party trackers (Sourcey) list it open to applicants meeting both conditions, no payment required.
Expiry
Free for exactly 1 year. No renewal, extension, or post-expiry pricing terms published; team expected to convert to paid Pro after expiry.
Gotchas
OpenTelemetry usage is a hard condition — teams not instrumenting with OTel are ineligible. 1-year term with no published renewal path. One third-party directory marked the offer 'stale' in a crawl, but the official page still lists it as of today — treat the official page as authoritative.
