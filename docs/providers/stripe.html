Stripe: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free sandbox & test mode (no credit card). 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free sandbox & test mode (no credit card) high confidence

Free isolated test environments: simulated payments, no real money movement, no card-network processing. Every Stripe account includes a test mode sandbox (shares some settings with live mode; cannot be deleted) plus up to 5 general sandboxes (fully isolated settings/data; recommended for new integrations; supports API v1 and v2 - test mode only supports v1 + partial v2). Stripe CLI can provision an anonymous sandbox with working API keys, no account registration required (`npm i -g @stripe/cli`, `stripe sandbox create`). No fees, no stated limits on test transactions. IC+ pricing cannot be tested in any sandbox. Docs, API reference and libraries are free.

Eligibility
Anyone, worldwide. No .edu email, no startup age/funding requirement, no credit card (anonymous CLI sandbox needs no Stripe account at all), no regional limit.
How to claim
1) Create a free Stripe account, 2) toggle test mode in the Dashboard (or create a general sandbox under the account picker), or 3) use Stripe CLI: `stripe sandbox create` for an anonymous sandbox.
Expiry
None - indefinite, no expiration; sandboxes can be created/deleted at will.
Gotchas
Test mode sandbox shares some settings with live mode, so Dashboard changes can leak into live mode (look for notification banners). Test objects are not accessible from live mode and vice versa. General sandboxes support API V2; test mode is the legacy path.
Source
docs.stripe.com

Stripe Startups program (venture-backed startups): fee credits or waived processing fees, free community high confidence

By-application program for early-stage, venture-backed startups. Benefit = financial benefits as fee credits (in ~31 countries incl. US, UK, Canada, most of EU, AU/NZ/JP/SG/TH/AE...) or waived payment-processing fees (all other countries); plus a focused community of builders/Stripe leaders and expert resources. No fixed public amount: offer amounts vary by offer type and referral source (investor referrals get elevated benefits; VCs/accelerators apply at stripe.com/startups/partners). Fee credits offset Stripe fees on Products like Payments, Connect, Billing, Tax, Sigma; they do NOT apply to Crypto, Capital, Atlas (incl. registered agent renewals), Terminal devices, Link passthrough, PayPal fees, professional services. Waived processing fees only cover card + local payment method fees (incl. BNPL, ACH); not currency conversion, manual card fees, Terminal, Atlas, product fees. Offer activates in Dashboard (banner/setup-guide task); credits may take up to 2 days to apply; tracked under Reports > Fee Credits. One offer per startup lifetime, per account; stacks with Atlas credits (earliest-expiring used first).

Eligibility
Early-stage, venture-backed companies; application must include proof of institutional funding (PitchBook link, funding-announcement articles, or other docs). No funding cap or age cap published. No .edu. No credit card required to apply; must have an active, fully verified Stripe account on standard pricing, located in a supported country, not already received an offer, and not connected to a platform that controls your pricing.
How to claim
Apply at stripe.com/startups (form + verify your Stripe account) or via an investor's private referral link (Stripe Startup Partner program). Review in 2-3 business days; if approved, accept the offer via the Dashboard banner; 12-month activation window, then 12 months of offer usage.
Expiry
Expiring offers: offer expires 12 months after activation or when offer limit reached, whichever first; you have 12 months to activate; cannot be paused; no expiry reminders sent.
Gotchas
No retroactive effect if you forget to activate; waived-fee offers do not cover Stripe product fees; offer is converted to your default bank account currency; Connect usage depends on controller fees.payer configuration; if you process >$100,000/month for 3 consecutive months you can request a custom-pricing review instead. Fee credits are 'replacing waived processing fees over time' by region.
Source
support.stripe.com

Stripe Atlas: $500 one-time incorporation with $2,500 fee credits + $50,000 partner perks high confidence

Stripe Atlas forms a Delaware C corp or LLC in 1-2 business days for a one-time US$500 setup fee (includes government fees + first year of registered agent; the agent auto-renews annually after). Includes EIN retrieval, founder equity vesting, 83(b) elections, legal documents developed with Cooley LLP, free legal document template downloads, and a US business bank account via Stripe Treasury. All Atlas companies incorporated on or after Oct 16, 2025 automatically get $2,500 in Stripe product fee credits, valid 1 year from incorporation date (usable on Payments, Connect, Billing, Tax, Sigma; NOT on Crypto, Capital, Atlas/registered agent renewal, Terminal devices, Link passthrough, PayPal fees, professional services). Plus US$50,000 in exclusive partner benefits (discounts from AWS, Mercury, Carta and other tools, plus legal/tax experts).

Eligibility
Founders anywhere in the world (forms US/Delaware entities). No .edu, no funding or age requirements, no credit card to apply (the $500 fee is paid at incorporation). Requires a US phone number to activate US card payments (Atlas partner perks can help if you lack one). Without a US SSN, EIN takes 10-35 business days instead of 1-2. C-corp founders' shares are purchased with IP, not cash.
How to claim
1) Create account at dashboard.stripe.com/register/atlas, 2) submit company/founders/ownership/officers info and pay the $500 one-time fee, 3) incorporation in 1-2 business days, 4) EIN + $2,500 credits auto-applied, 5) activate US card payments from the confirmation email or Atlas Dashboard.
Expiry
The $2,500 offer activates automatically on the incorporation date, cannot be extended, and expires 365 days after incorporation. Registered agent renews annually (first year included in the $500).
Gotchas
Credits are not transferable to other Stripe accounts. Excluded fees: currency conversion, manual card fees, Terminal, Atlas renewal fees, PayPal, professional services. Atlas provides no legal/tax/accounting advice. If you qualify for Stripe Startups program credits too, the two stack.
Source
support.stripe.com

Atlas + Stripe Treasury refund offer (new 2026): Atlas fee refunded up to $500 high confidence

Full refund of the Atlas incorporation fee (up to US$500, in cash or USD-backed stablecoins) when you deposit at least US$5,000 in aggregate into your Stripe Treasury financial account within 90 days of incorporating AND maintain a minimum balance of US$5,000 for at least 30 days. Reward is deposited 30 days after meeting the requirements. Makes Atlas effectively free for eligible startups.

Eligibility
Users who incorporate through Atlas and open a Stripe financial (Treasury) account on or after April 13, 2026; account must be open and in good standing at reward time. No .edu, no funding cap, no credit card. Stripe can disqualify misuse/abuse/fraud 'gaming'.
How to claim
1) Incorporate via Atlas ($500), 2) open a Stripe Treasury financial account, 3) deposit US$5,000+ within 90 days of incorporation and hold it for 30 days, 4) refund is deposited automatically ~30 days after requirements are met.
Expiry
No published end date; Stripe 'reserves the right to alter, change or end this offer at any time'. Reward has no monetary value until distributed; user is responsible for any tax on the reward.
Gotchas
Ties up US$5,000 of real capital for 30+ days to recoup at most US$500; refund may be paid in USD-backed stablecoins; not a perpetual perk - program-modifiable at any time.
Source
docs.stripe.com

Students: GitHub Student Developer Pack Stripe perk - $25 in Stripe credits (only student offer found; no Stripe education license) high confidence

Stripe has no direct student program, no .edu-based education license, and no stripe.com/student(-s)/education page (both 404). The only student-facing offer found is a perk inside the GitHub Student Developer Pack: 'Get $25 in Stripe credits to offset fees on products including Stripe Payments, Billing, and more.' Credits are a fixed US$25 amount (not a fee waiver on revenue volume).

Eligibility
Verified GitHub student status (GitHub Education verification, typically a school-issued email or enrollment documents; GitHub requires 18+). No credit card required to claim. No Stripe-specific requirements.
How to claim
1) Sign up for the GitHub Student Developer Pack at education.github.com/pack, 2) complete GitHub student verification, 3) claim the Stripe offer from the pack's offers list ('Get help at Stripe support').
Expiry
Tied to the pack: GitHub pack access is renewable while you are a student (12-month cycles); the Stripe $25 credit's own expiry is not stated on the pack page.
Gotchas
Third-party aggregators (e.g., freEDU, marked 'verified March 7, 2026') describe this perk as 'waived transaction fees on first $1,000 in revenue' - that wording contradicts GitHub's live listing and is likely stale; trust the live GitHub pack page. The $25 credit is minor relative to startup offers; not a fee waiver. Verify live terms before claiming, as pack perks change.
Source
education.github.com

Free base pricing & product free allowances, plus 30-day free trials (time-limited, not permanent tiers) high confidence

Core Payments: no setup fees, no monthly fees, no hidden fees like closure fees (guaranteed in Stripe's pricing FAQ); pay-per-transaction: US domestic cards 2.9% + 30c per successful transaction (+0.5% manually entered cards, +1.5% international cards, +1% if currency conversion; ACH 0.8% with $5 cap; disputes $15.00 per SPT). Standing free allowances: payouts on the standard schedule are free; P2P payments to other Stripe businesses are free; micro-deposit verifications free; Data Pipeline Postgres sync has 5,000 rows synced free per month (beyond: $0.0025/row pay-as-you-go); basic usage-based billing via the Meters API under Billing pricing includes up to 100M events/month. Payment Links included at no additional charge (custom domains $10/month is a paid extra). 30-day free trials (then paid subscription, 'subscription and cancellation terms apply'): Stripe Sigma ($15/mo, or from $10/mo annual), Data Pipeline warehouse/cloud sync ($65/mo, from $50/mo annual, includes Sigma), Revenue Recognition ($25/mo, $190/mo annual). No credit card requirement stated for trials; Invoicing Starter is pay-as-you-go at 0.4% per paid invoice (no recurring fee, not free); Tax Basic is pay-as-you-go (0.5%/txn no-code, $0.50/txn API).

Eligibility
Any Stripe account, any region (rates differ by country). No .edu, no startup requirements, no credit card required to open an account or run trials.
How to claim
Create a free Stripe account; products unlock pay-as-you-go immediately; trials start via the 'Try for free for 30 days' buttons on stripe.com/pricing.
Expiry
Trials are 30 days, then auto-convert to the listed subscription unless cancelled; free allowances (5,000 rows/month, free standard-schedule payouts, 100M Meters events) have no stated end date.
Gotchas
Trials are time-boxed, not free tiers; the free Data Pipeline allowance is rows-synced-based (updates/deletes also count); 'no monthly fees' does not mean no transaction fees; custom domains ($10/mo) and post-payment invoices (0.4%, $2 cap) are small paid extras on otherwise no-code tools; instant payouts and premium services carry fees; refund fees may apply to bank transfers.
Source
stripe.com

2025–26 changes

  • 1) Oct 16, 2025 - Atlas startup offer materially changed: companies incorporated before that date received 'waived payments processing fees on your first $100,000 USD in payments, for 1 year after incorporation' (limited to card + local payment method processing fees); companies incorporated on/after Oct 16, 2025 instead receive $2,500 in Stripe product fee credits (1 year from incorporation, broader product coverage). The prior waived-processing-fee offer remains active only for earlier incorporations. 2) Apr 13, 2026 - new 'Stripe Treasury refund offer for Atlas users' (full Atlas-fee refund up to $500 for $5,000 deposit + 30-day hold) - brand-new 2026 program with no published end date. 3) 2025 - general sandboxes launched as the recommended test environment for new integrations; the legacy test mode sandbox is retained for existing integrations (API V2 only supported in general sandboxes; Stripe CLI can create anonymous sandboxes with no account). 4) Stripe Startups FAQ: fee credits are 'replacing waived processing fees over time' by country, so more regions will shift from waived fees to credits; also note the FAQ's 'Stripe Treasury benefits' $1,000 USD offer is event-tied (sign up for a Stripe card within 7 days of the event), not a standing free program. 5) Pricing-page footnote: basic usage-based billing features are moving to Stripe's Meters API under Billing pricing with up to 100M events/month included (metronome remains a separately signed-up product). 6) No student/education program was sunset - no direct Stripe student program exists; the only student perk is via the GitHub Student Developer Pack ($25 in Stripe credits per GitHub's current listing; some aggregators still show older 'first $1,000 revenue fee waiver' wording). 7) Baseline card rates confirmed current: US domestic 2.9% + 30c; +1.5% international cards; +1% currency conversion (US page; some country pages show +2% conversion).
