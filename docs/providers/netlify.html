Netlify: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Netlify Free plan (credit-based, current default). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Netlify Free plan (credit-based, current default) high confidence

$0/month with a hard 300-credit monthly limit. Confirmed on the official pricing page and docs today: 300 credits/mo, 'You'll never be charged for the Free plan', hard limit means no auto-recharge and no credit packs. At current credit rates (bandwidth 20 cr/GB, production deploys 15 cr each, compute 10 cr/GB-hr, web requests 2 cr/10k) this is ~15GB bandwidth OR ~20 production deploys per month. Includes: unlimited deploy previews, Functions + Edge Functions, AI models, Netlify Database, Blob storage, global CDN, custom domains with SSL, 1 concurrent build, 1-day observability/analytics retention. Sign up on the Free plan for a new account (all new accounts default to credit-based plans as of 9 Sep 2025).

Eligibility
Open to anyone (individual audience). No .edu email, no startup age/funding requirement, no referral. Global. No credit card required to sign up or use — the hard credit limit means there is no billing mechanism and no auto-recharge/add-on purchase path on this tier. New accounts can start on Free without payment details.
How to claim
Sign up at app.netlify.com / netlify.com and select the Free plan, then deploy from Git, AI, or API. No card, no application. Just create an account and push a site.
Expiry
Credits reset monthly; the $0 tier is ongoing (no sunset). If a project exceeds 300 credits it pauses until the next billing cycle instead of charging.
Gotchas
Credit-based, NOT the legacy 100GB/300-build-minute metered plan — do not compare by bandwidth. Unused credits do not roll over. Only 1 concurrent build and 1-day observability retention. Credits-out = project paused (not billed). AI inference is metered separately ('varies by model') and can burn credits faster than static hosting.
Source
netlify.com

Netlify Open Source plan high confidence

Free plan for qualifying open-source projects: 10,000 credits/month, free production deploys, deploy previews on every PR, unlimited team members, and sites stay live even if credits run out (extra credits at Pro rates if needed). This is a genuinely free offer distinct from the 300-credit Free tier.

Eligibility
Project-based, not student/startup-specific. Requirements: OSI-approved (or Creative Commons with attribution/public-domain) license, a Code of Conduct in the repo, a visible 'This site is powered by Netlify' link/badge, and the project must be non-commercial. Commercial projects excluded. No .edu or funding requirement; open to any qualifying OSS project (docs sites, changelogs, issue trackers, team blogs).
How to claim
Apply via the Open Source application form (opensource-form.netlify.com, linked from the open-source page/policy). Netlify reviews the repo (license, CoC, activity) and provisions the plan; approval can take days to a week.
Expiry
Ongoing; renews monthly. Netlify will downgrade (with notice) if the project no longer meets the criteria or the Netlify link is missing.
Gotchas
Non-commercial only — even commercial support/hosting disqualifies. Requires the backlink. It applies to the project/repo, not to individual maintainers. (Secondary source corroborates 10,000 cr/mo confirmed against the policy page.)
Source
netlify.com

Netlify for Startups (VC-backed program) low confidence

Discounted pricing — NOT a free-credit or free-tier offer. Eligible startups receive a discount on Netlify (including AI Gateway and Agent Runners), shared only on approval. It is referral-only: you enter through an accelerator, investor, or partner that works with Netlify.

Eligibility
VC-backed portfolio companies of Netlify's approved venture partners. Referral only — apply through that partner (accelerator/investor), not a self-serve form. Not available to bootstrapped/non-VC startups, and not .edu-based.
How to claim
Ask your VC/accelerator whether they are an approved Netlify partner, then apply through that partner. There is no public self-serve application (the official /startups/ URL currently 404s; details come from a third-party directory).
Expiry
Ongoing, shared on approval; no fixed stated expiry.
Gotchas
It is a discount, not free hosting/credits — do not list it alongside the free tier as a 'free' offer. Requires VC backing and a partner referral. Official page not retrievable today (404), so terms were read from a third-party directory (last checked 11 Oct 2026); verify directly with your investor before relying on specific numbers.

Netlify Creator Program (earn free Pro) high confidence

Secondary reward program, not a student/startup free tier: publish educational blog post or video about Netlify (topics: shipping with AI, Netlify platform, everyone-is-a-developer). Reward per published piece: 3 months of Netlify Pro (at $20/mo value) plus Netlify credits/resources at onboarding and a swag pack.

Eligibility
Content creators who produce original, ready-to-publish, educational content on Netlify. Not gated on .edu or startup status; new and returning creators welcome. Content must be original and not fully AI-generated.
How to claim
Fill in the application form on the Creator Program page (topic, format, audience, links to past work). Editorial team reviews; ~3 weeks pitch-to-publication; accept → onboard → create → publish → receive 3 months of Pro.
Gotchas
Rewards are per published piece, so recurring but requires new content each time. 3 months of Pro is a time-boxed grant, not a permanent free tier. This is a creator incentive, not a student or startup license.
Source
netlify.com

No dedicated student / education license (and no trial credits) high confidence

Netlify has no separate education plan, student discount, .edu-verified license, or one-time trial-credit offer. Netlify support staff have repeatedly stated there is no educational plan; students are directed to the self-serve Free plan or the Open Source plan (for open repos). No .edu requirement anywhere — access is via the universal Free plan.

Eligibility
Not applicable — no such offer exists.
How to claim
No claim mechanism; there is nothing to apply for. Students simply use the $0 Free plan (300 cr/mo, no card) or, for open-source coursework repos, the Open Source plan.
Source
answers.netlify.com

2025–26 changes

  • Big 2025-2026 change: the old usage-metered Free/Starter plan (100GB bandwidth, 300 build minutes, 125,000 function invocations, 1M edge invocations, 10GB storage, no card) is now a LEGACY plan. From 4 Sep 2025 all NEW accounts use credit-based plans (Free = 300 cr/mo). Existing legacy accounts keep their old plan but may switch to credit-based (irreversible). So the 'known' free tier was restructured from metered (100GB) to credit-based (300 credits ≈ ~15GB or ~20 deploys). Other timeline: 1 Oct 2025 credit-pack rebalance (Personal 500cr/$5, Pro 1500cr/$10; Pro monthly cut 5000→3000 credits); 17 Apr 2026 Pro = unlimited seats, form submissions made free on all credit plans, meter rates changed (bandwidth 10→20 cr/GB, compute 5→10 cr/GB-hr, web requests 3→2 cr/10k). No student/education program was launched or sunset; one never existed. Startup program is a VC-backed discount (referral-only) and is not a free-credit offer; its official /startups/ page returned 404 today, so specifics come from a third-party directory.
