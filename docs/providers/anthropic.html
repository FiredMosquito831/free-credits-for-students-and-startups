Anthropic: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Claude Startups program (free Claude Team year + $1,000 API credit, Oct 2026 expansion). 18 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Live check of “AI for Science program — up to $20,000 API credits for 6 months (researchers/education)”: Partly confirmed

Anthropic's AI for Science program exists and is still open on a rolling basis (applications reviewed the first Monday of each month, no deadline): the help center page (dated March 16, 2026) states "up to $20,000 in API credits" for a 6-month period to researchers in academia and nonprofit organizations attached to research institutions, credits for API use only. However, Anthropic's newer official announcement of August 27, 2026 (anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists) expanded the program and says researchers "can apply to our AI for Science program for up to $50,000 in credits per project" with "Any researcher is eligible to apply", so the $20,000/6-month figure is the older, un-updated tier rather than the current maximum; the separate rare-disease call ($50,000 over six months, deadline Aug 2, 2026) has closed.

Offers

Claude Startups program (free Claude Team year + $1,000 API credit, Oct 2026 expansion) high confidence

Launched May 2026 (originally invite-only via partner VCs; self-serve form added 21–31 May 2026). On Oct 6, 2026 Anthropic expanded it: (a) one free year of Claude Team with up to 5 Premium seats for companies new to Team (~$6,000 at the $100/seat/mo list price), (b) a one-time $1,000 API credit 'available as soon as you're approved', (c) 'Claude Startup Stack' partner discounts/credits worth up to $45,000 (combined partner max, not a cash grant), (d) up to $100,000 in additional API credits claimable through partner VCs, (e) 45-min Applied AI office hours biweekly, Claude Marketplace listing help, Founder House/events. Combined Claude value 'up to $7,000'. After a surge of '21x the expected applications' (hundreds of thousands), the free Team + $1,000 credit offers were paused ~Oct 9–13 and all applications are being re-reviewed; as of today the official page says 'Our current cohort is now full, and we're not accepting new applications right now.' Existing claimed offers are unaffected. Credits apply only to the first-party Claude API via Claude Console — not AWS Bedrock, Vertex, Azure or other third-party platforms.

Eligibility
Startups 'founded in the last five years or funded in the last two' (Oct 2026 expansion wording). Bootstrapped, pre-seed and VC-backed companies may apply. Older FAQ text for the credits tier additionally required institutional equity funding, founding within 4 years, and no prior Anthropic startup credits. Requires a Claude Console account and a company email matching the company's website domain. Geographic exclusions in the official program terms (last updated May 22, 2025): Belarus, China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Sudan, Syria, Crimea, DNR/LNR; Anthropic-affiliated persons excluded. No .edu email; no CC requirement stated.
How to claim
1) Sign in to Claude Console; 2) submit the application at claude.com/programs/startups (currently closed — cohort full); 3) if approved, $1,000 credit and Team year activate automatically; 4) if funded, mention/partner with a partner VC for up to $100K additional credits. To hear when the next cohort opens, subscribe to the Claude Startup newsletter.
Expiry
Cohort currently full / paused as of 2026-10-22; next cohort TBA. Secondary coverage reports the $1,000 API credit expires six months after it is granted; Anthropic's general Credit Terms (cited by a Sept-2026-checked tracker) say promotional credits otherwise expire one calendar year from issuance. No published sunset for the program itself.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: the 'free year' is a subsidized paid-plan grant, not a permanent free tier — after it lapses the account bills at list price. The $45K figure is max combined partner value, not a grant. Credits are first-party-API only (Bedrock/Vertex not eligible). The free offers were paused within days of launch due to capacity; applying now = waitlist. VC-linked $100K tier is separate from the $1,000 and goes through the investor, not a direct Anthropic award. One award per company.

Claude for Open Source — 6 months of free Claude Max 20x for maintainers high confidence

Official page (read today): '6 months of free Claude Max 20x' — a subscription for the Claude apps and Claude Code, not an API credit. Overage beyond Max limits billed at standard rates; when it ends 'your account returns to the free plan' (or resumes prior paid plan). Six qualifying thresholds (any one): (1) 500+ dependent repos or 100+ dependent packages, or 200,000+ combined monthly registry downloads; (2) committer/maintainer of a recognized foundation or language project (CPython, Rust team, Node.js TSC, Apache PMC, CNCF, Kubernetes, Linux kernel, Django, Rails, or similar); (3) 100+ merged PRs into repos you don't own in the last 12 months; (4) one of your repos with 20+ unique external contributors (merged PRs, last 12 months); (5) OpenSSF criticality score >= 0.4 on a repo you maintain; (6) discretionary 'ecosystem impact' catch-all. Baseline requirements (secondary, Sept-2026-checked): GitHub account 2+ years old, public activity in prior 90 days, OSI-approved license, 18+. A Sept-2026-checked tracker adds: cap of 10,000 approved recipients and a 90-day activation window on the approval link.

Eligibility
Open-source maintainers/contributors meeting one threshold; not limited to students or startups — relevant to students who maintain OSS projects. Criteria were overhauled in July 2026 (the older '5,000+ stars / 1M+ npm downloads' gate was replaced by these six thresholds).
How to claim
Apply now at claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss ('Apply now' CTA; form mechanics beyond that not detailed on the page). Approved maintainers get an activation link (use within 90 days per secondary source).
Expiry
Subscription lasts 6 months; 'complimentary subscription ends' with a reminder email beforehand; account falls back to Free plan after. No program sunset date published.
Gotchas
Subscription, not API credit — does not fund API-based products. Overage after Max limits costs standard Max rates. A separate linked Terms document (anthropic.com/claude-for-oss-terms) governs details not shown on the page.
Source

Claude via AWS Bedrock using AWS Activate credits (startup free-compute route) medium confidence

Anthropic's own program excludes Bedrock/Vertex for its credits, but startups can reach Claude through AWS Activate: founders tier $1,000–$5,000 self-funded (pre-Series B, founded within last 10 years; AWS says 'you'll hear back within 5–10 business days'); portfolio/VC-accelerator tier up to $200,000 via a VC or accelerator with an AWS Org ID; invite-only 'AWS Credits for AI Startups' tier $200,000+. The AWS Activate FAQ (checked Sept 2026) states credits 'are also redeemable for third-party models on Amazon Bedrock... like AI21 Labs, Anthropic, Cohere.' Counterpoint: Google for Startups credits are NOT usable for third-party models on Vertex, and Microsoft Foundry docs list credit-based accounts as unable to use Claude — so AWS is the viable credit path to Claude on a hyperscaler.

Eligibility
Startups pre-Series B, founded within the last 10 years (AWS Activate); US global eligibility per AWS program terms; region limits apply to AWS accounts. Not a student program (student routes on AWS are separate, e.g., AWS Educate — not verified today).
How to claim
1) Apply for AWS Activate at aws.amazon.com/activate (self-serve or via your VC/accelerator); 2) claim the Org-ID-based credits; 3) enable Amazon Bedrock in your AWS account and select Anthropic Claude models; 4) spend Activate credits on those model calls.
Expiry
Activate credit expiry is per-AWS-timer rules (typically fixed windows per tier, not re-verified today). No Anthropic-side expiry on Bedrock usage; AWS Bedrock usage pricing differs from first-party API pricing.
Gotchas
Secondary source (checked 2026-09-25) — re-verify amounts on aws.amazon.com/activate before relying on them. Bedrock Claude pricing, rates and model availability differ from the first-party API; egress/data-transfer and Bedrock service fees can erode the credit value (data/egress costs gotcha). Anthropic startup credits cannot be used on Bedrock/Vertex — the two credit pools don't mix.
