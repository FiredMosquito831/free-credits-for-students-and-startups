Canva Pro for Students: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Canva Free (free tier, open to all students). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Canva Free (free tier, open to all students) high confidence

Official US pricing snapshot 2026-10-22: US$0/year. Includes drag-and-drop editor with 1,000+ design types, 1.6M+ templates, 4.7M+ photos/videos/graphics/audio, 1 Brand Kit (3 colors), 5GB cloud storage, ad insights/AI ad creation, access to Affinity, and a shared AI allowance of up to 200 Standard AI uses or 20 Premium AI uses (new 'AI Pass' era layout; older layout shows 'Limited AI access'). Higher-ed students whose school lacks Canva for Campus land on exactly this tier. Prices vary by country; today's page renders localized currency.

Eligibility
Open to everyone — no student verification, no .edu email, no credit card, no region limit. Sign up at canva.com with email/Google/Apple/Facebook/Microsoft.
How to claim
1) Go to canva.com or canva.com/education/students -> 'Sign up for free'. 2) Create an account. 3) Use the Free tier; optionally log in with your university email in case your institution has Canva for Campus (upgrades you to premium automatically).
Expiry
Indefinite free tier; no renewal. Content persists; premium assets/templates become locked/watermarked if a paid or campus license is lost.
Gotchas
Free tier excludes the 141M+ premium asset library, 3.6M premium templates, background remover, social scheduler, and paid-tier AI allowance. A 'free' university sign-in only gets Pro features if the institution actually bought Campus. AI allowance on Free is ~200 Standard / 20 Premium uses per month.
Source
canva.com

Canva Education (K-12 license): 100% free Pro features high confidence

Official: 'Canva Education will remain 100% free for K-12 educators, their students, as well as qualified school districts and institutions' — no paywalls, available worldwide, not restricted to any country. Includes most premium features (100M+ premium photos/videos/graphics/fonts, Brand Kits, background remover, Magic tools), plus educator features: LMS integrations (Canvas, Google Classroom, Schoology, Blackboard, D2L, Moodle, MS Teams), SSO/Clever/ClassLink, enterprise-grade security, class management. Free delivery on print orders. Canva states it never uses student data to train AI models.

Eligibility
K-12 (primary/secondary) only: certified active K-12 teachers, school librarians, learning-support staff, curriculum specialists, tech/vocational K-12 teachers, and government-recognized accredited K-12 schools/districts/education departments — and the students they enroll. Explicitly EXCLUDED: colleges/universities, college/university students, tutors, public librarians, unlicensed homeschoolers, parents, early-stage educational startups. Verified education email = instant access; otherwise upload teacher license/cert/school ID — reviewed within 48h (US/JP/AU/CA/NZ/UK/KR teachers need only a teaching license copy). No credit card required.
How to claim
Teachers: sign up at canva.com/education with a verified K-12 school email, or upload proof (48h review). Students: ask the teacher to invite you to their Canva class, or sign up with your school email / district SSO — if it matches a school district you get instant Canva Education access; else enter the teacher's email to request access. You cannot self-verify as a K-12 student.
Expiry
Lasts while you are active and eligible; all Canva Education users are prompted to re-verify credentials every 3 years. Students who leave the class or graduate revert to the free plan — copy designs to a personal account first.
Gotchas
Premium features can be disabled by school administrators. Access is class/team-scoped, not a personal permanent Pro account: on graduation the account reverts to Canva Free. Intended for educational use — commercial licensing differs from Pro. Verified-email domains must be recognized Canva education domains; personal Gmail/Yahoo not accepted.
Source
canva.com

Canva for Campus (higher education): free premium student access via institutional license high confidence

Official: Canva Campus = Canva Enterprise plan 'plus free premium student access included' — all premium student seats free, priced like Enterprise at special higher-ed pricing (no public price; contact sales). Unlock condition: institution purchases a minimum number of staff/faculty licenses calculated from its full-time student body (a Canva consultant sets the threshold; <25 seats -> Canva Business, no HE pricing). Included: SSO/SCIM, FERPA/SOC 2/GDPR compliance, 1TB storage, LMS integrations (Blackboard, Canvas, D2L, Moodle), brand kits, admin AI on/off controls, and per the page, access to Affinity and Flourish within Campus. 'Free access for all students... as part of Canva Step Two' (every enrolled student gets full premium from day one at no cost to them). Examples: UT Austin rolled out free Canva Pro to all students 2025-10-17 (EID sign-in, ~800 sign-ups in first 30 min); Lehigh entered a campus-wide agreement March 2026 with pro-rated rebates for students who paid individually; HKU ran a student-society campaign 2025-11-06 to 2026-11-05 at HK$35/year (~96% below the HK$960 list price).

Eligibility
Enrolled students of universities/colleges that have purchased Canva Campus (or Enterprise on the path to it). No individual application, no .edu verification, no credit card. Excluded: student employees, part-time/full-time staff and interns of the institution. Not available to individuals at non-adopting schools — they fall back to Canva Free or pay full Pro price.
How to claim
Student at an adopting campus: sign in to Canva with your university email / campus SSO (or use the institution's Canva sign-in page, e.g. canva sign-in for UT EID); if the campus has Campus you automatically get premium. Otherwise: there is nothing to claim individually — the university's IT/student union must procure the plan ('let your IT team or student union know you want it - student demand is often what gets Campus started').
Expiry
Lasts while enrolled; on graduation you may lose the school email and with it the free account — copy designs to a personal account. Institutions re-provision via SSO/SCIM; de-provisioning is automatic.
Gotchas
Institution-paid but admin-gated: faculty/IT can switch AI tools off per course/role/cohort. No public price; 'minimum seat thresholds apply'. If your school has no Campus you pay consumer Pro (US$120-180/yr snapshots) — no individual student discount listed officially in 2026. Individual campus campaigns (HKU HK$35/yr) are local student-union deals, not a Canva-wide student program; the HKU offer window closed 2026-11-05.
Source
canva.com

Canva Pro / Canva Business 30-day free trial (any user, incl. students) medium confidence

Official help center: Pro and Business offer a free trial, started via 'Start a free trial' on the pricing page; auto-renews into the chosen monthly/yearly plan unless cancelled; trial AI allowance equals the Free tier's (the paid 10x/20x AI allowance only kicks in after conversion); one trial per Canva account AND one per payment method ('if you've used a trial before, you won't see the free trial option; try a new payment method'); trial end date visible under Settings > Billing & Teams with email reminder; 'If you started a free trial without providing payment details, your trial ends automatically and you won't be charged' (a no-card flow exists; other flows/regions require card or PayPal to activate). Third-party 2026 sources describe the standard US trial as 30 days of full Pro (some regions 14 days). After cancel: account reverts to Free, designs retained but premium assets re-watermarked. Paid US list prices (2026 snapshots, transition in progress): Pro US$120-180/yr, Business US$200-250/yr per person, 'Save 16%' on yearly.

Eligibility
Anyone, not student-specific. No student status needed; no credit card strictly required (a payment-less trial flow exists, though most activations collect card/PayPal up front). Available where Canva accepts payment; trial window is region-dependent.
How to claim
1) canva.com/pricing -> 'Start a free trial' under Pro or Business. 2) Log in / create account. 3) Choose monthly or yearly (this only sets what you're charged at conversion). 4) Choose payment method (or the no-card flow if offered). 5) Confirm. 6) Cancel before day 30 via Settings > Billing > Cancel plan to avoid the charge.
Expiry
30 days in most regions (14 days in some); ends automatically if no payment details were provided, else converts to paid automatically on the anniversary date.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free trap: auto-renews and charges the full chosen plan; the elevated 10x/20x AI allowance is NOT available during the trial (only Free-tier allowance). One trial per account and per payment method — switching card does not reset it. Charges may appear as Canva / Canva*invoice_id / APPLE*Canva / GOOGLE*Canva (app-store billing if subscribed via mobile). New 2026 add-ons (AI Pass monthly add-on; 30-day AI top-ups for Business) cost extra and are not part of the trial.
Source
canva.com

Canva for Startups — no free offer exists high confidence

canva.com/startups is a marketing/resource hub only ('Design your success. Canva is the all-in-one content collaboration platform...'): startup template/pitch-deck content, video tips, a 4-part growth series, and guidance to standard plans ('If there's more than one person, Canva Business is the best plan for startups... pricing tiers to fit every startup's budget, no matter which round of funding you're in'). No discount, free seats, trial credits, or funding/age caps for startups. Canva's eligibility guidelines also explicitly list 'early stage start-ups with an educational focus' as NOT eligible for Canva Education. Related adjacent program (not student/startup): Canva Nonprofits — 100% free Pro features + team tools for registered nonprofits/NGOs, one team of up to 50 users, extra seats 50% off; government agencies, political orgs, educational institutions and for-profits excluded.

Eligibility
None — no startup-specific eligibility because no startup offer exists. (Nonprofit program: registered 501(c)(3)-equivalent orgs, up to 50 free users.)
How to claim
N/A — nothing to claim. A startup can only use Canva Free, the standard Pro/Business trial, or the nonprofit program if it is a registered charity.
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
Don't confuse the 'Canva for Startups' content hub with a startup discount program. Also 'Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades' (2026) — team features now live under Canva Business.
Source
canva.com

2025–26 changes

  • The known '~50% off Canva Pro first year for verified students (~$49.99/yr vs $99.99 via .edu or national student verification)' does NOT appear on any official Canva page as of 2026-10-22: the students page offers only Canva Free (higher ed) or free K-12 Canva Education, the eligibility guidelines explicitly exclude college/university students from free Canva Education, and the pricing page lists no student price. The individual ~50% student Pro plan appears to have been sunset and replaced by two structural programs: (1) free Canva Education for K-12, and (2) Canva for Campus — where universities buy Enterprise-priced licenses and all premium student seats are free (e.g. UT Austin, free since 2025-10-17; Lehigh, March 2026; HKU student-union campaign HK$35/yr valid 2025-11-06 to 2026-11-05). US Pro's list price is also no longer $99.99: 2026 pages show US$120-180/yr (a transition-era A/B or recent increase; one third-party check recorded $144/yr on 2026-10-16). 2025-2026 material changes: Canva Pro trials now grant only the Free-tier AI allowance during the trial, plans auto-renew, Canva Teams was discontinued for new sign-ups, and new paid add-ons (AI Pass monthly add-on; 30-day AI top-ups for Business) were introduced. Note: canva.com blocks WebFetch (403); official pages were verified today via direct curl. Local university campaigns (HKU etc.) are institution-specific and expire on their own dates, not Canva policy.
