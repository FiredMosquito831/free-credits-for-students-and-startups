Stripe Startups: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Stripe Startups program — fee credits + exclusive Invoicing cap. 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Stripe Startups program — fee credits + exclusive Invoicing cap high confidence

Official program for early-stage venture-backed companies. Core perks: (1) credits that offset Stripe fees on Payments, Connect, Billing, Tax and Sigma for 12 months; (2) exclusive capped Invoicing pricing starting at 0.4% per paid invoice, capped at US$10 (or local-currency equivalent, e.g. 45 lei, £7, €8, AU$14) for the first 12 months, auto-activated alongside fee credits; (3) expert education, tailored advice, and founder/Stripe-leader community; (4) pricing review opportunity when monthly payments exceed $100,000 for 3 consecutive months. No equity taken (no mention of equity/warrants anywhere in the program).

Eligibility
Early-stage, venture-backed companies; application must include proof of institutional funding (PitchBook link, funding-announcement articles, or other documentation). Must be on Stripe's standard pricing, no other platform may control your pricing, no prior offer received; one offer per startup in its lifetime. No founding-date cap, funding-amount cap, or headcount limit is published. Region: fee-credit offer is available in 31 named countries (US, UK, EU countries, AU, NZ, JP, SG, HK, etc.); in all other countries the equivalent benefit is waived processing fees instead.
How to claim
Apply directly at stripe.com/startups (redirects to dashboard.stripe.com/register/startup?partner_code=PUBLIC) or via an investor/accelerator partner link (Builtfirst partner pages show partner-specific credit amounts: $500 credits via AWS Activate, $5,000 credits via Emerson Collective/Inovia). Complete the form, verify your active Stripe account, submit; Stripe responds in 2-3 business days; accept the offer via the Dashboard banner or a setup-guide task (admin/owner login). Offer must be activated within 12 months of acceptance.
Expiry
Once activated: offer expires 12 months after activation or when the credit limit is reached, whichever comes first; cannot be paused. Unused credits expire 1 year after activation. Invoicing cap ends after 12 months (standard pricing applies). No renewal; Stripe can extend the offer only once.
Gotchas
Credit amounts are NOT fixed — the FAQ says 'Offer amounts vary by offer type... and by your referral source'; partner-specific pages list $500 (AWS Activate) to $5,000 (Emerson/Inovia) in credits, and where credits are unavailable, waivers on $20K-$150K USD of processing volume. Excluded products: Crypto, Capital, Atlas, Terminal devices, Link passthrough, PayPal fees, professional services. Cap is based on payment date, not invoice creation date; each installment payment gets its own $10 cap. Invoicing fee is otherwise 0.4% uncapped (Starter) — the $10 cap is the perk. Users with custom/volume pricing or who already redeemed a discount are ineligible. No .edu/student pathway exists; the only route is proving institutional (VC) funding. No credit card is required to apply or hold a Stripe account (usage-based billing).
Source
support.stripe.com

Free usage-based standard pricing (no setup/monthly fees) — de facto free to start high confidence

Stripe has no subscription 'free tier'; instead, standard pricing has 'No setup fees, monthly fees, or hidden fees' — you pay only per transaction. A new company (including a student side-project) can create a live Stripe account and accept real payments for $0 upfront. Included at no extra charge on standard pricing: 3D Secure, Authorization Boost, Adaptive Pricing, Radar Lite (AI fraud protection), dispute deflection lookups, and Connect (embedded payments) with 0.25% starting fee only if the platform sets its own rates. Per-transaction fees vary by locale (e.g. Romania: 1.5%+lei1.00 domestic EEA cards; US standard is 2.9%+30c card-present/online card rates). No credit card required to open or use a Stripe account (pay-as-you-go).

Eligibility
Anyone who can open a Stripe account (business or sole proprietor in supported countries). Not student-specific, not startup-specific; no funding proof or .edu email needed.
How to claim
Sign up at stripe.com and create a live account in minutes; no credit card, no monthly fee. Start processing immediately; costs are purely per-transaction plus product fees.
Expiry
Permanent pricing model; no trial period needed.
Gotchas
'Free to start' is not free to run: card fees, a 1% instant-payouts fee (where available), dispute fees (per-locale, e.g. 100 lei per dispute received/countered in Romania), and per-transaction product fees (Invoicing 0.4% uncapped outside the Startups program; Billing, Tax, Identity, etc.) all still apply. This is a pricing model, not a student startup offer — do not list it as a discount in a comparison of free offers.
Source
stripe.com

30-day free trials: Sigma, Data Pipeline, Revenue Recognition + free-usage tiers high confidence

Official pricing page offers 'Try for free for 30 days' on: Stripe Sigma (query tool), Data Pipeline (warehouse/data sync), and Revenue Recognition. Separate perpetual free-usage allowances: Workflows — 10,000 steps per month free, then ~0.078 lei per additional step; Data Pipeline Postgres sync — 5,000 rows per month free. (Figures seen on the Romania-locale pricing page; US equivalents: Workflows $0.02/step overage, Sigma/Data Pipeline/Revenue Recognition priced per usage.)

Eligibility
Any Stripe account holder; no student or startup status required; no credit card required to start a trial.
How to claim
Create a Stripe account, navigate to the product (Sigma / Data Pipeline / Revenue Recognition), and activate the 30-day trial directly in the dashboard.
Expiry
30-day trial per product; free monthly allowances (10k workflow steps, 5k data rows) are recurring and not a trial.
Gotchas
These are trials, not free tiers — after 30 days the product bills per its standard plan (e.g. Sigma in the locale page is billed by plan: ~44 lei/mo annual or 66 lei/mo). If you only use under the free allowances, costs can be near-zero even post-trial.
Source
stripe.com

Stripe Startups separate Treasury benefit: $1,000 cash for opening a Stripe card medium confidence

The official FAQ lists a separate Treasury benefit: sign up for a Stripe card 'within 7 days of the event' and Stripe deposits US$1,000 in cash into your Treasury account within 30 days of card creation.

Eligibility
Startups participating in the Stripe Startups program events; exact trigger ('the event') and program enrollment required.
How to claim
Create a Stripe card within 7 days of the qualifying event; $1,000 deposited to your Stripe Treasury account within ~30 days.
Expiry
One-time; 7-day window to act.
Gotchas
Only mentioned in the FAQ's benefits list — no standalone terms page found; confirm details at application time.
Source
support.stripe.com

Student / education licensing: no free offer found medium confidence

No student-only free tier, .edu-based discount, or education license exists for Stripe as of 2026-10-22. The official pricing page lists no student or education pricing; partner marketplaces and search results contain no .edu-gated Stripe offer. The only education-adjacent benefit is the 'Education' pillar of the Stripe Startups program (expert insights/how-to programming), which is venture-funding-gated, not student-gated. Free paths for students: the usage-based standard pricing ($0 to start, no credit card) and the 30-day product trials above; Stripe's test mode/API has always been free.

Eligibility
N/A (no offer)
How to claim
N/A
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
Students building projects should budget real card fees for live traffic; test mode is free and card-free but does not process real money.

stripe.com

Partner-specific credit amounts (referral-dependent tiers) medium confidence

Because offer amounts vary by referral source, Builtfirst partner pages publish the tiers: AWS Activate — $500 USD in Stripe credits (or, in countries where credits aren't yet available, waived fees on the equivalent of $20,000 USD in processing volume), new Stripe users only; Emerson Collective / Inovia — $5,000 USD in credits (or $150,000 USD volume waived where credits unavailable), available to new AND existing customers, plus the 0.4% Invoicing cap up to $10/invoice for year one.

Eligibility
Venture-backed startups referred through that specific investor/program (or via Builtfirst login claim); must be on standard pricing and not hold custom/volume pricing or a prior redeemed discount.
How to claim
Claim through the partner's Builtfirst login page (login to claim, redirected to Stripe registration with partner code), or the partner's partner-page link.
Expiry
Credits: unused credits expire 1 year after activation. Invoicing cap: first 12 months.
Gotchas
Amounts are partner-specific — direct application (stripe.com/startups, PUBLIC partner code) may yield a different, often smaller, offer than a VC referral; check which partner tier you qualify for before assuming the number.
Source
emersoncollective.builtfirst.com
