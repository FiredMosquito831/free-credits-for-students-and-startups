NVIDIA: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free rate-limited NIM hosted inference (NVIDIA Developer Program). 16 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free rate-limited NIM hosted inference (NVIDIA Developer Program) high confidence

Free API access to the NIM API Catalog (160+ models, incl. Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B, DeepSeek V4.x, GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Llama 3.x) at build.nvidia.com, callable via OpenAI-compatible base URL https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1. Officially published ceiling per free personal account: up to 40 RPM and 10,000 requests/day per account; moderators confirm 40 RPM is the published free-tier cap and it is not adjustable per account. Governed by the NVIDIA API Trial Terms of Service (PDF last modified 2025-10-07): trial/prototyping purposes only, 'without use of the API Service or Generated Content in production'; NVIDIA may extend 'trial service credits' but must have a paid Subscription for production use. No credit card required; no per-token price. Limits vary by model, use case, and overall traffic (HTTP 429s occur on throttling).

Eligibility
Anyone with a free NVIDIA Developer Program account (no .edu email, no corporate email, no funding requirement). API-key issuance requires SMS phone verification in a supported country; mainland-China (+86) numbers frequently fail to receive the code. Not offered in sanctioned/embargoed regions: Russia, Belarus, Iran, Kazakhstan, Ukraine-adjacent set incl. Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan, Bangladesh plus 6 others (build.nvidia.com publishes an otp-unsupported-countries list; ~5.5% of developers worldwide excluded).
How to claim
1) Create an NVIDIA account at build.nvidia.com (Developer Program is free). 2) Verify phone number via SMS OTP (country must be supported). 3) Generate a personal API key in the API Keys section. 4) Call https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1 (OpenAI-compatible chat/completions) with model IDs copied from the catalog (only endpoints marked 'Free Endpoint'). Support: forums.developer.nvidia.com NIM section.
Expiry
No expiry while the Developer Program and the offering remain live; individual free endpoints rotate (models get deprecated and replaced by newer versions, routine catalog rotation). Free access lasts for the 'duration of program membership'.
Gotchas
Trial, not a permanent free tier: production use (serving real end users, business transactions) requires a paid NVIDIA AI Enterprise license. Inputs/outputs are recorded and used to improve NVIDIA products incl. AI models (Trial Terms 3.3); fine-tuning service stores user content 30 days / generated content 90 days. Arbitration + class-action waiver (30-day opt-out window). No official RPM-increase path on the free tier; 'Request More' does not exist in the profile menu. Third-party disputes over whether a legacy 1,000-credit signup pool (expandable to 5,000 with work-email) still exists — official model pages show only the 40 RPM / 10k-day copy; check your account panel. New personal keys can 404 on some chat calls until endpoints provision.
Source
build.nvidia.com

Free self-hosted NIM license (downloadable NIM containers, up to 16 GPUs) medium confidence

Developer Program members may download NIM microservice containers from NGC and self-host them for research, application development, and experimentation on up to 16 GPUs on any infrastructure (cloud, data center, workstation) at no charge from NVIDIA — your only cost is your own GPU infrastructure. Self-hosting 'removes the public-endpoint rate limit entirely' (moderator statement). Additional GPUs 'upon request'.

Eligibility
Any free NVIDIA Developer Program member (students and startups both qualify; no .edu or funding requirements, no CC needed). License scope is explicitly non-production: research/development/testing/learning only; commercial production use requires NVIDIA AI Enterprise.
How to claim
1) Join the free Developer Program. 2) Browse NGC catalog / build.nvidia.com model cards, pull NIM container (pull command + Helm chart shown under each model's Deploy tab). 3) Deploy on your own NVIDIA GPU(s), up to 16 for free dev/test.
Expiry
Tied to Developer Program membership; no published end date. Terms change with the NVIDIA Software License Agreement.
Gotchas
16-GPU cap: commercial/production deployments are a license violation without AI Enterprise. You still pay for GPUs (cloud or hardware). This offer is the 2024-era framing from the official NIM FAQ; current self-host pricing is also bundled into the paid AI Enterprise product (from ~$4,500/GPU/yr).

forums.developer.nvidia.com

NVIDIA AI Enterprise 90-day free trial license (self-hosted, production-grade) medium confidence

One-time 90-day evaluation license for the full NVIDIA AI Enterprise suite (NIM microservices, NeMo, Omniverse, K8s tooling; NVIDIA-hosted trial also mentions a free trial of NIM endpoints). Each evaluation license includes 128 concurrent users. Trial clock starts when the activation key is redeemed in the licensing portal (nvid.nvidia.com/dashboard). Renewal: no auto-renewal; a second 90-day eval license can be requested via a form, and longer POCs are referred to NVIDIA Partner Network partners.

Eligibility
Organizations with corporate/business email — personal emails (Google/Yahoo etc.) are rejected on application. Compatible infrastructure required (NVIDIA Certified Systems / product support matrix); individuals without NVIDIA hardware are steered to NVIDIA sales. No credit card or payment info visible in the registration flow; it is a license, not a bill.
How to claim
1) Go to nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/ai-enterprise/get-started/ → 'Request Free 90-Day License' (or the Test Drive redirect URL). 2) Register with a corporate email at enterpriseproductregistration.nvidia.com. 3) Wait ~24h for approval email with license-portal access. 4) Redeem the Product Activation Key, download software from NGC, run a license server.
Expiry
Exactly 90 days from activation-key redemption; one-time per org, extension via a re-application or partner POC. Trial includes Omniverse but NOT Run:ai (separate contact needed).
Gotchas
Personal/freemail addresses are auto-denied — students without an org email effectively cannot claim it, so the 90-day trial is an org/startup offer, not a student offer. 'Failed to acquire license' message = trial over. Production use of the free Developer-Program NIM endpoints remains disallowed even while holding a trial — the trial is for self-hosted eval on your GPUs.
Source
nvidia.com

NVIDIA Inception startup program (free membership, cloud credits, no equity) high confidence

Free global virtual program for AI startups: no application fee, no membership fee, no equity taken; 'no deadlines or cohorts' — apply anytime, review ~2-4 weeks. Members get: free cloud credits from NVIDIA and partners (NVIDIA publishes NO fixed dollar figure on the page; third-party 2026 guides cite up to $100,000 in NVIDIA DGX Cloud credits for H100 capacity and up to $100,000 AWS credits via AWS Activate, plus free NIM API endpoints for prototyping), free NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute self-paced courses + discounted workshops, preferred pricing on select NVIDIA hardware/software (rebate off supplier price), full Developer Forum access, VC introductions via Inception Capital Connect, co-marketing badges/events. A 30% DGX Cloud discount tier reportedly requires a 4-node / $75,000 minimum spend (funded teams).

Eligibility
Company must be: officially incorporated, less than 10 years old, with a clear use case for NVIDIA technology (AI/ML/CV/robotics/physical AI/simulation/HPCC), actively operated by at least one founder, with a working website. No revenue, no VC funding, no US incorporation required. Excluded: consulting/outsourced-dev firms, crypto-associated companies, cloud service providers, resellers/distributors, public companies. Students/individuals cannot apply (incorporated company required).
How to claim
1) Go to nvidia.com/en-us/startups/ → Apply. 2) Sign in with an NVIDIA Developer account, fill company profile (name, site, founding date, team, focus area). 3) Describe the product in 3-4 sentences with explicit AI/GPU angle. 4) Submit; after review a partner manager activates benefits (credits, NIM, training) via intro call. Contact: inceptionprogram@nvidia.com.
Expiry
Membership is open-ended (no published end date; benefits 'for the duration of membership'). Credit expiry: NVIDIA does not publish on the official page — partner credit windows vary (AWS Activate typically 1-2 years).
Gotchas
No published dollar figure exists on NVIDIA's page for cloud credits — the $100K DGX Cloud and $100K AWS numbers come from third-party 2026 guides, so verify with your account manager before budgeting. Credits burn out while the ongoing GPU bill continues. No guaranteed access to scarce GPUs. Preferred pricing is a rebate, not a guarantee of supply.

Free hosted NIM endpoints covered by the API Trial Terms (data/usage caveats for everyone, incl. students) high confidence

The formal NVIDIA API Trial Terms of Service (PDF, last modified 2025-10-07, read in full) govern all free build.nvidia.com inference: access is 'for limited trial purposes only and without use of the API Service or Generated Content in production' (§1.2). NVIDIA 'may extend trial service credits' (amounts per API service; §1.4) — after credits are exhausted, production requires a separate paid Subscription. §3.3: NVIDIA collects session metrics, error/execution logs, feedback, and User/Generated Content 'to improve NVIDIA products and services, including AI models', and usage is logged for security/fraud and shared with third-party providers. §2.4: fine-tuning service stores user content 30 days and generated content 90 days unless a subscription is purchased. §11.2: NVIDIA may terminate your use 'at any time without notice'.

Eligibility
Applies to every free-tier user (students, individual devs, startups alike); requires an adult of legal age in the country of use, Developer Program account + phone-verified API key, and a supported country (sanctioned-region exclusions apply). No .edu email, no CC, no funding cap.
How to claim
Nothing to claim — it is the legal layer under the free tier above. Read the Trial Terms PDF linked from every build.nvidia.com model card ('The trial service is governed by the NVIDIA API Trial Terms of Service').
Expiry
No expiry of the terms themselves; each trial/credit grant, model endpoint, or service can be terminated by NVIDIA at any time (§11.2, §1.3 pre-release clauses). Arbitration/class-waiver opt-out must be sent within 30 days of starting use to be valid (§14.4).
Gotchas
Do not send PII, financial/health/government data or PCI data (§4.3). Do not use free endpoints to build a product that 'compete[s] with the API Service' (§4.12). No sublicensing or redistribution of outputs (§4.2). Because content can be used to improve NVIDIA models, treat the free tier as unsuitable for confidential workloads. 'Free' ≠ perpetual: endpoints and rate limits can change or be sunset at NVIDIA's discretion with no liability.

assets.ngc.nvidia.com
