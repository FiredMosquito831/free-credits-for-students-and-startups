Google TPU Research Cloud: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: TRC free Cloud TPU quota for accepted researchers. 1 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

TRC free Cloud TPU quota for accepted researchers high confidence

Accepted researchers get no-cost Cloud TPU quota in their own Google Cloud project, usable within minutes of accepting an invitation. The About page describes access to more than 1,000 Cloud TPU devices (also described as thousands of Cloud TPU chips). Per-participant TPU type, device count, and access length are not stated; access is granted on a temporary basis. No dollar credits or cash amounts are offered. Supported frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Julia, JAX. Only TPU use is free; other Google Cloud services are billed (see gotchas).

Eligibility
Researchers accepted into the TRC program. Anyone may express interest, but invitations go only to approved applicants, and the approval criteria are not published. The official pages name no student-only, startup-only, or country restriction, and they do not name students or startups as a target group. Students are not excluded by the text, but no student track exists. Startups are not mentioned at all, so a startup has no stated path in.
How to claim
1) Submit the TRC interest form, reached from the Express interest button on https://sites.research.google/trc/about/ (direct link https://forms.gle/uwWjUewyxZ5okL3W7). 2) Wait for an invitation. Invitations are sent on a rolling basis to approved applicants; no review timeline is published. 3) Accept the invitation and agree to Google's Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. 4) The Cloud TPU quota appears in your Google Cloud project. The quota is not compatible with CMLE, CAIP, or Vertex-based workflows; the FAQ recommends GCE tutorials instead.
Expiry
No end date or renewal terms are published. Access is only described as 'on a temporary basis'. The pages carry no dates.
Gotchas
(1) Approval is discretionary and undisclosed; expressing interest does not guarantee an invitation. (2) Required in return: share TRC-supported research publicly (peer-reviewed papers, open-source code, blog posts, or similar), give Google detailed feedback, and keep research consistent with Google AI Principles. A suggested acknowledgment line is 'Research supported with Cloud TPUs from Google's TPU Research Cloud (TRC)'. (3) Non-TPU GCP services are not free: the FAQ names small VMs (n1-standard-2) and Cloud Storage buckets for training data, described as 'generally minimal'. Networking and egress are not addressed. Billing questions go to trc-support@google.com. (4) Access is temporary with no stated length, so do not plan on it as permanent compute.

2025–26 changes

  • No closure or 2025-2026 change was found, but the About and FAQ pages are undated and say nothing about 2025 or 2026, so the program's current status is unconfirmed. WebSearch was unavailable (session search budget exhausted), so no third-party or news check of 2025-2026 status was possible. The 2026 TPU Research and Education Awards (unrestricted gift up to $100K plus TPU access, June 30, 2026 deadline) could not be verified: neither official page mentions it, and https://sites.research.google/trc/awards/ returned HTTP 404 (a guessed URL). It is not listed as a finding. Its deadline is before today (2026-10-22), so it cannot be claimed in any case. The TRC homepage (https://sites.research.google/trc/) returned only its title on fetch, so its content was not checked. The About page also says Cloud TPUs can be used free in Google Colab with no setup, framed for researchers and students; Colab's limits and terms were not verified, and it sits outside TRC, so it is not a finding here. Verdict: the TRC offer is genuine for accepted researchers, but it is not a student or startup offer as such, because no student or startup track is published.
