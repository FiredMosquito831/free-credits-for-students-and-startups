Blender: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Blender 3D suite — 100% free, no license, all uses (students, universities, startups included). 3 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Blender 3D suite — 100% free, no license, all uses (students, universities, startups included) high confidence

Blender 5.2.3 LTS (5.3 expected 2026-11-10; also beta, daily/Early Access builds) is free for everyone: GPL (source v2+; binary distributions compatible under v3+), 'Free to Use. Free to Change. Free to Share. Free to Sell Your Work.' No license, registration, account, credit card, or internet required. Output work is the user's sole property. Foundation commitment: 'keep Blender free and open for everyone, forever.' Platforms: Windows x64/ARM, macOS (Apple Silicon), Linux, Steam, source code. Startups and companies get the same free use as students — there is no commercial 'seat' cost at all.

Eligibility
Universal. No .edu or student email, no proof of status, no region limits, no org size cap. Universities can deploy it on unlimited machines free of charge. This is the entire 'student offer': nothing to discount because there is no license to buy.
How to claim
Download directly from https://www.blender.org/download/ — no signup, no payment, no trial. Daily builds at builder.blender.org. Nothing to claim or renew.
Expiry
Never — perpetual free access; version updates are also free. Renewal not applicable.
Gotchas
Free tier vs paid ecosystem: the app is free, but Blender Marketplace add-ons/assets are paid, and Blender Studio (see next finding) is a paid subscription that names students as an audience — do not confuse the free app with paid services. Optional donation via fund.blender.org supports development but is not required.
Source
blender.org

education.blender.org — free open-licensed education resources for teachers and students high confidence

Blender Foundation-backed community site offering: 'Education Recipes' (lesson plans/curricula, e.g. 'Modeling & animation for absolute beginners', 'VR Class'), 'Quests' (step-by-step tutorials: Party Monkey, House, Rocket, Maze, Chair, Landscape, etc.), 'Concepts' material, and 'Education Badges' — a tiered 7-discipline skill framework (Modeling, Animation, 2D Art, Sculpting, Rigging, Lighting/Camera/Rendering, Video Sequencer, 3-4 levels each). All content released under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or later); source repo is open at projects.blender.org/blender/blender-education-docs. Site framing: 'unrestricted access to high-quality tools without financial barriers or privacy concerns.'

Eligibility
Open to all teachers and students; no account, no .edu verification, no fee, no region limit. Educator support via chat channel #blender-education on chat.blender.org.
How to claim
Browse/download directly at education.blender.org; contribution via its 'Get Involved / Contribute' pages. No application.
Expiry
Perpetual — CC BY-SA license; no sunset date.
Gotchas
This is curriculum/reference material, not a software license or certification — badges are a self-reference framework, not graded credentials. It complements the free software rather than adding to it.
Source
education.blender.org

Blender Studio (studio.blender.org) — official paid training/production platform; NOT a free offer; no student discount (gotcha) high confidence

Paid subscription funding the Studio team's films and training. Individual plan: €17/month, or €34.50 per quarter (€11.50/month equivalent, 'Save €5.50/month'). Team plan: from €500/month, unlimited seats on a company email domain, up to '8 hours of dedicated training' on higher tiers. Includes: full production-asset access, Open Movie production logs, unlimited trainings (courses, workshops, docs, production lessons), commenting. Payment: major credit cards, PayPal, direct bank transfer. Fees are non-refundable. cloud.blender.org 301-redirects to studio.blender.org — there is no separate free 'Blender Cloud' rendering service.

Eligibility
The individual plan is explicitly marketed 'for freelancers, students, and instructors' — but at standard price: no free tier, no trial, no student/education discount, no startup program. Students pay the same €17/month.
How to claim
Join at studio.blender.org/join/ (account via OAuth login required) or /teams/ for the team plan. No free claim path exists.
Expiry
Subscription cancels per terms; content access ends with the subscription.
Gotchas
Most likely thing to misread as a student offer because 'students' is in the audience line — it is a paid plan with no concession. The free software itself (finding 1) already covers students at zero cost; Studio adds the Foundation's film production content only. Display bug seen on the studio homepage ('€1150/month') — /join/ page is authoritative: €17/mo.
Source
studio.blender.org
