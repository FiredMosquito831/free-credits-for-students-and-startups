$200 Azure credit usable within the first 30 days (forfeited if unused; not banked/transferable). Eligibility
New Azure customers only (never had an Azure account / 12-mo free services). Individual or org. 12-month free services NOT available to customers signing up directly for P2P in China and India. Country/region is locked at sign-up and cannot be changed later.
How to claim
1) Go to azure.microsoft.com/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account. 2) Choose 'Azure free account'. 3) Complete the 3-step account setup (profile, billing address, payment card). 4) A spending limit is on by default; you immediately get $200/30 days + 12-mo free amounts. 5) To keep free services after 30 days, remove the spending limit (convert to P2P) — only usage above the free amounts is then charged.
Expiry
$200 credit expires at the end of day 30. The 12-month free-service amounts end at 12 months (the 65+ 'always-free' services continue). Inactive accounts (>90 days) may have compute resources removed with 30 days' notice.
Gotchas
Credit card IS required at signup (temporary $1 authorization; non-prepaid card). It is a trial-then-pay offer, not a perpetual free tier. Services not on the free list (notably Azure OpenAI/LLM tokens, and egress) bill immediately. Unused $200 is lost. Free quantities don't roll over month to month.
