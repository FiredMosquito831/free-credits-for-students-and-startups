Google Cloud Free Trial — $300 welcome credit (90 days) high confidence

New customers get $300 free credit, valid 90 days from Free Trial billing account creation. Credit covers any product including overages beyond Free Tier limits; it does not roll over (expires 90 days from signup even if you upgrade to paid). Free Trial billing account auto-closes when the $300 is spent or 90 days pass unless you manually upgrade to a Paid billing account (then you keep remaining credit until the 90-day mark and keep Free Tier access). Eligibility: you must have NEVER been a paying user of Google Cloud, Google Maps Platform, or Firebase, and never signed up for the Free Trial before (one per person/billing profile). GPUs, Windows servers and some features are not covered. During trial you are not billed — only if you manually upgrade to paid do charges begin. 30-day data-export window after the trial ends. Compute Engine trial restricted to 8 concurrent cores.

Eligibility
Anyone, any country with GCP access (no .edu or startup status needed). Credit card/other payment method REQUIRED at signup for identity verification (Google may run a pending authorization, not a charge). US/Mexico government entities excluded.
How to claim
Sign up at cloud.google.com (Get started for free) -> add card -> $300 credit is preloaded into the Free Trial billing account automatically; spend over 90 days on any product incl. Vertex AI/Gemini API; optionally click Upgrade in console to keep credit alive beyond trial features. Do NOT click 'Activate a paid account' unless you want charges.
Expiry
90 days from signup or until $300 is exhausted, whichever first; unused credit does not survive account closure. Free Tier usage continues if you convert to a paid account.
Gotchas
Trial vs Free Tier: the $300 is for trial billing only; Free Tier allowances are separate, not charged against it. CC required but you are only charged if you manually upgrade to paid. Egress, third-party models (e.g. non-Google LLMs on Vertex) cost above free allowances. 30-day migration window after end; GCE resources may be deleted and are not recoverable.
