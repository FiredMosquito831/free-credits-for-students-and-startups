1) Go to github.com/education/students (or education.github.com/pack). 2) Start an application under Education benefits settings in your GitHub profile. 3) Submit proof of enrollment; approval lands in the GitHub Education portal. 4) Claim Microsoft offers (Azure $100 credit 18+, etc.) from the pack page.
Expiry
Valid while student status holds (revalidated every 2 years; the pack is typically held until end of 4th year of study). Individual partner offers have their own durations (e.g., Heroku 24 months, GitKraken 6 months + 80% off).
Gotchas
Offers are partner-controlled and change or drop out (see changelog: DigitalOcean's $200 credit sunset July 31, 2026; Bump.sh offer ended Dec 9, 2025; 1Password expanded to 2 years in Oct 2026). The $100 Azure credit is the SAME offer as Azure for Students (claim one, not both — one per person). Copilot student entitlements 'are evolving in 2026' per the official discussion; check the live allowance on activation. This is a GitHub program — included here because the Microsoft offers (Azure credit, and DFTT-tied tooling) flow through it.
