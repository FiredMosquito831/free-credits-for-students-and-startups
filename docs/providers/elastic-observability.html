Elastic Observability: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Elastic Cloud 14-day free trial (Observability incl. logs, APM, metrics, traces, RUM, synthetics/uptime). 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Elastic Cloud 14-day free trial (Observability incl. logs, APM, metrics, traces, RUM, synthetics/uptime) high confidence

14-day trial of Elastic Cloud (Hosted and Serverless) covering the full Elastic Observability suite: log monitoring, APM, infrastructure monitoring, AIOps/generative AI, digital experience monitoring (RUM, synthetics, uptime), OpenTelemetry, plus AutoOps. Official pricing FAQ states the trial 'includes a cluster with 8GB of RAM and 240GB of storage'; the Observability trial page says 'Get up to 360GB of storage during your trial' (figures differ between pages). No credit card required to sign up; after the trial you only pay if you add a card, pay-per-use with no long-term commitments.

Eligibility
Anyone, any email — no .edu, no company stage, no funding cap, no region limit (deploys in AWS/Azure/GCP regions; note sign-ups routed through Azure/GCP marketplaces get the 7-day marketplace trial instead).
How to claim
1) Sign up at cloud.elastic.co (email/Google/Microsoft), 2) choose a cloud provider, 3) create a deployment — up and running in <5 minutes. Trial clock starts when the first cluster is created.
Expiry
14 days from cluster creation. No extension or renewal; deployments/data persist afterward but using them beyond the trial requires adding a credit card.
Gotchas
Trial is not free-forever: post-trial, adding a card converts you to a paid subscription and billing begins. Official pages disagree on storage cap (240GB in pricing FAQ vs 'up to 360GB' on trial page) — actual limits may vary by provider/region. Azure/GCP-marketplace sign-ups get a 7-day trial, not 14.
Source
elastic.co

AWS Marketplace / GCP Marketplace 7-day trials (no credit card to Elastic) medium confidence

Subscribing via AWS Marketplace gives a 7-day free trial (single deployment, not charged for first 7 days) during which you automatically get an Enterprise-level subscription; you pay Elastic via your AWS bill with no separate card to Elastic. GCP Marketplace: 7-day trial, one deployment plus three projects; usage-based billing starts automatically after 7 days; one Elastic Cloud org per GCP billing account; if you unsubscribe, the trial ends immediately and cannot be resumed even after resubscribing.

Eligibility
Anyone with an AWS or GCP account (billing account used for the marketplace purchase). No .edu or startup requirement.
How to claim
AWS: Elastic Cloud on AWS Marketplace > View purchase options > sign in > use; billing starts automatically after day 7 unless you delete deployments. GCP: subscribe on the GCP Marketplace Elastic page, link/create an Elastic Cloud account, verify email; delete resources to stop charges.
Expiry
7 days; automatic conversion to usage-based billing on the marketplace bill (Elastic Cloud billed through AWS/GCP, egress/snapshot charges metered in US East for AWS).
Gotchas
Billing starts automatically after 7 days — delete deployments to avoid charges. These are separate from the 14-day direct trial; a marketplace subscription replaces/links to the trial account, it does not extend it. AWS Marketplace charges metered in us-east-1 regardless of deployment region.
Source
elastic.co

Self-managed Elastic Stack (Elasticsearch/Logstash/Kibana/Beats) — free 'Basic' license, perpetual high confidence

The Elastic Stack (Elasticsearch, Kibana, Beats, Logstash) can be downloaded and run for free, forever: 'the OSS features as well as the free Basic features of our software at no cost', including native security features, role-based access control, and true Kibana multitenancy. Elastic explicitly says it is free 'to learn, to develop, and to run production at scale'. No trial, no credit card, no cap. (Note: since the 2021 re-licensing, the stack is under Elastic License v2 / SSPL rather than Apache-2.0, and 'free' = the Basic feature set.)

Eligibility
Anyone, including students and startups; no .edu, no region or usage limit.
How to claim
Download the Elastic Stack from elastic.co/downloads and self-host on your own infrastructure (containers, VMs, on-prem).
Expiry
Perpetual — no expiry, no renewal; free Basic features have no stated time limit.
Gotchas
'Free ELK' ≠ full feature set: APM scale-out, many log-scaling/security (SIEM/XDR) and ML capabilities sit in paid Gold/Platinum/Enterprise tiers; Basic has no vendor support. Budget for your own infrastructure and operations; self-hosting is free but not costless. License is ELv2/SSPL, not classic Apache OSS.
Source
elastic.co

Elastic Cloud Serverless: built-in free allowances (50 GB/month egress, 10k free workflow/agent executions) with 14-day trial — no perpetual free tier high confidence

There is no free-forever tier for Elastic Cloud Serverless; instead the pay-as-you-go plans include standing free allowances: 50 GB free egress per month (then $0.05/GB), 10,000 free workflow executions (then ~$0.0108/execution), and 10,000 free Agent Builder executions (then ~$0.025/execution). Below that, logs/traces ingest starts ~$0.07/GB, metrics ingest ~$0.023–0.09/GB, retention ~$0.005–0.019/GB-month. Higher support tiers add 5–15% of the bill. A 14-day free trial of Serverless is also available (serverless-registration); uptime monitoring in production is paid: synthetic browser tests $0.0123/test run, lightweight ping $28.00/location/month.

Eligibility
Anyone with a free Elastic Cloud account; no .edu or startup requirement; pay-as-you-go with no minimum commitment. New serverless metrics pricing effective July 1, 2026.
How to claim
Sign up via cloud.elastic.co/serverless-registration (or a 14-day trial), create a serverless project; free allowances apply automatically each month — no voucher or form.
Expiry
Free allowances recur monthly (egress allowance resets per billing month). Trial itself is 14 days; allowances have no stated sunset.
Gotchas
Not a free tier — every GB ingested/retained above the free egress/execution allowances bills automatically. Uptime monitoring is essentially not free at scale (ping locations are the costliest add-on at $28/location/month). No credit card needed for trial, but continuing serverless usage beyond the trial requires billing info.
Source
elastic.co

Up to $5,000 Elastic Cloud credits via AWS Activate (self-founded startups: $1,000) — not offered on elastic.co itself medium confidence

Elastic's startup credits are distributed through AWS Activate, not Elastic's own site: 'Up to $5,000 in Elastic Cloud credits for AWS Activate members' covering search, observability (logs, metrics, traces) and security, incl. serverless; a separate channel line lists '$1,000 in Elastic Cloud credits for Self Founded Startups' (per Superscout, last checked 2026-09-29; the $5K figure was confirmed in an official AWS Startups LinkedIn post, 2026-09-01, describing August 2026 Activate vendor offers).

Eligibility
Startups: AWS Activate membership (funded/accelerator path for up to $5K; 'Self Founded' path for $1K). No .edu requirement. Application-based, not open to everyone.
How to claim
Apply through the AWS Activate Perks page (aws.amazon.com/startups/perks, now serving at startups.aws.com) — the Elastic entry requires an application through the AWS channel.
Expiry
No published expiration; perk amounts/terms set by AWS Elastic partnership (aggregator last-checked dates: 2026-09-29).
Gotchas
Elastic's own site publishes no startup program (elastic.co/startups is a 404; StartupPerks verified 2026-10-07 that the $5,000 figure appears only on third-party lists). The AWS Perks page returned 404 to direct fetches (likely JS-rendered), so exact current amounts should be re-verified on the AWS page or via AWS startup support before relying on them. Credits apply to Elastic Cloud (not self-managed licenses).
Source
superscout.co

Elastic education/training: free self-paced courses and a free GenAI Associate exam (no .edu product licenses, no exam vouchers found) medium confidence

Elastic's training hub (elastic.co/training) offers 'Learn Elastic for free' self-paced modules at no cost (e.g., 'Getting started: What is Elastic?', 'RAG foundation', 'Semantic search foundation', 'Intro to MCP with Elasticsearch MCP Server'). The 'GenAI Associate Accreditation Exam' is listed with a direct free 'Take exam' link. Instructor-led courses (Elastic Observability Engineer, Elasticsearch Engineer, Kibana Data Analysis, Security for SIEM) carry no visible student discount or voucher on the page; standard certification exam pricing and voucher programs are not published on the site. There is no .edu-based product licensing program found — students get the same free Basic self-hosted stack and 14-day trial as everyone else.

Eligibility
Open to anyone; no .edu email, no regional limit, no cost for the free modules/exam. No student-specific tiers or discounts found.
How to claim
Browse elastic.co/training, click 'Start module' for free courses; 'Take exam' link on the GenAI Associate exam; register a free account if prompted.
Expiry
Free modules are standing; the free GenAI exam is promotional and may change without notice. No product offer has expiry.
Gotchas
Free exam appears limited to the GenAI Associate accreditation — other certifications (Engineer-level) are paid; don't assume all exams are free. No academic software license exists; 'education license' for Elastic Cloud does not appear anywhere on elastic.co as of 2026-10-22.

elastic.co

2025–26 changes

  • 1) Elastic's own 'Elastic for Startups' program (announced ~2021–2022: Elastic Cloud credits + developer-advocate support, targeting seed/pre-Series A) no longer exists on elastic.co — /startups returns 404 and a 2026-10-07 check of elastic.co (StartupPerks) found no startup program in Elastic's sitemaps; the 'up to $5,000' figure surviving in aggregators traces to the AWS Activate channel, not to any Elastic-published program. The current official route for startup credits is AWS Activate (up to $5K funded / $1K self-founded, per AWS Startups' own 2026-09-01 announcement). 2) No perpetual free cloud tier ever existed for Elastic Cloud — the only standing free offers are the 14-day trial, marketplace 7-day trials, serverless free allowances (egress/executions), and the free self-hosted Basic license. 3) Pricing-page changes in the 2025–2026 window: Elastic Cloud Serverless is now GA (AWS/GCP/Azure) with pay-as-you-go or prepaid models, and new serverless metrics pricing took effect July 1, 2026; the classic 'Essentials' entry tier is no longer listed — current subscription tiers are Standard/Gold/Platinum/Enterprise. 4) Trial terms stable: still 14 days, no credit card required. 5) Gotcha for students specifically: Elastic does not publish any .edu verification, academic license, or student voucher program — students and startups get the same offers as general consumers plus the AWS Activate credit channel for startups.
