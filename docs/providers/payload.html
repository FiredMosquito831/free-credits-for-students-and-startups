Payload: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Self-hosted open-source (MIT) — free forever, no usage limits. 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Self-hosted open-source (MIT) — free forever, no usage limits high confidence

Official pricing page states: 'Payload is open source and MIT licensed. Deploy it anywhere you can run a Node.js app — for free, forever.' The core framework, admin UI, and local development are completely free with no seat caps, record limits, or feature paywalls. Requirements: any JS package manager (pnpm preferred), Node.js 20.9.0+, and any compatible database (MongoDB, Postgres, or SQLite). FAQ on the pricing page confirms: 'Payload remains a self-hosted solution, so you can bring your app to any hosting provider you prefer.'

Eligibility
Anyone — no .edu email, no startup age or funding cap, no credit card, no region limits stated. MIT license permits commercial use. Post-acquisition FAQ: 'Figma is committed to maintaining and investing in Payload's open source core.'
How to claim
Run `npx create-payload-app` to scaffold; deploy to any Node.js host (VPS, serverless, container). No signup, no trial period, no CC.
Expiry
None — MIT license is perpetual; offer has no sunset date as of 2026-10-22.
Gotchas
Software is free but you pay for infrastructure: server/VPS, database (or bring Mongo/Postgres/SQLite), media storage, and egress bandwidth. Paid 'Enterprise Features' (SSO, AI auto-embedding, publishing workflows, visual editor, static A/B testing, enterprise AI) are gated behind the Enterprise tier and not in the free core. Support is community-only on the free track.
Source
payloadcms.com

Official instant-deploy templates: Vercel and Cloudflare (ride on the hosts' free tiers) medium confidence

The pricing page lists two one-click deploys of the official Payload website template: Vercel (Vercel blob storage + Neon database, Users auth, Media, Layout Builder, Live Preview) and Cloudflare (R2 image storage + D1 database, Users auth, Media, Layout Builder). Each is a Deploy button that lands you on the host's own free tier, making a real-world Payload app free within provider limits.

Eligibility
Anyone with a Vercel or Cloudflare account; no .edu, no funding cap. CC policy follows the host provider. Cloudflare free tier has historically not required a CC; Vercel Hobby likewise.
How to claim
Click Deploy on payloadcms.com/get-started, log in to Vercel/Cloudflare, and the template provisions automatically. Free within each provider's free-tier limits.
Expiry
Tied to provider free tiers; no Payload-side expiry.
Gotchas
Provider limits apply and I did not re-verify exact free-tier numbers (Vercel build minutes/function seconds; Cloudflare requests/day, D1 storage, egress) today — check current host pricing. Free DB/storage tiers have hard caps; overages bill the host account. Media on free tiers may be small (GBs, not TBs).
Source
payloadcms.com

Payload Cloud / Enterprise Free Trial (time-limited, not a free tier) medium confidence

Cloud terms of service §1.5: 'Free Trials are offered for a limited period of time,' license is 'internal evaluation purposes only,' provided 'as-is,' and 'Customer may be required to enter payment information in order to sign up for a Free Trial' with no charge 'until the Free Trial has expired'; use after expiry is prohibited unless a paid Subscription is purchased. No duration, feature scope, or student/startup-specific trial is published. Cloud subscriptions are billed monthly in advance; Enterprise-tier quarterly; fees non-cancellable, non-refundable; auto-renew with renewal avoided only by deleting the project before the next period.

Eligibility
Anyone applying for the cloud/enterprise service; no .edu requirement, no startup cap found. CC may be required for trial signup.
How to claim
Request via 'Schedule a Demo' / 'Talk to Sales' on payloadcms.com; trial is granted at Payload's discretion ('limited period').
Expiry
Unstated trial window; subscription auto-renews at end of each period.
Gotchas
Trial ≠ free tier: after it ends the service is paid-only; CC may be required; no refunds ever; overages beyond usage caps charged at then-current rates; mid-period downgrades take effect next renewal and may lose content/features.
Source
payloadcms.com

Self-hosted Payload under the MIT license (free forever) high confidence

$0 license fee. The official pricing page (payloadcms.com/get-started) says: 'Payload is open source and MIT licensed. Deploy it anywhere you can run a Node.js app — for free, forever.' Stated requirements: Node.js 20.9.0+ and MongoDB, Postgres, or SQLite. The LICENSE.md on the main branch of github.com/payloadcms/payload reads 'MIT License' with 'Copyright (c) 2018-2026 Payload CMS, LLC'. No user, site, storage, or time limit is stated for the self-hosted software.

Eligibility
Anyone. No .edu email, startup age or funding cap, or region rule appears on the official pages. No credit card or sign-up is described for the self-hosted path. Students and startups get the same terms as everyone else.
How to claim
Scaffold with npx create-payload-app (the command shown on payloadcms.com), or clone github.com/payloadcms/payload. Run the app on your own Node.js 20.9.0+ host with MongoDB, Postgres, or SQLite.
Expiry
None stated ('forever'). Figma's June 2025 post says open source remains.
Gotchas
Free covers the software only. Hosting, database, file storage (S3, Azure Blob, Google Cloud Storage, Vercel Blob, and Uploadthing are named as options), bandwidth, and egress are your costs, and Payload's pages give no figures for them. Enterprise (SSO, visual editor, audit logs, publishing workflows, multi-player editing) is paid, unpriced online, and reached only through 'Schedule a Demo'. It has no trial and no startup or education discount. payload.com is an unrelated payments company, so use payloadcms.com.
Source
payloadcms.com

Payload Cloud 14-day free trial (new sign-ups, 2023) low confidence

The July 5, 2023 post announcing that Payload Cloud left beta says 'Free for 14 Days (new signups only)' and 'New sign-ups will have a 14-day trial period.' The Payload Cloud terms (payloadcms.com/cloud-terms) define 'Free Trials' as access to the hosted Service 'free of charge for a limited period of time' and give no length. The 2023 post names no Cloud plans, prices, or resource limits.

Eligibility
New sign-ups only, per the 2023 post. No student, startup, or region eligibility is stated.
How to claim
Sign up for Payload Cloud and choose the trial. The signup flow was not reached or verified in this research.
Expiry
14 days, per the 2023 post. No current expiry is published. Current availability is unconfirmed: the pricing page (/get-started) lists no Cloud plan or trial, only Free (self-hosted) and Enterprise.
Gotchas
The cloud terms say users 'may be required to enter payment information' to start a Free Trial, so a card may be needed. No credits were found. Paid Cloud prices after the trial are unverified. Figma's June 2025 post does not mention Cloud. Do not assume the trial is still open.
Source
payloadcms.com
