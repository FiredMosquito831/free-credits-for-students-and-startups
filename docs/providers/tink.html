Tink: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free Tink Console account + sandbox with Demo Bank (free developer tier). 3 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free Tink Console account + sandbox with Demo Bank (free developer tier) high confidence

Free self-serve developer account at console.tink.com (email + verification, no credit card, no pricing at signup). 'By default, all our products are available to test in our sandbox environment. You can access it by creating a sandbox app.' Demo Bank is a simulated bank with test users per market, so no real bank credentials are needed. FAQ confirms: 'you are welcome to create a free account to try Tink products with test data in a sandbox environment before upgrading to an Enterprise account' and 'create a free account at console.tink.com, where you can use test data to try our platform.' Demo Bank markets cover 18 countries (AT, BE, CZ, DK, EE, FI, FR, DE, IE, IT, LV, NL, NO, PT, PL, ES, SE, UK); the supported demo-provider list was reduced on 31 Oct 2023 and is a fixed list since. No public call/volume limits or expiry are stated; FAQ notes 4 background data refreshes every 24h platform-wide. Public demo of Account Check/Transactions at demo.tink.com. Production/live-bank access is Enterprise: custom, sales-gated pricing plus a 'Know Your Business' process; pre-production apps that use your own real bank credentials are production-type apps and fall under the same sales-gated access.

Eligibility
Open to any individual/company with an email address; no .edu requirement, no startup age/funding cap, no region limit on the account itself (Demo Bank markets are European). Credit card NOT required. Students can sign up and use it; Tink makes no student-specific promise.
How to claim
Go to console.tink.com/signup, create an account, verify the email, log in, create a sandbox app (app type is fixed at creation; one sandbox app per product recommended), pull API credentials under App settings > API client. In Console select Demo Bank, pick a Product and a Market, use the named test users (each user = a test case, e.g. User 1 = success path; Sweden requires SSN-style credentials). Copy the Tink Link URL and test end-to-end. To use live bank data: contact sales, complete the 'Know Your Business' process; Tink creates/configures your production app.
Expiry
No stated expiration or renewal on the free Console/sandbox account; it persists while the account exists. Demo Bank provider list frozen since 31 Oct 2023 (only a Tink announcement changes it).
Gotchas
Sandbox = simulated bank/test data only: you cannot pull live account data on the free account. 'Pre-production' apps using real bank credentials are production apps -> Enterprise-gated. App type cannot be changed after creation. No published rate/egress limits for the free tier, so don't assume unbounded production-style volume; there is no pay-per-use option at all (FAQ: 'No'). Client secrets are one-time (up to 5 per app). Tink docs say '3000+ connections in 18 countries' while the homepage claims 6000 connections - marketing variance, check the current coverage matrix per market.
Source
tink.com

Visa Innovation Program Europe - Nordics & Baltics (VIPE), startup program medium confidence

Visa's open-innovation startup program (run with Tenity), not a Tink product-level program - included here because the provider front is 'Tink (Visa)' and the program hands selected fintechs 'Visa's APIs via the Visa Developer Platform', mentorship, investor networks, and regional bank partnerships. Free to join: 'no Company equity requirement' and no participation fee is stated anywhere; there is also no direct funding amount for participants. Season 1 (Nordics & Baltics): applications opened 6 Oct 2025; first cohort of 8 fintechs (Beneflo, Enable Banking, Kardio, Juuli, Ledyer, Betalo, Beep, Myver) announced at the Nordic Banking Forum, Helsinki, on 4 Dec 2025; ~6-8 month program running to showcase at Baltic Fintech Days, Riga, in May 2026. Program focus: B2B money movement, embedded payments. AS OF 2026-10-22: Season 1 is concluded (8-month window from Dec 2025 ends ~Aug 2026; Tenity's page shows news through 22 Sep 2026 with no second-season announcement).

Eligibility
Startups only, not students: 'fintechs based in the Nordics & Baltics with a strong market presence' (Sweden, Finland, Norway, Iceland, Denmark, Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia). Competitive/selection-based - Season 1 picked 8 companies; no published company-age or funding cap. No .edu path, no self-serve access: it is a program, not a free tier.
How to claim
Application was via Tenity's VIPE Nordics & Baltics page (tenity.com); per directory listings the 2025 deadline was 27 Oct 2025 (missed - program since concluded). To re-engage: watch tenity.com/cases/visa-innovation-program-europe/ for a second season announcement, or use the free Visa Developer sandbox in the meantime.
Expiry
Season 1 applications: opened 6 Oct 2025, deadline ~27 Oct 2025 (per Startup Lithuania directory; Tenity's own page states no explicit deadline). Program window ran Dec 2025 - May 2026. No Season 2 application window announced as of 22 Oct 2026.
Gotchas
Selection-based, so not a guaranteed free offering; 'access to Visa's APIs' means Visa Developer Platform APIs, i.e. the same free sandbox any developer gets, plus pilot/mentorship value - the program's worth is network/POC, not API freebies. Equity-free but also no funding; benefits are partnership-led. Status uncertainty: an announcement page does not track closures.
Source
tenity.com

Visa Developer Center free sandbox (parent-level free tier) high confidence

Free developer account and sandbox at developer.visa.com: 'Sandbox access is free. Fees apply only when you move a project to Certification or Production and sign a Visa Developer agreement.' Includes mock data, sample code, SDKs, and an onboarding dashboard; credentials expire periodically and must be renewed. No trial credits or dollar-based free tier - the 'free' is the entire sandbox environment. Some product APIs are gated behind a 'Request access' form even within a project.

Eligibility
Anyone (developer or company) can create the free account; email + verification only, no credit card at this stage, no .edu or startup requirement, no published region limit on the account (product availability is regional - check regional availability before picking products). Tink-specific note: this is the parent (Visa) dev platform, distinct from Tink's own Console/Demo Bank open-banking sandbox.
How to claim
Register at developer.visa.com ('Create your free account' + email verification), add a project, select products (some require 'Request access' approval), read Sandbox credentials (Two-Way SSL / X-Pay tokens), run the Hello World sample code. Moving to Certification/Production requires requesting access and signing a paid Visa Developer agreement.
Expiry
No stated expiry on sandbox access; SSL certificates/tokens expire and are renewed in the dashboard.
Gotchas
Certification/Production are paid and contract-gated; don't build production assumptions on sandbox behavior. It's Visa-level, not Tink-level: Tink's open-banking products use Tink Console, Visa's payments APIs use this center - useful only if the 'Tink (Visa)' front means the whole Visa dev umbrella.
Source
developer.visa.com
