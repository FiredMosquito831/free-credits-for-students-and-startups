Xata: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Xata Open Source (self-hosted, free forever). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Xata Open Source (self-hosted, free forever) high confidence

Xata's core Postgres branching platform is open source under Apache 2.0 (github.com/xataio). Self-hosting it on your own infrastructure is $0 platform fee and includes copy-on-write branching, scale-to-zero (wake-up ~10s), OpenEBS/Mayastor storage and community support. It does NOT include managed observability, SOC 2/GDPR compliance, multi-region, or dedicated support — you operate your own cluster. This is the only PERMANENT free path; there is no permanent free hosted/managed tier.

Eligibility
Anyone; no .edu email, no startup age/funding cap, no credit card, no region restriction. Requires you to provision and operate your own infrastructure (Kubernetes + CloudNativePG + OpenEBS). Self-managed.
How to claim
Free forever, no trial clock, no CC required. Clone the xataio GitHub org and deploy on your own cluster to use the platform at $0.
Expiry
None (permanent, Apache 2.0 open source).
Gotchas
It is self-hosted, not a managed service: you carry all ops burden, no multi-region, no SOC 2/HIPAA compliance tooling, no 24/7 support, and wake-up is ~10s (vs ~1s on managed Xata Cloud). The managed convenience (SOC 2, multi-region, dedicated support) requires paid Xata Cloud or BYOC.
Source
xata.io

Xata Cloud 14-day free trial (managed) high confidence

The fully managed Xata Cloud (SOC 2/GDPR, multi-region on AWS+GCP) is offered as a 14-day free trial with NO credit card required during the trial. After the trial you must add a credit card to continue. Post-trial billing is pure usage: from $0.012/hr for a micro instance (<=2 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, ~$9/mo) up to $1.536/hr (32 vCPU, 128 GB), plus $0.28/GB/month storage billed by the minute. No per-branch or per-user fees; scale-to-zero means idle branches cost no compute.

Eligibility
Anyone who signs up at console.xata.io; email required, no credit card during the 14-day trial, no .edu or startup proof. A credit card IS required to keep using it after the trial. Managed Xata Cloud currently runs on AWS us-east-1 and eu-central-1 (third-party-reported), so US/EU regions apply.
How to claim
Start the 14-day managed trial at console.xata.io; no card needed to begin. Add a card afterward to convert to pay-as-you-go.
Expiry
14 days, then pay-as-you-go.
Gotchas
This is a trial, not a free tier: the clock runs and a card is mandatory after 14 days. After the trial you still pay for compute hours plus $0.28/GB/mo storage — a side-project database that wakes up frequently keeps accruing cost, and idle branches still incur the storage line. The 'no CC required' promise applies only to the trial window. The docs (n8n quickstart) confirm: 'To keep using Xata after the free trial, add a credit card in the billing system.'
Source
xata.io

2025–26 changes

  • Major 2025–2026 sunset/change: the permanent free hosted tier that students/startups relied on is GONE. Xata's original product was a hosted 'serverless data platform' (spreadsheet-style UI, REST/TypeScript data API, full-text search, file attachments over Postgres) that shipped a permanent free tier with 15 GB of storage, no cold starts and no pausing. Sunset timeline: (1) On January 10, 2025 Xata pulled the search and files APIs out of the free plan; (2) in May 2025 Xata announced a rebuilt product centered on vanilla, unmodified Postgres with copy-on-write branching and data anonymization (pgstream) — the old platform was briefly renamed 'Xata Lite' and now returns HTTP 410 Gone at lite.xata.io; (3) in late 2025 Xata open-sourced its core under Apache 2.0, and the permanent free hosted tier was replaced by 'self-host the open-source version' plus a 14-day Xata Cloud trial (with a reported $100 signup credit) and no permanent free cloud plan. Consequence: if you used the old Xata free tier for a managed database, it no longer exists — the only permanent-free option is self-hosted open source; the managed path is the 14-day trial then pay-as-you-go ($0.012/hr + $0.28/GB/mo) or BYOC (quote-based). Note: WebSearch budget was exhausted mid-task, so verification relied on the official pricing page + pricing.md + n8n docs read today plus multiple corroborating 2026 third-party trackers; the $100 credit could not be confirmed on the live official page.
