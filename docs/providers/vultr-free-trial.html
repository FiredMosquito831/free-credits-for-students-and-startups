Vultr Free Trial: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: New-account trial credits (up to 30 days, amount promo-tied). 8 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

New-account trial credits (up to 30 days, amount promo-tied) high confidence

Official docs state 'Vultr offers free credits for up to 30 days for you to try our services.' No dollar amount is published in official docs; the amount is tied to the promo link used (e.g. try100 = $100). Payment method required for verification, but card linking works with $0.00 deposit — no prepay forced. Credit card NOT required in the strict sense: linking only, $0 charge (temporary auth hold possible). Applies to all products (trial covers general services; GPU applicability not officially stated).

Eligibility
New accounts only; valid email; payment-method verification required; no region limit stated; no .edu or startup requirement
How to claim
Register at vultr.com (promo/try100 links auto-attach trial credit), verify email, add payment method — a credit card can be linked with the 'I just want to link my credit card - $0.00 deposit' option (bank may place a temporary auth hold), optionally apply a promo coupon in Billing > Summary, accept ToS. Deprovision resources before credits expire to avoid charges.
Expiry
Credits valid up to 30 days from registration (per docs 'up to 30 days'); unused credit is forfeited at expiry; no renewal possible on the same account
Gotchas
Amount not officially fixed — whatever the promo link grants (commonly $100, promos $200-$300). Credit is not a spending cap: running instances keep billing after credit runs out or the 30 days elapse. Stopped (not destroyed) instances still accrue charges. Data egress beyond plan allowance, snapshots, and extra IPs bill extra. Duplicate accounts not eligible. The exact dollar amount for a bare try100 signup is not stated on the official docs page.
Source
docs.vultr.com

Promo signup links (try100 / try250 trial pages) medium confidence

Vultr runs per-page signup promos: https://www.vultr.com/promo/try100/ (historically $100 credit) and https://www.vultr.com/promo/try250 ($250). Both pages were live/indexed as of late 2026 (try250 confirmed in Oct 2026 crawl of vultr.com, now listing 34 regions). The promo page returns 403 to automated fetchers, so the exact on-page credit figure couldn't be read directly today; amount is tied to the link and can change.

Eligibility
New Vultr customers only; duplicate accounts excluded; payment method required; no .edu or startup age requirement
How to claim
Sign up through the specific promo page (vultr.com/promo/try100 or /try250) instead of the plain signup; credit attaches to the new account and expires ~30 days after registration; no coupon code needed.
Expiry
Trial credit valid 30 days after registration (consistent with docs 'up to 30 days')
Gotchas
Promo credit amount varies by link and over time; a copied old link may attach a different amount. CC needed for verification ($0-deposit card link allowed). Verify the actual credit in Billing before deploying. Non-stackable with other promos.
Source
vultr.com

Official coupon codes: $200 / $250 / $300 trial credits, VULTRMATCH, 50% off medium confidence

Vultr's official coupons page (Oct 2026 listings confirmed by a 2026-10-06 check): FLYTWOHUNDRED = $200; 250VULTRFLY = $250; FLY300VULTR = $300; VULTRMATCH = match first deposit dollar-for-dollar up to $100 (credit expires 12 months after issuance; applied 50/50 — $5 promo + $5 real per $10 of hourly spend); 50off page = 50% off first two invoices (invoices generated on the 1st monthly). New customers only; duplicate accounts not eligible; offers non-combinable ('limited time').

Eligibility
New customers only; valid credit card or PayPal for account verification; no .edu or startup requirement; no stated region limit
How to claim
Create a new account, then apply the code in Billing > Summary (or on the promo landing page). VULTRMATCH: make a first deposit (minimum deposit required by the page); credits auto-match 50/50. 50off: just sign up new and the discount applies to first two invoices. Check the exact code list at vultr.com/coupons before use.
Expiry
Standard $200/$250/$300 trial credits: ~30 days. VULTRMATCH match credit: 12 months. 50% discount: first two monthly invoices only. Promos explicitly 'limited time'.
Gotchas
The coupons page itself is bot-blocked (403) — codes rotate; verify which are live at vultr.com/coupons. Larger credit usually means longer minimum deposit or tighter product eligibility. 50off is a discount, not free credit. If card verification is denied, a small deposit (~$5) may be required to unlock credits.
Source
vultr.com

Free Tier Program — free 1vCPU/512MB/10GB SSD instance high confidence

Genuinely free instance (not a trial): 1 vCPU / 512 MB RAM / 10 GB SSD / 1 IPv4. Available only in Miami, Seattle, and Frankfurt data centers. 'Anyone is eligible to apply'; admission is randomized using a per-account weighted score computed during identity/usage validation; limited quantities, rolled out in phases. No hard dates or end date published; no duration limit on the instance itself is stated (adjustments made 'as we go').

Eligibility
Open to anyone (no .edu, no funding cap, no age limit stated); real-person validation required (anti-bot); credit card NOT required; region availability limited to Miami/Seattle/Frankfurt only
How to claim
Log in or sign up at vultr.com, apply for early access on the Free Tier Program page (vultr.com/free-tier-program/); provide personal info and intended use to raise weighted score; wait for email/console admission notice; deploy under Cloud Compute > Regular > Free Instance.
Expiry
No expiry stated; program is phase-rolled and 'fine-tuned as we go' — instances could be affected by program changes; no renewal concept (it is a permanent free instance for admitted accounts)
Gotchas
Admission is not guaranteed (randomized + weighted score); you simply may not be admitted — Vultr does not say why. Free tier instance is a 512MB/1vCPU/10GB plan, not the full product lineup. Availability in just 3 regions. Verify the instance still appears on the deploy page (Cloud Compute > Regular > Free Instance); admission notified by email + console banner.
Source
vultr.com

Vultr Digital Start-up Program (up to $100K credits + 35% discounts) high confidence

Up to $100K in cloud credits plus up to 35% long-term discounts, executive sponsorship, dedicated account management, architecture reviews, and priority support. Eligibility: must have secured recent Series A, B, C, D, or E financing (no company-age threshold published). Application requires: senior-most technical executive contact (CTO), senior-most cloud-infrastructure lead contact, last 6 months of cloud-compute invoices, and agreement to be publicly referenceable as a program member. No program duration, renewal, or product exclusions are published on the page. Read directly from the official page today.

Eligibility
Startups with recent Series A–E funding (institutional VC backing implied); registered company; no .edu or small-startup/seed stage allowed — post-Series A only
How to claim
Submit the application on the Vultr Discover startup program page (Apply Now form / Join Now link) with company details, CTO and cloud-infra-lead contacts, 6 months of cloud-compute invoices, Series A–E proof, and public-reference consent; Vultr evaluates and activates credits on acceptance.
Expiry
No credit validity period published; 'long-term' discounts imply a commitment; subject to Vultr internal approval
Gotchas
'Up to' amounts — actual credit set case by case. Requires public referenceability and sharing 6 months of invoices (operational transparency). Not self-service: evaluation by a Vultr team. No stated end date, but approval is discretionary.
Source
discover.vultr.com
