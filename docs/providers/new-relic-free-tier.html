New Relic Free Tier: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Perpetual Free Tier (all accounts; no .edu or startup requirement). 3 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Perpetual Free Tier (all accounts; no .edu or startup requirement) high confidence

Free forever plan verified on newrelic.com/pricing as of 2026-10-22: 100 GB data ingest/month (account-wide, not per user), 1 full-platform user with access to all 50+ platform capabilities (APM with distributed tracing, infrastructure monitoring, error tracking, digital experience monitoring), unlimited basic users (view custom non-quickstart dashboards, query, alert), 500 synthetic checks/month, unlimited hosts/agents/containers/devices/AWS Fargate tasks/cloud functions, default data retention at least 8 days, default log obfuscation. Explicitly no data egress or hydration charges. Overage: if you exceed 100 GB in a month, data ingest ceases and you lose platform access until you upgrade or the 1st of the following month (auto re-enables). Paid contrast for context: Pro full user $349/user annual or $418.80/month; Core user $49; overage $0.40/GB standard or $0.60/GB 'Data Plus' add-on; EU data-center storage +$0.05/GB/month.

Eligibility
Anyone: no .edu email, no startup age/funding cap, no credit card required ('No credit card required to start' per pricing FAQ), no stated region limits.
How to claim
1) Create a free New Relic account at newrelic.com (no credit card). 2) Free tier is active immediately with the 100 GB/mo + 1 full user + 500 synthetic checks. 3) Overage suspends ingest until the 1st or an upgrade — never billed.
Expiry
Perpetual; no expiry date. Monthly limits reset on the 1st of each month.
Gotchas
Exceeding 100 GB halts ingest AND platform access for the month (not a bill, but you go dark). Only 1 full-platform user is free — team collaboration relies on unlimited basic users (restricted access). The '10,000 synthetic monitors' figure that appears in a pay-as-you-go FAQ is a different (paid-edition) benefit, not the free 500 checks. No separate time-boxed free trial is advertised — the perpetual tier is the trial replacement.
Source
newrelic.com

New Relic for Students (education program) high confidence

Verified on newrelic.com/students as of 2026-10-22: 500 GB data ingest per month and 3 full-platform users (vs. 100 GB / 1 user on the standard free tier), plus access to 30+ platform capabilities, a monthly newsletter, exclusive certifications/workshops, potential sponsorships, and office hours with New Relic staff. Benefits are gated behind linking an education partner account.

Eligibility
Students (and select technical-bootcamp students) at accredited colleges, universities, or technical schools; 'proof of current student status is required'. Two routes: (a) GitHub Education — students in the GitHub Student Developer Pack, verified via GitHub; (b) Platzi — open to Spanish/Portuguese-speaking learners in Latin America and beyond, but Platzi's side requires a monthly course subscription. Select bootcamps only; others email students@newrelic.com. No .edu-email-only shortcut is documented.
How to claim
1) Create a free New Relic account (no credit card). 2) Have an active account with a partner: GitHub Education (verify student status in GitHub) or Platzi. 3) GitHub path: in New Relic go to Help icon → 'New Relic for Students' → 'GitHub Education' → 'Authorize with GitHub'. Platzi/other-partner path: submit the form with your 7-digit New Relic account number; benefits land in 5–10 business days. 4) Non-eligible bootcamp students email students@newrelic.com for manual verification.
Expiry
Not stated on the page. No renewal date, cap, or grace period is published; presumably reverts to the standard 100 GB/month free tier if student status is lost.
Gotchas
Perks only activate after linking a partner account. Platzi route: the New Relic tier is free but Platzi itself costs a subscription. Graduating or losing student status has no documented re-enrollment or grace language — expect to drop to the base free tier. 3 full users is for 'collaborative projects' (class group work).
Source
newrelic.com

New Relic for Startups program medium confidence

Verified on newrelic.com/startups as of 2026-10-22: 5 full-platform users plus 'generous data ingest per month, free' — the exact GB figure is not published on the page. Full platform 'from day one — no gating, no tool switching' (AI Monitoring, APM, Logs), explicitly 'not just bankrolled with credits'; also includes a New Relic engineer alongside launch, office hours, and quarterly architecture reviews.

Eligibility
Early-stage startups; the page itself publishes NO startup-age cap, funding cap, headcount cap, or region limit — thresholds are applied during application review. No credit-card requirement mentioned (consistent with the no-CC free signup).
How to claim
1) Submit the multi-step 'New Relic for Startups Registration' form on newrelic.com/startups. 2) 'Our team will reach out shortly, to process your application, and to confirm your place and next steps' — benefits are confirmed post-review rather than instantly granted.
Expiry
Not disclosed on the page (no program length, renewal terms, or end date).
Gotchas
Key numbers (monthly GB allotment, program duration, funding/age caps) are not public — the page is a marketing/registration funnel, so treat the exact benefit as 'confirmed after application'. Do not assume 100 GB: that figure on the site belongs to the standard free tier, not the startup program. If not accepted, you fall back to the perpetual free tier.
Source
newrelic.com
