RunPod Startup Program: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Startup Program — Starter Tier: $1,000 free credits for pre-Series A startups. 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Startup Program — Starter Tier: $1,000 free credits for pre-Series A startups high confidence

Read runpod.io/startup-program on 2026-10-22. Starter tier grants $1,000 in free credits with full platform access (Pods, Serverless, Clusters, Public Endpoints, Storage), self-serve onboarding, and 'Direct Support' from the Runpod team. Credits have no product-type limitations. Best fit stated as 'Pre-Series A startups with working products and near-term GPU needs.' The program is explicitly selective ('we can't accept every applicant').

Eligibility
Pre-Series A (earlier-stage) startups with a working product and near-term GPU needs. Page 'strongly prefers' venture backing (Seed–Series B+) with active investors validating traction/runway; pre-funding or bootstrapped applicants are steered to the self-serve platform instead. Must be new to Runpod (program 'primarily designed for new customers onboarding for the first time'), ready to onboard now (not 'in 6 months'), and have infrastructure as a current bottleneck. No .edu requirement, no stated region limit, no credit-card requirement stated on the page, and no published company-age cap (stage language only: 'Pre-Series A').
How to claim
Click 'Apply now' on https://www.runpod.io/startup-program (self-serve form). Existing Runpod customers below the Growth threshold should contact their account manager or sales@runpod.io; rejected/non-applicant users can simply sign up for pay-as-you-go self-serve without applying.
Expiry
No stated expiration for Starter credits on the page (only the Growth tier's 12-month agreement is referenced). One-time program; no credit reload mentioned for Starter. Verify expiry in writing when the credits are granted — treat as finite, likely loaded as account credit.
Gotchas
Not for existing customers unless via account manager. 'Strongly preferred' VC backing means bootstrapped/pre-funding startups may be told to just use self-serve pay-as-you-go instead. Once credits run out it's pure pay-as-you-go with storage/effective costs: volume disks bill ~$0.10/GB/mo running and $0.20/GB/mo idle; stopped (not terminated) pods still bill container disk. Credits are platform-wide but not infinite runway — $1,000 covers e.g. ~300h on an H100 SXM at $3.99/hr.
Source
runpod.io

Startup Program — Growth Tier: $25,000 bonus credits on $50,000 committed spend (credit match, NOT free) high confidence

Depositing up to $50,000 towards a committed contract earns $25,000 in bonus credits ($75,000 total), usable across Pods, Serverless, or Clusters. 12-month agreement with dedicated onboarding call, enterprise-grade uptime/SLA options, and a 'Warm handoff to sales' Enterprise Path. Target: 'Series Seed–B+ startups spending (or ready to spend) $5K–$25K+/mo on GPU infrastructure.' Credits load immediately alongside the deposit; bonus is one-time, explicitly 'no credit reload.'

Eligibility
VC-backed Seed–B+ startups, new to Runpod, ready to onboard now, 'pre prepared to invest upfront for credit match,' open to enterprise conversation. No .edu or region requirements stated.
How to claim
Same 'Apply now' CTA on https://www.runpod.io/startup-program; existing customers contact their account manager or sales@runpod.io.
Expiry
Credits loaded under a 12-month agreement; one-time bonus with no reload/renewal. No separate renewal track published.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free caveat: this is a 50% credit match on $50k committed spend, not a free offer — do not count it as free compute. Commitment is upfront and contracted. One-time only.
Source
runpod.io

No public student/.edu free offer — Academic Research program is institutional and sales-led high confidence

The pricing page (read 2026-10-22) shows no free tier, sign-up bonus, trial credits, or student discount anywhere. The Academic Research program targets universities, departments, and labs: lab/department-level projects prioritized, individual students/solo researchers only 'on a case-by-case basis,' access via institutional enrollment with university SSO integration, billing via custom invoicing aligned to academic calendars/grants/shared lab accounts, and manual application review with follow-up in 7-10 business days then a discovery call. No free GPU hours, no dollar-amount education discount, and no .edu sign-up-and-go tier is published.

Eligibility
Institutions (university SSO, grant/department funding) rather than individuals. No .edu free allocation; individuals only on a case-by-case basis.
How to claim
Contact form or phone +1 (888) 478-6012 via https://www.runpod.io/academic-research ('Get in touch' / 'Request a demo').
Expiry
N/A — no published free/discounted terms to expire.
Gotchas
If the goal is free student compute, RunPod is a weak option versus Kaggle (free P100 notebook quota), HF Spaces, or Modal's up-to-$10k academic credits. Any education discount is negotiated, not self-serve; expect no public number.
Source
runpod.io

Partner/event-sponsored free compute (secondary, non-self-serve offers) medium confidence

Several one-off free-compute offers around RunPod exist but are not standing self-serve programs: (1) Santa Cruz Accelerates 2025 cohort via Santa Cruz Works — eligible accelerator founders can apply for three tracks: up to 1,000 free H100 GPU hours, up to 1,000,000 free Serverless requests, or up to 750 free multi-node H100 compute hours (partner announcement, Jul 31, 2025). (2) $100 free GPU credits for ARC-AGI-2 participants (RunPod LinkedIn post, Mar 2025, application form, event-limited). (3) TreeHacks 2026 (Stanford, Oct 2026): RunPod donated $20k+ in credits to hackathon teams — sponsorship, not claimable by the public. Hackathons/events like these recur but each is separate and time-boxed.

Eligibility
Santa Cruz: founders enrolled in the Santa Cruz Accelerates 2025 cohort (apply through Santa Cruz Works or mention them on the referral form). ARC-AGI-2: participants of that specific challenge. TreeHacks: hackathon teams only.
How to claim
Santa Cruz: apply through Santa Cruz Works (santacruzworks.org) or the RunPod referral form mentioning Santa Cruz Works. ARC-AGI-2: via the event application link. General: watch runpod.io/blog for current sponsorships.
Expiry
All time-boxed/cohort-specific (2025 cohort, 2025 challenge, 2026 event). Not renewable; likely expired or past for 2025 items as of 2026-10-22 unless re-run.
Gotchas
These do not constitute a recurring student/startup free tier; amounts are per-cohort/per-event and require the external partner's program eligibility, not just a RunPod account.
Source
santacruzworks.org
