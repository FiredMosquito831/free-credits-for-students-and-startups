Discord Nitro: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: UNiDAYS x Discord Nitro — 1 month of full Nitro free for verified students. 2 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

UNiDAYS x Discord Nitro — 1 month of full Nitro free for verified students medium confidence

One month of Discord Nitro (full tier, not Nitro Basic) claimed through UNiDAYS student verification. Offer window: 2026-07-09 10:00 PT to 2027-07-09 11:59 PT; claimed codes must be redeemed on a Discord account by 2027-08-08 11:59 PT. Nitro includes HD streaming, 500MB uploads, 2 server boosts, custom emojis anywhere, profile/avatar customization, Super Reactions. After the 1-month trial, the subscription auto-converts to a monthly recurring Nitro plan and Discord bills the account's payment method each month ($9.99/mo in US per live discord.com/nitro page read 2026-10-22; cost varies by region/country) until cancelled in User Settings. One claim per user.

Eligibility
Must be a UNiDAYS-verified student (UNiDAYS verifies via institutional/.edu email, student ID, or its regional ID-verifier e.g. SheerID in the US; GRADLiFE recent-graduate accounts up to 3 years post-graduation may also qualify in some regions). Account must NOT have had: an active Nitro subscription or Nitro trial in the last 12 months, any prior Nitro subscription at any point, or a Nitro Classic/Basic subscription in the past 12 months — i.e., effectively new-to-Nitro accounts. If the account has Nitro Basic, Nitro Classic, or Server Boosting active, redeeming overrides it and leftover time is forfeited (no refund). Region-limited: NOT available in Syria, Russia, Turkey, Iran, North Korea, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson, Donetsk, Luhansk. UNiDAYS live offer pages exist for US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, France, Spain, Italy (not all countries).
How to claim
1) Open UNiDAYS website/app for your country, find the Discord offer tile, sign in and complete student verification. 2) Select 'Get now' / 'Activate' on the Discord offer page to unlock a Nitro promo code. 3) Redeem the code on your Discord account (desktop app or web) before 2027-08-08. 4) Cancel in User Settings > Nitro before the first renewal if you do not want the $9.99/mo (US) recurring charge.
Expiry
Claimable 2026-07-09 to 2027-07-09 (11:59 PT); redeemed code valid until 2027-08-08 (11:59 PT). Auto-renews to paid monthly Nitro after the 1-month trial; cancel anytime in User Settings to stop billing.
Gotchas
Trial, not permanent free access: converts to recurring paid Nitro ($9.99/mo US, varies by country) — a payment method will be billed at renewal unless you cancel first. Exclusion list is strict: any Nitro trial in the last 12 months disqualifies you, and ever having had Nitro disqualifies you. Redeeming wipes out unrefunded time on active Nitro Basic/Classic/Server Boost. The .edu email is not directly required by Discord — it's UNiDAYS's verification that gates it (email or student ID depending on country). Excluded countries include several large markets (Russia, Turkey, Iran). Support cannot manually add eligibility. NOTE: official support article returned 403 to direct fetch (bot protection); article content was read via a crawler snapshot updated 2026-07-08, so treat exact wording as high-fidelity but not live-verified today.

Discord free tier (baseline, not student-specific) high confidence

Discord itself is free permanently with no card required: text/voice/video, standard-quality streaming (no HD), ~10MB file uploads, 80-char messages on some surfaces, no server boosts, no Nitro perks. Nitro Basic is $2.99/mo and full Nitro $9.99/mo in US (confirmed on live discord.com/nitro, 2026-10-22). This free tier is the baseline everyone gets; the education front (UNiDAYS) only adds a 1-month Nitro trial on top.

Eligibility
Everyone; no verification, no card required for the free tier.
How to claim
Sign up at discord.com; nothing to claim.
Expiry
Permanently free; no expiry.
Gotchas
Not a student offer; listed for completeness of the 'free tier' category. No trial credits, startup program, or education license for Nitro exist on the live pricing page — the only student-facing offer is UNiDAYS.
Source
discord.com
