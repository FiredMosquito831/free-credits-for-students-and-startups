Airwallex: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Explore plan — free tier (US). 7 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Live check of “Airwallex for Startups (US) — startup program with fee credits”: Confirmed

Airwallex for Startups (US) is live and offers $150,000 in fee credits usable across FX, payments, payouts and platform fees for 12 months, plus no FX fees on the first $50,000 in transfers and 12 months of no monthly fees on multi-currency accounts. Eligibility is venture-backed startups from pre-seed through Series A that have raised $500K+ and have a US-registered business entity; existing Airwallex customers are not eligible, and the program is free to join and rolling (no stated deadline).

Offers

Explore plan — free tier (US) high confidence

US pricing page (read today) lists Explore at $0 per user/month with NO deposit/balance threshold in the fine print: multi-currency accounts in 20+ currencies, up to 10 free Spend users, free batch transfers to 120+ countries, unlimited multi-currency corporate cards with zero international fees, up to 2% cashback on local USD spend, 3.44% on USD with Yield, domestic USD & CAD transfers free, invoicing free. No credit card required to open (business KYC onboarding only). Business/legal-entity account required — no personal/student accounts exist.

Eligibility
US-registered business (any size). New sign-up via airwallex.com/en-us/signup; KYC/onboarding required. No funding stage, no .edu, no minimum balance listed on the US page. Note: in AU/UK/CA the same plan historically is free only with a deposit/balance threshold (AU: A$5,000/mo deposit or A$10,000 balance, else A$29/mo; UK: £10,000, else £19/mo), so verify the threshold for your entity's registered market — third-party 2026 reviews still describe a $29/mo fallback in some markets even though Airwallex's own US page and 2026 blog say flat $0 with no minimum balance.
How to claim
Sign up free at airwallex.com/en-us/signup (no CC, no deposit on US page); plan is ongoing while you remain a customer.
Expiry
Ongoing — no expiry or renewal conditions stated on the US pricing page.
Gotchas
Free ≠ costless: card acceptance 2.80%+$0.30 domestic / 4.30%+$0.30 international, SWIFT transfers $15–$25 each, FX markup 0.5% (major) / 1% (other) above interbank still apply. Marketing 'free' claim in US changed over 2025: previously threshold-based ($0 if $5K/mo deposit or $10K balance, else $29). Legacy customers who signed up before June 1, 2025 were placed into Explore with no monthly fee as of July 1, 2025.

Airwallex for Startups (US) — startup program with fee credits high confidence

US marketing page (read today): $150K in fee credits usable across FX, payments, payouts and platform fees for 12 months; no FX fees on your first $50,000 in transfers; no monthly global-account fees for the first 12 months (60+ countries); 6 months of t0 AI-CFO access; up to 2% cashback/fee savings on your first $800K when investors join the partner network. Partner-stack credits: Google Cloud $250K in credits, Nebius US$100,000 AI compute credits, 6 months of Notion Business (with Notion AI) free, 20% off first-year Carta subscription + waived onboarding, 20% off Deel first year. T&C (last updated Sept 9, 2026): card cashback = 10% rebate on eligible transactions for first 90 days of the Promotion Period, 2% thereafter (non-withdrawable, must offset future card spend/fees); a fee-waiver option of up to 'USD 25,000,00 equivalent' payments volume or 'USD 10,000,00 equivalent' FX volume (figures appear garbled in the fetched text — likely USD 250K / USD 100K volume caps; verify in a real browser). Rewards post by the 2nd of the following month; refunds/chargebacks reverse rewards.

Eligibility
Venture-backed US-registered startups, pre-seed through Series A, having raised at least USD 500,000 in venture capital; Series B or later or valuations over USD $1B disqualify; NEW Airwallex customers only (existing customers explicitly not eligible per marketing page); enrollment via a direct Airwallex invitation link, acceptance at Airwallex's sole discretion. No .edu; no credit card required; company age not stated (funding stage is the filter).
How to claim
Apply/enroll on the Airwallex for Startups page (airwallex.com/en-us/startups) — no promo code for the US; sign up at /en-us/signup through the startup invitation flow. T&C at airwallex.com/en-us/terms/airwallex-for-startups-promotion.
Expiry
Application window closes 31 December 2027, 23:59 PST (extendable/shortenable at Airwallex's discretion). Benefits run 12 months (fee credits/monthly fee waiver) or 6 months (t0 AI CFO); unused waiver capacity auto-expires; participation may be revoked at any time.
Gotchas
Marketing-page figures ($150K fee credits, $50K FX waiver, $800K cashback base) do not line up cleanly with the fetched T&C text (garbled 'USD25,000,00'/'USD10,000,00' caps) — confirm exact caps before relying on them. Cashback is non-withdrawable and excludes card-present transactions, cash/ATM, gift vouchers, balance transfers, gambling, crypto/investments, government payments. Promotion cannot be combined with other Airwallex offers. Series B+/>$1B valuation and existing Airwallex customers are out.

Airwallex for Startups — regional offers (AU/NZ, UK, Singapore) medium confidence

AU/NZ: pre-seed = 3 months free on Explore tier; seed = 3 months free on Grow tier + waived fees on first AUD$100,000 of payments + fee-free FX on first AUD$100,000 of conversions + Airwallex Yield on AUD/USD; Series A+ = contact sales; redeem code AIRWALLEX2 when enquiring via partner Scendar; requires AU ABN or NZ NZBN and venture backing. How to claim
AU/NZ: apply on airwallex.com/en-au/startups and redeem AIRWALLEX2 with partner Scendar (or via approved accelerator/VC partner links; Series A+ only via VC partner links). UK: register interest on airwallex.com/en-uk/airwallex-for-startups or email the startups team. SG: open account through the SMU IIE referral URL.
Expiry
No calendar end date published; tier offers (3-month Grow/Explore) are time-boxed to 3 months; AU terms document updated Oct 2025. Each company may redeem only one offer after Seed stage.
Gotchas
Regional coverage is at Airwallex's discretion and varies by market; pre-seed AU applicants must be incorporated in Australia within 12 months before account activation and be building a technology product; eligibility/stage reclassification at Airwallex's discretion; requires an active Airwallex account and completed KYC.

Student / education offers — none public; education-adjacent programs only medium confidence

No .edu-based free tier, student license, or student discount exists: Airwallex is B2B-only (no personal accounts; a registered business + KYC is required), and /en-us/education and /en-us/students do not exist (404). What does exist (education-adjacent, mostly AU): (1) Airwallex Impact (Nov 2024, Pledge 1%): 1% of company equity pledged to philanthropy (~US$56M at pledge), funding scholarships/training/incubators; 43 University of Melbourne scholarships (up to A$30K each) via an A$3M 2022–2025 partnership (now extended with a further ~A$2M donation, announced 24 Apr 2026); 'discounted Airwallex product for select non-profits' was planned for mid-2025. (2) Latitude 37 (launched 30 Apr 2026): A$100,000 EQUITY-FREE grants to 10+ Australian AI founders under 25 each year, plus Airwallex network access and an SF/Singapore immersion tour; applications opened late May 2026 (window likely closed by Oct 2026 — verify). (3) UNSW Sydney Founders community perk: bonus A$100 credit plus free multi-currency account, no monthly fee — open only to UNSW Founders and requires an active ABN.

Eligibility
Scholarships: University of Melbourne IT students (alumni program, not self-serve). Latitude 37: Australian AI founders under 25 (post-grad), grant-based. UNSW perk: UNSW Founders community members with an ABN. No .edu email pathway to a free Airwallex account for individual students.
How to claim
No self-serve student claim path. University programs are administered by the partner institutions (contact via the universities or sarah.kenny@unimelb.edu.au for UoM); Latitude 37 via its application window; UNSW perk via founders.unsw.edu.au/airwallex.
Expiry
UoM 'Opportunity Fund' bursary closed (funding capacity reached); initial 2022–2025 partnership term ended, extended April 2026; Latitude 37 applications opened late May 2026.
Gotchas
These are grants/scholarships/partner perks, not product free tiers — a student cannot simply open a free Airwallex business account as an individual; 'discounted product for nonprofits' (mid-2025) applies to non-profits, not students, and no percentage is published.
