Paddle: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: No fixed costs: pay-as-you-go only (no monthly, setup, or migration fees). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

No fixed costs: pay-as-you-go only (no monthly, setup, or migration fees) high confidence

Paddle's billing platform charges only 5% + 50¢ per Checkout transaction — 'No monthly fees, migration fees, or hidden extras.' The rate includes payments, checkout, billing, global tax/regulatory compliance in 130+ countries, fraud protection, chargeback handling, and buyer support (Paddle is merchant of record). 'No lock-in periods' and customer data is '100% yours'. Custom/enterprise pricing exists only for high-volume businesses via demo; bespoke pricing available on request for products under $10.

Eligibility
Any business that signs up (B2B; no student-specific path). No credit card required to sign up; no region-specific pricing — 5% + 50¢ applies globally.
How to claim
Self-serve: sign up at login.paddle.com (no demo required). No claim steps, codes, or applications.
Expiry
Standing pricing, no expiry or sunset date published as of 2026-10-22.
Gotchas
Not actually free per-transaction: every revenue dollar costs 5% + 50¢ (and volume pricing for high-growth startups is negotiated, not published). Paid add-on 'Premium Advisory' for large SaaS volumes. No trial credits or trial period on the billing platform itself.
Source
paddle.com

Paddle Metrics (ProfitWell Metrics) — 100% free subscription analytics high confidence

Standalone subscription-analytics product described as 'completely free' / 'Real-time subscription analytics, 100% free'. Included free: out-of-the-box KPIs (MRR, churn, upgrades/downgrades, LTV, active customers, revenue per customer), health scores, churn alerts, benchmarking against 30,000+ companies, advanced segmentation (cohorts, segments, plan data) enriched with Clearbit and FullContact data, and customizable alerts. Integrates with Stripe, Chargebee, Braintree, Zuora, Recurly, and Paddle's own stack. GDPR and CCPA compliant. No paid tier, add-on, or numeric caps stated on the page.

Eligibility
Aimed at 'subscription and SaaS companies' (business, not student-specific). No .edu email or institutional license. No credit-card requirement mentioned.
How to claim
Click 'Get ProfitWell Metrics free' → signup at www2.profitwell.com/signup/start. For Paddle customers it integrates 'one click'. Live demo link also offered.
Expiry
Presented as permanently free; no trial expiry, renewal date, or sunset stated as of 2026-10-22.
Gotchas
Free product is analytics only — it does not waive Paddle billing fees or provide a free tier of the billing platform. No seat/API/data-retention caps published. Data is enriched via third parties (Clearbit, FullContact) — review privacy implications. Sign-up lives on profitwell.com (Paddle's acquired ProfitWell brand), not paddle.com.
Source
paddle.com

Developer sandbox — free test/evaluation environment (no live billing, no payment card in the flow) medium confidence

Separate sandbox account for testing and evaluation of the Paddle API/dashboard: point code at https://sandbox-api.paddle.com, sandbox API keys contain '_sdbx', client-side tokens prefixed 'test_'. Test cards only (e.g. 4242 4242 4242 4242; sandbox rejects real card numbers, live rejects test cards). Webhook delivery limited to 3 attempts within ~2 minutes (vs up to 60 in live). Refund adjustments auto-approve every 10 minutes; cancellation flows work; checkout shows a 'Test Mode' watermark. No payouts or monthly statements in sandbox; credentials, products, prices, and customers are not shared with a live account.

Eligibility
Any developer; signup asks only 'personal and business details' — no payment method step and no .edu/institutional requirement. Sandbox sign-up is separate from a live account (you need one even if you already have a live account).
How to claim
1) Go to https://sandbox-vendors.paddle.com/signup, 2) enter personal/business details, 3) click Continue. Docs: developer.paddle.com/sdks/sandbox.md; quickstart mentions sandbox tokens start with 'test_'.
Expiry
Standing developer tooling; no expiry or sunset stated. 'Free' is inferred (no charge/payment step in the documented signup flow) — the page does not print the word 'free'; go-live later requires a separate live account where 5% + 50¢ applies.
Gotchas
Mixing sandbox and live credentials against the wrong API endpoint returns 'forbidden'. Sandbox email delivery is limited to addresses on your registered domain. Payment Recovery and Term Optimization work only on live accounts. No refunds/payouts in sandbox, so it cannot validate real money movement.
Source
developer.paddle.com

Paddle for Startups — solutions positioning, no published startup discount or credits high confidence

The 'Paddle for Startups' page (solutions/startups, 'Billing Infrastructure for Startups') is marketing/solutions content, not a fee program. It confirms the standard 5% + 50¢ all-in rate vs '~7% and above' for assembling separate PSPs, 'no hidden fees, no paying for add-ons', and 'Volume pricing available for high growth startups' (negotiated via demo/enterprise path — amounts unpublished). Trusted-by examples: n8n, DataforSEO, Runna. No trial credits, no startup-only fee waiver, no funding-cap or age cap, no application form.

Eligibility
Startups of any age/funding level can simply sign up at the standard rate; volume pricing requires a booked demo. No credit card, .edu, or region requirements.
How to claim
Standard rate: self-serve sign-up at login.paddle.com. Volume pricing: 'Book a demo' (/demo). No application or discount code mechanism described.
Expiry
No program expiry; 'volume pricing' terms are opaque, set per deal.
Gotchas
'Startup program' here ≠ fee discount: a startup with high volume pays negotiated custom pricing, not a published discount. Don't assume startup status changes the default 5% + 50¢.
Source
paddle.com

Student / education license — no offer found high confidence

No Paddle education program, student license, .edu discount, or nonprofit program exists on paddle.com as of 2026-10-22: /education, /paddle-for-education, /startups all return 404; the homepage Product/Solutions/Resource/Footer menus contain no 'education', 'student', or 'nonprofit' links (solutions cover SaaS, apps, app studios, digital products, software, games, startups, enterprise only). Paddle's free offers that do exist are the two standalone items above (free Metrics, free sandbox) plus zero fixed fees.

Eligibility
n/a — no offer. (No .edu email requirement exists because there is nothing to apply to.)
How to claim
Not claimable. Nearest free items: Metrics (profitwell.com signup) and developer sandbox (sandbox-vendors.paddle.com signup).
Expiry
n/a
Gotchas
Paddle is a B2B merchant-of-record platform for businesses selling software/digital goods; an individual student building a side project would be priced like any other business (5% + 50¢, no fixed fees). Do not confuse paddle.com with other 'Paddle' brands.
Source
paddle.com
