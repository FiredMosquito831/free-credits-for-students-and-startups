Vercel: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Hobby plan (permanently free, no card). 9 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Hobby plan (permanently free, no card) high confidence

$0/mo. Per official pricing page fetched 2026-10-22: 100 GB fast data transfer, 1M CDN requests, 1M function invocations, 4 hrs Fluid Active CPU, 360 GB-hrs Fluid Provisioned Memory, 1 GB blob storage, 5K image transformations/mo, unlimited deployments, 1 developer seat. Per /docs/limits (last updated 2026-09-16): 200 projects, 100 deployments/day, 25 projects per Git repo, 1 concurrent build, 45-min max build time, 32 GB disk, 120s proxied-request timeout. Fair-use doc adds Hobby includes first 10 GB fast origin transfer, image cache reads 300K/writes 100K per mo. Hobby is a hard cap: you cannot purchase extra usage; exceeding limits pauses/degrades the team. Hobby is restricted to non-commercial personal use only.

Eligibility
Any individual account; .edu not required; no credit card or payment method required to sign up or use Hobby; no region limits documented (ToS sanctions restrictions apply as usual). Limited to 1 developer seat; teams/commercial use require Pro.
How to claim
1) Sign up free at vercel.com (email/GitHub/Google) - no card. 2) Hobby plan is the default team plan. 3) Deploy via CLI or import a Git repo; usage accrues against monthly allotments.
Expiry
Never expires; allotments reset monthly. No renewal needed. Paused if caps hit; commercial use on Hobby is a ToS violation that can get the account actioned.
Gotchas
Non-commercial only: any ad revenue, payments, ads, affiliate purpose = commercial and requires Pro. No overage billing on Hobby - you get paused, not billed. 200-project and 100-deploys/day caps. 'Unlimited deploys' on the pricing page coexists with the 100/day general limit. Free Vercel Remote Cache also applies.
Source
vercel.com

Pro trial: 14 days with $20 credit (one per user) high confidence

14-day Pro trial including a $20 usage credit and trial limits: up to 10 team members, 200 projects, 16 CPU-hrs Active CPU, 1,440 GB-hrs Provisioned Memory, 1M function invocations/mo, 10K image transformations/mo, 50 domains per project. No payment method is required to run the trial; if you add one, billing starts immediately after the trial. Excluded from trial: Drains, account webhooks, free first-year domain, sandbox failover regions. Trial pauses when any of Active CPU / Provisioned Memory / invocations hits 100%; ends at 14 days or limit hit, whichever first, then reverts to Hobby.

Eligibility
Any user account; exactly one Pro trial per user account (cannot repeat or transfer). No card, no student or startup status required.
How to claim
Dashboard -> team switcher -> Create Team -> name team -> select 'Pro Trial'. To continue on Pro, add a payment method at Settings > Billing before trial end; otherwise it auto-reverts to Hobby.
Expiry
14 days from start, or earlier when trial usage limits are reached. Post-trial: upgrade to paid Pro ($20/mo base seat) or automatic revert to Hobby limits.
Gotchas
Owners cannot be changed during trial; charges apply only if a payment method exists; free first-year custom domain (a Pro benefit) is explicitly excluded from the trial; downgrading removes all team members.
Source
vercel.com

Vercel for Startups: up to $30,000 Flexible Commitment + Enterprise tier for a year high confidence

Enterprise-grade contract with a Flexible Commitment Amount up to $30,000 usable across seats, data transfer, compute, AI Gateway, v0, Eve, Workflows, Sandboxes and the rest of the platform; account runs on Enterprise T&C for 1 year or until the amount is consumed. Since Aug 6, 2026 acceptances the amount is partner-determined (varies between partners, max $30,000). Per-month sub-cap: max 50% of each month's credit may go to Vercel AI Gateway usage; excess is billed at list rates even with unused credit. Extras: Vercel Team Slack, demo days/hackathons, marketing features, swag.

Eligibility
Startup must be affiliated with an approved Vercel Startups Partner (VC/accelerator network) with proof (screenshot of partner dashboard/hub/wiki/console); Series A or less raised; must apply within 12 months of the most recent funding round; company website with matching email; must not have previously received Vercel startup credits. Bootstrapped startups without a partner affiliation do not qualify.
How to claim
1) vercel.com/startups/credits -> fill application (email, select/create team, choose partner, upload partner proof, country). 2) Accept Flexible Commitment terms. 3) Add a valid payment method (required for activation). 4) Credits and Enterprise access go live 5-7 business days after acceptance.
Expiry
1 calendar year from when the Flexible Commitment is applied; unused amount does not roll over. If consumed early, account auto-moves to standard ToS at list rates; benefits cannot be postponed; one-time only.
Gotchas
Credit card/payment method IS required (unlike Hobby). Vercel can revoke credits for abuse. Partner-dependent amounts - often far less than $30K (some partner deals, e.g. Paralect Kickstart, provide ~$2,400). The AI Gateway 50% cap binds AI-heavy apps. Credits are the 'discount' mechanism - overage is billed at standard rates, so it is not free hosting, it is subsidized paid hosting.
Source
vercel.com

Vercel AI Accelerator: cohort credits (40 AI startups, $8M+ pools) high confidence

6-week cohort of 40 early-stage AI teams; per current official page: 'Over $8M in credits' from Vercel, v0, AWS and partner AI platforms. Per-participant pools listed on the page include: Vercel $30k/participant ($12k winner), v0 $5.4k/participant ($1k winner), AWS $25k/participant, Anthropic $15k/participant, OpenAI $2.5k/participant ($10k winner), plus dozens of sponsor pools (Modal, ElevenLabs, Supabase, Neon, Auth0, etc.); finalists earn $100K+ in additional credit prizes per program blogs. Also weekly fireside chats/workshops, private AI-builder community, and SF demo day with investors.

Eligibility
Selection-based, early-stage AI startups; application via external Typeform; no eligibility criteria published on the page. Cohorts capped at 40 teams - not an open offer.
How to claim
Apply at vercel.com/ai-accelerator ('Apply now' -> Typeform). Most recent cohort: applications closed Feb 16, 2026 for a 6-week program; new windows announced via blog.
Expiry
Credits are distributed within the cohort (6-week program); per-platform credit terms govern each pool; winner prizes at demo day.
Gotchas
Competitive and intermittent, not a standing program. Pool sizes vary by year: $4M+ (May 2025 cohort) -> $6M+ (Feb 2026 cohort) -> $8M+ on the current page. Payment-method requirement not stated on the page.
Source
vercel.com

v0 for Students: 1 year of free v0 access at 15 active schools medium confidence

Program launched ~Oct 2025. Official page (fetched 2026-10-22) grants '1 year of complimentary v0 access' to enrolled students at the 15 active schools: Stanford, Harvard, Princeton, UC Berkeley, MIT, San Francisco State, Arizona State, Georgia Tech, University of Waterloo, NYU, Grand Canyon, Northwestern, University of Washington, Cornell, USC ('more coming soon'). The exact tier/credit is NOT published on the official page; the launch post advertised '1 year of free v0 premium' - note v0 no longer sells a 'Premium' tier, it now has Free / Plus ($30/user/mo, shown discounted from $90, incl. $30 monthly credits) / Business ($100/user/mo). For reference, v0's regular Free plan: $0, 7 messages/day, $5 one-time credit for new users.

Eligibility
Must be enrolled at one of the 15 active schools; sign-in is via school email and the verification form requires a .edu domain (reported verbatim: 'Email must be from a .edu domain'), which excludes non-US institutions on the list (e.g., University of Waterloo). No manual verification path; other schools join a waitlist.
How to claim
1) Go to v0.app/students. 2) 'Join with student email' with a school email. 3) Verify via confirmation link. 4) Free student access for 1 year. If school not listed, join the waitlist.
Expiry
1 year from verification. Renewal/re-verification terms are not published.
Gotchas
What students actually get (tier/credit level) is unpublished - reports say some only get free-tier-equivalent credits. .edu-only means most international and even some non-US schools are excluded. The program covers v0 (Vercel's AI app builder), not Vercel hosting plans - it is not a discount on Vercel Pro/Hobby.
Source
v0.app

No Vercel education license or student/nonprofit discount exists (for core Vercel hosting) medium confidence

Vercel has no .edu discount, education license, or nonprofit discount for its core platform - Vercel support/community staff stated in Oct 2025 ('we don't have a discount specifically for students at the moment') and reiterated Jan 2026 ('Vercel doesn't offer student or non-profit discounts. All services must be paid at full price'). The only education-adjacent offers are: the v0 for Students program above, and Vercel Academy (vercel.com/academy) which is free self-paced courses/workshops only, with no hosting credits or plan discount.

Eligibility
n/a - absence of offer. Students get the same Hobby (free) plan as everyone else, subject to the non-commercial-use restriction.
How to claim
Nothing to claim; use Hobby free plan or v0 for Students (15 schools).
Expiry
n/a.
Gotchas
Assume there is no .edu path to free Pro; any non-.edu or non-listed-school request is told to join the waitlist. Commercial work (e.g., a client project) cannot run on Hobby even for students.

Expiry
Granted for a 12-month period per the (unverified) third-party listing; program runs in recurring windows.
Gotchas
Amounts/tier officially unpublished; treat the $3,600 figure as unverified; window closes periodically.

Vercel for Startups (Flex Commit credits, startups accepted on or after 6 Aug 2026) high confidence

Active program, checked 2026-10-22. Its terms changed on 6 Aug 2026. Startups accepted on or after that date receive a Flexible Commitment Amount (a prepaid balance; Vercel's Flex Commit docs value each unit at $1.00 USD) under Vercel's Enterprise Terms & Conditions. It can be spent on seats, data transfer, compute, AI Gateway, v0 and other Vercel products. Amount: Vercel's startups page and FAQ say 'up to $30,000'. The FAQ says the amount depends on the arrangement between Vercel and the startup's partner and may vary, so $30,000 is a ceiling, not a verified per-startup award. Term: one year from when the amount is applied, or until used up if sooner. Non-cash extras: Enterprise-tier access for the term, Slack access to the Vercel team, events, swag and featured marketing. Startups accepted before 6 Aug 2026 follow the terms in their original acceptance email. A WebFetch summary of the FAQ says those earlier grants were split into 12 monthly allowances; that was not confirmed verbatim. Corroborated by the CloudRoute changes page (checked 2026-10-10) and Programscape (checked 2026-10-20).

Eligibility
Not open to every startup or to students. Must be affiliated with an approved Vercel for Startups partner (VC firms, accelerators or startup programs in the application dropdown). Proof: a full browser-window screenshot of the Vercel credit offer in the partner's dashboard, hub, wiki or console; screenshots of the acceptance email or profile are rejected. Series A or earlier. Applied within 12 months of the most recent funding round. Never previously received Vercel for Startups credits. Company website with an email on the same domain. Vercel reviews and decides at its discretion; one claim per company. No application fee or equity is reported by third-party sources, though the official FAQ text retrieved did not state either.
How to claim
Apply at https://vercel.com/startups/credits. Steps: (1) confirm your partner is on the approved list; (2) sign in, select or create the Vercel team that will hold the credits, and enter a company-domain email; (3) upload the partner-portal proof screenshot; (4) wait for approval (Vercel says 5-7 business days); (5) accept the Flex Commit startup terms by email; (6) add a valid payment method in workspace settings, because credits stay inactive without one. The amount is issued to the applied team within 5-7 business days of accepting the terms.
Expiry
One year from when the Flexible Commitment Amount is applied, or until used up if sooner. Any unused amount on the one-year anniversary does not roll over. If used up early, Vercel invites a conversation with a startups advocate about extending or moving to Pro. After the term, the account moves from Enterprise Terms to standard terms and is billed monthly at list rates to the payment method on file.
Gotchas
(1) AI Gateway cap: the FAQ text retrieved says you may use up to 50% of your Flexible Commitment Amount on AI Gateway. Sources differ on the window. CloudRoute describes a '50% monthly cap'. Programscape says the FAQ states it both as 50% of each month's amount and as 50% of the total, and that AI Gateway usage over the cap is billed at list rates even while credit remains. Confirm how it is measured with Vercel before relying on AI Gateway. (2) Size: the amount is partner-dependent and may be less than $30,000; no per-startup amount was verified. (3) Usage beyond the amount, or after the year, bills the card on file at list rates. Credits do not apply to already-paid invoices or mid-cycle add-on invoices (Programscape). (4) Starting early can leave credit unused when the window ends, and benefits cannot be postponed once activated. (5) Timeline: Causo Hub says the contract takes effect on acceptance, with credits and Enterprise access live within 5-7 business days. (6) One Vercel team per contract; the commitment is issued to the team you applied with, so pick the production team. (7) Startups accepted before 6 Aug 2026 follow different terms from their acceptance email. (8) Flex Commit teams can use Spend Management at no extra cost to set spend limits (Vercel docs).
Source
vercel.com

Vercel AI Accelerator (six-week cohort, credits from Vercel and partners) medium confidence

Real, Vercel-run six-week cohort of 40 AI teams with weekly fireside chats and workshops and a demo day in San Francisco. Credits come from Vercel, v0, AWS and many AI platforms (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, ElevenLabs, Modal, Supabase). Vercel's Feb 5, 2026 blog says 'over $6M in credits' and that finalists earn over $100K each in extra credit prizes. The current page says 'over $8M in credits' and lists per-participant values by partner. Fees and equity terms are not stated on the pages fetched. Not a standing offer; it runs one cohort per application window.

Eligibility
Application-based, selective cohort for AI builders. The current page lists no eligibility rules (no stage, geography or student criteria were found). Acceptance is competitive, with 40 teams per cohort.
How to claim
Apply at https://vercel.com/ai-accelerator when a window is open. The February 2026 window closed on 16 Feb 2026. The current page shows an 'Apply now' button but no dates, so an open window on 2026-10-22 could not be verified.
Expiry
The cohort runs six weeks. Credit expiry was not stated on the pages fetched.
Gotchas
Selection is competitive, so acceptance is not guaranteed. Totals differ across Vercel's own pages ($6M in the Feb 2026 blog vs over $8M on the current page). Many credits are partner-specific, not Vercel credits. A third-party page's claim of a Vercel Ventures investment for first place was not verified and is not counted here.

2025–26 changes

  • No free offer was sunset in 2025-2026, but several materially changed: (1) Pro plan was restructured in 2025 ('Simpler Pricing' / Fluid compute era): it now carries a $20 included usage credit and plan limits are expressed in Fluid Active CPU hours / Provisioned Memory GB-hrs rather than old per-function allowances; Hobby's core limits (100 GB transfer, 1M CDN requests, 1M function invocations, 4 CPU-hrs, 360 GB-hrs memory, 200 projects) are unchanged from previously known values as verified on the official pages today. (2) Vercel for Startups: new 'Flexible Commitment' credit terms apply to startups accepted on/after August 6, 2026 - the credit amount became partner-negotiated (up to a $30,000 max) rather than a fixed amount, with a 50%-of-monthly-credit cap on AI Gateway usage. (3) v0 plan lineup was rebranded - the '$20 Premium' plan referenced by older student-program marketing no longer exists; v0 is now Free / Plus ($30/user/mo, $30 monthly credits) / Business ($100/user/mo), so the exact value of the 1-year free v0 for Students offer is unpublished. (4) AI Accelerator credit pools scaled up between cohorts: $4M+ (2025) -> $6M+ (Feb 2026 cohort, apps due 2026-02-16) -> $8M+ on the current page. (5) v0 for Students launched Oct 2025 with 5 schools (Stanford, Harvard, MIT, Princeton, UC Berkeley) and has since expanded to 15; the .edu-only verification has not been loosened as of Jan 2026. (6) Vercel Remote Cache became free (changelog item, 2025-2026 era), adding to the Hobby free offering.
  • Verdict: a genuine, active offer exists for startups (Vercel for Startups, now on Flex Commit terms for startups accepted on or after 6 Aug 2026). It is gated by an approved Vercel Startup Partner, so it is not open to every startup. It was changed, not sunset. The CloudRoute change note (6 Aug 2026, Flex Commit, 50% AI Gateway limit) matches Vercel's own FAQ text. Size: Vercel's text says 'up to $30,000', a partner-dependent ceiling; no per-startup amount was verified. Student offers: none verified. The official startups credits FAQ lists general educational perks but no student offer (fetched 2026-10-22). A dedicated web search for a Vercel student offer could not run because the session's web search budget was used up, so a student-specific Vercel offer remains unverified. Vercel's standard Hobby plan is free per SaaSTweaks, but it is a general plan, not a student or startup program. Vercel Open Source Program (not a student or startup program): Startup Perk List (reviewed 2026-10-16) says new applications are closed, and sources conflict on the amount ($3,600 over three years vs over 12 months). Verification method: the official FAQ was read via a narrower WebFetch after the first full fetch failed on output size. The legal terms page (vercel.com/legal/startup-program-terms), cited by East Bridge Global, returned 404, so the legal terms were not checked directly.
