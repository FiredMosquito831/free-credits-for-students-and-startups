Unity Personal: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Unity Personal (free tier). 8 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Unity Personal (free tier) high confidence

Perpetually free editor + cloud for hobbyists and small indie teams. No credit card required (stated on the pricing page). Includes: publish to web/desktop/AR/VR/mobile, Unity Cloud ecosystem, MCP access, 1,000 AI tool credits/month. As of the 3/1/2026 DevOps change the free cloud tier includes: unlimited public-cloud Version Control seats, 25 GB standard storage per org/month, 10 GB Asset Manager storage, 100 GB free egress, 2 concurrent build units, and 200 Windows (Micro) + 100 Linux (Micro) + 100 Mac (Standard) free build minutes/month. Made with Unity splash screen optional on Unity 6. No Runtime Fee (canceled Sep 2024). Overages: $0.75/GB Asset Manager, $0.10/GB standard storage >25GB, $0.10/GB egress >100GB, Micro $0.02/min, Standard Linux $0.06 & Mac/Windows $0.07, Premium $0.11-0.12, Ultra $0.18-0.20, Turbo Windows $0.37; extra editor concurrency $10/mo.

Eligibility
Individuals/hobbyists/small businesses whose total finances (revenue + funding, trailing 12 months) are under $200,000 USD; if serving third-party clients, the aggregate client revenue/funding must be under $200K. Gaming/entertainment apps only — non-gaming apps where total company finances exceed $1,000,000 require Unity Industry, and Industry customers may not use Personal. No age floor, no credit card, self-reported finances subject to audit. Region: global; page/ToS in USD.
How to claim
Create a free Unity ID, download Unity Hub and Editor from unity.com/download, select the Personal plan. Self-certify finances in the Unity dashboard. No card required. Console (PS/Xbox/Switch) and Apple Vision Pro builds need Pro instead.
Expiry
No expiry while under the $200K threshold; must upgrade to Pro once exceeded (immediately upon crossing; tiers cannot be mixed).
Gotchas
Exceeding $200K forces paid Pro ($2,310/yr or $210/mo post-Jan 2026); no downgrades during a commitment window; cloud storage/egress/build-minute overages bill even on the free plan; splash screen is optional only on Unity 6; industry (non-game) use above $1M total finances is ineligible.

Unity Student plan (free education license) high confidence

Free plan giving students the full Unity Pro-tier editor (no education watermark) plus: free Odin Inspector + Validator education licenses, a premium Synty asset bundle, Unity Cloud personal tier (3 Version Control seats, 5 GB storage), and a 20% Asset Store discount. Valid 1 year; renews while enrolled (renew any time up to 60 days before expiry or after it expires). A school email is NOT required — 'You don't need a school email to sign up'; verification is via SheerID (or SAML sign-in to school where available). K-12 tier explicitly 16+. One Unity ID per verified account; student projects are isolated to the Student-plan account.

Eligibility
Students of legal age to consent to personal data processing (age 13 in the U.S., 16 in the E.U.; secondary tier stated as 16+), currently enrolled in credit-bearing classes at an accredited institution. No .edu email required. Global (page published in 9 languages); SheerID does regional verification.
How to claim
1) Apply at unity.com/products/unity-student, choose Post-Secondary or Secondary. 2) Create/log in to Unity ID. 3) Complete the SheerID form with academic details + documentation (instant most of the time; up to ~5 business days). 4) Redeem via the emailed instructions: download Unity Hub, then activate the license key (Unity Hub > Licenses > Activate with serial number).
Expiry
1 year; renewable indefinitely while actively enrolled, re-verified through SheerID. Graduating = plan lapses after its year (no special post-graduation path stated).
Gotchas
Cannot be shared; friends/family must apply separately; may log in at school but it's a personal (not lab/classroom) license — institutions use the Education Grant License; projects under the Student plan can't be opened in Personal/Educator/EGL accounts; commercializing student projects is allowed under the Editor ToS; legacy GitHub Student Pack references in some university IT articles are outdated (SheerID is current).
Source
unity.com

Unity Educator plan (free education license) high confidence

Free individual educator plan (cannot be purchased): Unity Pro-tier editor without watermark, free Odin Inspector + Validator education licenses, Synty asset bundle, an 'educator asset pack' (industry-built modular assets for demos/sample projects), 20% Asset Store discount, and Version Control with 3 seats / 5 GB. For creating/teaching/sharing Unity projects at home, school, or elsewhere — not for deploying across institutional machines.

Eligibility
Higher education: educators at accredited post-secondary degree-granting institutions globally (SheerID affiliation verification; .edu email not strictly required). K-12: available in roughly 30 listed countries (US, CA, AU, UK, etc.); K-12 educators in unlisted regions must use the Education Grant License instead. One Unity ID per verified account.
How to claim
1) Apply at unity.com/products/unity-educator, choose K-12 or Higher Education. 2) Create/log in to Unity ID. 3) Complete SheerID form, upload affiliation proof if asked. 4) License key arrives by email; activate in Unity Hub > Licenses > Activate with serial number.
Expiry
1 year; renew by returning to the page and re-verifying with SheerID (no purchase path exists).
Gotchas
Not for multi-computer classroom/lab deployment (use Education Grant License); K-12 is region-gated; educator projects can't be accessed from Student/EGL accounts; individual-use only.
Source
unity.com

Unity Education Grant License (institutional / university program) high confidence

Free multi-seat license that institutions deploy across lab/classroom computers and servers (no Unity ID sign-in required on the machines). Covers the core Unity engine (build/optimize/deploy across 25+ platforms) 'regardless of classroom size'. A full year of licenses at no cost, renewable annually at no cost; seat counts modifiable after initial approval; one Unity ID tied to the verified institutional account; no Unity Cloud access; EGL projects cannot be accessed via Student/Educator accounts. Accredited institutions: free. Nonprofits: available at a discounted (paid) cost instead. Students can pair EGL on lab machines with the free Student plan on personal devices.

Eligibility
Academic institutions accredited by an authorized governmental agency (local/state/provincial/federal/national) whose primary purpose is teaching enrolled students; .edu/institutional email used for the application. Outside-U.S. K-12 educators whose region lacks the K-12 Educator plan may use EGL. No age floor (institutional).
How to claim
1) Sign in at unity.com/products/unity-education-grant-license. 2) Select institution type. 3) Complete the SheerID verification form (up to ~48 h). 4) Submit the license request; follow the Unity Learn activation tutorial. Renew via the Unity Education License Information dashboard > Renew License, entering seat count.
Expiry
1-year term, renewable; renewal window opens 90 days before expiry; annual re-grant.
Gotchas
Changing the associated institutional email requires a support ticket; the page still notes Unity Cloud is not part of EGL; research/industry/commercial projects at an institution should instead buy discounted Unity Pro through an education rep.
Source
unity.com

Unity AI tools free trial + plan AI credit allowance high confidence

Unity AI (MCP/AI tools) offers a 14-day no-commitment free trial (link from the products page into the Unity service store, variant 'uty-pts-no-commitment-14-day-trial'); after the trial a paid monthly points subscription. Separately, every Unity plan includes a monthly AI-credit allowance: Unity Personal 1,000 credits/month, Pro 2,000, Enterprise/Industry 3,000, with 'special terms apply to Unity Credits'.

Eligibility
Any Unity ID holder can take the 14-day AI trial; the free monthly allowance attaches to a Personal (or higher) plan. No credit card stated for the trial variant ('no-commitment').
How to claim
From unity.com/products, click 'Try Unity's AI tools free' (14-day variant). Or just use the plan's included monthly AI credits (Personal = 1,000/month).
Expiry
Trial: 14 days, then paid. Free allowance: renews monthly with the plan while eligible.
Gotchas
Trial is trial, not a perpetual free tier; post-trial requires paying subscription; credit special terms apply; AI feature is newer so limits may shift.
Source
unity.com

Unity DevOps free-tier expansion (Mar 1, 2026) — free cloud build/VC layer on Personal high confidence

Effective 3/1/2026 Unity expanded the free (pay-as-you-go) cloud tier: seat charges for public-cloud Unity Version Control removed (the old 3-free-seat cap for Personal becomes unlimited; 1:1 seat limits dropped on paid plans), free storage 5 GB -> 25 GB per org per month, 100 GB free egress added (no egress billing at all in March-April 2026), free build minutes now 200 Windows (Micro) + 100 Mac (Standard) + 100 Linux (Micro) monthly, and Unity Editor Concurrency doubled from 1 to 2 (extra = $10/mo). This applies to everyone on a Unity ID, so students and sub-$200K startup teams get it free.

Eligibility
All Unity plans; effectively free for Personal users (students, hobbyists, startups under $200K revenue/funding).
How to claim
Automatic — included with a Unity Personal (or any) account; manage usage in the Unity Dashboard (Administration > Costs and usage reporting).
Expiry
Ongoing as of Oct 2026; announced 2025-11-10, effective 3/1/2026.
Gotchas
Beyond free limits, usage bills: storage >25GB $0.10/GB (premium $0.30/GB), egress >100GB $0.10/GB, Micro $0.02/min, Standard $0.06-0.07/min, Premium $0.11-0.12/min, Ultra $0.18-0.20/min; no downgrade during a commitment window; 'later in 2026' Unity DevOps gains were promised but may still be rolling out.
Source
unity.com

Unity Multiplay Hosting $800 startup sign-up credit (service in transition) medium confidence

New Unity Multiplay Game Server Hosting organizations received $800 of sign-up credit, valid 6 months from sign-up, automatically applied across the whole organization (shared by all projects) until exhausted; consumed on subsequent invoices; dashboard shows real-time consumption. Caveat found today: Unity is winding down direct support — support runs through 31 March 2026, the software is licensed to Rocket Science Group, and the official docs page now issues a 308 redirect to rocketscience.gg/services/hosting, so new credits likely come via Rocket Science Group rather than Unity.

Eligibility
New Unity Multiplay Hosting users (i.e., game startups adding server hosting); no CC-free claim — standard Unity account.
How to claim
Historically: subscribe to Unity Gaming Services/Multiplay Hosting; the $800 credit applied automatically org-wide; track in Dashboard > Administration > Costs and usage reporting.
Expiry
Credit: 6 months from sign-up. Service: Unity direct support ends 31 March 2026; continuity via Rocket Science Group.
Gotchas
Trial-style credit, not a free tier; once credit expires, pay-per-usage applies; migration off Unity's direct service may be required after the support cutoff.
Source
docs.unity.com

Unity for Humanity grants (social-impact creator grants) medium confidence

Grant program providing financial support + mentorship + audience amplification to creators using real-time 3D for social impact (environment, health, education, accessibility). 2026 winners already announced (e.g., Amaru, I NEED SPACE, Project Ember); also runs the UNEP/Project Drawdown Environment & Sustainability Grant and Imagine Grant (with Common, 2021). Not a license or startup-specific program, but a real free-money front that students/indies run into.

Eligibility
Creators/changemakers (any region) applying through periodic grant calls; application-based and selective; open on unity.com/humanity.
How to claim
Apply via the Unity for Humanity grant application on unity.com/humanity when a cycle is open; winners and honorees are announced per year.
Expiry
Recurring annual/periodic grant cycles (2026 cycle ran; next cycle timing follows Unity's announcement).
Gotchas
Grants, not subscriptions — no guaranteed amount, selective; distinct from Unity's education/startup licensing.
Source
unity.com

2025–26 changes

  • As of 2026-10-22, no dedicated 'Unity Startup Program' exists on unity.com (no link in the products navigation; unity.com/startups and similar paths 404). Startups are covered by the free Unity Personal tier (<$200K revenue+funding/12 months) plus the expanded free DevOps cloud tier; paid Pro is $2,310/yr or $210/mo. Material 2024-2026 changes: (1) Sep 12, 2024: Unity canceled the controversial Runtime Fee for games; Personal's cap doubled from $100K to $200K USD at the same time, and the 'Made with Unity' splash screen became optional starting with Unity 6. (2) Jan 1, 2025: plan restructure — Unity Plus was absorbed/renamed to Unity Pro; Pro became mandatory above $200K and Enterprise above $25M total finances; Pro was $2,200/yr at that date. (3) Jan 12, 2026: 5% Pro/Enterprise price increase (Pro -> $2,310/yr, $210/mo); Havok Physics for Unity dropped from Pro/Enterprise/Industry on 6.0 LTS (and from Unity 6.3). (4) Mar 1, 2026: free DevOps cloud tier expansion (unlimited VCS seats, 25GB storage, 100GB egress, added Mac/Linux build minutes, 2 editor concurrency) — all free tiers above reflect this. (5) 31 Mar 2026: Unity Multiplay Game Server Hosting direct support ends; service licensed to Rocket Science Group, docs 308-redirect there, so its $800 startup credit is in transition. (6) Aug-Sep 2026: Unity Economy (Gaming Services) became free to use starting 25 Aug 2026 and stopped accepting new project signups 8 Sep 2026 — a de-facto free tier for existing configs but the product is sunsetting (no new signups; IAP/Live Content positioned as the successors). (7) Verification path: the legacy 'GitHub Student Developer Pack' route for Unity Student has been replaced by SheerID (plus optional SAML sign-in to school); .edu email is explicitly not required by Unity's current Student plan page. Older third-party university IT articles still cite the GitHub Pack requirement — outdated. No announced sunset of the Personal, Student, Educator, or Education Grant License offerings as of Oct 22, 2026.
