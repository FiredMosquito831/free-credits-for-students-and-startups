Render: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free web services (Free tier). 7 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free web services (Free tier) high confidence

Free web service instance: $0/mo, 512 MB RAM (pricing table lists Free as '$0/month, 512 MB RAM, plan id free'; CPU share not stated on pricing page, third-party trackers cite 0.1 vCPU). 750 free instance hours per workspace per calendar month, shared by all free web services; spun-down services do not consume hours. Features kept: custom domains, managed TLS, service previews, log streams, rollbacks to 2 most recent deploys. Limits: single instance, no persistent disk, no SSH, no autoscaling, no one-off jobs, no reserved ports 18012/18013/19099, no private-network inbound traffic; outbound SMTP ports 25/465/587 blocked since 2025-09-26; local filesystem wiped on spin-down/restart/redeploy; Render may restart or suspend a free service 'at any time'.

Eligibility
Any new Render account; no credit card, .edu email, or region requirement stated; region chosen at creation (US/Europe/Asia).
How to claim
Create a Render account (no card), click New > Web Service (or Static Site/Postgres/Key Value), choose the 'Free' compute plan, deploy. Free web services: spins down after 15 min of idle (HTTP+WebSocket) and takes ~1 min to wake; all 750 hours shared across every free web service in the workspace; hours reset monthly with no rollover; exhaustion suspends all free web services until next month.
Expiry
Perpetual; 750 hours reset on the 1st of each calendar month, unused hours do not roll over.
Gotchas
Official docs: 'Do not use them for production applications.' Free web services and static sites count against the Hobby workspace's 5 GB included bandwidth and 500 included pipeline minutes; exceeding them with no payment method on file suspends all Free services for the rest of the month (new builds disabled for pipeline minutes). A pinger defeats the point: keeping one service awake ~744/750 hours a month.
Source
render.com

Free Postgres database high confidence

One free Render Postgres database per workspace: 256 MB RAM, 1 GB SSD storage, 100 connections. No backups of any kind, no managed connection pooling. Expires 30 days after creation, then a 14-day grace period; afterwards Render deletes the database and all its data. After expiry you may create a new free database. Paid upgrade starts at $6/mo (0.1c-256mb).

Eligibility
Any account; no credit card required; no .edu requirement.
How to claim
New > Postgres, choose the Free compute plan. One per workspace (individual and team accounts).
Expiry
30 days from creation + 14-day grace period, then data deleted.
Gotchas
Despite being called 'free', this is effectively a 30-day trial: it is the only free datastore with a hard expiry. Since 2024-05-20 the expiry was cut from 90 to 30 days. No backups means the 14-day grace period is the only recovery window; export data before day 30.
Source
render.com

Free Key Value instance high confidence

One free Key Value (Redis-compatible) instance per workspace: 25 MB in-memory, ~50 simultaneous connections. In-memory only — data is lost on every restart and also when the instance is upgraded to a paid plan. No continuous on-disk persistence.

Eligibility
Any account; no credit card required.
How to claim
New > Key Value, choose the Free plan.
Expiry
Perpetual as long as the instance exists; data itself is never durably stored.
Gotchas
Useful only for cache/queue experiments, not durable state; a restart silently wipes keys. 2026-10-01: Render added two new paid Key Value plans (3 GB up to 3,000 connections; 50 GB up to 50,000 connections) and lowered prices on 1 GB+ plans — free tier unchanged.
Source
render.com

Free static sites high confidence

Static sites are free to deploy on Render (HTTPS, global CDN). They count against the workspace's monthly included outbound bandwidth (5 GB on Hobby) and build pipeline minutes (500/mo on Hobby).

Eligibility
Any account; no credit card required.
How to claim
New > Static Site, deploy from Git; no compute plan selection needed.
Expiry
Perpetual; usage limits reset monthly.
Gotchas
Bandwidth overage: $0.15/GB if a payment method is on file; otherwise services are suspended for the remainder of the month. Note the 2026 Hobby bandwidth cut (100 GB -> 5 GB) applies to static-site egress too.
Source
render.com

Hobby workspace plan ($0/mo + usage) high confidence

Hobby workspace plan: $0/mo + usage-based compute/storage. Included: 5 GB outbound bandwidth (then $0.15/GB), 500 build pipeline minutes/month (then $5 per additional 1,000), up to 25 services (all types incl. suspended), 2 custom domains (then $0.25/domain/month), 1 team member. Pro is $25/mo flat (seat fees removed, unlimited members/services, 25 GB bandwidth, 1,000 pipeline minutes). Scale $499/mo. If a Pro/Scale workspace has no services and no activity, the subscription fee is waived for that month.

Eligibility
Any account; no credit card required to use the Hobby plan; egress/build-minute overages require a payment method or free services are suspended.
How to claim
Nothing to claim — every new Render account starts on the $0 Hobby plan automatically.
Expiry
Perpetual; usage quotas reset monthly. New workspaces got the new plans on 2026-04-23; existing workspaces auto-migrated on 2026-08-01.
Gotchas
The 2026 restructure narrowed the free offer: Hobby bandwidth went from 100 GB to 5 GB/month and services are capped at 25 (legacy Hobby was unlimited). Free-instance specs themselves were unchanged. Egress billing widened in 2025-08 to service-initiated outbound, WebSocket responses and Private Link traffic.
Source
render.com

Render Startup Program credits (Prototype/Build/Scale/AI) high confidence

Render startup credits, valid 12 months from approval. Tiers: Prototype — $500, open to any qualifying new customer; Build — $2,500 (majority of partner programs) up to $5,000 (select partners, e.g. Aurelia Ventures' $5K program); Scale — $10,000 (startups with < $1M funding) or $25,000 ($1M+ funding) with top-tier VC funding; AI — $100,000 (AI-native/data-heavy products) plus 1:1 onboarding, 1-year dedicated support Slack, co-marketing. Perks: startup-specific onboarding resources and exclusive startup events on partner tiers.

Eligibility
New Render customer; Series A or earlier; less than $50M raised total; company < 4 years in operation (< 5 years for Scale tier). Prototype: minimum $25K seed funding. Build/Scale/AI: must apply through an enrolled partner accelerator/VC; AI additionally requires at least $2.5M funding from an enrolled top-tier VC. No .edu or student path; no equity ask stated; geography global.
How to claim
Create a Render account (Step 1 'Start'), complete the short application on the startups page referencing your Render instance (Step 2 'Apply'); on approval credits are applied to the account (Step 3 'Launch'). Build/Scale/AI tiers: apply through your enrolled accelerator/VC's partner link. Contact: startup-program@render.com.
Expiry
Credits valid 12 months from time of approval.
Gotchas
This is a funded-startup program, not a student offer — the free tier is Render's student path. Not open to existing Render customers. The /startups page is JS-rendered; the tier figures above were read from the live official page via the search index today and corroborated by partner pages (Aurelia $5K; COMPOUND, builtfirst marketplace 'up to $100,000'). Verify exact tier numbers on the live page before applying.
Source
render.com

Hackathon/education partner credits (event-specific) medium confidence

Event-specific, not a standing program. Render is the official deployment partner for Hack Days by Atria Summer of Code (announced 2026-06-04): $50 in Render platform credits to every participant plus additional credits for winning teams, with OSCode (MLH network) as a partner. Render's startup-program page also says it partners with 'accelerators, incubators, educational institutions, and startup programs to help students and founders' — such partner programs can surface credits for students at events, but there is no standing education license.

Eligibility
Students via event organizers (hackathon registration); no .edu email requirement stated for the credits themselves; availability tied to specific 2026 events.
How to claim
Not claimable through Render directly — credits are granted by the event organizer at registration (e.g., Atria Summer of Code Hack Days, hacks.osatria.in).
Expiry
Event-specific; not a recurring offer.
Gotchas
Do not expect these to recur or generalize; check the specific event's page. No .edu pricing, student discount, or academic license exists anywhere on Render's site (no education link in navigation).
Source
linkedin.com

2025–26 changes

  • No Render free offer was fully sunset in 2025-2026, but several were materially narrowed: (1) 2026-04-23 'Better pricing for fast-growing teams' (auto-migration completed 2026-08-01): Hobby bandwidth cut from 100 GB to 5 GB included/mo, overage now $0.15/GB, Hobby capped at 25 services (was unlimited) and 2 team-member environments became unlimited projects x 2 environments; Pro moved from $19/member to $25 flat; free-instance specs (512 MB web / 256 MB Postgres / 25 MB KV / 750 hours) were explicitly unchanged. (2) 2025-09-26: free web services block outbound SMTP ports 25/465/587 (changelog 2025-09-16). (3) 2025-08-01: bandwidth pricing halved ($30 -> $15 per 100 GB, now per-GB) and egress billing expanded to service-initiated outbound, WebSocket responses and Private Link traffic (changelog 2025-07-01). (4) 2026-02-24: free web services now stay awake while receiving WebSocket messages (previously only HTTP prevented spin-down). (5) Context: free Postgres expiry was cut from 90 to 30 days on 2024-05-20. Education: Render has never offered a standing student/education license or .edu pricing; the only student-facing free offers are the general free tier plus event-specific hackathon credits (e.g., Atria Summer of Code Hack Days, June 2026, $50/participant). Related but not student/startup: Render also advertises 'up to $10K migration credits' for production infrastructure migrations. Known-so-far claims verified: 750 instance-hours/mo, 512 MB free web instance, 30-day free Postgres expiry, no-card signup — all confirmed on the official pages today.
