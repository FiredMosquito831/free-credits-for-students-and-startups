AWS Free plan (new-account credits: $100 base + up to $100 earned, 6-month window) high confidence

As of 2026 AWS replaced the classic 12-month per-service free tier with a Free plan vs Paid plan model. New accounts are auto-enrolled in the Free plan and get $100 in AWS credits immediately, plus up to $100 more earned by completing 'Explore AWS' activities (up to $200 total). The Free plan runs at no cost for up to 6 months or until credits run out, whichever comes first, and the account closes automatically. Base credits expire 6 months from account creation; activity-earned credits expire 12 months; if you upgrade to the Paid plan remaining credits apply to bills and expire 12 months after account creation. 30+ 'always free' services with monthly caps remain on both plans. A payment method is NOT required to sign up for most new customers (a major change from the old credit-card-required model). Credits are USD, non-transferable between accounts, and can offset usage on eligible services including Amazon Bedrock model invocations and AI/ML workloads. Featured Bedrock access on the Free plan; Amazon Bedrock AgentCore is listed as a Paid-plan-exclusive service.

Eligibility
Any new AWS account (personal or business). No .edu requirement, no funding criteria, no credit card required for most customers. Free plan restricts access to a subset of services; Paid plan allows a monthly spend limit starting at $20.
How to claim
1) Create a new AWS account (auto-enrolls in Free plan, no payment method needed for most customers) -> 2) $100 credits are applied on signup -> 3) Complete Explore AWS activities in the console to earn up to $100 more -> 4) Use credits on eligible services incl. Bedrock within the 6-month window -> 5) Optionally upgrade to Paid plan; remaining credits roll onto invoices until 12-month expiry.
Expiry
Base $100 credits: 6 months from account creation. Earned up to $100: 12 months from account creation. Free plan itself auto-closes 6 months after account open or when credits are exhausted. No renewal or extension; credits cannot be transferred.
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free confusion: this is a 6-month credit window, NOT the old 12-month tier. Once upgraded to the Paid plan you CANNOT downgrade back to Free. Legacy per-service allowances (EC2 750 hrs, S3 5 GB, Bedrock free-token lists) no longer appear in the new structure. Data transfer/egress and some advanced features may be excluded or Paid-plan-only. The old Bedrock 12-month free-token offer is gone from the free-tier FAQ and docs (see sunset notes). Credits only offset eligible services - check the Free Tier Categories list.

AWS Activate Founders tier (self-serve; $1,000 start, up to $5,000) high confidence

The self-serve AWS Activate tier. $1,000 in AWS Activate Credits to start; select startups may qualify for additional credits up to $5,000 over time. Credits (USD, subject to AWS Promotional Credit T&Cs) can offset 200+ eligible AWS services including third-party foundation models on Amazon Bedrock (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Mistral AI, Meta, etc.) via a single API. Application is reviewed within 5-10 business days.

Eligibility
Self-funded / bootstrapped (no VC or accelerator backing needed). Must be: new to AWS Activate Credits, have a fully functioning company website, founded in the past 10 years, Pre-Series B, and have an AWS account on a Paid Tier plan (note: the new paid-plan requirement means the free-plan-only account must be upgraded). No .edu requirement. Business email matching the startup domain is required for the application.
How to claim
1) Create/verify an AWS Builder ID (personal email) -> 2) Complete the one-time AWS Activate profile with your business email -> 3) Choose the Founders tier -> 4) Provide startup details (product, funding stage, company profile) -> 5) Link an AWS account (on Paid plan) and submit -> 6) Hear back in 5-10 business days. Apply at https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/ ('Apply here' / 'Join now' on https://aws.amazon.com/startups/).
Expiry
Promotional credits: no public per-tier expiry stated on the credits page today; AWS has said Activate credits generally expire in 1-3 years depending on package (the $200K-class credits are on 3-year expiry per the 2024 announcement). Credits are in USD, subject to Promotional Credit T&Cs, non-transferable.
Gotchas
Only $1,000 is guaranteed; the $5,000 extension is selective. 'New to Activate Credits' - you cannot re-claim unless you are requesting more than previously received. Egress/data-transfer and some fees may be excluded from promotional credits (check the credits FAQ 'What is not covered'). Requiring a Paid-plan AWS account means a card/payment setup may be needed at the account level even though the Free plan itself needs no payment method.

Expiry
The $200K credit package expires 3 years from issuance (per the June 2024 change; previously the $100K package expired in 1 year). USD, subject to Promotional Credit T&Cs, non-transferable.
Gotchas
You cannot apply yourself without a provider's Org ID or invite - a consulting partner cannot file it for you. 'Within 12 months of your latest funding date' is a hard window. Credits cannot be sold or transferred; check the 'not covered' list (data transfer and some support fees historically excluded, though Support Plan application is permitted). The old ~$100K ceiling is outdated in most third-party guides.

AWS Free Account (new credit-based Free plan) — the restructured AWS Free Tier high confidence

New AWS accounts automatically enter a credit-based Free plan: $100 USD credits at sign-up + up to $100 more earned by activating foundational services = up to $200 total, spendable across 90+ services. Free plan runs up to 6 months (account auto-closes at 6 months or when credits run out, whichever is first). 30+ services also have recurring 'Always Free' monthly limits on both plans. On the Paid-plan path, the same credits are valid for up to 12 months from account creation. Free-plan accounts are migrated from the legacy usage-based 12-month Free Tier for accounts created on/after July 15, 2025; a new sign-up experience launched October 2026 (older accounts still use the legacy credits console view).

Eligibility
New AWS customers only (existing/previous account holders excluded). No .edu requirement; open to students and startups alike. Payment method NOT required to sign up for most new customers, but AWS may request a payment method or extra info to verify identity. Unavailable in China (Beijing and Ningxia) regions and AWS GovCloud (US) (with a note that some services such as Lambda are exceptions in GovCloud).
How to claim
1) Create a new AWS (Builder ID) account via the registration portal — Free plan is selected automatically; 2) use the $100 signup credit, then earn up to $100 more by activating foundational services (credits appear in billing console within ~30 min); 3) optionally upgrade to Paid (remaining credits become bill credits expiring 12 months post-creation).
Expiry
Free-plan credits: 6 months from account opening or exhaustion, whichever first; account then suspends, data retained 90 days, then account and data permanently erased if not upgraded. Paid-path credits: 12 months from account creation. No renewal; downgrading Paid back to Free is not allowed.
Gotchas
Legacy 12-month per-service Free Tier no longer exists for new accounts. Activating 'advanced features' is irreversible and voids remaining earnable credits; joining an AWS Organization or deploying a Control Tower landing zone immediately voids remaining earnable credits. Paid-only services are excluded from the Free plan. After 6 months the account closes itself — you lose access to resources/data unless you upgrade within the 90-day grace window. Egress/data transfer and overages beyond limits are paid.

Amazon RDS free database (legacy 12-month tier for accounts created before July 15, 2025) high confidence

For legacy accounts: up to 750 hours/month of a single-AZ instance on select database classes, across MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, or SQL Server (Express Edition only), plus 20 GB General Purpose SSD (gp2) storage and 20 GB automated backup storage per month, for up to 12 months. New AWS accounts created before July 15, 2025 (or existing legacy accounts still within their 12-month window). Not available in AWS GovCloud (US) or China (Beijing) regions. No extra eligibility gate beyond a new account.
