Axiom Free Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Personal free plan (permanent free tier). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Personal free plan (permanent free tier) high confidence

Free forever Personal plan: 500 GB/month data-loading (ingest) compute, 10 GB-hours query compute, 25 GB storage, 30-day retention. Includes full APL access and all integrations; community support. Soft limits on datasets / fields per dataset / query concurrency that 'can be lifted on request'. No hard numeric caps published for those. Verified on the official pricing page on 2026-10-22: '500 GB load / 10 GB-hours / 25 GB storage, free forever' is also restated on the Startups solution page.

Eligibility
Open to everyone, including students and early startups. No credit card required (pricing page: 'No credit card required'; quickstart: 'No schema, no credit card'). No .edu email required, no age/funding/region limits stated.
How to claim
Sign up at axiom.co (no payment method), create a dataset, ship logs via OTel or Axiom SDKs (5-minute quickstart at /docs/getting-started). No application needed. Request soft-limit lifts via support (axiom.co/support) if needed.
Expiry
Permanent ('Permanent' on pricing page; FAQ calls it 'free forever with a generous allowance'). No expiry or renewal.
Gotchas
Only 30-day retention and 25 GB storage on free tier — long-term archives need paid Axiom Cloud (customizable/Forever retention). The free plan is a real perpetual tier, not a trial. Soft limits on dataset counts/concurrency can bite heavy users. Previously-seen '5 GB ingest + $10 credit' figures are stale — see sunset_notes.

Axiom for Startups program (compute discount + platform-fee waiver) high confidence

For qualifying startups: 30% off Data Loading and Query Compute, 'up to $10,000 in compute savings' over 'up to 12 months' (whichever comes first), plus the $25/mo Axiom Cloud platform fee waived for 12 months. The always-free included usage (first 1 TB load + 100 GB-hours query per month, 100 GB storage) still applies. Worked example on page: 10 TB load + 2,000 GB-hrs query/mo saves ~$5,340/yr including the fee waiver. Verified on official page 2026-10-22.

Eligibility
Startup stage 'between Seed and Series A'. Must be NEW to paid Axiom Cloud — free-tier users may apply; existing paid customers are NOT eligible. Company must not have received an Axiom startup benefit before. No startup-age, funding-dollar-cap, or location restrictions stated beyond the Seed–Series A stage.
How to claim
Submit the form at https://axiom.co/axiom-for-startups: describe your company, how you found the program, and provide a funding announcement, investor portfolio page, or accelerator profile (or an access code / confirming contact if private). Axiom reviews and replies by email. Once approved the program is enabled on your org; the 12-month clock starts when you upgrade to Axiom Cloud (which requires a credit card on file).
Expiry
30% compute discount lasts up to 12 months OR until $10,000 in savings, whichever comes first (hitting $10k ends the discount early but the fee waiver runs its full 12 months). After 12 months, standard pricing including the $25 platform fee resumes. No renewal path is described. No program sunset date published.
Gotchas
Not self-serve: application must go through a participating accelerator, investor, or startup community and requires proof. Cannot combine with committed-use pricing or another promotional compute discount. Storage and add-ons (SSO $100/mo, Directory Sync $100/mo, RBAC $50/mo, Audit Logs $50/mo) stay at standard pricing. Axiom Cloud itself needs a card on file. Billing caps pause (not bill) overage, which can interrupt queries/monitors at the cap.
Source
axiom.co

Axiom Cloud 'always free' allowance baked into the $25/mo plan (startup-adjacent offer) high confidence

Axiom Cloud: $25/month platform fee, no minimum commitment, with an always-free allowance of 1 TB (1,000 GB) data loading + 100 GB-hours query compute + 100 GB storage per month; usage beyond is credit-based (1 credit = $1 by default; data loading from 0.12 credits/GB down to 0.06 at high volume; committed-use pre-purchase up to ~47% off at 2.5M credits/yr on 36-month term; pre-purchased credits 'never expire'). A hard spend cap can be set in the Console — overage is paused rather than billed (cap feature introduced 2025-09-22). Useful for students/startups who need more than the Personal tier but want a cost ceiling: worst case is the $25 fee.

Eligibility
Anyone who upgrades to Axiom Cloud; credit card required (card on file). No age/funding/education restrictions. Students can use it with a spend cap to guarantee max cost = $25/mo.
How to claim
Upgrade from Personal at axiom.co/pricing (add card), optionally set a monthly spend limit in the Console; claim the Axiom for Startups program instead if you qualify for the 30% discount + fee waiver.
Expiry
Ongoing; allowance renews monthly with the plan. No expiry date published.
Gotchas
Credit card required (differs from the free Personal plan). Hitting the spend cap PAUSES usage (queries/monitors stop) until next cycle — 'may cause service interruptions'. Overage beyond the 1TB/100GB-hr free block can still add cost within the cap. Add-ons (SSO, RBAC, audit logs, Directory Sync) are extra $50–100/mo.
Source
axiom.co

Education / student program: none found medium confidence

No dedicated Axiom education license, student program, or .edu-based offer exists on the site as of 2026-10-22: /education returns 404, the sitemap has no education/student/campus/trial URLs, the pricing page, FAQ, and 2025–2026 blog index contain no student or academic pricing. Students' effective free offer is the credit-card-free Personal plan (finding 1); startups' is the Axiom for Startups program (finding 2).

Eligibility
N/A — no program to be eligible for. Students use the general Personal free plan (no .edu email required, no credit card).
How to claim
Nothing to claim separately; students simply sign up at axiom.co for the free Personal plan.
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
Absence was verified via sitemap, 404 checks on /education, and blog index, but WebSearch budget was exhausted so I could not sweep press/third-party sources for a partner-run student deal; confidence medium rather than high on the negative.

2025–26 changes

  • 1) The previously known '5 GB ingest/month + $10 signup credit' is STALE. On 2025-04-21 Axiom 'Reimagined pricing' (blog/reimagining-pricing): the old ingestion-focused Personal/Team/Enterprise tiers were replaced by credit-based Axiom Cloud pricing ($25/mo platform fee, 1 credit = $1, always-free 1TB/100GB-hr/100GB block) plus a new credit-card-free Personal free tier now at 500 GB load/mo (current pricing page), 10 GB-hrs query, 25 GB storage, 30-day retention. 2) The $10 signup credit no longer appears on the pricing page, FAQ, or 2025–2026 blog; it is not a documented current offer (treat as discontinued/unverified). 3) 2025-09-22 changelog added compute-credit pre-purchase discounts and spend caps (overage pauses rather than bills) — a material change for cost safety. 4) Axiom's docs moved from docs.axiom.co to axiom.co/docs in 2026. 5) No 2025–2026 sunset of the startup program found — /axiom-for-startups is live with current terms as of today's fetch.
