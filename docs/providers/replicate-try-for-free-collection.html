Replicate 'Try for Free' collection: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Replicate 'Try for Free' free runs (no-credit-card free tier). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Live check of “YC Summer Fellows (2025): Replicate $10,000 credits for students”: No longer available

The 2025 YC Summer Fellows Grants post did list "Replicate ($10k)" in credits alongside Fly.io ($50k), Supabase ($10k) and Resend ($3k), available to any undergraduate computer science or engineering student, so the $10,000 amount was accurate for that cohort. However the 2025 cycle is closed (applications ended March 2025, program ended Aug 22 2025), replicate.com/startups now returns 404 with no startup or student credit program on Replicate's site, and Replicate is absent from YC's current 2026 AI Stack student deal list, so no new user can claim it as of 2026-10-22.

Offers

Replicate 'Try for Free' free runs (no-credit-card free tier) high confidence

New accounts can run a rotating set of select models a limited number of times without purchasing credit and without a credit card. Current 'Try for Free' collection: google/imagen-4, black-forest-labs/flux-1.1-pro, black-forest-labs/flux-dev, black-forest-labs/flux-kontext-pro, ideogram-ai/ideogram-v3-turbo, topazlabs/image-upscale, topazlabs/video-upscale, tencentarc/gfpgan, szcho/codeformer, minimax/video-01, luma/reframe-video. Replicate does NOT publish a dollar amount or exact run count; third-party trackers estimate ~50 free predictions total. It is a one-time allowance with no monthly reset.

Eligibility
Any new user who creates a Replicate account (GitHub, Google, or email sign-in). No .edu, no startup, and no credit card required to start. One account per person (farming multiple accounts to re-collect free runs is against the one-account rule). Global. Verified today on the official collection page + FAQ.
How to claim
1) Sign up at replicate.com (GitHub/Google/email — no card). 2) Go to replicate.com/collections/try-for-free. 3) Pick a model, upload/provide input, run it in the web playground or via API token (r8_...). 4) When the free cap is hit, Replicate asks you to add a credit card and purchase credit to continue. Claim is automatic on signup; no application.
Expiry
One-time allowance, no renewal/monthly reset. Models in the collection rotate over time. After the cap you move to pay-per-second or per-output billing (e.g., FLUX 1.1 Pro $0.04/image, FLUX dev $0.025/image, deepseek-r1 $0.01/1k output tokens).
Gotchas
Trial-vs-free: these are free RUNS, not a free subscription — once used you must add a card and buy credit. No published $ or exact run count. Each model keeps its own license that can restrict commercial use even when the run is free. Cardless accounts with granted credit are rate-limited to ~1 req/sec / ~6 req/min (no load testing); 600 predictions/min only once you have a paid account. Free set rotates, so a model you want may not currently be in it. A banner ('Wan 3.0 is 30% off this week') is a temporary discount, not a free offer.
Source
replicate.com

YC Summer Fellows (2025): Replicate $10,000 credits for students high confidence

In YC's 2025 Summer Fellows Grants, Replicate was one of the YC companies contributing $10,000 in compute credits to selected fellows (alongside Fly.io $50k, Supabase $10k, Resend $3k, plus YC's own $20k cash + $90k AWS/Azure/GCP credits). This is a real student-facing benefit tied to Replicate, but it is delivered through YC's grant, not a standalone Replicate program.

Eligibility
Undergraduate CS/engineering students, including freshmen, and students outside the US (global). Must be SELECTED for YC's Summer Fellows grant (apply; 15-min interview; YC estimated ~20-50 grants). It is merit/selection-based, not a .edu-email self-serve discount. This is a 2025 cohort (events Jun-Aug 2025).
How to claim
Apply to YC Summer Fellows Grants (ycombinator.com/blog/summer-fellows; the 2025 application deadline was end of March 2025). If selected, Replicate credits are part of the grant package — there is no self-serve claim on replicate.com. No company/business required; grant is to the individual student.
Expiry
2025 cohort only (now closed for that cycle). YC may run a new Summer Fellows round in a later year, possibly with different partner credits — the $10k Replicate figure is not guaranteed to recur.
Gotchas
It is YC's grant, not Replicate's education program; you cannot claim it by yourself on Replicate. Credits land via YC selection, not a .edu program. Do not confuse with Replit/Google education programs that DO offer .edu discounts.
Source
ycombinator.com

Replicate Startup Program (claimed up to $10,000 credits) — UNVERIFIED / DISPUTED low confidence

Third-party aggregators list a 'Replicate Startup Program' of up to $10,000 in inference credits (~$1,000-$10,000), ~6-month validity, pre-seed to Series A/B, no VC referral, global, company-domain email, no equity. HOWEVER: replicate.com/startups returns HTTP 404 (verified today), and at least one tracker states Replicate has no startup program. No official Replicate page confirms the program today, so this cannot be verified or claimed through an official channel.

Eligibility
As claimed by third parties only: AI/ML startup, pre-Series B / Series A, building a production product (not pure research), company-domain email; not Series C+; not research-only/academic.
How to claim
Per third parties: (1) create a Replicate account with a company-domain email + API token, (2) submit the startup application (form was said to be at replicate.com/startups), (3) receive credits on approval. Because replicate.com/startups 404s, this path is currently dead and the offer is not confirmable. Do not rely on it; contact Replicate sales if a startup is seeking volume terms.
Expiry
Unknown — no official terms or live application page. Treat as possibly sunset or never-officially-live.
Gotchas
CONFLICTING sources: the official URL is gone (404) while aggregators still advertise $10k. Low confidence it is a live, claimable program as of 2026-10-22. If it does exist it likely requires a company email + production use case. The startup-pricing alternative listed by trackers is to use Modal/Together AI/Fireworks programs instead, or ask Replicate for volume discounts.
Source
replicate.com

Replicate invite links can be pre-loaded with a small amount of credit granted by a partner/distributor (e.g., an IBM Granite invite that reads 'you've got $0.50 of credit to run and fine-tune models'). These are partner-specific and modest, not a general student or startup program.

Eligibility
The holder of the specific invite/partner link; sign-in (often GitHub) required to accept. Not gated on .edu or startup status.
How to claim
Open the specific partner invite URL and sign in as instructed (e.g., GitHub) to accept the credit.
Expiry
Per-invite; varies and not a recurring program.
Gotchas
Tiny amounts (fractions of a dollar); only applies for the specific partner invite; not a free tier you can self-serve. Illustrative only.

2025–26 changes

  • As of 2026-10-22: (1) Replicate's official free offer is the 'Try for Free' collection — a limited number of free runs on select models with no credit card and no published dollar amount or exact run count; it is a one-time allowance, not a recurring free tier. (2) replicate.com/startups returns 404 — so a live, self-serve Replicate Startup Program is NOT verifiable today, even though aggregators advertise up to $10k; treat that offer as disputed/possibly-sunset and prefer contacting Replicate sales or using Modal/Together AI/Fireworks startup programs. (3) No dedicated Replicate education/.edu program was found (unlike Replit or Google, which do run .edu programs). (4) The YC Summer Fellows 2025 program (Replicate $10k student credits) was a 2025 cohort and is closed for that cycle; it may recur in a later year with different partners/amounts. (5) Recurring 'Wan 3.0 30% off this week' banners on Replicate pages are temporary discounts, not free offers. Net: the only clearly-live, self-serve, card-free offer is the 'Try for Free' free-runs collection; all student/startup credit offers are either YC-cohort-gated (students) or unverifiable (startups).
