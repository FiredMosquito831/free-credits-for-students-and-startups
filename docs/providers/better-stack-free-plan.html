Better Stack Free Plan: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Better Stack Free Plan ("Free for personal projects"). 5 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Better Stack Free Plan ("Free for personal projects") high confidence

$0/month forever tier (verified on official pricing page fetched 2026-10-22, plus pricing.md copy and a PulseSignal change-history snapshot verified 2026-10-20). Exact free allowances: 10 monitors and 10 heartbeats combined; 1 public status page (1,000 status-page subscribers included; custom sub-domain on a betterstack subdomain or your own CNAME sub-domain listed in the feature set); 100,000 exceptions/month for error tracking; 5,000 session replays/month; 3 GB logs/month retained 3 days; 3 GB traces/month retained 3 days; 30 GB metrics; 3 GB web events/month retained 3 days; Slack and e-mail alerts only (no phone/SMS on free); on-call scheduling and basic single-channel Slack/Teams integration listed as included; 3-minute minimum check interval on free (30-second requires a paid Responder). Team member (Telemetry access) roles are $0/month — unlimited dashboard-only viewers free.

Eligibility
No student-specific or .edu requirement, no region limit: the free plan is open to anyone who signs up (e-mail/Google sign-up at betterstack.com/users/sign-up), but the plan is explicitly labeled "Free for personal projects" — signaling personal/non-commercial intent. No credit card is listed as required for the free plan (only the 60-day money-back guarantee, which applies to paid plans).
How to claim
1) Sign up free at betterstack.com/users/sign-up (no CC step advertised). 2) In the dashboard create up to 10 monitors + 10 heartbeats. 3) Create the included status page and attach monitors. 4) Add up to 3 GB/month logs, 30 GB metrics, 100K exceptions, 5K session replays under the free caps.
Expiry
No expiry — it is a permanent $0/month plan, not a trial. Paid plans carry a 60-day money-back guarantee; that does not apply to or alter the free tier.
Gotchas
1) Labeled for personal projects — commercial/startup production use may fall outside the stated intent of the free plan (no explicit commercial prohibition published on the pricing page). 2) Alerts on free are Slack + e-mail only; phone-call, SMS and unlimited push require a Responder license ($29/mo annual, $34/mo monthly billed). 3) Free check interval is 3 min vs 30 s paid; 4) log/trace/web-event retention is only 3 days; 5) status-page upgrades are steep (white-label, SSO, IP allowlisting $250/page/mo; custom CSS/JS $15/page/mo; extra page $15/mo). 6) Additional monitors cost $25/mo per 50-pack (monthly) and heartbeats $20/mo per 10-pack — the free plan is not a trial of paid volume.
Source
betterstack.com

Free Telemetry team seats (and new "Free responder access" entry, added 2026-10-06) medium confidence

On the official pricing page, the "Team member (Telemetry access)" role is $0/month with no stated seat cap, so any number of dashboard-only viewers is free. A third-party price tracker (PulseSignal, verified 2026-10-20) records that on 2026-10-06 the plan previously named "Free" was renamed "Free for personal projects" and a new plan entry "Free responder access" ($0) was added — i.e., Better Stack has recently added free responder-tier access for at least some users. I did not see a "Free responder access" row in today's pricing fetch, so treat the exact scope (all accounts vs. one free responder per account) as unconfirmed.

Eligibility
Free for any Better Stack account (team-member roles); the new free-responder entry's qualification rules are not published where I could read them.
How to claim
Invite extra team members as Telemetry-access members ($0 each). Check the plan picker at betterstack.com/pricing for the "Free responder access" option and select it when creating a team.
Expiry
No published expiry on the free roles; the free-responder entry is brand new (2026-10-06) and its terms may still be evolving.
Gotchas
Telemetry members cannot receive phone/SMS alerts or be on-call; anything on-call-capable is a paid Responder. The 2026-10-06 change means older third-party comparisons describing Better Stack's free offer are one revision stale.
Source
getpulsesignal.com

Startup deal via third-party aggregators ("6 months free Pro" on SaaSOffers; 25% off annual via Freelance Stack) — NOT a vendor-run startup program low confidence

betterstack.com/startups returns 404 and the pricing page contains no startup program. SaaSOffers (aggregator, self-labeled 'verified October 2026') advertises a '6 months free Pro' deal for startups (~$300 claimed value, 'accelerator-backed startups' eligibility described with generic template language); Freelance Stack advertises an 'additional 25% off annual plans' Better Stack partner promo for new customers. Neither is a Better Stack-published program with stated eligibility (funding cap, company age, headcount).

Eligibility
As documented by the aggregators only: SaaSOffers says qualifying/accelerator-backed startups, no referral needed, apply via the aggregator; no .edu or funding-cap criteria published by Better Stack itself. Region: global.
How to claim
SaaSOffers path: create a free SaaSOffers account (no CC) -> open saasoffers.tech/offers/betterstack -> click 'Claim Deal' and follow the activation steps to apply the discount to a new Better Stack paid plan. Freelance Stack path: sign up through their deal link, subscribe to a new account, then request cashback/discount per their instructions.
Expiry
Third-party deals; no vendor-published end date. '6 months' is the deal value itself, not a program duration; both aggregators note offers change frequently.
Gotchas
These are affiliate/deal-aggregator offers, not an official Better Stack startup program — eligibility and mechanics are enforced by the aggregator, not the vendor. No funding cap or age cap is documented anywhere on betterstack.com. A paid subscription is required to benefit, so this is a discount/trial-credit path, not a permanent free offer; if you outgrow the free plan, the free tier remains available independently.
Source
saasoffers.tech

Education/student program: none found (explicit negative) medium confidence

As of 2026-10-22 there is no Better Stack education license or student offer: betterstack.com/education is 404, the pricing page lists no student/education tier, the official docs FAQ mentions no education discount, and two independent price trackers explicitly mark Better Stack education terms as 'not verified/unknown'. Students can only use the general 'Free for personal projects' plan (finding 1). A nonprofit plan does exist ('We're happy to support non-profit organizations... apply for our non-profit plan' in the official docs FAQ) — adjacent, but not a student/startup offer.

Eligibility
n/a — no eligibility rules exist because no program exists.
How to claim
Nothing to claim for students. A student's only free options: the standard free plan (betterstack.com/users/sign-up) or the third-party aggregator deals (finding 3).
Expiry
n/a
Gotchas
Absence confirmed by negative evidence (404s + trackers saying 'not verified') as of 2026-10-22; Better Stack could introduce an education offer at any time. Do not cite SaaSOffers' '6 months free Pro' as a student offer — its stated audience is startups.
Source
betterstack.com

2025–26 changes

  • 2025-2026 changes, none of which sunsetting the free plan: (1) 2026-10-06 — the plan previously named "Free" was renamed "Free for personal projects" and a new "Free responder access" ($0) entry appeared (PulseSignal change history, vendor page verified 2026-10-20). (2) 2026-09-11 — Responder monthly price rose from $29/mo to $34/mo (annual rate stayed $29/mo) per FrontDeskReview's correction log. (3) 2026-08-01 — telemetry pricing was restructured into per-region bundles (US / Europe / Singapore) with old "Bundle" plan names removed. (4) 2026-08-25 — a standalone "Transaction Monitoring" plan was removed; transaction (Playwright) monitoring moved to per-minute usage pricing, and "1 responder included in the starting offer" was added to Responder terms. (5) Macro pivot 2025-2026: after an $18.6M Series A (led by Creandum), Better Stack stopped selling monitor-count tiers (old "Freelancer/Small Team/Business" names seen in third-party articles are stale) and now prices per Responder seat plus telemetry bundles — so third-party pages still showing "$24 Basic / $45 Freelancer" pricing are outdated. (6) The free-tier telemetry allowances have grown over time: secondary 2026 early-year sources cite 1 GB logs free; the current official page lists 3 GB logs, 3 GB traces, 30 GB metrics — no limits were removed, only expanded. (7) Rebrand context: Better Uptime + Logtail were merged into the "Better Stack" platform (2022-2023 press post); pricing survived the merge unchanged. (8) An adjacent non-profit plan exists per the official docs FAQ but no student or formal startup program ever existed on betterstack.com (betterstack.com/education and /startups both 404 as of 2026-10-22).
