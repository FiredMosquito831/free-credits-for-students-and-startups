Plaid: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free Sandbox (mock data, unlimited test Items). 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free Sandbox (mock data, unlimited test Items) high confidence

Sandbox is free with no time limit and unlimited test Items against mock data, covering all API endpoints so developers can test before going live. No credit card required. Confirmed on the official pricing page ('Start testing in sandbox for free') and the Plaid Help Center ('Plaid is free to use in the Sandbox environment').

Eligibility
Anyone - no .edu, no region limit stated for sandbox, no company formation needed. Students get full sandbox access with just a Plaid developer account.
How to claim
Sign up free at dashboard.plaid.com (pricing page 'Try it now' link), create a team, obtain sandbox client_id/secret, build and test against mock institutions and canned data.
Expiry
No expiry; sandbox access has no published duration limit.
Gotchas
Sandbox data is entirely mocked - no real bank data, no real OAuth institutions (Bank of America/Chase etc. connect only in Production/Trial). Going live requires the Trial plan, Limited Production, or a paid plan. Pay-as-you-go (first paid tier) requires a card on file, and once products are enabled in production the card is charged per connected items.
Source
plaid.com

Free Trial plan with real production data (new US/CA teams, up to 10 Production Items) high confidence

For Plaid teams in the US and Canada created on or after April 15, 2026, the Trial plan is free and supports up to 10 Production Items with real bank data. It bundles 8 products: Auth, Transactions (incl. Transactions Refresh), Balance, Identity, Assets, Liabilities, Investments (incl. Investments Refresh), Statements. Almost all OAuth institutions (incl. Bank of America, Chase, Wells Fargo) are accessible immediately without the full Production registration process.

Eligibility
New US or Canada teams created on/after 2026-04-15; account must be new to Production - teams with a pending/approved Production application or existing Limited Production access are NOT eligible, and there is no self-service switch. Credit card: the official FAQ says 'at no cost' but does not explicitly state CC-not-required; that part of the known-so-far claim is unverified on official pages. EU/UK teams are not covered (they get Custom plans only).
How to claim
Sign up at dashboard.plaid.com/signup; the Trial plan is applied automatically to eligible new US/CA teams - no separate application. To exceed 10 Items or add non-bundled products, apply for full Production access and choose a paid plan.
Expiry
No published expiry date; the 10-Item cap is a hard limit - scaling beyond it requires upgrading to a paid plan (Pay-as-you-go/Growth/Custom).
Gotchas
Trial is not the paid 'Limited Production': only 8 products bundled - Layer, Signal, Transfer, Protect, Identity Verification, Monitor, Enrich, Investments Move, and all Plaid Check (Consumer Report) products are NOT included. Some institutions need extra connection steps. EU/UK customers only get Custom plans - no free tier at all in those regions. Trial replaced Limited Production for new teams as of the 2026-04-15 cutoff, so 'limited free Production access' now means Trial for new accounts.
Source
support.plaid.com

Limited Production free tier - 200 live API calls per product (legacy teams created before 2026-04-15) high confidence

The official pricing page describes Limited Production as a free service: 'up to 200 API calls with each available product using live data.' Per the Help Center, this is the free live-data path for Plaid teams created BEFORE April 15, 2026; teams on/after that date get the 10-Item Trial plan instead. The 200-calls-per-product cap is the hard limit of the free live-data tier.

Eligibility
Existing US/CA Plaid teams created before 2026-04-15 that have not moved to paid Production. New teams cannot claim it - Trial plan is the replacement. No .edu or startup requirement.
How to claim
Already available to eligible legacy teams at no charge; new teams should instead get the free Trial plan at dashboard.plaid.com/signup.
Expiry
No sunset date published, but it is structurally legacy-only: the 2026-04-15 team-creation cutoff means the free tier stops being offered to new accounts.
Gotchas
200 live calls PER PRODUCT - easy to burn through; each product counts separately, and hitting the cap blocks further live calls until upgrading to a paid plan. It coexists with (and is replaced for new teams by) the Trial plan.
Source
plaid.com

Fintech Sandbox partner credits for startups (up to $21,000) high confidence

Through the Fintech Sandbox Data Access Residency, startups new to Plaid get 'up to $500 worth of each API endpoint per month for 6 months, with waived monthly minimums' - aggregate value up to $21,000. Eligibility is 'New customers only' on Plaid's side. The Fintech Sandbox residency itself is free ('$0 fees'), takes no equity, lasts up to 6 months, is global, and targets companies 'typically bootstrapped through Series A.'

Eligibility
Startup (early stage through ~Series A; one third-party guide cites Fintech Sandbox's published bar as a product or demo and revenue under $1M ARR - that figure comes from an aggregator, not the Fintech Sandbox page I read), global, incorporated company, and must be a NEW Plaid customer. No .edu; student status not required. No equity taken. Third-party guides describe a two-interview process (~25-min and ~45-min with demo).
How to claim
Apply to the Fintech Sandbox residency (fintechsandbox.org, 'Apply' / Data Access Residency form, interviews), get accepted, then claim the Plaid partner credit through the residency - terms are per Fintech Sandbox's partner page.
Expiry
Credits run for 6 months with monthly minimums waived during that window; the residency itself is 'up to 6 months' of free partner data access.
Gotchas
Credit value is per-endpoint ($500/endpoint/month), so the $21,000 maximum only materializes if you use many endpoints; some accelerators' versions of the deal carry a minimum-commitment rider (e.g., Aurelia Ventures lists 'Up to $30,000' as $500 x 10 products x 6 months BUT 'Requires minimum contract of $6,000/year'). Confirm which channel (Fintech Sandbox vs. your own accelerator/investor) grants the credits - terms differ.
Source
fintechsandbox.org

AI startup program (launched ~March 2026): free credits + lower minimums for early-stage AI startups medium confidence

Plaid launched a new program for AI-native startups in early 2026: 'free usage credits, production-ready tools, and lower minimums for AI startups that qualify' (announced via Plaid's LinkedIn, March 2026). The official page (plaid.com/support-for-early-stage-ai-startups/) offers 'Free credits to build and test,' 'Production-ready tools,' and 'Lower minimums to get started' - but publishes NO exact dollar amounts, no minimum thresholds, no term or expiry.

Eligibility
Only 'early stage AI startups' - no funding cap, company-age limit, or stage definition published. The application form restricts country to United States, United Kingdom, or European Union (relevant because EU/UK normally get Custom-only pricing). No .edu requirement.
How to claim
Fill the form on the official page (first/last name, company, company email, optional phone, country) and submit; Plaid reviews and 'gets back to you within 2 business days with next steps.' Reach out via sales per the March 2026 announcement.
Expiry
Not specified on the page - terms appear to be negotiated per applicant rather than a fixed self-serve grant.
Gotchas
This is a sales-gated program, not a self-serve free tier: all concrete numbers (credit size, minimums, duration) are undisclosed until Plaid responds. Do not conflate it with the free Trial plan. If you apply from EU/UK, the program's lower minimums matter because EU/UK customers otherwise only get Custom plans.
Source
plaid.com

Investor/accelerator channel credits (e.g., Aurelia Ventures: up to $30,000) medium confidence

VC funds and accelerators that partner with Plaid advertise Plaid credits for their portfolio. Example: Aurelia Ventures lists 'Up to $30,000 in Plaid Credits' structured as $500 x 10 products x 6 months, but with the rider '*Requires minimum contract of $6,000/year' and 'New Plaid customers only.' Per StartupPerks' audit (Feb 2026), Plaid startup programs broadly serve pre-seed to Series A fintech product teams, no referral needed, credits/fees 'program-dependent.'

Eligibility
Depends on the funding channel: being a portfolio company of a Plaid-partnered fund/accelerator (Aurelia's version) or qualifying via Fintech Sandbox; new Plaid customers; US-centric. No .edu.
How to claim
Ask your accelerator/investor for the Plaid partner credit (their partners page documents the deal), or route through Fintech Sandbox; terms are negotiated, so confirm the minimum-commitment rider before accepting.
Expiry
Typically 6 months of credits per partner page, but the Aurelia variant requires a $6,000/year minimum contract - a paid commitment, not pure free credits.
Gotchas
The $30,000 figure only looks free; the Aurelia rider converts it to a commitment-backed deal. The waived-minimums version (Fintech Sandbox, up to $21,000) is the cleaner no-commitment offer. Confirm current terms with Plaid - channel deals drift.
Source
aureliaventures.com

2025–26 changes

  • Material 2025-2026 changes: (1) 2026-04-15 cutover - Plaid split free live-data access by team creation date: teams created on/after Apr 15, 2026 get the new free Trial plan (10 Production Items, 8 bundled products, OAuth institutions) instead of Limited Production; Limited Production (free, 200 live calls per product) persists only for legacy teams created before that date, with no self-service switch between the two. (2) No student/education license found as of 2026-10-22 - plaid.com/education/ returns 404 and no .edu-gated program appears on official pages; students are covered only by the free Sandbox and, for US/CA teams, the free Trial plan. (3) Plaid Accelerate (2021-2022, founders-of-color seed cohort with free product access for ~5 months, last blog-announced cohort in 2022) appears to have ended and was superseded by the AI startup program announced around March 2026 (free credits + lower minimums for early-stage AI startups, US/UK/EU). (4) Plan lineup per the Help Center is now Trial (free), Pay-as-you-go, Growth, and Custom (also called Scale) - EU/UK customers are Custom-only and several products (Layer, Protect, Transfer, Signal Transaction Scores, Payment Initiation/Virtual Accounts, Plaid Check beyond Base Report/Income Insights) are unavailable on Pay-as-you-go. Note: the 'no credit card' part of the known-so-far claim for the Trial plan could not be confirmed on any official page I could reach - support.plaid.com blocked direct fetch (403), and the Trial terms came via the official Help Center article text; the pricing page states Pay-as-you-go requires a card on file, so treat CC-not-required as unverified for Trial specifically.
