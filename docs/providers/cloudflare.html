Workers AI free tier: 10,000 free Neurons/day (no signup offer needed) high confidence

Cloudflare's Workers Free AND Workers Paid plans each include 10,000 Neurons per day at no charge; the cap resets daily at 00:00 UTC; exceeding it makes further Workers AI requests fail. 72 models currently listed (39 LLM, 6 embeddings, 6 image, 10 audio, 11 other). Pricing page was restructured: "updated to be more granular, with per-model unit-based pricing presented" (per-model $/token, $/tile, $/minute alongside Neuron equivalents); backend billing still uses Neurons. On Workers Paid, usage above the daily allowance costs $0.011 per 1,000 Neurons; prepaid AI Gateway credits can also cover frontier-model usage and raise rate limits for those models.

Eligibility
Anyone with a Cloudflare account on the Workers Free plan (no credit card, no application, no .edu/funding requirements); the same allowance also ships with Workers Paid. US region: no student-only or startup-only gate.
How to claim
Create a free Cloudflare account (no payment method required for the Free plan) and call Workers AI (HTTP API or Worker binding). 10,000 Neurons/day auto-applies; nothing to redeem.
Expiry
Permanent free tier; daily 10k-Neuron window resets at 00:00 UTC. No fixed end date.
Gotchas
8 frontier models (kimi-k2.6, kimi-k2.7-code, glm-5.2, glm-5.3, glm-5.3-flash, deepseek-v4-flash-0731, deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017, deepseek-v4-pro-0813) require a paid billing method — unavailable on Free plan alone, even within the free Neuron allowance. Neurons-per-request varies by model and input/output direction, so the effective free daily quota in tokens differs widely per model; per-model caps also exist. On Paid, overage is $0.011/1k Neurons. Egress/data costs: Workers AI calls from a Worker are same-edge, but if your app is outside Workers, standard API call costs apply.

Cloudflare for Startups: up to $350,000 in credits (3 tiers: $10k / $100k / $350k) high confidence

Free at every tier: 3 enterprise domains, unlimited Pro/Business plans, DDoS/bot protection, WAF with 1,000 custom rules, CDN/DNS, LB (2 origins), 100% SLA. Business email matching company domain required; a valid payment method on file is required.

Eligibility
For-profit tech company incorporated within the last 10 years; funding up to Series B, funded within the last 12 months; live public website; active LinkedIn/X/GitHub; first-time program applicants only; business email matching your company domain (no personal email). Tier 2/1 require funding by an affiliated (approved VC/accelerator) partner.

1) Create a standard Cloudflare account and add a billing/payment method. 2) Fill the application at /lp/startups (company domain email, website, social link, Cloudflare account ID). 3) Manual review ~48 hours; on approval credits and plan upgrades are applied to the account. Expiry
Credits: 12 months from approval or until consumed; no renewal/extension offered (upgrading tier does not reset the 12-month window). Program expansion (Sept 2025) also covers non-profits/civil-society orgs (up to $250k, per Birthday Week 2025 announcement).

Workers AI sub-cap ($2.5k–$50k depending on tier) means most of the $350k is NOT spendable on Workers AI. AI Gateway excluded for now. Non-US regional pages (e.g. en-in) still show the outdated $250k/4-tier structure — the US .com page is authoritative.

Free: 100,000 key reads/day, 1,000 writes/deletes/lists/day, 1 GB stored data; limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Paid: 10M reads/mo (+$0.50/M), 1M writes (+$5.00/M), 1 GB (+$0.50/GB-mo).

Eligibility
Any free Cloudflare account; no credit card; no .edu or regional restriction.
How to claim
Create a KV namespace in the dashboard / `wrangler kv`; automatic.
Expiry
No expiry; daily reset. KV legacy API routes (/accounts/.../workers/namespaces/*) are deprecated — migrate to /storage/kv/namespaces/* before 2026-10-15.
Gotchas
The write cap (1,000/day) is the binding constraint for most apps; legacy API route deprecation deadline 2026-10-15 (past due as of 2026-10-22, so use the new routes).

Cloudflare Pages Free plan (free tier) high confidence

$0/mo, permanently free, no application. Static hosting: unlimited requests and unlimited bandwidth ('Requests to static assets are free and unlimited' — Workers platform pricing doc). Monthly/structural limits: 500 builds (deploys) per month, 1 concurrent build, 20-minute build timeout; up to 100 projects per account (limit 'not routinely increased'; new-project creation throttled for first 48h of a new account); 100 custom domains per project (free; Pro 250, Business/Enterprise 500); 20,000 files per project on Free (paid plans get 100,000 via env var PAGES_WRANGLER_MAJOR_VERSION=4); 25 MiB max per file; unlimited preview deployments; unlimited dashboard users; _headers 100 rules (2,000 chars/value); _redirects 2,100 total (2,000 static + 100 dynamic). No region limits stated in the limits doc. Docs last updated Sep 5, 2026; overview doc also states '500 deploys per month on the Free plan'.

Eligibility
Anyone with a Cloudflare account; no .edu, no company, no approval. No credit card required for the free plan in practice (dashboard sign-up is cardless) — this is not explicitly stated on the docs pages I read today, so treat the no-card sub-claim as medium confidence; the official limits/pricing docs simply never gate the Free plan on payment.
How to claim
Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com (no card), then deploy a Pages project via Git integration, C3 CLI, or direct upload. Project lands on the Free plan automatically; add a custom domain in the project's domains tab if needed.
Expiry
Permanent free tier, no expiration or renewal. Quotas reset monthly (builds) or daily (Workers-side quotas).
Gotchas
(1) Pages Functions are billed as Workers: free Workers plan = 100,000 requests/day and 10 ms CPU time per invocation, plus free quotas for KV (100k reads/1k writes, 1 GB), D1, Queues, DO — functions that exceed these fall under the paid $5/mo minimum or overage rates. Static-asset traffic stays free and unlimited regardless. (2) Large assets (>25 MiB) don't fit — use R2 public buckets instead. (3) 100-project cap is effectively a hard limit; beyond that Cloudflare points to Workers for Platforms. (4) Strategic shift 2025–2026: Pages was folded into the Workers platform — docs now say 'Start new projects with Workers' and 'Workers supports most Pages use cases'; Pages itself is not being sunset but new features land in Workers. (5) Paid plan column in the limits doc (Pro/Business) corresponds to the Workers platform pricing (from $5/mo min) — Pages alone has no standalone paid subscription; '100 custom domains/project' on Free is per-project, multiplied across up to 100 projects.
