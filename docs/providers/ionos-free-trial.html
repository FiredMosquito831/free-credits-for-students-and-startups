IONOS Free Trial: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: IONOS VPS '30-day free trial' (30-day money-back guarantee). 6 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

IONOS VPS '30-day free trial' (30-day money-back guarantee) high confidence

The 'Free VPS Trial' on IONOS's US site is a 30-day money-back guarantee, not a no-cost trial: you pay for the VPS up front (current VPS+ lineup: S+ $2/mo, M+ $5/mo, L+ $8/mo for 3 months with a 1-year term; older 'VPS' line from $2-4/mo on 3-year terms) and IONOS refunds 'no questions asked' if you cancel within 30 days. VPS includes unlimited traffic up to 1 Gbps, NVMe storage, wildcard SSL, KVM root access, Linux (AlmaLinux/Debian/Rocky/Ubuntu) or Windows Server 2022/2025. Not a perpetual free tier; renews into the paid term after the window.

Eligibility
New consumers/businesses; any customer. No .edu email, no startup age or funding cap. Payment card (credit card) IS required to start. US storefront sells into US, UK, German, Spanish data-center locations; other IONOS country sites (CA, UK, NZ) run the same 30-day trial with local pricing (e.g., CA adds a $15 setup fee; CA prices $3-6/mo).
How to claim
Order any VPS plan (e.g. VPS S+ at $2/mo, 3-mo intro, 1-yr term), use it up to 30 days, cancel for a full refund.
Expiry
30 days from order/activation; cancel inside the window for full refund. After that the 1-year term (or 3-year for older VPS line) applies with list prices.
Gotchas
Trial = refund offer, NOT free: CC charged immediately and refund processing is required; sales tax/state tax applies; Windows OS, extra IPv4 ($5/mo), Plesk ($6/mo US) and Cloud Backup (~$0.065/GB/mo) are add-ons that also factor into the effective cost; 'unlimited traffic' is only up to a 1 Gbit/s connection; US data-center availability is the practical region limit for the .com offer.
Source
ionos.com

$200 IONOS Cloud server credit ('free server hosting' trial, 30 days) high confidence

IONOS US 'Free Cloud Server Trial': packages contain starting credit of $200 (UK: £200) entitling the user to 30 days of free cloud-server hosting as long as booked resources don't exceed the credit. Cloud cubes are pay-as-you-go, billed down-to-the-minute: Basic Cube XS $5.76/30d, S $10.08/30d, M $18.72/30d, L $34.56/30d, XL $65.52/30d; Memory cubes $13.68-90.00/30d. Cubes available in EU data centers and Newark, NJ, USA. 'Free server hosting' marketing also appears on ionos.com/servers/free-server-hosting. HostingAdvice (updated Aug 2026) additionally describes $100 first-month credits for Compute Engine, S3 storage and Managed Kubernetes plans.

Eligibility
New IONOS cloud customers; no student/.edu requirement, no startup age or funding cap. Credit card required for the account (pay-as-you-go billing). Data-center locations: EU + Newark NJ for cloud cubes; other regions via IONOS CLOUD enterprise sales.
How to claim
Create an IONOS account, order a cloud server (Cube) sized so expected 30-day consumption stays under the $200 credit; no minimum contract, cancel anytime; if credit is exceeded you are billed only the difference.
Expiry
30 days or credit exhaustion, whichever first; account then converts to production billing automatically. Extensions only by contacting the account manager.
Gotchas
This one is genuinely free up to the credit (unlike the VPS refund trial) but the CC gets charged automatically if you exceed $200 or run past 30 days; Windows Server licenses (from ~$0.0072/vCPU/hr, i.e. ~$5.18/30d/vCPU) and RHEL licenses add on top of the base cube price; outbound traffic is metered; traffic/Windows/backup/object-storage usage is not shown in the dashboard and must be watched via the Billing API; no data-egress free allowance stated.
Source
ionos.com

IONOS CLOUD 30-day trial account (up to EUR 500 of resources), incl. private cloud/bare-metal via sales high confidence

Official IONOS CLOUD trial conditions: every customer can test the production environment. Default trial resources: 8 cores, 20 GB RAM, 600 GB HDD + 600 GB SSD Standard/Premium, 1 static IP; trial limited to 30 days OR maximum resource consumption of EUR 500. Account creation requires company + credit card details and phone verification; the CLOUD sales team calls within 24h on working days. Private cloud (VMware on dedicated hardware, i.e. the closest bare-metal/DCC-free trial) can also be tested free for 30 days 'contact IONOS CLOUD sales directly.' Trial accounts auto-convert to production with billing at expiry or when resources are exhausted.

Eligibility
Corporate/enterprise prospects ('every customer' may test); trial signup asks for company details and a credit card. No student or .edu path through this program. Region: IONOS CLOUD data centers incl. US (Las Vegas/DC-region mentions in docs), EU (Karlsruhe, Frankfurt); EU-only CLOUD regions possible.
How to claim
Apply via IONOS CLOUD sales (phone +49 30 57700 840 or beratung@cloud.ionos.de); private-cloud/bare-metal trial by speaking with the sales department; trial converts to production account automatically at expiry.
Expiry
30 days or EUR 500 consumption, whichever first; extensions require the account manager.
Gotchas
CC required and used for billing after conversion; resources, not just time, cap the trial (500 EUR); traffic, Windows licenses, backups, S3 storage aren't itemized in the dashboard - watch via Billing API; data must be deleted before trial end or it becomes billable production data; this is a separate enterprise funnel from the consumer VPS/cube trials.
Source
docs.ionos.com

IONOS CLOUD Startup Program (special offers for students, freelancers, startups) medium confidence

IONOS's official CLOUD FAQ confirms: 'Do you offer discounts on contracts? ... Does IONOS CLOUD have special offers for students, freelancers, or startups? We have special offers for students, freelancers, and startups. For more information, see IONOS CLOUD Startup Program.' The dedicated startup-program URL on ionos.com returned 404 today, so exact credit amounts, company-age or funding caps, and application steps are not publicly documented. Treat amounts as unknown/confirm-with-sales.

Eligibility
Per the FAQ, aimed at students, freelancers and startups; no .edu-only requirement stated publicly. Credit card likely required as with all IONOS CLOUD accounts. No published funding cap or company-age limit found.
How to claim
Contact IONOS CLOUD sales (beratung@cloud.ionos.de / +49 30 57700 840) to apply; no self-serve portal found on ionos.com.
Expiry
Unknown - not publicly documented.
Gotchas
Details (credit size, duration, renewal) require direct contact with IONOS CLOUD sales; region likely EU (the program sits on IONOS CLOUD, sold via German sales team); third-party 'IONOS startup credit' claims should be treated as unverified.
Source
docs.ionos.com

Rotating promos: free 1-year VPS with purchase / coupon-driven deals low confidence

IONOS has historically run rotating bundle promos such as 'VPS free for 12 months with a purchase' (typically a paid web hosting or domain purchase making the VPS free for its first year). No such 1-year-VPS bundle was visible on the pages fetched today (2026-10-22); the special-deals page currently shows 1-year-term deals (Website Builder $1/mo, WordPress $1/mo, Web Hosting $6/mo) but no free-12-month VPS bundle. Status: possibly inactive right now or gated behind checkout/coupon codes.

Eligibility
New customers; no student restriction; CC required; US storefront.
How to claim
Watch IONOS storefronts (ionos.com/special-deals, coupon-codes) and third-party deal aggregators; offers are time-boxed and rotating.
Expiry
Time-boxed promo; check the deals page for current codes.
Gotchas
'Free 1-year VPS' in these bundles usually means 12 months at $0 on top of a paid anchor purchase (hosting/domain), often with a longer-term commitment on the paid item afterwards; verify the promo's T&C at checkout, as IONOS rotates and expires these frequently.
