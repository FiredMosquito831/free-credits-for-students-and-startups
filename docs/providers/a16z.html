a16z: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: AWS Activate Portfolio Credits via a16z Org ID (up to $200K). 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

AWS Activate Portfolio Credits via a16z Org ID (up to $200K) high confidence

a16z is a confirmed AWS Activate Provider (listed as 'Andreesen Horowitz' in the Featured Activate Providers, alongside nvidia, carta, Y Combinator). a16z-backed startups use a16z's secure Organization ID to claim AWS Activate Portfolio credits: up to $200,000 USD in AWS Activate Credits at the Activate Portfolio (Pre-Series B) tier. Self-funded startups (no provider) are limited to the Activate Founders tier at up to $5,000. Invite-only AWS Credits for AI Startups ($200K+) are available post-Portfolio. All credits in USD under AWS Promotional Credit T&Cs.

Eligibility
Must be backed/referred by an AWS Activate Provider (here a16z = a16z portfolio company or a16z referral). Pre-Series B, founded within the last 10 years, AWS account on the Paid Tier plan, and either new to Activate Credits or requesting more than previously received. No .edu email; global program (US-centric). Self-funded founders are NOT eligible for the Portfolio tier.
How to claim
1) Create an AWS Builder ID with a professional email and complete your profile; 2) choose the Activate Portfolio tier; 3) enter a16z's Org ID (request it from a16z); 4) provide startup product/funding/profile details; 5) link an AWS account and submit. Response in 5-10 business days.
Expiry
Credits governed by AWS Promotional Credit Terms & Conditions; portfolio credits are usable within a defined window from issuance (exact window not stated on the public page).
Gotchas
Requires a live provider relationship (a16z investment/referral) and an Org ID; Paid Tier plan required; pre-Series B cap; self-funded startups are capped at $5K via the separate Founders tier; additional AI-startup credits are invite-only via an account manager.
Source
aws.amazon.com

a16z Speedrun Marketplace credits ($10M+ pool across AWS/GCP/OpenAI/Anthropic/Microsoft/NVIDIA/Stripe/Deel) medium confidence

a16z's Speedrun residency (an a16z operating/accelerator program for early-stage startups) advertises a '$10M+ in free credits' speedrun Marketplace spanning AWS, GCP, OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Stripe, Deel and others. Other perks: dedicated advisors/experts, Launch Lab, Brand Lab, Creator Connect, Press Club, talent matchmaking, immigration support (Global Founders Program), in-person Demo Day and access to a network reaching 1,000+ investors. No per-founder dollar cap is disclosed — '$10M+' is the aggregate program pool.

Eligibility
Must be accepted into an a16z Speedrun residency; open to founders/companies worldwide, but the program takes place in the US (in-person Demo Day + residency component). No revenue/stage/headcount thresholds are published.
How to claim
Apply to Speedrun (a16z.com/speedrun); specific credit allotments and claim steps are shared post-acceptance. Next cohort SR008: Feb 1 - Apr 16/18, 2027 in San Francisco; applications close Nov 1, 2026.
Expiry
No credit or perk expiry dates disclosed on the public page.
Gotchas
Entirely gated behind residency acceptance (not open/self-serve); in-person/US component (international founders use the Global Founders Program); per-founder credit amounts, claim mechanics, and post-program access windows are withheld until after acceptance.
Source
speedrun.a16z.com

a16z x Alchemy onchain Startup Program ($5,000 AWS credits + Alchemy credits + 24/7 engineering support) medium confidence

An Alchemy co-branded program for a16z portfolio companies building onchain: qualified companies receive Alchemy credits applied directly to their account (offsetting pay-as-you-go or enterprise invoices) plus $5,000 in AWS credits and 24/7 dedicated engineering support on the infrastructure used by Visa/Stripe/Robinhood.

Eligibility
a16z portfolio company building onchain (eligibility subject to team qualification). To receive the AWS credits specifically: pre-Series B, a company website/profile, founded in the last 10 years, an AWS account on the Paid Tier plan, NOT previously having received $5,000+ in AWS credits, and an active Alchemy Enterprise account.
How to claim
Fill out the form at alchemy.com/startup-program/a16z (mention a16z portfolio status to fast-track review); no pitch deck or lengthy application; team qualifies and credits can hit the account within days. Non-a16z teams are directed to the general Alchemy Startup Program.
Expiry
No expiry date stated on the page.
Gotchas
Onchain/blockchain-building only; requires an active Alchemy Enterprise account; the AWS-credit leg is blocked for teams that already received $5K+ in AWS credits (explicit anti-double-dip), so it complements rather than stacks with the main AWS Activate award.
Source
alchemy.com
