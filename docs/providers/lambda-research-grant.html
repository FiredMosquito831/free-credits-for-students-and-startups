Lambda Research Grant: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Lambda Research Grant (up to $5,000 GPU cloud credits for researchers). 2 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Lambda Research Grant (up to $5,000 GPU cloud credits for researchers) high confidence

Lambda Research Grant: up to $5,000 in Lambda cloud (GPU) credits for developing and showcasing research work on Lambda Instances. Rolling, competitive; select accepted projects get featured on lambda.ai. The page markets the grant as backing named research efforts ("Breakthroughs backed by Lambda"). Official pages read today: https://lambda.ai/research (amount + application link), https://lambda.ai/pricing (no other free offers), https://lambda.ai/instances (no startup/student program), https://lambda.ai/legal/terms-of-service (credit terms).

Eligibility
Page states only "qualifying researchers" — no explicit .edu requirement, student-vs-postdoc distinction, startup criteria, funding cap, or region limits are published on the page. Selection is competitive/discretionary. The "Startups and Researchers" nav label on the homepage simply routes to /instances; it is a product audience segment, not a program with benefits.
How to claim
Apply via the TypeForm linked on https://lambda.ai/research ("Apply for a grant" / "Apply now"), form ID form.typeform.com/to/Wn8YWMIj. No account or payment info is asked at the form level as far as the public page shows; Lambda contact/sales ("Talk to our team") handles the rest.
Expiry
No application deadline or program end date published (rolling intake). Per Lambda Cloud Terms of Service, issued credits expire 12 months from issuance unless the credit instrument states an earlier date, and Lambda may revoke or expire credits at any time for any or no reason.
Gotchas
Credits have no cash value, are non-transferable, and can be revoked early (ToS §3d). A payment method is required on the account generally (ToS Hardware §7.2: valid credit card or accepted payment method), so the grant is compute offset, not a card-free account. Mentorship mentioned in prior notes is NOT stated on the current public page — only "featured on our website" is promised. Web search was unavailable in this run, so 2025-2026 program changes/sunsets of any earlier Lambda startup program or trial-credit offers could not be independently verified; only live-page evidence is reported.

No standing free tier, trial credits, startup program, or education license (verified absence) high confidence

Verified absence of standing free offers: the pricing page lists only on-demand per-GPU-hour rates (e.g., H100 SXM $3.99/GPU-hr, B200 $6.69/GPU-hr, A100-40GB $1.99, A10 $1.29, "plus applicable sales tax/VAT/GST") with no free tier, trial credits, education license, or startup credit program. /startups, /education, /labs all return HTTP 404. Sign-up (cloud.lambda.ai) is app-rendered and blocked direct inspection (403); no promotion is advertised on the homepage or pricing page. The only "free" mechanisms found: (a) ToS-permitted Beta Services offered "at no additional charge" while designated beta (trial expires at the earlier of 1 year or Lambda's written date), and (b) one-off event giveaways on lambda.ai (AI Infra Summit Giveaway 2026, 2026 GTC Berlin Giveaway, 2025 Microsoft Ignite Cloud Credit Giveaway) — attendee/contest-style, not standing programs for students or startups. Free educational resources (deep learning curriculum, Open-Sora/diffusion notebooks, Lambda Stack software) are content, not compute credits. Marketing note: instances advertise "no egress fees", which is a cost perk, not a free offer.

Eligibility
n/a — this finding documents that no free tier, trial credit, startup program, or education license exists as of 2026-10-22 outside the Research Grant.
How to claim
Nothing to claim. Event giveaways are one-off and attendee-restricted; the only standing free compute path for students/researchers is the Research Grant.
Expiry
n/a
Gotchas
Beta-service free access ends when a feature leaves beta or at the 1-year trial cap. Event giveaway pages may be stale by the time you read them. Because search was unavailable, if a legacy startup program existed pre-2025 I could not confirm its status; the 404s on /startups and /labs plus the absence on pricing indicate it is not currently offered.
