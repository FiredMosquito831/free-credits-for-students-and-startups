Cerebras Inference Free Trial: free credits and offers

Best offer on record: Free Trial: one-time $5 credit for new accounts. 4 verified findings, checked 2026-10-22.

Offers

Free Trial: one-time $5 credit for new accounts high confidence

New accounts receive a one-time $5 promotional credit, usable across all public models (currently gpt-oss-120b and qwen-3.8-27b). Free Trial rate limits per model: 5 RPM, 30K uncached TPM, 90K total TPM, 1M tokens/hour, 1M tokens/day; qwen-3.8-27b vision capped at 2 images per request / 10 MiB payload. Free context is truncated vs paid: gpt-oss-120b 65k vs 131k, qwen-3.8-27b 64k vs 128k. Official changelog entry 2026-07-16 ('Free Trial credits for new accounts') introduced it; it replaces the prior always-free tier.

Eligibility
Any new account — no .edu email, no startup status, no funding cap. A verified payment method (credit/debit card) IS required before Playground/API activate; you are not charged unless you buy PayGo credits. No region limits stated on official pages (one third-party tracker notes access is intended outside mainland China, unverified).
How to claim
Sign up for Cerebras Inference → add a valid payment method (activates Playground + API and grants the $5 credit) → build within Free Trial limits; a first PayGo credit purchase upgrades the account to Developer tier (much higher limits, e.g. gpt-oss-120b 1K RPM / 1M uncached TPM; no hourly/daily caps).
Expiry
Credit expires 30 days after it is granted; no renewal. When the credit is exhausted or expires, API and Playground access pause (keys/projects/settings stay intact); you must purchase PayGo credits to resume.
Gotchas
Card required despite being a 'free' trial (unlike most free tiers); credits are non-transferable, no cash value, and may be withheld/revoked for fraud, abuse, or duplicate accounts; 5 RPM breaks any agent that fires parallel tool calls (instant 429s); limits are per organization and per model; 'free' context windows are shorter than paid; model menu keeps shrinking (GLM-4.7 and Gemma 4 31B no longer on public endpoints as of Aug/Sep 2026); many guides still quote the old '1M free tokens/day' tier which no longer exists.
Source
cerebras.ai

YC Startup Deal: up to $22,500 in Cerebras Cloud inference credits high confidence

YC startups from any batch receive up to $22.5K worth of inference on Cerebras Cloud, plus priority support and co-marketing opportunities. Official page states 'No setup fees, no strings.' No published credit expiration, deadline, or per-startup amount caps.

Eligibility
YC (Y Combinator) startups from any batch — the current offer is YC-only on the official page. No funding/age caps stated. Non-YC startups are instead routed through Cerebras channel partners.
How to claim
Apply via the 'Apply Now' button on https://www.cerebras.ai/yc-startup-deal (links to a HubSpot application form); an RSVP button on the same page points to the same form.
Expiry
No end date or credit-expiry stated on the official page; 'up to $22.5K' is discretionary and no separate terms page is on record.
Gotchas
Eligibility is YC-batch-only (the earlier open 'Cerebras for Startups' program is marked no longer available on third-party trackers); the exact credit amount you receive is not published — 'up to $22,500'; co-marketing commitment is mentioned but undefined.
Source
cerebras.ai

No student/education program exists (negative finding) medium confidence

As of 2026-10-22 there is no .edu/student or academic licensing offer from Cerebras. Neither the pricing page, the inference docs, nor searches of 2025-2026 coverage surface a student discount, education license, or academic credit program. The only education-adjacent item is a free DeepLearning.AI short course 'Fast LLM Inference with Cerebras' (built with Cerebras, launched ~July 2026) — instructional content, no API credits.

Eligibility
N/A — nothing to claim for students specifically; students use the standard $5 trial (card required) or Cerebras models hosted on partner platforms (OpenRouter, HuggingFace, AWS Marketplace), where the partner's own free allowances may apply.
How to claim
None on Cerebras itself; students may take the free DeepLearning.AI course (enroll at deeplearning.ai, free) for hands-on Cerebras inference learning.
Expiry
N/A
Gotchas
No .edu verification anywhere in Cerebras's onboarding; if a tool offers 'Cerebras student credits', it is not an official Cerebras program. Partner front-ends (OpenRouter free models, HuggingFace free tier) are how cardless users actually reach Cerebras-hosted models for free.

Expiry
Unknown; no stated deadline in the third-party source.

2025–26 changes

  • 1) The old always-free tier ("1M free tokens/day", no card required — announced June 2025 when Cerebras opened the API without a waitlist) ended in July 2026: the official changelog shows the $5 Free Trial credit launching 2026-07-16 and a third-party tracker (pricepertoken.com) dates the old free tier's end to 2026-07-21. Cerebras now states plainly there is no permanently free or auto-renewing tier; a verified payment method is required for any access. 2) The earlier general "Cerebras for Startups" program (up to $22,500 GPU/inference credits, direct application, no partner required) is marked "No Longer Available" on third-party trackers; the surviving startup offer is the YC Startup Deal (YC startups any batch, up to $22.5K), recorded on the official site as of early Aug 2026. 3) Public shared-inference model menu shrunk through 2025-2026: Llama 3.3 70B and Qwen 3 32B deprecated (Feb 2026), zai-glm-4.7 deprecated 2026-08-17, gemma-4-31b removed from public endpoints 2026-09-03 (dedicated only), qwen-3.8-27b added 2026-09-03; as of Oct 2026 the public catalog is just gpt-oss-120b and qwen-3.8-27b. 4) Cerebras Code Pro ($50/mo, ~24M tokens/day) and Code Max ($200/mo, ~120M tokens/day) flat-rate coding plans were sold out / on waitlist at time of reporting — neither is free. 5) No region restrictions are stated on official pages; third-party trackers suggest access outside mainland China.

Critic corrections

  • Cerebras Inference Free Trial: Renamed and restricted. The Free Trial was merged into one Developer tier in Sept-Oct 2026. The pricing page now describes that tier as for development, evaluation, and experimentation and not intended for production use, so production needs an Enterprise contract. The one-time $5 credit (needs a valid payment method, expires 30 days after activation) still matches.
