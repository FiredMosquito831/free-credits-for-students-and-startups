Frequently asked questions

Are these offers free?

Most are free tiers, trial credits, startup programs or student offers. Each has its own limits, duration and eligibility, listed on the provider page here.

Do I need a credit card?

Some offers say no card is required. Read the eligibility line for each one, because some ask for a card at a later step.

Who can use startup programs?

Usually companies that meet criteria such as incorporation, funding stage or an accelerator invitation. Check each program’s eligibility text.

How were the offers checked?

Research agents read provider pages during the sweep and rated each finding high, medium or low confidence. 45 headline offers were re-checked against official pages; the verdicts are on the live checks page.

How often is this updated?

This is a snapshot as of 2026-10-22. It is not updated automatically, and offers change, so check the provider before you claim.

Is this affiliated with the providers?

No. This is independent research. Provider names are used only to identify the services.
