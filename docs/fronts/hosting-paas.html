15 providers and 226 verified findings for this front, as of 2026-10-22.

Mapping summary. Frontend/edge hosting and app-deployment PaaS that, as of 2026-10-22, has a genuine free tier, free-trial credit, student offer, or startup credit program — verified against official pages. 2025–26 shifts worth knowing: Netlify moved all new accounts to credit-based plans (Sep 4, 2025; Free = 300 credits/mo, thin ~15 GB); Railway restored a permanent $1/mo Free plan (Aug 27, 2025) after running it down in 2024; Render flattened Pro to $25/mo with unlimited seats and cut Hobby bandwidth from 100 GB to 5 GB (Apr 23, 2026); Koyeb (now under Mistral AI, Feb 2026) closed its free Starter plan to new users; Vercel's Hobby is strictly non-commercial. Defunct / no genuine free offer in this front: Glitch (stopped hosting projects Jul 8, 2025 under Fastly), Heroku (no free tier since Nov 2022; from $5/mo "Eco" dyno), Cyclic (cyclic.sh redirects to GitHub; effectively down), Deta (Hugging Face–acquired; deta.space unreachable), and Kinsta / Pantheon / Pika / Cloudron (no perpetual free tier for new users). Entries 13–16 are from secondary 2026 comparisons (Northflank, Val Town, Puter, HF Spaces) — confirm on the vendor page before relying on them.
