GPU, ML training & notebooks

21 providers and 310 verified findings for this front, as of 2026-10-22.

Mapping summary. GPU, ML training & notebooks — free tiers, trial credits, student offers and startup programs, verified against official pages (checked 2026-10-22). Verified live: Kaggle 30h/wk GPU quota, Colab free T4 (dynamic limits, 12h sessions), HF Spaces free ZeroGPU (5 min/day; as of July 2026 all new compute Spaces incl. CPU Basic are paywalled, free accounts keep only 2 ZeroGPU Spaces + free Static), Paperspace free M4000 notebooks, Modal $30/mo Starter, GCP $300/90d trial + e2-micro, Azure $200/30d + B1s 750h/mo x12mo, AWS new $200/6mo free plan (old p3 12h/mo free tier ended July 2025). NOT free (checked 2026-09/10, official pages show nothing free): TensorDock (no free credit), Vast.ai (no free credit), DataCrunch — now rebranded Verda (pure pay-as-you-go, no free tier/credit), Spheron (paid per-minute marketplace, H100 ~$2.98/hr; only sporadic community token giveaways), RunPod for ordinary users (no free tier; only a $1,000 startup-tier credit and enterprise/academic programs). Also closed: AWS SageMaker Studio Lab (free Jupyter+GPU) and Colab Pro for Education, both closed to new signups 2026-07-30. Lambda has no signup credit — only its competitive up-to-$5,000 Research Grant for academic researchers. Local open-source runtimes (Ollama, llama.cpp, LM Studio, GPUStack, SGLang) remain free-for-all on your own GPU.
